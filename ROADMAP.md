# Eco Agent System Roadmap

Eco Agent System is an open-source prototype for translating environmental system observations into practical decision-support logic for planning, project delivery, ESG interpretation, climate resilience, and long-term environmental maintenance.

This roadmap describes the intended development direction. The project is currently experimental and should be treated as a human-reviewed decision-support framework, not as an autonomous control system.

---

## Phase 1 — Core Prototype

The first phase establishes a simple working structure:

```text
Environmental input
↓
Eco Decision DNA
↓
Planning Agent
↓
Delivery Agent
↓
Operations Agent
```

The goal is to test whether environmental conditions such as water, heat, vegetation, resilience, and maintenance needs can be translated into clear planning and operational decisions.

Current priorities:

- keep the prototype simple and readable
- describe environmental inputs in structured form
- connect physical environmental logic with professional decision roles
- provide basic CLI examples for testing and demonstration
- avoid overstating AI capability or replacing expert judgement

---

## Phase 2 — Agent Framework Expansion

The second phase will expand the prototype into a clearer multi-agent framework.

Potential agent modules include:

- Planning Agent — feasibility, land-use, environmental suitability, ESG screening
- Delivery Agent — project implementation risks, construction constraints, site coordination
- Operations Agent — long-term maintenance, monitoring, adaptation, and resilience review
- ESG Interpretation Agent — translation between environmental evidence and governance reporting
- Resilience Review Agent — stress testing for climate, water, heat, vegetation, and disaster exposure

The purpose is not to create a fully autonomous decision-maker. The purpose is to support better human judgement through structured environmental reasoning.

---

## Phase 3 — Eco Chain Contribution Logic

Eco Chain is treated in this project as a contribution-recording and environmental value interpretation concept.

It explores how environmental actions, field observations, maintenance work, risk-reduction activities, and resilience outcomes can be recorded, compared, and reviewed as long-term ecological contribution.

This phase may explore:

- contribution records for environmental maintenance
- links between actions and observed environmental outcomes
- transparent records of local ecological work
- non-speculative ecological value interpretation
- human-reviewed evidence trails for public-good projects

This project does not promote speculative token activity. Eco Chain is not presented here as a financial product. It is treated as a governance and evidence-recording logic for environmental contribution.

---

## Phase 4 — Climate Risk, Insurance and Resilience Finance Interface

A future direction of the project is to explore how climate prediction, microclimate governance, and long-term maintenance records may support climate-risk reduction evidence.

The practical logic is:

```text
Climate and weather prediction
↓
Microclimate and environmental intervention
↓
Risk-score reduction
↓
Maintenance and monitoring records
↓
Evidence for insurers, local governments, communities, and resilience-finance partners
```

This phase may include:

- climate and weather risk indicators
- bushfire, flood, drought, heat, and storm risk interpretation
- microclimate monitoring and intervention logic
- risk-score reduction models
- maintenance evidence for environmental assets
- structured reports for local governments, land managers, communities, and insurers
- early interfaces for climate insurance and resilience finance collaboration

The project does not provide insurance, financial advice, actuarial certification, or investment products. It aims to build an open decision-support and evidence-recording framework for climate adaptation, disaster-risk reduction, and long-term environmental resilience.

---

## Phase 5 — Integration with Simulation, GIS and Digital Spatial Tools

Eco Agent System should not exist as an isolated tool. A long-term goal is to connect with existing and emerging software used in environmental modelling, spatial planning, engineering simulation, and urban data analysis.

Future integration directions may include:

- GIS platforms for spatial data, land-use analysis, and regional mapping
- CFD and fluid simulation tools such as ANSYS Fluent or equivalent systems
- hydrology, terrain, vegetation, and heat-flow modelling tools
- digital twin and smart city data platforms
- urban-scale and regional-scale environmental simulation systems
- open data standards for climate, land, water, and infrastructure datasets
- APIs or export formats that allow Eco Agent System to read from or write to other modelling environments

The long-term vision is to create an interface layer between traditional spatial simulation tools and agent-based decision-support systems.

In this direction, the project may help translate complex simulation outputs into practical questions such as:

- What risk is increasing?
- Which area needs maintenance first?
- What intervention may reduce climate exposure?
- What evidence should be recorded for governance, insurance, or community resilience?
- How can technical modelling become understandable to planners, land managers, and local communities?

---

## Phase 6 — Open Field Pilots and Community Governance

The project may later support field pilots in rural, dryland, agricultural, and small-town contexts.

Possible pilot themes include:

- regional climate resilience
- dryland and semi-arid environmental management
- water, heat, vegetation, and soil monitoring
- bushfire and flood preparedness
- community infrastructure maintenance
- environmental planning for small towns and agricultural regions

Governance principles:

- public-good first
- human review before automation
- transparent environmental reasoning
- respect for local knowledge
- environmental responsibility before financial interpretation
- practical usefulness over technical complexity

---

## Current Status

Eco Agent System is an early-stage prototype. Contributions are welcome in documentation, agent logic, environmental indicators, regional use cases, modelling interfaces, climate-risk interpretation, and open-source governance design.
