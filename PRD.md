# ClimateOS Product Requirements Document

## Product Name

ClimateOS

## Positioning

ClimateOS is an evolving Earth System Governance Runtime architecture and a
Reality-to-Evidence Engineering Runtime architecture.

It is not a dashboard, reporting platform, NASA data viewer, carbon accounting
tool, completed system, or finished software application.

ClimateOS is being built as a structured repository and framework for moving
from Natural Reality, Operational Reality, and Governance Reality toward
evidence, validation, governance, and future Proof of Reality systems.

## Primary Audience

- Project maintainers

## Secondary Audience

## Problem

Reality is observed through many disconnected sources: satellites, weather
agencies, sensors, community notes, local projects, field records, governance
records, operational records, and future scientific engines.

These observations often remain isolated. They are difficult to connect into
relationships, evidence, validation history, governance language, or long-term
decision memory.

ClimateOS addresses the architecture gap between reality data, evidence,
validation history, and governed response.

## Vision

ClimateOS provides an Earth System Governance Runtime architecture that connects:

```text
Natural Reality + Operational Reality + Governance Reality
-> Reality Data Assets
-> External Observation Sources
-> Observation Layer
-> Relationship Layer
-> Radar Layer
-> Evidence Layer
-> Validation Layer
-> ClimateOS Runtime
-> EcoEngine
-> Governance
-> EcoChain
-> Proof of Reality
-> RWA
```

The long-term vision is to make environmental reality traceable, reviewable,
explainable, and usable for governance without pretending that early framework
documents are already operational systems.

## Core Concepts

### Observation

Observation records what happened. Observation sources may include NASA,
Copernicus, ECMWF, BOM, Open-Meteo, local sensors, community observation, and
future providers.

### Relationship

Relationship explains why something may have happened by connecting systems
such as rainfall, soil moisture, vegetation, temperature, fire risk, water flow,
and human activity.

### Radar

Radar identifies what is changing. Radar is a change detection framework, not a
search engine, news feed, alert engine, or monitoring implementation.

### Evidence

Evidence is not raw data, a document, or a report. Evidence is a synthesized
relationship structure that can be reviewed and strengthened or weakened over
time.

### Reality Data Assets

Reality Data Assets are records generated from Natural Reality, Operational
Reality, and Governance Reality.

Earth-System Related RDA also includes planning reality, infrastructure,
resource-flow, energy, water, land, agriculture, carbon, ESG, and operational
process records when they remain traceable to real-world reality.

Forecast outputs are Forecast Candidates, not truth, until validated.

### Evidence Assets

Evidence Assets combine Reality, Data, Evidence, Context, Validation, History,
and Confidence.

### Proof of Reality

Proof of Reality treats all statements as Reality Claim Candidates until they
are supported by evidence packages, cross-validation, confidence
classification, and traceability.

It does not assume prediction equals truth, a single witness equals false, or
crowd consensus equals truth.

### Validation

Validation is not approval or auditing. Validation is systematic evaluation of
evidence quality, confidence, uncertainty, transparency, and traceability.

### Governance

Governance responds to validated reality. Governance capability is currently
architectural and conceptual, not an automated decision system.

## System Relationships

### ClimateOS And EcoEngine

ClimateOS and EcoEngine are separate systems.

ClimateOS provides governance runtime architecture, observation management,
relationship analysis framework, evidence management, validation framework, and
governance coordination.

EcoEngine provides scientific computation capability such as future threshold
detection, boundary detection, pattern discovery, and scientific modelling.

```text
ClimateOS
-> Call
-> EcoEngine
```

ClimateOS may use EcoEngine. ClimateOS does not replace EcoEngine. EcoEngine
does not replace ClimateOS.

### CCZPS

CCZPS is the climate zone reasoning framework. It supports climate zone
interpretation, spatial reasoning, and context generation.

### ESG++

ESG++ is the governance translation layer. It connects environmental reality
with governance language, reporting context, and decision-making.

### EcoChain

EcoChain is the validated evidence chain. It supports traceability, validation
history, and the future Proof of Reality foundation.

## Product Requirements

### PRD-1: Architectural Clarity

The repository must clearly describe ClimateOS as an evolving Earth System
Governance Runtime architecture.

### PRD-2: Layered Reasoning

Documentation should preserve the flow from observation to relationship, radar,
evidence, validation, governance, EcoChain, Proof of Reality, and RWA.

### PRD-3: Provider Independence

ClimateOS must not depend on a single external data provider.

### PRD-4: Engine Independence

ClimateOS must treat EcoEngine and future engines as external or separate
scientific computation systems.

### PRD-5: Human Review

All evidence, validation, governance, and external data concepts should remain
human-reviewable.

### PRD-6: Transparency

Future calculations must document formula, inputs, outputs, units, assumptions,
scientific basis, limitations, validation method, and calculation type.

### PRD-7: Documentation Before Runtime

Foundational tasks should document scope, boundaries, and review requirements
before executable systems are added.

## Non-Goals

ClimateOS does not currently aim to be:

- a finished application
- a dashboard
- a NASA data viewer
- a single-provider data platform
- a standalone scientific computation engine
- a replacement for EcoEngine
- a regulatory approval system
- a financial recommendation system
- an automated decision system

## Success Criteria

Near-term success means:

- Future contributors understand what ClimateOS is and is not.
- AI agents can work safely in the repository.
- Foundational layers remain consistent and reviewable.
- Documentation distinguishes implemented, planned, and vision work.
- Provider and engine independence are preserved.
- Tests continue to pass after documentation-only tasks.
