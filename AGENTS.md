## Project Direction

Eco Agent System is an experimental environmental decision-support project.

The project explores how environmental observations can be translated into planning, resilience, maintenance, governance, and climate-risk decision support.

The goal is not to replace human judgement.

The goal is to support more transparent, reviewable, and environmentally informed decisions.

---

## Working Principles

When contributing to this repository:

1. Keep changes aligned with environmental decision support.
2. Prefer transparent reasoning over hidden assumptions.
3. Avoid exaggerated claims about AI capability.
4. Preserve clarity for both technical and non-technical users.
5. Document important assumptions and limitations.
6. Support long-term maintenance and resilience thinking.
7. Respect environmental evidence and physical constraints.
8. Encourage human review before critical decisions.

---

## Preferred Contribution Areas

Useful contributions may include:

* climate-risk interpretation
* GIS integration
* environmental modelling interfaces
* resilience assessment methods

## Final Reminder

When uncertain:

* explain assumptions
* document reasoning
* seek clarity
* prioritize maintainability

A simple, understandable solution is often preferable to a complex solution that cannot be maintained.
