ClimateOS begins with a simple belief:
care for a place is not a single prediction, or a single report.
It is a relationship that grows over time.

For years, Simon has been building toward this idea through EcoAgent,
EcoChain, evidence-first claims, Eco Decision DNA, CCZPS-Lite,
and now ClimateOS.
Build Week did not create that history.
It gave us a chance to make part of it visible and usable.

During Build Week, Simon worked with Codex and GPT-5.6
to turn this foundation into a human–AI runtime.
The first version was technically functional,
but Simon's hands-on review found that it did not yet feel like meaningful environmental work.

We did not erase the foundation.
We learned from the failure and changed the experience.

The current demonstration begins with a real question about climate,
fire, drinking water, and wastewater.
It organises the evidence needed to investigate that question responsibly,
and refuses to invent a regional conclusion.

A separate fictional rehearsal shows how human approval,
deterministic execution, a Run Receipt,
an Evidence Passport, and human review can work together.

ClimateOS begins with environmental stewardship,
but its deeper direction is universal.
Every person has a relationship with a place.
Over time, every person should be able to use a human–AI system
that remembers, understands, and helps them care for that place.

Simon brings lived experience, local observation, purpose, and responsibility.
Codex and GPT-5.6 bring long-term system memory,
scientific synthesis, implementation, testing, and traceability.

This demonstration is not the whole of ClimateOS.
It is one honest window into a much longer journey.
