ClimateOS Evidence Passport

Static review surface for candidate-only evidence workflow visibility.

Static-only / mock-only No backend. No API. No database. No scoring. No deployment.
No-conclusion rule: This skeleton displays provisional candidates only. It does not verify, approve, score, certify, assure, determine compliance, or create ESG/carbon, standards, or framework conclusions.

Case Overview

Source Intake

Source records are mock candidates. They are not verified final evidence.

Signal Register

Signals organize observations for review. They do not prove outcomes.

Claim Candidates

Claim candidates are provisional and remain blocked from public conclusion use.

Knowledge Objects

Knowledge Objects are candidate groupings only. No registry runtime exists.

Evidence Candidates

Evidence candidates are not admitted evidence and cannot issue an Evidence Passport.

Evidence Readiness

Readiness labels are non-scoring metadata only.

Risk Flags

Risk flags are stop controls. They do not resolve automatically.

Human Review

Human Review is visible but not automated. No review decisions are persisted.

Founder Gate

Founder Gate remains above future authorization and execution.
