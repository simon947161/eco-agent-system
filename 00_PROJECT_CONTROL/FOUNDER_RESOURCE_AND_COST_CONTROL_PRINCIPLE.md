# Founder Resource And Cost Control Principle

Status: Repository-wide collaboration prerequisite

Founder: Simon

Recorded: 2026-07-12

## Principle

ClimateOS and all other work in this repository should prefer resources that
are free or low-cost, open or inspectable, and operationally controllable.
This is a cost-governance and autonomy principle, not a prohibition on real
data, external evidence, commercial products, or necessary investment.

## Required Order Of Work

1. Define the actual need before selecting a provider or product.
2. Look first for credible free, open, official, or already-owned options.
3. Verify licence, permitted use, attribution, privacy, security, provenance,
   quality, coverage, update continuity, exportability, and lock-in risk.
4. Start with the smallest useful controlled sample or reversible trial.
5. Record expected download, storage, compute, API, subscription, maintenance,
   and exit costs before scaling.
6. If a paid option is materially better or the free options are inadequate,
   present the Founder with a clear decision brief before any commitment.

## Paid Decision Brief

Every request to spend money must state:

- the task need and why payment may be justified;
- provider, product, licence, and billing basis;
- estimated initial and recurring cost, currency, taxes, and cost ceiling;
- credible free or lower-cost alternatives and their limitations;
- data ownership, export, retention, cancellation, and lock-in conditions;
- expected benefit and what evidence would show that the purchase worked;
- a bounded trial, stop condition, and exit plan.

Silence is not approval. No agent may purchase, subscribe, enter payment
details, begin a chargeable trial, exceed a stated free tier, or create another
paid obligation without explicit prior Founder approval.

## Real Data And External Evidence

Real data should be used when it improves scientific or operational validity
and its use is authorised. Before ingestion, record the authoritative source,
licence and access conditions, retrieval time, spatial and temporal coverage,
variables and units, transformations, integrity checksum, limitations, and
human review status.

When licence, cost, or permission is uncertain, discovery and metadata review
may continue, but chargeable or irreversible ingestion must pause.

## Agent Preflight

All repository agents must read `AGENTS.md` and apply this principle before
introducing a new data source, dependency, API, model, service, subscription,
hosting commitment, or material compute/storage workload.

## Governance

Exceptions require explicit Founder approval and must be recorded with their
scope, cost ceiling, duration, owner, and expiry or review date. This principle
does not authorise work outside an active task gate.
