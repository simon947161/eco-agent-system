# Validation Name

## Purpose

Define the object, scope, and limits of this validation.

## Evidence Reviewed

List evidence records, versions, provenance, dates, and known gaps.

## Assumptions Checked

List assumptions and whether each is supported, unsupported, or unresolved.

## Uncertainties

Describe uncertainty sources, levels, sensitivity, and missing information.

## Findings

Record bounded findings supported by the reviewed evidence. Do not exceed the
reviewer's authority or the declared validation scope.

## Reviewer Notes

Record reviewer role, date, qualifications where relevant, comments, and
conflicts or limitations.

## Recommendations

Record review or evidence-completion actions only when appropriate. Clearly
distinguish them from approvals, designs, or implementation instructions.

## Status

Use a defined review status such as `not_started`, `in_review`,
`changes_required`, `validated_for_declared_scope`, or `archived`.

State explicitly that status does not grant statutory, engineering,
regulatory, construction, or investment approval.
