## Recommended description

ClimateOS is a growing human–AI operating-system direction for shared climate
stewardship and governance. It combines human life experience, local
observation, values and action responsibility with AI long-term memory,
scientific synthesis, multi-model reasoning, evidence organisation and system
execution.

ClimateOS existed before OpenAI Build Week. During the eligible event period,
we meaningfully extended that foundation toward an experienceable Runtime. The
video shows how a meaningful environmental question can become a structured,
human-reviewed research plan; how unsupported real-place conclusions remain
blocked; and how a separate fictional, local-only rehearsal can produce a Run
Receipt and quarantined Evidence Passport.

Simon Shu supplied the environmental purpose, lived and professional context,
meaningfulness tests, corrections and final decisions. Codex with GPT-5.6
helped connect the repository, implement bounded Runtime increments, write and
run tests, analyse failures, organise evidence and preserve long context.

The Task2003 interface shown as future direction remains Draft. Its current
Founder evidence is `HTML_CHECK_PASS` only; no completed monthly scientific
cycle or regional environmental conclusion is claimed.

emergency-warning service, professional certification, statutory decision,
engineering recommendation, financial advice or real-place environmental
finding.
