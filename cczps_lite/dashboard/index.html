CCZPS-Lite v0.5

Environmental evidence, explained for human review.

CCZPS-Lite is a local environmental planning-support prototype. It organises evidence, planning hypotheses, validation needs, governance records, and scenario comparisons.

07 / Capability boundary

What this system can and cannot do

The boundary is part of the product, not a footnote.

What CCZPS-Lite can do

  • Organise local evidence
  • Generate testable planning hypotheses
  • Trace outputs back to evidence
  • Show uncertainty and missing information
  • Identify required expert review
  • Compare scenarios transparently

What CCZPS-Lite cannot do yet

  • Approve a project
  • Replace planners, engineers, or other professionals
  • Run full GIS or DEM analysis
  • Run hydrology or CFD simulation
  • Guarantee scientific accuracy
  • Make legal or regulatory decisions
