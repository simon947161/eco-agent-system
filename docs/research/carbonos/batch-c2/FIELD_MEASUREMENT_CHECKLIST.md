# Field measurement readiness — no deployment or data collection authorised

The table is a research design checklist, not a certified sampling SOP or a claim that measurements exist. M1 supports wastewater boundaries; M2 is US regulatory-context guidance, NOT Australian legal requirements; M3 identifies factor editions, not a factor selected for this study. Rows marked PROPOSED_SITE_DESIGN are analyst proposals requiring specialist review.

| ID / gas | Required observation | Units | Quality / operating requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| P01 / All gases | Actual process temperature: Calibrated probes at lagoon depths / landfill core or cover; position and depth recorded | degC | Site-specific calibration and sensor access approval required |
| P02 / CH4/CO2 | Moisture and water balance: Cover/core moisture, rainfall, leachate and pond level separately | m3/m3 or kg/kg with wet/dry basis; mm; m3/day | Do not replace process moisture with rain; state porosity/bulk density for WFPS |
| P03 / CH4/CO2 | Organic load and composition: Weighbridge/composition samples; liquid inlet/outlet flow and lab COD/BOD/VS | kg wet/day; kg dry/day; mg COD/L; mg BOD5/L; m3/day | Chain of custody, uncertainty, solids basis; do not mix COD and BOD |
| P04 / N2O | Nitrogen and process environment: TN, NH4-N, NO2-N, NO3-N, dissolved oxygen, redox and aeration state | mg N/L; mg O2/L; pH; mV; degC | Species reported as N vs compound explicit; calibrated DO/pH/redox |
| P05 / All gases | Treatment process and controls: Process schematic; residence time, volume, cover, sludge age, maintenance, load changes | hours; days; m3; state codes | No inference of lagoon process from facility name; no access to private log now |
| G01 / CH4/N2O/CO2 | Atmospheric surface flux: Chamber/hood or validated micrometeorological method with background and footprint | mg species/m2/hour; ppmv or umol/mol for concentration | Concentration is not flux; calibration gas, blanks, replicates, leak and chamber-disturbance checks |
| G02 / CH4 | Dissolved gas and effluent transport: Dissolved sampling, temperature and flow, sampling losses and degassing | mg CH4/L; m3/day | Do not assume atmospheric surface flux includes downstream transport/storage |
| G03 / CH4/CO2 | Collection system gas: Flow meter plus CH4/CO2 analyser, temperature, absolute pressure, moisture | m3/hour with reference T/P and dry/wet basis; volume fraction; kPa | Calibration, standardisation, bypass/leaks; collected gas not generated gas |
| G04 / CH4/CO2 | Flare/engine destruction and uptime: Gas routing, inlet/outlet testing where appropriate, ignition, downtime and maintenance | hours; degC; fraction; device state | Design efficiency is not measured efficiency; no assumed capture fraction |
| G05 / CO2 | Carbon origin: Feedstock provenance, fuel records; source-apportionment method if needed | kg C; fraction biogenic/fossil | CO2 concentration cannot determine biogenic origin; radiocarbon only if justified |
| E01 / Energy-related gases | Electricity and fuels: Calibrated meter IDs, fuel type and purchases/use boundary | kWh imports/exports separately; L or kg fuel; MJ basis | Use matching year/jurisdiction factor version later; no factor applied now |
| E02 / Refrigerants | Equipment and leakage: Equipment inventory, service/recharge and recovery log | kg by named refrigerant | No absent-log-to-zero; GWP/version only if later approved |

All four facility candidates lack an authorised, event-aligned measurement pack. Do not purchase/install sensors, enter a site, contact operators or access Council-private systems. Instrument range, detection limit, calibration acceptance, safety/access and sampling frequency must be approved by qualified personnel before fieldwork. No numerical design efficiency or default gas yield is supplied. Capture, destruction, oxidation and emitted flux are not interchangeable. Current air-temperature observations do not establish any facility gas change.
