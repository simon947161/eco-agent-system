# Batch C evidence preparation protocol

Scope advances from B3 PROPOSAL_ONLY to **EVIDENCE_PREPARATION_ONLY** for climate observations. [B3 experimental design](../batch-b3/BATCH_C_PROTOCOL.md) remains proposal-only. No emissions calculations, fitting, simulation, gas response or national total.

Six availability-driven source records cover heat, dry-year context and rainfall for two regions. These are not a complete extreme-event catalogue or matched comparison. Rockhampton March 2017 is a monthly context record; a daily local storm accumulation was not obtained. Do not assign the whole month to cyclone Debbie. Cooma rainfall is station rainfall, not landfill inundation. Annual deficits do not define drought onset, hydrological drought or reactor moisture. Heat maxima do not establish a formal multi-day heatwave by themselves.

## Time, station and provenance

BOM date labels retained verbatim. C9 explains nominal daily rain to 9am on the labelled day and maximum temperature attributed to the preceding day (9am onwards). NSW clock/DST practices need station- and period-specific verification before UTC conversion; UTC endpoints intentionally null. QLD civil timezone is Australia/Brisbane, NSW Australia/Sydney; civil zone alone does not resolve historical observation convention. Monthly/yearly totals are reporting windows, not instantaneous observations. Missing accumulation/QC flags remain unknown; no interpolation, resampling or apparent precision added.

Cooma Airport AWS 070217 differs from Cooma Visitors Centre 070278; records are not spliced. Station ID is not facility ID. Rockhampton Aero 039083 is not South, West or Lakes Creek Road. Station coordinates cannot fill missing facility geometry. Spatial distance, catchment/raster matching and facility exposure are blocked.

Monthly summaries are publication-time statements, with limited preliminary checks and potentially later revisions. C6 explicitly says information available 7am 3 April 2017; it is not a fresh 2026 quality-certified series. C7 is issued 9 January 2020. Source date null means issue date not captured, not that event date is missing. C7/C8 describe observed retrospective climate context; no forecasts imported. In a future forecast test, index issue time must precede prediction time. Daily numerical index values and event-matched 2016/2017 phases are UNKNOWN. A late-2019 phase cannot be assigned to January 2019.

## Multi-gas evidence requirements

CH4 requires generation/oxidation/capture/destruction/storage/transport and atmospheric flux separated. N2O requires nitrogen loads, DO, aeration and calibrated flux. CO2 requires biogenic/fossil origins and explicit combustion boundaries. Energy needs metered imports/fuels and factor version; exported energy is separate and no avoided benefit is assumed. Refrigerant type and loss records cannot be set to zero when absent. Facility controls include load/composition, residence time, actual temperatures, redox, downtime, cover and structural upgrades. No climate index is a legal or scientific emissions multiplier.

Future review should freeze event thresholds, lag windows and controls BEFORE any outcome-based selection; raw series, QA, calibration and authorised measurement inputs are prerequisites. No time-lag chosen from this summary-only pack. Cooma vs Rockhampton is regional contrast, not a controlled trial. Weather association would not prove climate-change attribution. Historical AWT formulas remain unchanged.
