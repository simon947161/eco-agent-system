## 1. North Star

GEGG is an Australia-first, China–Australia-capability-corridor project-development and intelligent-governance enterprise. It converts public and authorised evidence into bounded services, project decisions and, only after stage gates, delivery coordination and revenue. It must not become a technology broker, an unqualified engineering adviser, or a system that turns hypotheses into sales claims.

The existing technical north star remains:

`Evidence → Trust → Governance → Action → Knowledge Evolution`

The enterprise operating loop added by this map is:

`Demand → Offer → Delivery → Acceptance → Invoice → Cash → Learning`

The two loops join through evidence objects, authority gates and measurable acceptance criteria.

## 5. Product architecture

### Entry offers — eligible for validation first

1. **Evidence Readiness Scan** — bounded evidence inventory, gap map and next-test register.
2. **Regional Industrial Heat Readiness Pack** — demand evidence, alternatives screen, approvals/carbon/grid/funding gaps; no technology recommendation.
3. **Public Evidence and Case-Pattern Atlas** — official-source comparison with claim states and limitations.
4. **Project Concept Governance Pack** — need, alternatives, boundaries, decision log and stop conditions.

### Development offers — only after entry evidence and gates

- Pre-feasibility Coordination Pack;
- Partner and Authority Readiness;
- Evidence Passport Preparation;
- Stage-Gate and Decision Secretariat.

### Deferred models

Software licence, success fee, commission, project equity, token/RWA revenue and long-term operating revenue remain `DEFERRED / NOT DEFAULT RIGHTS`. They require validated demand, legal/tax/accounting review, conflicts control and a separate Founder decision.

## 6. Customer and market architecture

The first market remains Australia. The China–Australia corridor is a capability-sourcing and qualification pathway, not proof of an Australian customer.

Prioritised buyer hypotheses for discovery, not customer claims:

1. regional manufacturers with industrial-heat transition evidence gaps;
2. councils or regional development bodies coordinating decarbonisation pipelines;
3. technology providers needing Australian evidence-readiness qualification;
4. project sponsors preparing for funding or pre-feasibility gates.

AKD and all named organisations remain potential evidence or demand contexts only. GEGG is not bound to any single partner, provider or facility.

## 7. Finance architecture

The financial control chain is:

`Service scope → delivery work breakdown → direct cost → risk allowance → approved price → contract → acceptance → invoice → receipt → margin review`

Until real discovery is authorised and completed, the following values must remain `UNKNOWN`, not zero and not invented:

- price and willingness to pay;
- customer acquisition cost;
- delivery hours and professional subcontract cost;
- gross margin;
- payment terms and working capital;
- 12-month revenue and cash runway.

## 9. Current diagnosis

GEGG has unusually strong evidence and governance infrastructure for an early enterprise. Its binding constraint is no longer document structure or validator design. The binding constraint is controlled market learning: confirming a real problem owner, buyer, urgency, procurement path and willingness to pay while retaining object-specific authority controls.

Therefore the next stage is not production deployment and not more broad architecture. It is a bounded Commercial Readiness Layer that prepares cost, price and discovery instruments, followed by a separate Founder gate before any real contact.
