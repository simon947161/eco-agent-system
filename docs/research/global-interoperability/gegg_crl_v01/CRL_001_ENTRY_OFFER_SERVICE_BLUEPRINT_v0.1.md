# CRL-001 — Entry Offer Service Blueprint v0.1

| Offer | Required input | Controlled output | Acceptance condition | Exclusions | Stop rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence Readiness Scan | public/provided evidence inventory and question | evidence map, gap register, provenance receipt | sources traceable; unknowns remain unknown | assurance, certification, engineering/legal conclusion | authority, consent or provenance absent |
| Regional Industrial Heat Readiness Pack | public regional and facility-class evidence | readiness profile and evidence gaps | scope, date, units and confidence stated | feasibility, investment or safety approval | site-specific or restricted data required |
| Public Evidence & Case-Pattern Atlas | public case records | comparable-pattern catalogue | each case has source, date and comparison limits | endorsement or prediction | licence/provenance uncertain |
| Project Concept Governance Pack | founder-approved synthetic concept | claim register, stage gates, risk and decision log | each claim has authority and evidence state | DA, engineering, finance or legal advice | requested action exceeds capability grant |

Common owner: `GEGG Commercial Readiness Steward (Shadow)`; final authority: `Founder`. No offer is externally available in v0.1.
