# GEGG Purpose Completion & Fair Exit Protocol v0.1

## Principle

GEGG is not designed to hold people together indefinitely. It is designed to create environmental and economic value, distribute earned value fairly, preserve trustworthy public-interest assets, and allow people to leave without hostage-taking or mission stripping.

## Six separations designed from Day 1

1. **Role from person:** authority expires with role; identity never owns a system capability.
2. **Labour from ownership:** pay for work, profit participation and equity are recorded separately.
3. **Economic rights from control:** return rights do not automatically convey mission-changing votes.
4. **Company IP from personal IP:** pre-existing, contributed, commissioned and open assets have separate registers.
5. **Project completion from company dissolution:** a completed mission may be archived, transferred or spun out without destroying the company.
6. **Relationship ending from misconduct:** ordinary departure must not be treated as betrayal.

## Participant exit ladder

1. voluntary notice and handover;
2. role/capability revocation and credential return;
3. accrued pay, expenses and approved profit share settled;
4. vested/unvested interests handled under pre-agreed rules;
5. independent valuation or formula-based price;
6. company/employee trust/remaining holders receive first purchase option;
7. instalments permitted only with security, timetable and solvency protection;
8. mutual release, continuing confidentiality limited to legitimate secrets, and non-disparagement balanced by lawful reporting rights;
9. evidence receipt closes the exit.

Default design: time-based vesting with a meaningful initial cliff; pro-rata vesting for a `Good Leaver`; unvested interests lapse; vested interests are bought at independently supportable fair value unless misconduct directly caused a quantified loss. A broad punitive `Bad Leaver` discount is not the default.

## Whole-company pathways

| Trigger | Preferred pathway | Mission safeguard |
|---|---|---|
| Founder succession | management/employee ownership or steward structure review | control passes to qualified stewards |
| commercial success and Founder liquidity | staged secondary sale or buyback | no automatic transfer of mission-control votes |
| mission achieved | completion declaration, impact audit, archive/transfer/spin-out | evidence and public-interest assets remain accessible |
| strategic acquisition | sale only after mission, stakeholder and asset review | reserved matters, successor covenant and reversion/transfer plan |
| viable but smaller future | orderly downsize or service-company conversion | liabilities and staff treated first |
| solvent closure | members' voluntary winding-up or eligible deregistration | settle creditors; dispose/transfer assets before deregistration |
| insolvency risk | immediate professional advice and statutory process | no insider preference or asset flight |

## Success and distribution waterfall

`Success` is not only a sale price. A completion decision must test: environmental outcome; evidence integrity; beneficiary/stakeholder outcome; financial sustainability; transferability; and whether continued operation creates more value than closure.

Subject to law and signed agreements, the design-order is:

1. taxes, employee entitlements, creditors and closure costs;
2. customer/project completion and remediation obligations;
3. secured and approved capital claims;
4. vested participant interests and declared profit share;
5. Founder/investor residual economic rights;
6. a pre-agreed environmental legacy allocation from true residual surplus;
7. transfer/archive of mission-critical evidence, standards and public-interest IP.

No distribution occurs from synthetic valuation or before a solvency test.

## Governance mechanisms recommended for GEGG

- constitution-level purpose and stakeholder clause when an Australian entity is formed;
- shareholders' agreement with vesting, valuation, right of first refusal, tag/drag safeguards, deadlock and succession clauses;
- annual `Stay / Scale / Transfer / Complete / Close` review;
- reserved matters requiring Founder initially and later a supermajority plus independent mission steward;
- an employee participation pool only after tax/legal design;
- a future steward-ownership or employee-trust feasibility gate, not an immediate foreign-structure copy;
- a Mission Asset Register with transfer, licence and archive rules;
- an Exit Reserve funded only after real cash economics are proven.

This is a governance design, not Australian legal, tax or financial advice.
