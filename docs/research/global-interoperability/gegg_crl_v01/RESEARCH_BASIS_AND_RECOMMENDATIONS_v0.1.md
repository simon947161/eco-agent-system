# Research Basis & Recommendations v0.1

## Models considered

| Model | Proven contribution | GEGG decision |
|---|---|---|
| Australian shareholder/partnership exit agreements | pre-agreed termination, transfer and settlement | adopt as future legal-document requirement |
| Australian Employee Share Scheme | employee economic participation; ATO provides startup-concession standard documents | feasibility after entity, valuation and tax review |
| B Corp AU/NZ purpose and stakeholder clauses | constitution-level purpose and stakeholder consideration | recommended when entity is formed |
| Employee Ownership Trust | founder succession with meaningful employee financial stake and voice | study as long-term option; UK rules not portable to Australia |
| Steward/perpetual purpose ownership | separates mission-control voting rights from economic rights | design principle only pending Australian advice |
| Patagonia Purpose Trust + Holdfast structure | documented example of voting control protecting purpose and non-voting economic value supporting environmental work | learn from separation; do not copy structure |
| Solvent winding-up/deregistration | lawful final closure and asset/liability settlement | mandatory statutory exit path |

## Recommendation

GEGG should use a staged hybrid:

1. now: purpose, stakeholder, fair-leaver, IP, valuation, deadlock, succession and mission-asset design;
2. on incorporation: Australian constitution and shareholders' agreement reviewed by counsel;
3. after verified cash flow: employee participation and Exit Reserve feasibility;
4. before major capital or Founder succession: compare conventional sale, management/employee buyout and steward-ownership pathways;
5. at mission completion: independent impact/solvency audit and Founder/board completion gate.
