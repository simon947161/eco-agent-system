# CRL-006 — Founder Contact Gate v0.1

Current decision: `HOLD / NO CONTACT`.

Before any real contact, Founder must approve a specific object containing: recipient identity and authority; purpose; exact message; permitted channel; evidence sought; data minimisation; prohibited claims; sender identity; expiry; reply-handling rule; withdrawal method; and post-contact evidence admission rule.

Approval of a sector, service, template or role never implies approval of a recipient. No Auto-Company role, Shadow Adapter or scheduler may issue, infer or inherit a Contact Grant.
