# CRL-003 — Internal Price Architecture v0.1

No number below is a quote or market evidence. Each offer uses three formulas:

- `floor = verified delivery cost / (1 - minimum gross-margin hypothesis)`;
- `base = floor + documented complexity/risk allowance`;
- `strategic = base adjusted only for a named learning, access or portfolio reason`.

Release requires verified costs, at least three comparable market references, Founder approval, scope and exclusions, tax treatment, payment milestones, expiry, and object-specific contact authority. Discounting may not hide work, transfer professional liability, or exchange mission control for short-term revenue.
