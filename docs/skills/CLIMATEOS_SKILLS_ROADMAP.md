# ClimateOS Skills Roadmap

## Purpose

This roadmap describes future reusable skills that may help ClimateOS agents and
contributors work consistently.

These are future skill concepts only. They are not implemented runtime agents.

## Observation Agent

Future purpose: help create, organize, and review observation records from
field notes, external providers, local sensors, and community observation.

Potential outputs:

- observation diary entries
- source context summaries
- uncertainty notes
- follow-up observation needs

## Evidence Agent

Future purpose: help organize observations and relationships into evidence
candidates without treating raw data as proof.

Potential outputs:

- evidence candidate summaries
- traceability notes
- relationship context
- missing evidence lists

## Validation Agent

Future purpose: help prepare validation reports and check transparency,
traceability, uncertainty, and confidence.

Potential outputs:

- validation report drafts
- confidence context
- limitation summaries
- review checklists

## Governance Agent

Future purpose: help translate validated evidence into governance coordination
language while preserving human decision boundaries.

Potential outputs:

- governance notes
- stakeholder context
- coordination options
- feedback records

## Grant Finder Agent

Future purpose: help identify potential funding and grant alignment for
ClimateOS-related research, stewardship, and resilience projects.

Potential outputs:

- grant opportunity summaries
- eligibility notes
- evidence requirements
- application task lists

## ESG Agent

Future purpose: help translate validated environmental evidence into ESG++ and
disclosure-oriented language without claiming compliance or assurance.

Potential outputs:

- ESG evidence summaries
- disclosure preparation notes
- governance translation drafts
- review limitations

## Skill Development Rules

- Keep skills narrow and reviewable.
- Preserve implemented, planned, and vision distinctions.
- Require human-readable outputs.
- Do not create automated decisions.
- Do not imply scientific, regulatory, financial, or professional authority.
