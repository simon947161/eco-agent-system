## Signal Summary

The Founder accepted the technical boundary discipline of Task631-640 but found
the task-name-only explanation insufficient. The important signal is that
ClimateOS must be explained as a learning system: it begins with real-world
questions, observes signals, forms hypotheses, gathers evidence, reviews
uncertainty, and only later moves toward runtime capability under Founder Gate.

Task documentation must therefore show why a step matters, what question it
serves, how it advances ClimateOS, and where it must stop.

## Knowledge Points

- Task numbers are useful for repository control but not enough for Founder
  review.
- ClimateOS growth must be narrated as a chain of inquiry, not a list of
  artifacts.
- Life System and BiodiversityOS work must start from living-world questions:
  how biodiversity, habitats, ecological function, water, land, heat, carbon,
  and resilience affect one another.
- Evidence Contract examples are useful, but they must serve scientific inquiry
  rather than replace it.
- Future runtime, interface, model, scoring, and agent work can be designed only
  after explicit authorization and staged review.

## Idea Points

- Create a recurring plain-language explanation layer for each task batch.
- Preserve a "how ClimateOS grew" narrative that can later become a public or
  internal article.
- Treat CRP as the conversation radar record and ACTP as the context-transfer
  packet for future work.
- Use milestone stops at Task700, Task800, Task900, and Task1000.

- Future work may drift from documentation into runtime, model integration,
  scoring, certification, deployment, or private EcoEngine access without a
  clear Founder Gate.
