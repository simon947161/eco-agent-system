---

# 1. Vision

CCZPS 2.0 is not a software product.

It is not a climate model.

It is not a digital twin platform.

It is not a multi-agent framework.

CCZPS 2.0 is a governance architecture.

Its purpose is to enable human society to explore, compare, evaluate, and select better futures under climate, ecological, social, financial, and technological constraints.

---

# 2. Core Philosophy

Traditional climate planning asks:

> What will happen?

CCZPS 2.0 asks:

> What futures are possible?

> Which future is worth implementing?

> What governance process should guide that choice?

This shift defines the transition from prediction to possibility computing.
