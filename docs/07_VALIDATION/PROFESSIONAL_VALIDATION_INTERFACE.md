# Professional Validation Interface

This interface defines how qualified reviewers may assess CCZPS-Lite outputs. It does not record or imply a completed professional opinion.

Each scenario review addresses planning validity, environmental validity, hydrology/terrain relevance, energy/infrastructure relevance, evidence sufficiency, uncertainty level, and recommendation status. Each category records a finding, evidence references, confidence, and required follow-up.

Allowed recommendation states are `not_reviewed`, `rejected`, `conditional`, and `supported_for_further_assessment`. None means planning approval. Relevant planners, environmental specialists, GIS/terrain or hydrology specialists, infrastructure or energy specialists, and local/community knowledge holders should sign off where required.

Generated examples are in `cczps_lite/output/professional_validation_interface.json` and `.md`.
