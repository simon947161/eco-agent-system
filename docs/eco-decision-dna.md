# Eco Decision DNA  
# 生态决策基因结构

## 1. Origin / 起点

Eco Decision DNA is the foundational structure of the Eco Engine system.

It is not designed as a single product, nor as a narrow environmental model.  
It is a living decision structure intended to connect climate, land, water, energy, industry, agriculture, and human action.

生态决策基因结构，是 Eco Engine 系统的底层原始结构。

它不是一个单一产品，也不是一个狭义的环境模型。  
它是一种可生长的决策结构，用于连接气候、土地、水、能源、工业、农业与人类行动。

---

## 2. Core Question / 核心问题

Every human action leaves a consequence in the physical world.

The question is:

> When an action enters the world, what does it change across space, time, water, energy, life, and risk?

每一个人类行动，都会在物理世界中留下后果。

核心问题是：

> 一个行动进入世界之后，它在空间、时间、水、能量、生命系统与风险之间，到底改变了什么？

---

## 3. Why DNA, Not MVP / 为什么是 DNA，而不是 MVP

This system is not only looking for a minimum working function.  
It is looking for the correct original structure.

If the original structure is right, it can grow into different branches:

- agriculture
- industry
- urban systems
- energy systems
- ecological restoration
- climate finance
- digital environmental assets

If the original structure is wrong, future expansion will also be distorted.

这个系统寻找的不是一个最小功能，而是一个正确的原始结构。

如果原始结构是对的，它未来可以生长为：

- 农业版本
- 工业版本
- 城市版本
- 能源版本
- 生态修复版本
- 气候金融版本
- 数字生态资产版本

如果最初的结构错了，后面的扩展也会偏离方向。

---

## 4. Eco Decision DNA Structure / 生态决策基因结构

Every proposal, project, workflow, or environmental action should carry a basic decision string.

每一个方案、项目、生产流程或环境行动，都应该携带一组基础决策信息。

```text
Eco Decision DNA =
[Place]
+ [Time]
+ [Climate]
+ [Water]
+ [Energy]
+ [Material]
+ [Life System]
+ [Action]
+ [Risk]
+ [Value]
```

---

## 5. The Ten Gene Layers / 十个基因层

### 5.1 Place Gene / 空间基因

Where does the action happen?

```text
location
altitude
slope
catchment position
upstream / downstream relationship
land-use type
```

Without space, there is no ecological judgment.

没有空间，就没有生态判断。

---

### 5.2 Time Gene / 时间基因

When does the action happen, and for how long?

```text
season
duration
frequency
before / during / after stage
short-term impact
long-term trend
```

Ecological systems do not only respond to events.  
They respond to cycles.

生态系统不仅响应事件，也响应周期。

---

### 5.3 Climate Gene / 气候基因

What climate pressures are present?

```text
temperature
wind
humidity
rainfall
solar radiation
extreme event signal
```

Climate change is not only about heat.  
It is about instability in energy, air, and water movement.

气候变化不只是变热，而是能量、空气与水分运动变得不稳定。

---

### 5.4 Water Gene / 水分基因

How does water enter, move, stay, or disappear?

```text
rainfall input
runoff
infiltration
evaporation
soil moisture
water balance
```

Water is one of the first signals of ecological stability or degradation.

水，是生态稳定或退化最早出现的信号之一。

---

### 5.5 Energy Gene / 能量基因

How much energy is consumed, stored, released, or wasted?

```text
electricity
fuel
heat release
thermal storage
waste heat
energy efficiency
```

This layer connects environmental systems with industry, agriculture, buildings, and infrastructure.

这一层把环境系统与工业、农业、建筑和基础设施连接起来。

---

### 5.6 Material Gene / 物质基因

What materials are used, transformed, emitted, or accumulated?

```text
biomass
soil organic matter
carbon
fertiliser
waste
emissions
```

This layer connects physical production with carbon accounting, resource efficiency, and environmental responsibility.

这一层连接物质生产、碳核算、资源效率与环境责任。

---

### 5.7 Life System Gene / 生命系统基因

How does the action affect living systems?

```text
vegetation
soil biology
crop resilience
habitat
biodiversity
human livelihood
```

Without this layer, the system becomes only engineering calculation.  
With this layer, it becomes ecological judgment.

没有这一层，系统只是工程计算。  
有了这一层，系统才成为生态判断。

---

### 5.8 Action Gene / 行动基因

What exactly is being done?

```text
planting
irrigation
construction
boiler operation
land restoration
energy control
industrial process
```

Action is the bridge between human intention and environmental consequence.

行动，是人类意图与环境后果之间的桥梁。

---

### 5.9 Risk Gene / 风险基因

Does the action push the system toward stability or degradation?

```text
degradation risk
fire risk
water deficit
heat stress
production instability
downstream impact
```

Risk is not only a warning.  
It is a direction of change.

风险不只是警告。  
风险代表系统正在朝哪个方向变化。

---

### 5.10 Value Gene / 价值基因

Does the action create real ecological value?

```text
environmental benefit
resource saving
risk reduction
resilience improvement
token / credit potential
public value
```

Digital value should not come before ecological value.  
It should be anchored in real environmental contribution.

数字价值不应先于生态价值。  
它应当锚定在真实的环境贡献之上。

---

## 6. From Workflow to Ecological Decision / 从流程到生态决策

Most industries already have workflows.

Agriculture has workflows.  
Industry has workflows.  
Construction has workflows.  
Energy systems have workflows.

The purpose of Eco Decision DNA is not to replace those workflows, but to add an environmental feedback layer.

大多数行业已经有自己的流程。

农业有流程。  
工业有流程。  
建设有流程。  
能源系统有流程。

Eco Decision DNA 的目的不是替代这些流程，而是在其中加入环境反馈层。

```text
Input
→ Process
→ Environmental Evaluation
→ Adjustment
→ Output
```

---

## 7. Decision Logic / 决策逻辑

The system asks three basic questions:

```text
1. What is happening?
2. Is the system moving toward stability or risk?
3. What action can reduce damage or improve resilience?
```

系统提出三个基础问题：

```text
1. 正在发生什么？
2. 系统正在走向稳定，还是走向风险？
3. 什么行动可以减少损害，或提高韧性？
```

## 9. Final Statement / 最终说明

Eco Decision DNA is not a finished system.

It is the first living structure of a future environmental decision framework.

Its purpose is simple:

> To help human actions become computable, comparable, and ecologically responsible.

Eco Decision DNA 不是一个完成品。

它是未来环境决策框架中的第一个生命结构。

它的目的很简单：

> 让人类行动变得可以计算、可以比较，并对生态后果负责。
