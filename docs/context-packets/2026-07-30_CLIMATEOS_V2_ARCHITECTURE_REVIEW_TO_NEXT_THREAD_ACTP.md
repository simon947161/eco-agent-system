## 2. Founder Scientific Correction

ClimateOS must **not** be designed to wait for an official declaration before it is permitted to identify an emerging environmental risk.

The governing distinction is:

> ClimateOS must respect official observations, source facts, legal authorities and warnings, but it must not require an official conclusion as the first permissible conclusion.

Official confirmation is one evidence stage, not the only stage.

ClimateOS v2 must support anticipatory reasoning such as:

```text
OBSERVED SIGNAL
→ EMERGING PATTERN
→ MULTI-SOURCE CONVERGENCE
→ LOCAL RISK ESCALATION
→ INTERVENTION WINDOW
→ OFFICIAL CONFIRMATION OR NON-CONFIRMATION
→ POST-EVENT REVIEW
```

The system must be able to state, with explicit uncertainty and expiry:

- a signal is emerging;
- a trend is strengthening or weakening;
- multiple models or observations are converging;
- a local consequence is becoming more plausible;
- precautionary review or bounded intervention should begin;
- evidence remains insufficient for a stronger claim;
- the assessment must be recalculated when specified conditions change.

ClimateOS must not falsely label an emerging pattern as officially confirmed. It also must not suppress an actionable early warning merely because a national agency has not yet issued a formal declaration.

## 4. Tree-to-Leaf Principle

ClimateOS must explicitly bridge the distance between global or national environmental intelligence and local consequences.

```text
GLOBAL DRIVERS / TREE TRUNK
        ↓
NATIONAL AND CONTINENTAL MODELS
        ↓
REGIONAL CLIMATE AND CATCHMENT SIGNALS
        ↓
LOCAL TERRAIN, WATER, VEGETATION, INFRASTRUCTURE AND COMMUNITY
        ↓
SITE / COMMUNITY / PERSON-SCALE DECISION WINDOWS
```

Examples:

- an ENSO trend is not itself a Cooma water-supply conclusion;
- a national fire outlook is not itself a Tumut or Cooma neighbourhood intervention;
- a regional rainfall anomaly is not itself a wastewater-plant resilience conclusion;
- a global model output must be translated through local terrain, hydrology, exposure, infrastructure and observations.

ClimateOS v2 must define the scientific and governance mechanism for this global-to-local translation.

## 5. Early-Warning and Evidence-Maturity Architecture

The review must replace any binary pattern of `NO CONCLUSION / OFFICIAL CONFIRMATION` with a structured evidence-maturity model.

Candidate stages to assess:

1. `SIGNAL_DETECTED`
2. `EMERGING_PATTERN`
3. `EVIDENCE_CONVERGING`
4. `LOCAL_RISK_ELEVATED`
5. `PRECAUTIONARY_ACTION_WINDOW`
6. `HIGH_CONFIDENCE_ASSESSMENT`
7. `OFFICIAL_CONFIRMATION`
8. `OUTCOME_OBSERVED`
9. `RETROSPECTIVE_VALIDATION`

Each stage must distinguish at least:

- observation strength;
- model agreement;
- spatial relevance;
- temporal relevance;
- causal uncertainty;
- local exposure;
- consequence severity;
- reversibility of action;
- false-positive cost;
- false-negative cost;
- human-review status;
- validity period;
- update triggers;
- stop conditions.

The system must support precautionary action recommendations where action is low-regret and reversible, while retaining stronger gates for high-cost, irreversible, legal, engineering or public-safety decisions.
