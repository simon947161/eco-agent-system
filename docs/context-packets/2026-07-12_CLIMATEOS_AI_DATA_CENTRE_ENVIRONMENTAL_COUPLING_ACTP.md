The domain provides a realistic coupled-system test involving:
- grid capacity and electricity demand;
- cooling technology and water demand;
- planning, land and biodiversity;
- construction labour, MEP capacity and materials;
- Scope 2/3 and embodied carbon;
- heat, flood, fire, smoke, outage and drought resilience;
- housing/infrastructure competition and regional development.

The purpose is not to chase a news trend. The purpose is to use a material Australian infrastructure transition as a governed, evidence-backed test of ClimateOS coupling capabilities.
