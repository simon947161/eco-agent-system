## Cooma mission memory

Cooma is now the primary long-term regional research anchor. The Founder may
undertake future work related to backflow prevention, liquid waste and trade
waste as part of protecting local water systems. That operational work belongs
in a separate Cooma WorkOS. ClimateOS provides broader evidence governance for
regional climate, water ecology, catchment, town development and planning
context.

The long ambition is to build a trustworthy environmental intelligence layer
that can connect global and Australian evidence to regional questions while
preserving scale, time, provenance, licence, uncertainty, non-stationarity and
human accountability. It should support professional judgment without silently
turning regional context into a property, legal, planning or compliance answer.

## Permanent separation

ClimateOS may eventually carry reviewed evidence metadata and regional context.
It must not carry a private customer/person identity, exact private address,
inspection note, enforcement status, compliance decision, legal advice,
operational instruction or private WorkOS record. A future interface requires
its own authorization and privacy review.
