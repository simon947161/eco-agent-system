## 4. Scientific and architecture decisions

### 4.1 Model-neutral architecture

ClimateOS will not treat UniCM as its only external model. External models are
bounded computational perspectives registered by identity, version, scale,
mechanism, variables, forcing, evaluation, regional applicability and
prohibited inference.

### 4.2 Active research pair

- UniCM: climate-mode and inter-mode relationship perspective;
- NeuralGCM: hybrid dynamical-atmosphere and learned-physics perspective.

NeuralGCM is retained as the second model world. This status permits the
completed documentation research only. It does not authorize acquisition or
execution.

### 4.3 Australian regional anchor

ACCESS and other Bureau of Meteorology resources remain the principal
Australian official product and regional-calibration candidates. A global
model grid does not become evidence for Sydney, Alice Springs, Snowy Valleys,
Riverina, a catchment or a site without a separate translation and validation
chain.

### 4.4 Evaluation and data separation

Model, Evaluation Framework and Data Resource are separate registry objects.
WeatherBench2, PhysMetrics.Weather and RealBench do not become model
components. CDS, ERA5, ERA5-Land, CMIP6, RESDC and Australian resources do not
become models.
