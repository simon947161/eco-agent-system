## Growth Narrative Through Task640

ClimateOS began by establishing how a system can observe the world without
turning early signals into false certainty. The early foundation work built
layers for observation, relationships, radar, evidence, validation, proof of
reality, participation, and governance.

Task100-200 strengthened the evidence question. ClimateOS learned that a claim
needs source context, method context, boundaries, uncertainty, review status,
and human judgment before it can influence decisions.

Task201-300 strengthened governance. The system learned that architecture,
runtime, product, scoring, certification, and public-facing claims require
explicit gates rather than automatic progression.

Task301-360 tested evidence work through a concrete use-trial pattern. The
important movement was from abstract governance toward real questions, candidate
signals, evidence readiness, risk flags, human review, and MVP requirements.

Task361-420 translated evidence-governance needs into architecture planning,
without implementation. This made the future Evidence Passport system visible
as a design problem.

Task421-600 explored visible and local controlled prototype work under strict
boundaries. The local prototype showed that candidate records, human review,
Founder Gates, audit trails, mock model assistance, and local export can be
controlled without becoming production runtime.

Task601-620 reopened the larger ClimateOS horizon beyond CarbonOS and Evidence
Passport. It introduced domain federation, Life System positioning,
BiodiversityOS, scientific autonomy, cross-domain evidence, Sustainable Office
concepts, and future gates.

Task621-630 tested cross-domain examples. Task631-640 tested evidence-state
examples. The correction after Task631-640 is that these governance tools must
serve a scientific inquiry path rather than replace it.
