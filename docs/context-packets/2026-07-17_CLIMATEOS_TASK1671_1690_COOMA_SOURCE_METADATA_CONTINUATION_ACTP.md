## Cooma mission

Cooma is ClimateOS's primary long-term regional observation anchor. ClimateOS
is intended to connect climate, water, ecology, catchment, town development,
planning, infrastructure and project context into one evidence language. It is
not limited to synthetic data forever; public real evidence may be admitted
progressively when source, licence, version, scale, uncertainty and review gates
are satisfied.

A separate future Cooma WorkOS may support backflow prevention, liquid waste and
trade-waste work. Private customer, address, inspection, enforcement and
compliance records remain in WorkOS. ClimateOS may later receive only reviewed,
purpose-limited, de-identified metadata or aggregates through a separately
authorized interface.

## Task1681–1690 capability

The batch records ten official HTML page identities across NSW legislation, NSW
Planning, Snowy Monaro Regional Council, the Bureau of Meteorology, Snowy Hydro
and the Australian Government environment department. Nine are reference-only
metadata records and one Council news page is discovery-only.

The system treats official websites as publication surfaces, not complete
databases. It retains plan transitions, missing visible dates, proponent bias,
scale mismatch, news quarantine and deferred source classes rather than filling
gaps with assumptions.

No specific Cooma-focused CSIRO page was identified in the bounded search. This
does not establish absence; it leaves CSIRO and other science resources for a
later structured discovery pass.

## Preserved ambition

```text
Public and scientific source identities
        ↓
licensed claim-level evidence
        ↓
scale- and time-aware Cooma regional context
        ↓
human-reviewed ClimateOS environmental intelligence
        ↓
purpose-limited support for future WorkOS and wider PlanetOS stewardship
```

Unity is achieved through shared evidence semantics, not by mixing private and
public raw records. ClimateOS should remain capable of learning from real public
evidence while keeping provenance, uncertainty and accountable human judgment.
