# ClimateOS v2 North Star and Scope

## North Star

> ClimateOS transforms lawful global, regional and local evidence into
> trustworthy, time-bounded local environmental intelligence before the
> relevant decision window closes, while preserving uncertainty, human
> responsibility and retrospective learning.

Chinese working expression:

> ClimateOS 在关键决策窗口关闭之前，把合法取得的全球、区域和地方证据，
> 转化为可信、限时、可行动、可复核并明确不确定性的本地环境智能。

## Primary users

- local environmental planners and land managers;
- communities and asset stewards;
- researchers and domain reviewers;
- infrastructure and resilience practitioners;
- responsible authorities that need evidence packages, not automated
  authority substitution.

## Primary product

A ClimateOS v2 product is not a raw data dashboard or a permanent prediction.
It is a **Time-Bounded Environmental Answer** containing:

- the question and decision use;
- spatial and temporal boundary;
- current evidence state and cut-off;
- bounded conclusion and confidence;
- supporting and conflicting evidence;
- local translation path;
- consequence and intervention window;
- proportionate actions;
- validity and expiry;
- update, stop and escalation triggers;
- responsible human review;
- later outcome and skill record.

## In scope

- evidence convergence and disagreement;
- global-to-local mechanism translation;
- QGIS-backed local system and exposure context;
- early, reversible, precautionary decision support;
- historical characterisation and near-current assessment;
- evidence expiry and revision;
- retrospective validation and calibration;
- external provider interoperability behind admission gates.

## Out of scope without separate authority

- public emergency warnings;
- statutory or engineering certification;
- autonomous infrastructure control;
- drinking-water safety declarations;
- guaranteed fire, drought, flood or ENSO predictions;
- private Council operational data;
- uncontrolled data scraping or redistribution;
- GEGG corporate governance;
- a new radar.

## Success criteria for Phase II

Phase II is successful when ClimateOS can:

1. select one real local decision question;
2. combine at least two independent fit-for-purpose evidence streams;
3. preserve missing and conflicting evidence;
4. translate the evidence through a declared local system model;
5. issue a bounded, expiring answer at an appropriate maturity level;
6. name a proportionate intervention or monitoring window;
7. obtain the required human review;
8. later compare the assessment with observed outcomes.

The success criterion is not a target task number.
