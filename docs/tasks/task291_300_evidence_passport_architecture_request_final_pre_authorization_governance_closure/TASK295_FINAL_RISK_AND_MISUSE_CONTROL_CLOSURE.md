# Task295 Final Risk And Misuse Control Closure

## Purpose

Close the risk and misuse control record for the pre-authorization governance sequence.

## Core Statements

Risk closure does not eliminate risk.

Risk closure only preserves the governance record for future review.

## False Authority Risk

False authority risk remains if governance closure is misread as approval, authorization, gate opening, request submission, architecture design, runtime, implementation, compliance, assurance, certification, or operational capability.

## False Certainty Risk

False certainty risk remains if closure language is treated as proof of readiness, maturity, completeness, correctness, or operational capability.

## False Compliance Risk

False compliance risk remains if governance records are misused as compliance guidance, compliance determination, compliance readiness, or regulatory conclusion.

## False Assurance Risk

False assurance risk remains if governance records are misused as audit guidance, verification outcome, assurance readiness, or assurance conclusion.

## False Certification Risk

False certification risk remains if governance records are misused as certification guidance, approval status, certification readiness, or certification conclusion.

## False ESG/Carbon Conclusion Risk

False ESG/carbon conclusion risk remains if governance records are misused as ESG performance, carbon performance, carbon accounting conclusion, emissions conclusion, climate claim, or environmental claim.

## Framework Misinterpretation Risk

Framework misinterpretation risk remains if governance records are misread as framework interpretation, framework comparison, framework mapping, or authoritative framework meaning.

## Standards Interpretation Risk

Standards interpretation risk remains if governance records are misread as standards interpretation, standards comparison, standards mapping, or authoritative standards meaning.

## Automation Misuse Risk

Automation misuse risk remains if governance records are misused to justify workflow execution, agent execution, autonomous decision systems, runtime operation, or scoring.

## External Misuse Risk

External misuse risk remains if records are used outside the repository as evidence of authority, readiness, compliance, assurance, certification, ESG performance, carbon performance, or operational capability.

## Partner Misuse Risk

Partner misuse risk remains if partners, vendors, clients, collaborators, funders, or third parties treat governance closure as approval, readiness, authorization, or operational status.

## Public Communication Risk

Public communication risk remains if governance records are used in public claims, marketing, reporting, policy discussion, investment material, or regulatory-facing material.

## Human Responsibility Dilution Risk

Human responsibility dilution risk remains if checklists, templates, closure records, automation, or agents are treated as substitutes for human judgment.

## Runtime Creep Risk

Runtime creep risk remains if future work introduces runtime planning, runtime architecture, runtime implementation, simulation, scaffolding, operation, or deployment without separate authorization.

## Architecture Creep Risk

Architecture creep risk remains if future work introduces architecture diagrams, components, services, interfaces, data structures, workflows, storage concepts, retrieval concepts, or design decisions without separate authorization.

## Implementation Creep Risk

Implementation creep risk remains if future work introduces code, schemas, APIs, databases, workflows, dashboards, CLIs, retrieval systems, model orchestration, automation, or agent execution without separate authorization.

## Authorization Creep Risk

Authorization creep risk remains if closure, recommendations, prior completion, checklists, package existence, or gate review are treated as authorization.

## Request Creep Risk

Request creep risk remains if future work creates, activates, submits, approves, or implies an active Evidence Passport Architecture Request without separate authorization.

## Gate Creep Risk

Gate creep risk remains if future work opens, passes, approves, activates, or authorizes a gate without separate authorization.

## Risk Closure Findings

The risk record is closed only as a documentation record.

All listed risks remain candidates for future Founder review, Human Authority review, Mission alignment review, deferral, rejection, or escalation.

## Risks Still Unresolved

Unresolved risks include false authority, false certainty, external misuse, partner misuse, public communication misuse, human responsibility dilution, architecture creep, implementation creep, runtime creep, authorization creep, request creep, and gate creep.

## Risks Requiring Future Review

Any future batch must review risks again before changing authorization status, opening a gate, creating a request, submitting a request, designing architecture, implementing, or creating runtime.

## Risks Requiring Founder Escalation

Founder escalation is required for any future work that could affect architecture authorization, active request creation, request submission, gate opening, implementation, runtime, external communication, partner use, public use, or Roadmap Task300 interpretation.

## Risks Requiring Human Authority Review

Human Authority review is required for Mission conflict, public-use risk, partner-use risk, high-risk evidence, ethics conflict, false authority risk, false certainty risk, and human responsibility dilution risk.
