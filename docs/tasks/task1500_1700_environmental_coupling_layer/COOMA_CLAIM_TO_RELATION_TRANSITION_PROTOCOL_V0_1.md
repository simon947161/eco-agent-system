# Cooma Claim-to-Relation Transition Protocol v0.1

## Semantic levels

1. `source identity` — the publisher and page exist at an access time;
2. `source assertion` — a controlled paraphrase records what the page states;
3. `reference evidence` — a reviewed assertion with adequate licence, version,
   geography, time and uncertainty controls;
4. `evidence relation` — two or more admitted evidence items support a bounded
   relationship without hidden scale translation;
5. `mechanism hypothesis` — a separately governed Task1701+ candidate.

Task1691–1700 reaches level 2 only. The word `admitted` never silently promotes
a source assertion to levels 3–5.

## Required relation gates

- source identity and exact claim locator;
- licence or lawful reuse decision;
- geographic overlap, distance, elevation and scale compatibility;
- publication, observation and access-time alignment;
- uncertainty and alternative explanations;
- non-stationarity review;
- relevant expert review;
- explicit prohibition of legal, compliance, operational and causal overreach.

## Pilot trials

The Council water-source assertion was compared with the federal broad-valley
description. The relation was rejected because common naming does not establish
a mapped Cooma catchment, current environmental state or reviewed link.

The Bureau station identity and location metadata were tested as a possible
bridge to Cooma climate context. The relation was rejected because no
observations were admitted and station representativeness was not assessed.

Both trials preserve their failed gates. Failure is a useful governed result,
not missing output.

## WorkOS boundary

ClimateOS may retain public, purpose-limited evidence semantics. Customer,
address, inspection, enforcement, job, backflow, liquid-waste, trade-waste and
compliance records remain outside this pack and require a separate WorkOS
authority and de-identification pathway.
