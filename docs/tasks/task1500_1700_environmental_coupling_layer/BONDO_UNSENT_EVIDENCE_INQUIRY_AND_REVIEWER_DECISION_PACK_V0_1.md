## 5. Consent-first contact sequence

Before any reviewer contact:

1. Founder confirms the exact person and permissible public lookup scope;
2. verify current public affiliation, expertise and suitable contact channel;
3. select a bounded role: wind science or data governance, not a generic endorsement;
4. prepare a one-page scope with evidence available, evidence missing and prohibited conclusions;
5. state whether the request is informal/unpaid or whether a fee may be considered;
6. ask only whether the person is willing to discuss the role;
7. record consent, conflicts, confidentiality, attribution and publication expectations;
8. do not transfer project documents whose licence or confidentiality is unclear.

## 6. Reviewer acceptance criteria

A reviewer is admitted only when all fields are resolved:

- verified identity and current relevant expertise;
- explicit willingness and scope;
- conflict-of-interest statement;
- evidence access and licence basis;
- expected deliverable and decision boundary;
- fee/cost approval if applicable;
- attribution/confidentiality/publication permission;
- acknowledgement that review does not create regulatory approval or engineering certification.
