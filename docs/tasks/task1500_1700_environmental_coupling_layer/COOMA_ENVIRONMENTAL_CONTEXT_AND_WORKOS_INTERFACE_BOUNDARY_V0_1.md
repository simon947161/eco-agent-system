# Cooma Environmental Context and WorkOS Interface Boundary v0.1

## Long-term intent

Cooma is a durable ClimateOS observation anchor because the Founder expects
future environmental work connected with protecting local water systems.
ClimateOS should eventually help organise defensible regional context across
climate, water ecology, catchment, town development and planning evidence.

This intent is a research and governance direction, not a claim that a job,
source, environmental condition or planning instrument has been confirmed.

## System boundary

| ClimateOS may govern | Separate Cooma WorkOS may govern later |
|---|---|
| Evidence identity and class | Customer and stakeholder workflow |
| Regional and catchment scale labels | Property and worksite records |
| Time scope and source revision | Backflow prevention activities |
| Licence and permitted-use state | Liquid waste and trade waste activities |
| Uncertainty and non-stationarity | Inspection and follow-up workflow |
| Review status and prohibited inference | Operational and accountable decisions |

No operational WorkOS capability is implemented in this batch. ClimateOS must
not receive customer/person identity, a private address, inspection notes,
enforcement state, compliance decisions, legal advice, job instructions or a
private WorkOS record.

## Future one-way context handoff

A later separately authorized interface may expose only a minimal, reviewed
context packet: place anchor; evidence identifier and class; spatial and time
scope; licence/visibility state; uncertainty and stationarity state; prohibited
uses; and human-review state. No write-back exists in v0.1.

Regional evidence can inform questions to ask. It cannot decide a site's
condition, risk, duty, approval or compliance state.
