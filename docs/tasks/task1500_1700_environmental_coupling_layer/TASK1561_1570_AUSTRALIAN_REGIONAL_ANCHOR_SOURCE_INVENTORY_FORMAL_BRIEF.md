# Task1561–1570 — Australian Regional Anchor and Scale Translation Source Inventory Formal Brief

Date: 2026-07-14

Status: PREPARED_FOR_FOUNDER_AUTHORIZATION / NOT_STARTED / READ_ONLY_RESEARCH

## 1. Purpose

Task1561–1570 prepares the Australian regional evidence anchor required before
ClimateOS can translate a planetary climate-mode or global atmospheric-model
reading toward continental, regional, local or site questions.

The task does not perform that translation. It establishes which authoritative
Australian sources, products, variables, scales and human responsibilities
would be required.

## 2. Architecture position

```text
Parallel Model Registry v0.1
    |
Parallel Model Evidence Contract v0.1
    |
Task1561–1570 Australian Regional Anchor Inventory
    |
future regional translation design
    |
future model or data acquisition gate
```

The task strengthens the regional evidence boundary before ClimateOS considers
a third global model such as GraphCast.

## 3. Authorized research question

If the Founder activates this brief in a new thread, the bounded question is:

> Which current official Australian model products, climate-driver resources,
> observations and metadata are necessary to judge whether a global
> climate-mode or atmospheric-model reading is meaningful at Australian
> continental, regional and local scales?

## 4. Scope

- confirm Task1561–1570 was not previously started;

### Task1562 — Australian anchor object taxonomy

Separate:

- model family;
- model configuration;
- public chart;
- analysis product;
- deterministic forecast product;
- ensemble forecast product;
- registered gridded product;
- reanalysis;
- station observation;
- radar or satellite observation;
- climate-driver information;
- climate projection or scenario resource.

No product may inherit the authority, access terms or resolution of another
product merely because they share a provider.

### Task1563 — ACCESS family and product inventory

Using official Bureau of Meteorology sources only:

- identify current ACCESS family names and versions;
- distinguish global, ensemble, city/regional and climate-related products;
- record documented domains, approximate resolutions, horizons and output
  forms;
- record public, registered, restricted or unknown access conditions;
- distinguish model availability from product availability;
- avoid downloading any grid file or sample dataset.

### Task1564 — Australian climate-driver source inventory

Using official Bureau of Meteorology or other Australian-government sources:

- ENSO;
- Indian Ocean Dipole;
- Southern Annular Mode;
- Madden–Julian Oscillation;
- other drivers only when an authoritative source establishes relevance.

Record definition, update cadence, spatial meaning, known interactions,
interpretation limits and source freshness. Do not produce a current climate
diagnosis or forecast.

### Task1565 — Observation and regional-resource inventory

Identify metadata and access conditions for possible future anchors including:

- station observations;
- radar and satellite products;
- rainfall and temperature products;
- climate normals and historical records;
- soil moisture, water, fire-weather or other domain resources only as named
  candidates;
- appropriate reanalysis and regional model products.

Do not retrieve observations, files or API responses.

### Task1566 — Scale-support examples

Prepare non-executable examples for:

- Australia as a continent;
- New South Wales or another state/territory scale;
- Sydney as a coastal metropolitan locality;
- Alice Springs as an inland arid locality;
- Snowy Valleys and Riverina as regional environmental contexts;
- catchment, property or project site as a prohibited unsupported level.

For each example, record what a global model can support, what it cannot
support and which regional anchors would be required.

### Task1567 — Variable and coordinate crosswalk

Create a metadata-only crosswalk covering, where officially documented:

- temperature;
- pressure;
- wind;
- humidity;
- precipitation;
- sea-surface temperature;
- soil and land variables;
- climate-mode indices;
- spatial grid, vertical coordinate, cadence, accumulation and units.

Unknown fields remain `UNVERIFIED`. No conversion or regridding is performed.

### Task1568 — Access, licence, safety and cost matrix

Record:

- official source and provider;
- access method;
- registration requirement;
- licence and redistribution boundary;
- expected file or product scale when published;
- API, FTP, cloud, registered-user or public-chart distinction;
- requester-pays or other possible cost surface;
- privacy, security and retention considerations;
- zero-cost alternative or metadata-only path.

No account, subscription, API call, paid trial or cost commitment is allowed.

### Task1569 — Human scientific responsibility map

Identify where review would be required from:

- Australian meteorologist or climatologist;
- numerical weather prediction specialist;
- climate-mode scientist;
- regional hydrology, fire, ecology, agriculture or infrastructure specialist;
- data-licence and governance reviewer;
- Founder or delegated human authority.

The task does not appoint a reviewer or claim that review occurred.

### Task1570 — Closure and next gate

- state whether a later regional translation design is ready, blocked or
  premature;
- prepare a closure record and next-thread packet if required;

## 6. Source hierarchy

Use sources in this order:

1. Australian Bureau of Meteorology official pages and technical documents;
2. Australian-government scientific agencies and official data catalogues;
3. provider-maintained technical documentation;
4. peer-reviewed papers for scientific interpretation;
5. secondary sources only to locate an authoritative source, never as the
   final authority for access, version or licence claims.

Every current product, version, access and licence claim must carry an access
date and direct source reference.

## 7. Permanent boundaries

Task1561–1570 does not authorize:

- GraphCast or another third-model research;
- cloning or downloading an external repository;
- downloading weights, model outputs or scientific datasets;
- accessing live observations or forecasts through an API;
- FTP, cloud-bucket or registered-user data acquisition;
- account creation or acceptance of new service terms;
- model installation, inference, training or evaluation;
- regridding, downscaling or regional translation execution;
- current weather, seasonal or climate diagnosis;
- Sydney, Alice Springs, Snowy Valleys, Riverina or site predictions;
- operational warning, planning recommendation or impact conclusion;
- paid commitment;
- PR #42 merge;
- automatic Task1571+ start.

## 8. Stop conditions

Stop and report if:

- PR #42 is no longer Draft, open or unmerged;
- PR Head differs from the transferred Head without explanation;
- a required official source cannot be authenticated;
- access requires account creation, payment or acceptance of new terms;
- source verification requires file or API retrieval outside the boundary;
- a regional conclusion would require unreviewed scientific interpretation;
- work begins to imply that ACCESS or another source is automatically
  authoritative for every Australian claim;
- the task would require GraphCast, model execution or real data acquisition.

## 9. Completion criteria

Task1561–1570 is complete when:

- all required metadata registries and matrices exist;
- sources and access dates are recorded;
- model, product, observation and evaluation objects remain separate;
- the continental-to-site scale boundary is explicit;
- unknown licence, access, variable or regional fields remain blocked;
- no external asset or live source was accessed;
- human responsibility is explicit;
- Task1570 closes with a hard stop.
