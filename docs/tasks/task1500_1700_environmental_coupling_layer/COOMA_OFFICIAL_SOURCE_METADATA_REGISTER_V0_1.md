## Interpretation boundary

Official status describes publisher identity, not completeness or universal
truth. A statutory page has authority for instrument identity and visible
version state, but ClimateOS does not provide legal interpretation. A Council
page proves what Council publicly exposes, not everything Council knows or does.
A proponent page proves the proponent made a statement, not independent project
performance. A station page identifies a station, not local representativeness.
