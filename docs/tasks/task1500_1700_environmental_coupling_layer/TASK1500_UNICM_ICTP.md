## 2. Founder intent to preserve

The Founder regards UniCM as a long-horizon scientific and architectural guide for ClimateOS because climate change and the coupled behaviour of environmental systems are more important than isolated short-term predictions.

The intended objective is to learn from the model’s open-source implementation, understand how coupled climate modes are represented, and eventually create a ClimateOS Environmental Coupling Layer connecting climate, water, land, carbon, ecology, energy, buildings and governance.

The formal return point is **Task1500**, with a broad development horizon through Task1700.

Do not embed UniCM directly into the ClimateOS core.

Preferred structure:

```text
External scientific model
    ↓

    ↓
Validated outputs
    ↓
Climate Mode Evidence Adapter
    ↓
Environmental Coupling Layer
    ↓
```

The adapter must preserve model identity, version, input datasets, uncertainty, spatial and temporal scope, limitations and evidence lineage.

## 6. Scientific caution

The future work must distinguish:

- model prediction from observed fact;
- association from causation;
- climate-mode signal from local impact;
- historical skill from future-climate generalization;
- research evaluation from operational forecasting;
- AI interpretation from expert scientific judgement.
