## 2. Selected single region and project

The selected study object is the **Bondo–Tumut wind evidence study area**, anchored on the proposed Bondo Wind Farm east of Tumut.

This is treated as a Snowy Valleys complex-terrain and plantation-landscape study, not as the whole Riverina and not as a Cooma study.

Official public records accessed on 2026-07-14 identify:

- NSW State Significant Development application `SSD-86276211`;
- current NSW status `Prepare EIS / Amend SEARs`;
- proposal for up to 149 wind turbine generators and about 1.2 GW;
- two proposed 400 MW / 1,600 MWh battery systems;
- EPBC referral `2026/10465`;
- project investigations and surveys in progress, but no construction or operating wind farm.

The developer page currently describes an indicative layout that may change. Project and government counts differ across evolving records; the exact current NSW Planning Portal record controls this task.

## 3. Claim under test

`BONDO_TUMUT_PUBLIC_EVIDENCE_WIND_REGIME_SCREENING`

Question:

> Can official, licence-compatible and reproducible public evidence characterize the regional wind-regime exposure of the Bondo–Tumut study area sufficiently to justify a later site-measurement and specialist-validation program?

This claim does **not** assert:

- project approval, construction or operation;
- wind-farm feasibility or bankability;
- hub-height wind resource;
- capacity factor, annual energy production or revenue;
- turbine suitability, micro-siting, wake effects or grid performance;
- environmental acceptability, safety or community consent.
