## 1. Authorization and selected claim class

The Founder authorized Task1581–1590 after authorizing and completing the
merge of PR #42. Following the Task1580 next-gate rule, this task selects one
claim class only:

`SCALE_AWARE_WIND_TRANSLATION`

The selection follows the Founder emphasis on wind as a clear demonstration
of scale relationships. It does not authorize a wind forecast, wind-resource
assessment, fire-wind conclusion, structural design value or site decision.

## 2. Purpose

Define how a future ClimateOS wind statement must preserve the identity,
height, averaging period, time support, grid or station support, terrain and
human responsibility of each source object while moving from planetary or
continental context toward a named region.

- API, FTP, registered-user, cloud-object or paid-data access;
- account creation, service-term acceptance or cost;
- model installation, inference, evaluation, regridding or downscaling;
- computation of wind speed, direction, climatology, bias or skill;
- wind-energy, structural, aviation, marine, fire or emergency advice;
- a statement about present or future wind at Sydney, Alice Springs, Snowy
  Valleys, Riverina or a project site;
- GraphCast or another third-model track;
- Task1591+ or merge of the new Draft PR.
