### Task121+ Boundary

Task120 preserves the required sequence:

```text

-> ChatGPT architecture gate
-> User final gate
-> merge / freeze
```

### Evidence Discipline

Task111-120 inherits the Task101 evidence discipline:

- raw data
- observation
- inference
- evidence
- claim
- recommendation

The package keeps CarbonOS pilot outputs illustrative and test-only. It does not present them as validated environmental conclusions.

### Expert Review Trigger Restoration

Task116 restores all 13 Task101 expert review triggers:

- high uncertainty
- conflicting evidence
- low confidence
- missing critical data
- regulatory consequence
- engineering consequence
- safety consequence
- insurance consequence
- legal consequence
- financial consequence
- public-impact consequence
- irreversible or high-cost project action
- domain-specific technical judgment

### Action-Authority Boundary

A CarbonOS / ClimateOS recommendation is not an action authority.

It may identify a possible next step, review requirement, or decision option, but it cannot authorize implementation, approval, construction, investment, compliance declaration, public claim, or operational action without the required human, expert, or governance approval.
