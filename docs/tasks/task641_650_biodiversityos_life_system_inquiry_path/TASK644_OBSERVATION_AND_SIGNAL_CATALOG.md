# Task644 Observation And Signal Catalog

## Purpose

Create a non-operational catalog of Life System signals that ClimateOS may
learn to observe, discuss, and review.

## Signal Categories

| Signal Category | Example Signals | Possible Related Domains | Evidence Need |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vegetation structure | Canopy gaps, understory loss, plant diversity changes | LandOS, WaterOS, BiodiversityOS | Baseline, spatial boundary, seasonal context |
| Wetland condition | Water level stress, edge vegetation change, habitat drying | WaterOS, LandOS, BiodiversityOS | Hydrology context, rainfall context, site history |
| Species activity | Pollinator decline, bird activity shift, indicator species change | BiodiversityOS, GovernanceOS | Survey method, observation frequency, expert review |
| Soil condition | Compaction, erosion, organic matter concern, reduced infiltration | LandOS, CarbonOS, BiodiversityOS | Soil method, land-use history, sampling limits |
| Habitat connectivity | Fragmented patches, corridor interruption, restored linkage | LandOS, BiodiversityOS, GovernanceOS | Map context, movement assumptions, planning boundary |
| Disturbance response | Recovery after flood, drought, heat, fire, construction, or mowing | WaterOS, LandOS, BiodiversityOS | Before/after record, disturbance context, uncertainty |

## What This Catalog Does

It gives ClimateOS a vocabulary for noticing living-system signals.

## What This Catalog Does Not Do

It does not classify real sites, calculate biodiversity value, run ecological
models, score resilience, certify outcomes, or integrate sensors.

## Human Review Need

Many Life System signals are easy to misread. A single observation may be
seasonal, local, method-biased, or caused by unrelated factors. Human review and
expert review remain necessary before interpretation.
