# Task645 Hypothesis And Evidence Pathways

## Purpose

Show how ClimateOS can move from a living-system signal to a cautious hypothesis
and then to evidence needs.

## Pathway Pattern

| Step | Question | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signal | What has been noticed? | Observation note |
| Relevance | Why might it matter? | Climate or resilience concern |
| Hypothesis | What might be true? | Cautious, testable statement |
| Evidence need | What would help check it? | Sources, baselines, methods |
| Review | What could be wrong? | Uncertainty and alternative explanations |
| Learning record | What can be said now? | Current understanding and next observation |

## Example Pathway A: Vegetation And Heat

Signal: a fictional site has reduced canopy cover and simplified vegetation
structure.

Hypothesis: reduced vegetation complexity may be associated with higher local
heat stress or lower comfort during heat events.

Evidence needs:

- prior canopy or vegetation records;
- temperature or comfort observations;
- land surface context;
- seasonal context;
- nearby comparison areas;
- expert review of vegetation interpretation.

Not allowed:

- heat-resilience score;
- cooling-effect claim;
- public recommendation;
- automated site ranking.

## Example Pathway B: Wetland Stress And Water Buffering

Signal: a fictional wetland edge appears dry and habitat structure appears
reduced.

Hypothesis: wetland stress may reduce local water-buffering and ecological
recovery functions.

Evidence needs:

- rainfall and water-level context;
- site history;
- vegetation and habitat observations;
- hydrology review;
- alternative explanation review.

Not allowed:

- flood-risk conclusion;
- engineering claim;
- biodiversity credit;
- certification statement.

## Current Capability

These pathways are reasoning scaffolds. They do not execute analysis.
