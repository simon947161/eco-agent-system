# Task643 Biodiversity Climate Question Map

## Purpose

Map initial safe inquiry questions that connect BiodiversityOS to climate
resilience without creating conclusions or scores.

## Question Families

| Question Family | Plain-Language Question | ClimateOS Relevance | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Heat resilience | Does vegetation structure or habitat diversity appear related to local heat stress? | Connects BiodiversityOS, LandOS, WaterOS, and human wellbeing. | No heat-island conclusion without evidence and review. |
| Water buffering | Do wetlands, riparian zones, or vegetated areas appear to buffer flood or drought stress? | Connects biodiversity, hydrology, land condition, and resilience. | No flood-risk claim or engineering conclusion. |
| Soil life | Does soil condition suggest reduced recovery capacity or carbon-cycle risk? | Connects living systems, soil, carbon, agriculture, and land use. | No soil-carbon calculation or certification. |
| Ecological connectivity | Are habitats becoming isolated or better connected? | Connects species movement, climate adaptation, and planning. | No conservation priority ranking. |
| Disturbance recovery | How does a living system respond after heat, drought, flood, fire, or human disturbance? | Connects resilience, observation, and governance. | No automated recovery score. |
| Human action | Which human actions may support or damage living-system resilience? | Connects Sustainable Office, community action, and governance. | No behavioral mandate or compliance claim. |

## Interpretation

These are inquiry questions. They help ClimateOS ask better questions before it
answers anything.

## Required Review

Each question family requires:

- observation source review;
- spatial and temporal boundary review;
- alternative explanation review;
- domain expert or human review before public-facing use;
- Founder Gate before scoring, certification, deployment, or runtime
  integration.
