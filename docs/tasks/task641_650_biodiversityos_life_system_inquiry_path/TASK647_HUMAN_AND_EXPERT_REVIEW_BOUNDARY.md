# Task647 Human And Expert Review Boundary

## Purpose

Define why BiodiversityOS and Life System inquiry requires human and expert
review before claims are reused.

## Why Review Matters

Living systems are context-sensitive. A change that looks like ecological
decline may be seasonal, transitional, site-specific, or method-biased. A change
that looks minor may be important if it affects habitat connectivity, recovery
capacity, water buffering, or community wellbeing.

ClimateOS should therefore treat BiodiversityOS inquiry as review-dependent.

## Review Roles

| Role | Review Contribution |
| --- | --- |
| Founder | Decides whether a batch, gate, or future capability may proceed. |
| Domain expert | Reviews ecological, hydrological, land, carbon, or governance interpretation. |
| Local observer | Provides site memory, lived experience, and practical context. |
| ClimateOS reviewer | Checks evidence structure, uncertainty, and boundary compliance. |
| Future agent reviewer | May assist only after agent-interface authorization and audit rules exist. |

## Human-Readable Rule

ClimateOS can help people ask better questions, but it must not pretend that
early evidence has already answered them.

## Expert Review Triggers

- Public-facing use.
- Site comparison.
- Biodiversity value claim.
- Climate resilience claim.
- Carbon, water, or land-management implication.
- Any movement toward scoring, certification, deployment, or automated action.

## Current Capability

No expert review workflow, agent review workflow, or runtime review queue is
implemented here.
