# Task646 Review And Uncertainty Loop

## Purpose

Define how Life System inquiry should keep uncertainty visible instead of
turning early observations into conclusions.

## Review Loop

1. Record the observation.
2. Name the possible interpretation.
3. Identify missing evidence.
4. Ask for alternative explanations.
5. Check domain boundaries.
6. Mark uncertainty.
7. Decide whether more observation, expert review, or Founder Gate is needed.
8. Update the learning record.

## Common Uncertainties

- Seasonal variation may mimic ecological decline.
- One site may not represent a larger region.
- Species observations may be incomplete or biased.
- Land management history may explain observed change.
- Heat, water, soil, and biodiversity signals may be correlated without proven
  causation.
- Community observations may be valuable but still need context and review.

## Review States

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Observation only | A signal was noticed but not interpreted. |
| Hypothesis candidate | A possible relationship was stated cautiously. |
| Evidence needed | The current record cannot support interpretation. |
| Expert review needed | Domain expertise is required before reuse. |
| Disputed | Multiple interpretations remain plausible. |
| Deferred | The record is useful but not ready. |
| Founder Gate required | The next use could affect governance, public-facing claims, or system direction. |

## Boundary

The loop supports disciplined learning. It does not produce automated
confidence scores, ecological grades, certification, or final climate claims.
