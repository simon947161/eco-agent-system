# Task238 Evidence Passport PRI Runtime Passport Distinction

## Purpose

Clarify the distinction between Evidence Passport, PRI, and any future Runtime Passport.

This document is governance-only and does not create Evidence Passport architecture, PRI implementation, Runtime Passport architecture, runtime, automation, or operational routing.

## Core Principle

PRI translates, routes, and governs routing.

ClimateOS preserves Mission.

Evidence Passport may preserve evidence context only after authorization.

Runtime Passport may preserve runtime continuity only after authorization.

## Evidence Passport Future Role

Evidence Passport remains a future-state concept.

After separate Founder authorization, it may be considered for preserving evidence context, traceability, review state, and governance history.

It must not create truth, compliance, assurance, certification, scoring, framework interpretation, ESG conclusions, or carbon conclusions.

## PRI Translation Role

PRI may provide governance translation between systems where separately authorized.

PRI translation does not convert PRI into ClimateOS and does not authorize ClimateOS implementation.

## PRI Routing Role

PRI may support routing governance concepts where separately authorized.

PRI routing does not become Evidence Passport operation, ClimateOS runtime, MCP implementation, n8n workflow, QCloud integration, or automation.

## PRI Governance Role

PRI governance records may inform boundary language and continuity principles.

PRI governance does not authorize Evidence Passport architecture, runtime, implementation, external use, scoring, compliance guidance, assurance guidance, certification guidance, or ESG / carbon conclusions.

## Runtime Passport Future Role

Runtime Passport remains a separate future-state concept.

After separate authorization, it may be considered for preserving runtime continuity context.

Task231-240 does not define Runtime Passport architecture, runtime behavior, implementation, services, data models, APIs, workflows, or automation.

## Boundary Between Evidence Passport And PRI

Evidence Passport may only be considered as a future ClimateOS evidence-context governance concept.

PRI may translate and route governance context where authorized.

PRI must not become Evidence Passport and Evidence Passport must not become PRI.

## Boundary Between Evidence Passport And Runtime Passport

Evidence Passport and Runtime Passport must remain distinct.

Evidence Passport concerns future evidence context preservation only after authorization.

Runtime Passport concerns future runtime continuity preservation only after authorization.

Neither is operational in Task231-240.

## PRI Does Not Become ClimateOS

PRI is not ClimateOS.

PRI governance assets may inform translation and routing boundaries, but they do not replace ClimateOS Mission, Human Authority, Founder review, or Architecture Enhancement Protocol controls.

## Evidence Passport Does Not Become ClimateOS

Evidence Passport must not become the governing identity of ClimateOS.

Evidence Passport may serve ClimateOS Mission only after authorization and within strict non-authority limits.

## Runtime Passport Does Not Become ClimateOS

Runtime Passport must not become the governing identity of ClimateOS.

Runtime continuity, if later authorized, remains subordinate to Mission, Human Authority, Founder review, and governance boundaries.
