# Task232 Evidence Passport Purpose And Non-Purpose

## Purpose

Define the future purpose and non-purpose of an Evidence Passport.

This document is governance-only. It does not create an Evidence Passport, define architecture, or authorize implementation.

## Future Purpose

A future Evidence Passport may be considered as a governed context-preservation mechanism for evidence-related records after separate Founder authorization.

Possible future purpose areas may include:

- Preserving context.
- Preserving traceability.
- Preserving review state.
- Preserving governance history.
- Showing which governance gates have or have not been completed.
- Supporting human review by making provenance and limitations visible.

These future purpose areas remain non-operational until separately authorized.

## Future Non-Purpose

An Evidence Passport must not be treated as:

- A truth engine.
- A compliance engine.
- An assurance mechanism.
- A certification mechanism.
- A scoring engine.
- A regulatory interpreter.
- A standards interpreter.
- A replacement for Human Authority.
- A replacement for Founder review.
- A substitute for Mission Alignment review.

## Mission Relationship

Evidence Passport must remain subordinate to ClimateOS Mission.

It may only support Mission-aligned evidence context after future authorization.

If Evidence Passport purpose conflicts with Mission, the work must be deferred for Human Authority and Founder review.

## Knowledge Object Relationship

Evidence Passport must depend on Knowledge Object governance.

A future Evidence Passport may not allow a source, signal, claim, or draft record to bypass Knowledge Object boundary rules, lifecycle status, or evidence-readiness review.

## Evidence Readiness Relationship

Evidence Passport may only be considered after Evidence Readiness governance has defined the status and limitations of a record.

Evidence Readiness does not automatically authorize Evidence Passport inclusion.

## Human Authority Relationship

Human Authority remains responsible for judgment, context, moral responsibility, and escalation.

Human Authority is not replaced by Evidence Passport status, labels, metadata, or future records.

## Prohibited Purpose Statements

Task231-240 prohibits statements that an Evidence Passport:

- Verifies truth.
- Certifies compliance.
- Provides assurance.
- Interprets standards.
- Scores evidence.
- Confirms ESG performance.
- Confirms carbon performance.
- Confirms regulatory readiness.
- Replaces human responsibility.

## Misuse Examples

Potential misuse includes:

- Treating a future Evidence Passport label as proof of truth.
- Using preserved evidence context as a compliance claim.
- Presenting review state as assurance.
- Presenting governance history as certification.
- Bypassing Human Authority because a record appears complete.
- Publicly implying operational readiness before runtime is authorized.

## Required Core Statements

An Evidence Passport does not automatically verify truth.

An Evidence Passport does not certify compliance.

An Evidence Passport does not provide assurance.

An Evidence Passport does not interpret standards.

An Evidence Passport does not replace human responsibility.
