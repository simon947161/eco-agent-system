# Task231 Evidence Passport Pre-Architecture Boundary

## Purpose

Define the boundary of Evidence Passport pre-architecture governance.

## What Pre-Architecture Governance Means

Pre-architecture governance means defining the constitutional, mission, authority, risk, review, and authorization rules that must exist before future Evidence Passport architecture may be considered.

It may define:

- Governance conditions.
- Prohibited uses.
- Human review triggers.
- Founder authorization requirements.
- Non-authority rules.
- Future authorization criteria.
- Deferral and rejection conditions.

## What It Does Not Mean

Pre-architecture governance does not define:

- Architecture.
- Runtime architecture.
- Implementation design.
- System components.
- Data models.
- Schemas.
- APIs.
- Databases.
- Workflows.
- Services.
- Agents.
- Dashboards.
- Automation.
- Operational Evidence Passport behavior.

## Why Architecture Is Not Authorized

Evidence Passport architecture is not authorized because the governance conditions for authority, risk, misuse prevention, Mission alignment, Knowledge Object dependency, and Human Authority have not previously been isolated as a dedicated gate.

Task231-240 closes that governance gap only.

It does not approve future architecture.

## Why Implementation Is Not Authorized

Implementation is not authorized because Evidence Passport remains future-state only.

No operational Evidence Passport may be created before separate Founder authorization, architecture approval, risk review, Human Authority review, and explicit runtime permission.

## Relationship To Task221-230

Task221-230 established Knowledge Object and Evidence Readiness Governance.

Task231-240 depends on that foundation. It may define how future Evidence Passport work must respect Knowledge Object status, Evidence Candidate status, Evidence-Ready Object status, Evidence Admission Gate, and Knowledge Object Lifecycle.

Task231-240 must not weaken or bypass Task221-230.

## Boundary Against Operational Evidence Passport

Task231-240 does not create an operational Evidence Passport.

It does not admit evidence, verify claims, certify outcomes, preserve live records, assign status, or generate review outcomes.

Evidence Passport remains a possible future concept requiring later authorization.

## Boundary Against Scoring

Task231-240 does not create scoring.

It does not create evidence scores, confidence scores, readiness scores, trust scores, risk scores, compliance scores, assurance scores, certification scores, or ESG / carbon performance scores.

Any future scoring discussion requires separate Founder authorization and a stronger misuse review.

## Boundary Against Compliance / Assurance / Certification

Task231-240 does not create compliance guidance, assurance guidance, certification guidance, regulatory interpretation, or standards interpretation.

Evidence Passport must not be treated as a compliance, assurance, or certification authority.

## Boundary Status

```text
Evidence Passport pre-architecture governance: DEFINED
Evidence Passport architecture: NOT AUTHORIZED
Evidence Passport implementation: NOT AUTHORIZED
Operational Evidence Passport: NOT CREATED
Scoring: NOT CREATED
Compliance / assurance / certification authority: NOT CREATED
```
