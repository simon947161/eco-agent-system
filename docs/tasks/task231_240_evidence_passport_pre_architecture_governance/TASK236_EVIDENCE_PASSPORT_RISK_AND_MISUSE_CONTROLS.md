# Task236 Evidence Passport Risk And Misuse Controls

## Purpose

Identify and control risks before any future Evidence Passport architecture is authorized.

This document is governance-only and does not create architecture, implementation, runtime, scoring, or operational Evidence Passport behavior.

## False Authority Risk

Risk: Evidence Passport language could be read as official authority, approval, verification, or institutional endorsement.

Control: Evidence Passport must be framed as context preservation only unless separately authorized. Authority claims must be denied or escalated.

## False Certainty Risk

Risk: Traceability or review history could be mistaken for certainty.

Control: Future Evidence Passport work must preserve uncertainty, limitations, open questions, and review state.

## False Compliance Risk

Risk: Evidence Passport context could be misused as compliance evidence or compliance readiness.

Control: Compliance language is prohibited unless separately authorized and reviewed outside this batch.

## False Assurance Risk

Risk: Evidence Passport context could be presented as assurance, audit readiness, or verification outcome.

Control: Assurance language is prohibited unless separately authorized and reviewed outside this batch.

## False Certification Risk

Risk: Evidence Passport context could be presented as certification, approval, or endorsement.

Control: Certification language is prohibited unless separately authorized and reviewed outside this batch.

## False ESG / Carbon Conclusion Risk

Risk: Evidence Passport context could be used to imply ESG performance or carbon accounting conclusions.

Control: ESG and carbon conclusions remain prohibited. Evidence Passport must not create performance conclusions.

## Framework Misinterpretation Risk

Risk: Evidence Passport context could be used to imply framework interpretation, standards mapping, or requirement satisfaction.

Control: Framework and standards interpretation remain prohibited. Future work must preserve non-interpretation boundaries.

## Automation Misuse Risk

Risk: Evidence Passport labels could become automated decisions, routing, approvals, or scoring.

Control: Automation remains prohibited. Human Authority and Founder gates remain required.

## External Misuse Risk

Risk: External readers could treat future Evidence Passport records as verified truth, compliance, assurance, certification, or product claims.

Control: External use requires Founder review, public communication review, and explicit authorization.

## Partner Misuse Risk

Risk: Partners could use future Evidence Passport records beyond approved scope.

Control: Partner-facing use requires review of allowed language, non-authority statements, and misuse prevention boundaries.

## Public Communication Risk

Risk: Public claims could overstate what Evidence Passport means.

Control: Public communication must avoid verification, certification, assurance, compliance, ESG conclusion, and carbon conclusion language unless separately authorized.

## Human Responsibility Dilution Risk

Risk: Evidence Passport could appear to carry responsibility that belongs to humans.

Control: Human Authority remains a constitutional responsibility layer. Future Evidence Passport context must not replace human responsibility.

## Mitigation Principles

Mitigation must follow these principles:

- Preserve Mission above evidence presentation.
- Preserve Human Authority above record status.
- Preserve uncertainty and limits.
- Preserve non-interpretation boundaries.
- Deny false authority.
- Defer high-risk uses.
- Require Founder review for scope expansion.

## Escalation Triggers

Escalate for Founder and Human Authority review when future Evidence Passport work touches:

- Authority expansion.
- External use.
- Partner use.
- Public communication.
- Compliance.
- Assurance.
- Certification.
- Regulation.
- Framework interpretation.
- ESG conclusions.
- Carbon conclusions.
- Runtime.
- Architecture.
- Implementation.
- Automation.
- Scoring.

## Deferral Triggers

Defer future Evidence Passport work when:

- Misuse risk is unresolved.
- Mission alignment is unclear.
- Human responsibility is weakened.
- Knowledge Object status is unclear.
- Evidence Readiness status is unclear.
- Evidence Admission Gate requirements are incomplete.
- Required review has not occurred.
- Runtime, architecture, implementation, automation, or scoring would be needed.
