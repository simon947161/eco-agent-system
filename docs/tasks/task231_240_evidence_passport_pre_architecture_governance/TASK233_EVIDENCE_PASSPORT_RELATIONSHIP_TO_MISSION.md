# Task233 Evidence Passport Relationship To Mission

## Purpose

Define how any future Evidence Passport must remain subordinate to ClimateOS Mission.

This document is governance-only and does not create Evidence Passport architecture or implementation.

## Core Statement

Evidence Passport serves Mission.

Mission does not serve Evidence Passport.

## Mission Supremacy

ClimateOS Mission remains the higher authority layer.

A future Evidence Passport may only preserve evidence context in service of Mission-aligned review, learning, resilience, and human responsibility after separate authorization.

Evidence Passport must not become an independent source of purpose, legitimacy, or authority.

## Evidence As Servant Of Mission

Evidence may support Mission by preserving context, uncertainty, traceability, and review limitations.

Evidence must not redirect Mission toward what is easiest to document, easiest to label, easiest to score, or easiest to present externally.

## No Evidence Authority Above Mission

No Evidence Passport record, future label, future status, or future context marker may override Mission.

Evidence Passport must not force ClimateOS into a decision, public position, compliance implication, assurance implication, certification implication, ESG conclusion, or carbon conclusion.

## Mission Conflict Trigger

Human review is required when future Evidence Passport work could:

- Pull ClimateOS away from Mission.
- Convert evidence context into false authority.
- Prioritize presentation over truthfulness.
- Prioritize completeness over uncertainty.
- Dilute human responsibility.
- Create external pressure to make unauthorized claims.

## Human Review Requirement

Human review is required for any future Evidence Passport-related work that may affect Mission interpretation, evidence authority, external communication, partner use, public use, high-risk claims, or governance boundaries.

Human review must remain substantive and cannot be treated as a checkbox.

## Founder Review Trigger

Founder review is required when Evidence Passport-related work may:

- Change Mission interpretation.
- Establish or revise Evidence Passport authority.
- Move from governance to architecture.
- Move from architecture to implementation.
- Allow external or partner-facing use.
- Touch compliance, assurance, certification, framework interpretation, ESG conclusions, or carbon conclusions.

## Misalignment Examples

Misalignment may include:

- Treating Evidence Passport completeness as proof of truth.
- Presenting Evidence Passport status as compliance readiness.
- Using Evidence Passport context to avoid human judgment.
- Prioritizing external credibility over uncertainty disclosure.
- Allowing future architecture to shape Mission rather than serve Mission.

## Deferral Rules

Evidence Passport-related work must be deferred when:

- Mission alignment is unclear.
- Human responsibility is diluted.
- Evidence authority is overstated.
- Compliance, assurance, or certification implications appear.
- Framework interpretation is required.
- Runtime, architecture, implementation, automation, or scoring would be needed.
