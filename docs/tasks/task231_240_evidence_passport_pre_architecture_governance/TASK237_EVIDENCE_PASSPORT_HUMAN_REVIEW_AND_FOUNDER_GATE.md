# Task237 Evidence Passport Human Review And Founder Gate

## Purpose

Define when Human Review and Founder Gate are required before any Evidence Passport-related work proceeds.

This document is governance-only and does not authorize Evidence Passport architecture, implementation, runtime, scoring, or operation.

## Core Statement

Human Authority is a constitutional responsibility layer, not an administrative checkbox.

## Founder Authorization Requirement

Founder authorization is required before:

- Task231-240 begins.
- Any future Evidence Passport architecture batch begins.
- Any future Evidence Passport implementation batch begins.
- Any future Evidence Passport runtime is considered.
- Any future Evidence Passport external use is considered.
- Any future Evidence Passport authority is expanded.

Task231-240 authorization does not authorize Task241-250 or any later work.

## Human Authority Requirement

Human Authority is required when evidence context may affect judgment, Mission, public communication, partner expectations, high-risk decisions, or responsibility allocation.

Human review must assess limits, uncertainty, misuse risk, non-authority, and whether deferral is required.

## Mission Conflict Trigger

Mission conflict requires Human Authority review and Founder review.

Mission conflict exists when future Evidence Passport work could shift ClimateOS away from Mission, convert evidence context into false authority, or prioritize presentation over responsible governance.

## High-Risk Evidence Trigger

High-risk evidence requires Human Authority review before any future Evidence Passport treatment.

High-risk evidence includes material that could affect public claims, community trust, environmental decisions, safety, resilience, resource allocation, partner commitments, compliance expectations, assurance expectations, certification expectations, ESG conclusions, or carbon conclusions.

## External-Use Trigger

External use requires Founder review.

External use includes any use outside internal governance review, including sharing with partners, funders, public audiences, customers, auditors, certifiers, regulators, media, or community decision groups.

## Partner-Use Trigger

Partner-facing Evidence Passport language requires review of scope, non-authority, limits, misuse risk, and communication boundaries.

Partner use must not imply verification, certification, assurance, compliance, ESG conclusion, carbon conclusion, or operational readiness.

## Public-Use Trigger

Public use requires Founder review and explicit public communication boundary review.

Public use must not imply that Evidence Passport is operational unless runtime has separately been authorized and implemented.

## Compliance-Related Trigger

Any compliance-related use, wording, request, expectation, or implication requires deferral and separate Founder review.

Task231-240 does not authorize compliance guidance.

## Assurance-Related Trigger

Any assurance-related use, wording, request, expectation, or implication requires deferral and separate Founder review.

Task231-240 does not authorize assurance guidance.

## Certification-Related Trigger

Any certification-related use, wording, request, expectation, or implication requires deferral and separate Founder review.

Task231-240 does not authorize certification guidance.

## Framework-Related Trigger

Any framework-related interpretation, comparison, mapping, requirement satisfaction, or standards-language implication requires deferral and separate Founder review.

Task231-240 does not authorize framework or standards interpretation.

## Deferral Rules

Evidence Passport-related work must be deferred when:

- Founder authorization is missing.
- Human Authority review is missing.
- Mission conflict is unresolved.
- Misuse risk is unresolved.
- Knowledge Object or Evidence Readiness dependency is unclear.
- Compliance, assurance, certification, regulation, ESG, carbon, or framework implications appear.
- Runtime, architecture, implementation, automation, or scoring would be needed.

## Rejection Rules

Evidence Passport-related work must be rejected when it proposes:

- False authority.
- False certainty.
- Automatic verification.
- Compliance authority.
- Assurance authority.
- Certification authority.
- Standards interpretation.
- ESG or carbon conclusions.
- Replacement of Human Authority.
- Runtime or implementation without authorization.

## Escalation Rules

Escalate to Founder review when scope moves beyond documentation-only governance, when external use is proposed, when authority changes, when architecture is proposed, or when any prohibited work appears necessary.
