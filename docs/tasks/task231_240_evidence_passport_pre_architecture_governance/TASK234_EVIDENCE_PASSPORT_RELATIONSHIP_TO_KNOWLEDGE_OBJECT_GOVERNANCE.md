# Task234 Evidence Passport Relationship To Knowledge Object Governance

## Purpose

Define how Evidence Passport depends on Knowledge Object and Evidence Readiness governance.

This document preserves Task221-230 boundaries and does not create Evidence Passport architecture or implementation.

## Relationship To Knowledge Object

A future Evidence Passport may only be considered for records governed through Knowledge Object rules.

Knowledge Object governance remains prior to any future Evidence Passport treatment.

Evidence Passport must not convert unreviewed material into governed knowledge.

## Relationship To Evidence Candidate

Evidence Candidate status indicates that a Knowledge Object may be reviewed for evidence readiness.

Evidence Candidate status does not authorize Evidence Passport inclusion, architecture, runtime, scoring, compliance use, assurance use, certification use, or public evidence claims.

## Relationship To Evidence-Ready Object

Evidence-Ready Object status may indicate that a record has completed readiness review under Task221-230 governance.

Evidence-Ready Object status does not automatically create an Evidence Passport, operational evidence, claim verification, compliance status, assurance status, certification status, or ESG / carbon conclusion.

## Relationship To Evidence Admission Gate

Evidence Passport must not bypass the Evidence Admission Gate.

Any future Evidence Passport architecture proposal must cite the relevant Evidence Admission Gate rule and show that no shortcut is being created.

This document does not perform that future review.

## Relationship To Knowledge Object Lifecycle

Knowledge Object lifecycle status must remain visible before future Evidence Passport consideration.

Draft, candidate, review-ready, deferred, rejected, superseded, or closed states must not be hidden by future Evidence Passport presentation.

## No Shortcut From Source To Evidence Passport

A source must not move directly into Evidence Passport treatment.

Source material must first pass through applicable source governance, signal review, Knowledge Object rules, and Evidence Readiness governance.

## No Shortcut From Claim To Evidence Passport

A claim must not move directly into Evidence Passport treatment.

Claim material requires boundary review, source linkage, non-interpretation controls, and Human Authority review where needed.

## No Shortcut From Signal To Operational Evidence

A signal must not become operational evidence through Evidence Passport language.

Signal status remains distinct from Knowledge Object status, Evidence Candidate status, Evidence-Ready Object status, and future operational evidence.

## Preservation Of Task221-230 Boundaries

Task221-230 remains intact.

Task231-240 adds pre-architecture Evidence Passport governance only and does not rewrite Knowledge Object definitions, Evidence Readiness levels, Evidence Admission Gate, Knowledge Object Lifecycle, or Founder / Human Authority gate substance.
