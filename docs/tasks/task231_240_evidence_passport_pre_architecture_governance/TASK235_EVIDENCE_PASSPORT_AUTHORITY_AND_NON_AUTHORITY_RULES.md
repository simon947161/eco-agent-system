# Task235 Evidence Passport Authority And Non-Authority Rules

## Purpose

Define what authority a future Evidence Passport may and may not have.

This document is governance-only and does not authorize architecture, implementation, runtime, scoring, compliance use, assurance use, or certification use.

## Preferred Framing

Evidence Passport may preserve context.

Evidence Passport may preserve traceability.

Evidence Passport may preserve review state.

Evidence Passport may preserve governance history.

Evidence Passport must not create authority by itself.

## Possible Future Limited Authority

After separate Founder authorization, future Evidence Passport work may be allowed to preserve limited governance context such as:

- Whether a record has identified sources.
- Whether review limitations are stated.
- Whether Human Authority review is required.
- Whether Founder review is required.
- Whether a record is deferred, rejected, superseded, or not authorized.
- Whether a future architecture decision remains blocked.

These are possible future governance markers only.

They do not authorize current architecture or implementation.

## Explicit Non-Authority

Evidence Passport must not have authority to:

- Verify truth.
- Certify compliance.
- Provide assurance.
- Certify a project, organization, method, claim, or result.
- Interpret regulation.
- Interpret frameworks or standards.
- Score evidence.
- Score ESG performance.
- Score carbon performance.
- Replace Founder judgment.
- Replace Human Authority.
- Override Mission.
- Make autonomous decisions.

## No Compliance Authority

Evidence Passport must not create compliance status, compliance readiness, compliance conclusions, regulatory conclusions, or compliance guidance.

## No Assurance Authority

Evidence Passport must not create assurance status, assurance readiness, assurance conclusions, audit conclusions, or assurance guidance.

## No Certification Authority

Evidence Passport must not create certification status, certification readiness, certification conclusions, approval claims, or certification guidance.

## No Regulatory Authority

Evidence Passport must not interpret, apply, or speak on behalf of regulatory requirements.

Regulatory interpretation requires separate authorized human and legal review outside this batch.

## No Scoring Authority

Evidence Passport must not create numeric, categorical, weighted, ordinal, or implied scoring.

Labels that imply ranking, approval, credibility, confidence, trust, compliance, assurance, or certification are prohibited unless separately authorized and reviewed.

## No Replacement Of Founder / Human Authority

Founder review and Human Authority remain above any future Evidence Passport governance marker.

Evidence Passport must not convert review state into delegated authority.

## Authority Escalation Rules

Founder review is required if future Evidence Passport work proposes:

- Any authority beyond context preservation.
- Any external-facing use.
- Any partner-facing use.
- Any public communication use.
- Any relationship to compliance, assurance, certification, regulation, ESG conclusions, or carbon conclusions.
- Any architecture, implementation, runtime, automation, or scoring.

## Authority Denial Rules

Evidence Passport authority must be denied when:

- The proposed use creates false authority.
- The proposed use creates false certainty.
- The proposed use creates compliance, assurance, or certification implications.
- The proposed use bypasses Human Authority.
- The proposed use bypasses Knowledge Object or Evidence Readiness governance.
- The proposed use requires standards interpretation.
- Runtime or implementation would be required.
