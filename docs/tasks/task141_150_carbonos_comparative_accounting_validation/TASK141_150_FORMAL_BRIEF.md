## Core Principle

CarbonOS does not act as a single jurisdictional claim-maker.

It compares carbon accounting methods, boundaries, evidence, and claim structures across systems, then connects them to ClimateOS / EcoEngine validation questions about real-world climate and ecological effects.

Chinese explanation:

```text
CarbonOS 不作为某一个国家或标准体系的单一裁判。
它比较不同碳核算体系的方法、边界、证据和主张结构，
再把这些结果接入 ClimateOS / EcoEngine，
继续追问这些披露、减排和净零主张是否对应真实世界的气候与生态改善。
```
