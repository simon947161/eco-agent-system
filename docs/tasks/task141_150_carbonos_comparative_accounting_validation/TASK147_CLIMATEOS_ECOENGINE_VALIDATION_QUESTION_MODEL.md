# Task147 ClimateOS / EcoEngine Validation Question Model

## Purpose

Task147 defines how CarbonOS comparative outputs may connect to ClimateOS / EcoEngine questions about real-world climate, ecological, physical-system, and risk-reduction effects.

This document creates a question model only. It does not implement modelling, simulations, APIs, EcoEngine calls, databases, calculators, scoring, or environmental conclusion claims.

## Validation Question Categories

CarbonOS comparative outputs may lead to validation questions in these categories:

- climate effect questions
- ecological effect questions
- physical-system questions
- resilience / risk-reduction questions
- evidence sufficiency questions
- uncertainty questions
- boundary adequacy questions
- method relevance questions
- claim-to-impact linkage questions

Questions are not conclusions.

## Climate Effect Questions

Future review may ask:

- What climate effect is being implied or claimed?
- What evidence would be needed to support that effect?
- What temporal scale would matter?
- What baseline or counterfactual question exists?
- What uncertainty prevents conclusion?
- What would distinguish accounting movement from real-world climate effect?

Task147 does not answer these questions.

## Ecological Effect Questions

Future review may ask:

- What ecological system is relevant?
- What ecological outcome is implied?
- What evidence type would be needed?
- What spatial boundary matters?
- What temporal lag may exist?
- What biodiversity, soil, water, habitat, or ecosystem-service uncertainty is unresolved?

Task147 does not claim ecological improvement.

## Physical-System Questions

Future review may ask:

- What physical process would need to change?
- What measurement or observation evidence would be relevant?
- What system boundary affects interpretation?
- What upstream or downstream physical dependency exists?
- What model or observation uncertainty remains?

Task147 does not create physical models or simulations.

## Resilience / Risk-Reduction Questions

Future review may ask:

- What risk is being reduced?
- Who or what experiences the risk reduction?
- What time horizon is relevant?
- What evidence would show reduced exposure, sensitivity, or vulnerability?
- What adaptation or resilience assumptions need review?
- What risk-transfer or displacement concern exists?

Task147 does not claim resilience improvement or risk reduction.

## Evidence Sufficiency Questions

CarbonOS may route evidence questions such as:

- Is the evidence traceable?
- Is the source current?
- Is the boundary supported?
- Is the method reference reviewable?
- Is uncertainty visible?
- Is expert review needed?
- Is ClimateOS / EcoEngine validation linkage only a question?

Evidence sufficiency remains a review question, not an automated decision.

## Uncertainty Questions

The model should preserve questions about:

- source uncertainty
- boundary uncertainty
- method uncertainty
- formula / factor uncertainty
- data completeness
- spatial limits
- temporal limits
- causality limits
- attribution limits
- review limits

## Relationship To CarbonOS Claim Boundaries

Task147 receives claim type, boundary type, evidence requirement, method reference, and uncertainty fields from Task146.

The validation question model asks what would need to be true, observed, sourced, reviewed, or modelled before a carbon accounting claim could be connected to real-world climate or ecological effects.

It does not make that connection as a conclusion.

## No Modelling Implementation

Task147 does not create:

- models
- simulations
- calculations
- EcoEngine integrations
- runtime sessions
- APIs
- data pipelines
- scoring
- automated validators

## No Environmental Conclusion Claims

Task147 may not claim:

- emissions reduction occurred
- climate benefit occurred
- ecological improvement occurred
- risk reduction occurred
- resilience increased
- project impact is valid
- disclosure claim is environmentally sufficient
