# Task146 CarbonOS Claim Boundary And Intake Upgrade

## Purpose

Task146 defines how CarbonOS should structure claim intake and claim boundaries for future comparative review.

This is a documentation-only architecture upgrade. It does not create claim forms, apps, database schemas, APIs, runtime workflows, live intake systems, calculators, scoring, compliance determinations, or real claim records.

## Claim Type Model

Future CarbonOS comparative review may classify claims as:

- inventory claim
- accounting boundary claim
- baseline claim
- reduction claim
- removal claim
- offset claim
- avoided emissions claim
- transition claim
- disclosure support claim
- climate effect claim
- ecological effect claim
- resilience / risk-reduction claim
- unresolved or mixed claim

Classification is descriptive only. It does not accept, reject, verify, assure, certify, or approve a claim.

## Boundary Type Model

Future claim intake may ask which boundary types are involved:

- organizational boundary
- operational boundary
- product boundary
- project boundary
- activity boundary
- value-chain boundary
- temporal boundary
- geographic boundary
- system boundary
- evidence boundary
- review boundary
- unresolved boundary

Task146 does not interpret any boundary as a standard-specific requirement.

## Evidence Requirement Model

Future claim review may record evidence needs such as:

- source document needed
- activity data needed
- measurement record needed
- method reference needed
- formula reference needed
- factor reference needed
- assumption register needed
- uncertainty statement needed
- boundary support needed
- independent review note needed
- ClimateOS / EcoEngine validation evidence needed

This model identifies evidence questions. It does not determine sufficiency.

## Method Reference Requirement

Future claim intake may require the claimant or reviewer to identify whether a method reference is:

- absent
- named but uncited
- cited but not reviewed
- primary-source supported
- versioned
- disputed
- superseded
- not applicable
- blocked pending Task143 protocol review

No method reference state authorizes calculation or compliance determination.

## Uncertainty And Limitation Fields

Claim boundary review should preserve:

- known data gaps
- unknown data gaps
- boundary uncertainty
- source uncertainty
- method uncertainty
- formula / factor uncertainty
- temporal limitation
- geographic limitation
- applicability limitation
- review limitation
- unresolved disagreement

Uncertainty fields must remain visible in any future handoff.

## Prohibited Claims

CarbonOS may not use this intake model to claim:

- legal compliance
- disclosure compliance
- assurance
- verification
- certification
- emissions reduction achieved
- removal achieved
- offset validity
- net zero status
- nature-positive status
- real-world climate or ecological outcome
- public disclosure sufficiency

## No Real Claim Intake

Task146 does not accept or process real claims from companies, projects, assets, products, disclosures, jurisdictions, or interventions.

Any future real claim intake requires separate governance, data protection, review, legal, and architecture approval.

## No Live Workflow

This task creates no workflow engine, form, application, database, API endpoint, queue, automation, agent, MCP connector, or QCloud dispatch.

## No Compliance Determination

Claim boundary architecture may identify missing evidence, uncertainty, or review needs. It may not determine whether a claim satisfies a standard, law, regulation, assurance requirement, certification program, or disclosure framework.

## Task147 Handoff Boundary

Task147 may use claim type, boundary type, evidence requirement, method reference, and uncertainty fields to define ClimateOS / EcoEngine validation question categories.

Task147 must not implement modelling, calculate impacts, or produce environmental conclusions.
