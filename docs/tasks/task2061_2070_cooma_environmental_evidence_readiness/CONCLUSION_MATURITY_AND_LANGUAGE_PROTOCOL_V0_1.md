# Conclusion Maturity and Language Protocol v0.1

## 1. Levels

| Level | Name | Minimum basis | Permitted language | Prohibited promotion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L0 | spatial presence | admitted spatial reference | “the mapped source contains/locates…” | risk, quality, sufficiency or performance |
| L1 | source fact | dated admitted source record | “the source records/reports…” | independent truth beyond the source scope |
| L2 | descriptive indicator | reproducible derivation, boundary, units and uncertainty | “for the declared period and method, the calculated indicator was…” | causal, safety, adequacy or compliance conclusion |
| L3 | reviewed environmental assessment | multiple fit evidence objects, alternatives, uncertainty and qualified review | “the reviewed evidence supports a bounded assessment that…” | statutory, engineering or emergency authority |
| L4 | accountable decision conclusion | applicable professional/authority method and signoff | language allowed by the accountable role and jurisdiction | autonomous ClimateOS determination |

Task2061–2070 prepares the protocol only. It does not issue L1–L4 Cooma
condition statements.

## 2. Language controls

### Allowed at readiness stage

- “Evidence required to answer this question includes…”
- “The current spatial project can locate…”
- “This evidence gap blocks…”
- “A future calculation would require…”
- “The following is an illustrative schema/example.”

### Blocked without the corresponding gate

- “Cooma has enough water.”
- “Cooma is not protecting enough rainfall.”
- “Evaporation will remove a specified quantity.”
- “The drinking water is safe/unsafe.”
- “The wastewater system is resilient/at risk.”
- “The bushfire risk is high/low.”
- “El Niño will arrive next year.”
- “A site or development is compliant, safe or suitable.”

## 3. Promotion gates

| Transition | Required gate |
|---|---|
| L0 → L1 | dated source identity, scope, licence and attributed statement |
| L1 → L2 | reproducible method, aligned units/boundaries, QC, uncertainty and Run Receipt |
| L2 → L3 | evidence triangulation, counterevidence, sensitivity, qualified review and stated validity |
| L3 → L4 | responsible authority/professional role, applicable standard, decision record and release approval |

An object may be demoted when it expires, is superseded, loses licence fitness,
fails validation, or is contradicted by stronger evidence.

## 4. Uncertainty and disagreement

Every L2+ claim must preserve:

- measurement and model uncertainty;
- missing data and residuals;
- alternative explanations;
- disagreement among sources or models;
- sensitivity to boundary, period and method;
- reviewer dissent.

“No evidence of a problem” must not be rewritten as “evidence of no problem.”

## 5. Validity

A conclusion is valid only for its declared:

- spatial boundary;
- time period;
- variable and units;
- method and version;
- evidence cut-off;
- reviewer role;
- decision use.

Monthly monitoring may update or retire a conclusion but cannot silently mutate
the old record.
