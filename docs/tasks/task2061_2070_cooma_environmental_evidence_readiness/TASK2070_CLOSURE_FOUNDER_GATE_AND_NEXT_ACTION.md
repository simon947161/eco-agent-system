## 2. Recommended operating model

Use the existing `COOMA-WATER-FIRE-WASTEWATER-WATCH` program as the question
memory and cycle controller. Add future evidence only through admission and
lineage gates. Align admitted evidence spatially through the accepted QGIS
foundation. Run bounded indicators or models only through reproducible
receipts. Bind outputs to claims, apply the conclusion ladder, require the
correct human review, and publish a versioned Passport without changing prior
records.

This creates a continuing inquiry loop:

```text
ask
→ observe
→ account
→ challenge
→ review
→ record
→ ask again
```

## 3. Recommended next priority

The next implementation should be a **Water Balance Question Runtime
Readiness Slice**, not a claim that Cooma has or lacks sufficient water.

Recommended objective:

> Make one declared water-accounting question machine-readable and prove that
> ClimateOS can identify every required term, distinguish observed/estimated/
> scenario/missing values, reconcile units and boundaries, calculate no result
> when required evidence is absent, and produce a gap-first Passport.

Why first:

- it directly answers the Founder’s water-protection concern;
- it forces rainfall, snow, storage, evaporation, wind, flow, use, leakage and
  discharge to share one accounting boundary;
- it reuses the greatest number of existing ClimateOS capabilities;
- it exposes missing evidence before expensive acquisition or modelling;
- it creates a foundation that can later connect wastewater wet-weather stress
  without mixing water quantity, quality and operational conclusions.

## 7. CRP Harvest Block

### 核心知识点

- Cooma spatial foundation is complete, but a map is not a water account or
  environmental conclusion.
- ClimateOS already contains most governance and continuity components needed
  for persistent inquiry.
- Water balance requires one boundary, period, units, uncertainty budget and
  explicit missing terms.

### 想法点

- Reuse the monthly program as question memory.
- Make gap-first Passports a normal output, not a failure.
- Treat El Niño as scenario/outlook/observation according to evidence class.

### 愿望点

- Produce rational, repeatable understanding of whether local water is entering,
  stored, lost, used and protected adequately.
- Connect climate, terrain, water and wastewater without confusing their claims.

### 推理点

- A water-balance readiness slice forces the strongest cross-system integration
  while remaining safe before real evidence acquisition.
- Missing evidence should block a number rather than invite an invented number.

### 关键决策

- Task2061–2070 remains documentation-only.
- L0–L4 conclusion language is gated.
- Water Balance Question Runtime Readiness is recommended next.

### 未解决问题

- lawful source availability, licence and reviewer roles;
- which evaporation/ET method would be fit after evidence admission.
