# Cooma Four-Theme Minimum Evidence Packs v0.1

Status: REQUIREMENT DEFINITIONS ONLY / NO DATA ACQUISITION

## 1. Snow and climate background pack

Minimum components:

1. declared Cooma study boundary and elevation bands;
2. dated precipitation, minimum/maximum temperature and precipitation-form
   observations with station/grid metadata;
3. snow-depth, snow-cover or defensible proxy evidence with limitations;
4. declared baseline and comparison periods;
5. official climate-state/outlook objects kept separate from observations;
6. spatial representativeness assessment;
7. missing-data, homogenisation and uncertainty record;
8. reproducible seasonal indicators and Run Receipt;
9. qualified climate interpretation before L3.

This pack must not infer local water availability from snowfall alone.

## 2. Bushfire context pack

Minimum components:

1. declared decision use and spatial/time boundary;
2. dated official fire history and incident identity;
3. fuel/vegetation type, condition and currency metadata;
4. weather, drought and fuel-moisture evidence appropriate to the method;
5. terrain and exposure context;
6. ignition, suppression and asset/exposure evidence when relevant;
7. approved or reviewed risk method, uncertainty and limitations;
8. explicit separation from operational warnings and property safety advice;
9. accountable fire-domain review before L3/L4.

Terrain or vegetation presence alone does not establish bushfire risk.

## 3. Drinking-water and water-balance pack

Minimum quantity/accounting components:

1. declared hydrological and operational accounting boundary;
2. consistent period and units;
3. precipitation and relevant snow contribution;
4. measured inflows/outflows and imported/exported water;
5. storage capacity and time-varying volume/level with conversion method;
6. withdrawals, consumption, leakage and managed releases where applicable;
7. evaporation/evapotranspiration inputs, method fitness and validation;
8. groundwater/soil-water terms or an explicit exclusion;
9. residual and complete uncertainty budget;
10. scenario assumptions separated from observations.

Minimum water-quality components:

1. sampling point identity and relation to source/treatment/distribution;
2. sample time, analyte, units, method and detection/quantification limits;
3. laboratory accreditation and QA/QC;
4. applicable authority/standard version;
5. treatment and operational context where lawfully available;
6. responsible authority interpretation.

Quantity cannot establish quality, and quality cannot establish supply security.

## 4. Wastewater resilience pack

Minimum components:

1. system boundary, asset identity and service population/load context;
2. approved/design and operational capacity definitions;
3. influent flow/load time series and data quality;
4. dry-weather and wet-weather separation;
5. rainfall/infiltration/inflow relationship with lag assumptions;
6. bypass, overflow, incident and maintenance records;
7. effluent monitoring and receiving-environment evidence;
8. licence/consent conditions and reporting basis;
9. climate/stress scenarios, sensitivities and uncertainty;
10. operator, engineering and regulatory review roles.

Mapped wastewater assets, if later added, would remain L0 until operational and
monitoring evidence is admitted.

## 5. Common pack gate

Every pack must include:

- question and decision-use statement;
- evidence object register;
- licence/privacy/admission review;
- spatial and temporal alignment;
- method and software identity;
- validation, uncertainty and failure record;
- claim graph with counterevidence and gaps;
- conclusion level and human reviewer role;
- Passport, Receipt and expiry/recheck condition.
