# Task257 Future Architecture Scope Constraint Governance

## Purpose

Define constraints that any future architecture batch would have to obey, without designing that architecture.

This document must not create architecture diagrams, data models, schemas, APIs, workflows, or service designs.

## Permitted Future Architecture Discussion Areas

A separately authorized future architecture batch may discuss only the areas explicitly allowed by its future Founder-approved brief.

Potential discussion areas may include:

- Governance boundary preservation.
- Scope limits.
- Review responsibilities.
- Decision record structure.
- Non-authority constraints.
- Required references to prior governance.

These are not architecture designs in this document.

- Framework interpretation.
- Standards interpretation.
- ESG conclusions.
- Carbon conclusions.

## Scope Limit Principles

Any future architecture batch must:

- Stay within the exact Founder-authorized scope.
- Preserve Mission subordination.
- Preserve Human Authority.
- Preserve non-interpretation boundaries.
- Preserve no-runtime and no-implementation boundaries unless separately authorized later.
- Preserve the no-automatic-continuation rule.

## No-Runtime Constraint

Future architecture scope must exclude runtime unless a later Founder-approved phase explicitly opens runtime.

## No-Implementation Constraint

Future architecture scope must exclude implementation unless a later Founder-approved phase explicitly opens implementation.

## No-Scoring Constraint

Future architecture scope must exclude scoring, ranking, weighting, trust scoring, readiness scoring, ESG scoring, carbon scoring, compliance scoring, assurance scoring, and certification scoring.

## No-Framework-Interpretation Constraint

Future architecture scope must exclude framework interpretation, standards interpretation, framework comparison, standards comparison, and requirement satisfaction claims.

## No-Compliance Constraint

Future architecture scope must exclude compliance guidance, compliance readiness, compliance determination, regulatory interpretation, and compliance conclusions.

## No-Assurance Constraint

Future architecture scope must exclude assurance guidance, audit readiness, verification outcomes, and assurance conclusions.

## No-Certification Constraint

Future architecture scope must exclude certification guidance, approval status, endorsement, certification readiness, and certification conclusions.

## Mission Subordination Constraint

Future architecture scope must keep Evidence Passport subordinate to ClimateOS Mission.

## Human Authority Constraint

Future architecture scope must keep Human Authority as a constitutional responsibility layer.

## PRI Boundary Constraint

Future architecture scope must preserve the distinction between PRI and ClimateOS.

PRI may not become ClimateOS and may not become Evidence Passport.

## Runtime Passport Boundary Constraint

Future architecture scope must preserve the distinction between Evidence Passport and Runtime Passport.

Runtime Passport remains future-state and separately authorized only.
