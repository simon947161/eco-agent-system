# Task253 Architecture Authorization Decision Criteria

## Purpose

Define minimum criteria required before any future Evidence Passport architecture batch may be authorized.

Meeting criteria does not automatically start architecture work.

Separate Founder authorization remains required.

## Mission Alignment Criteria

A future authorization decision must confirm that:

- Evidence Passport remains subordinate to ClimateOS Mission.
- Evidence Passport serves Mission.
- Mission does not serve Evidence Passport.
- Mission conflict triggers Founder and Human Authority review.

## Knowledge Object Governance Criteria

A future authorization decision must confirm that:

- Knowledge Object governance remains prior.
- Source, signal, claim, Knowledge Object, Evidence Candidate, and Evidence-Ready Object states remain distinct.
- No shortcut from source, signal, or claim to Evidence Passport treatment is allowed.

## Evidence Readiness Criteria

A future authorization decision must confirm that:

- Evidence Readiness governance remains prior.
- Evidence Admission Gate remains active.
- Evidence-Ready status does not automatically create Evidence Passport inclusion.

## Evidence Passport Pre-Architecture Governance Criteria

A future authorization decision must confirm that Task231-240 constraints remain active, including:

- Purpose and non-purpose.
- Authority and non-authority rules.
- Risk controls.
- Human Review and Founder Gate.
- PRI / Runtime Passport distinction.
- Future authorization criteria.

## Architecture Readiness Review Criteria

A future authorization decision must confirm that Task241-250 closure has been reviewed and that unresolved issues remain visible.

## Human Authority Criteria

A future authorization decision must confirm Human Authority responsibility for:

- Mission conflict.
- High-risk evidence.
- External use.
- Partner use.
- Public use.
- Responsibility allocation.
- Deferral and rejection review.

## Founder Authorization Criteria

A future authorization decision must be made only through explicit Founder authorization.

The authorization must state the allowed scope and must restate no-runtime and no-implementation boundaries unless those are separately authorized in a later phase.

## Risk Control Criteria

A future authorization decision must review false authority, false certainty, false compliance, false assurance, false certification, false ESG / carbon conclusion, framework misinterpretation, standards interpretation, automation misuse, external misuse, partner misuse, public communication, and human responsibility dilution risks.

## Non-Interpretation Criteria

A future authorization decision must preserve the non-interpretation boundary for frameworks, standards, regulation, ESG claims, and carbon claims.

## No-Compliance Boundary Criteria

A future authorization decision must state that Evidence Passport architecture design does not create compliance guidance, compliance status, compliance readiness, or regulatory conclusions.

## No-Assurance Boundary Criteria

A future authorization decision must state that Evidence Passport architecture design does not create assurance guidance, audit status, verification outcomes, or assurance conclusions.

## No-Certification Boundary Criteria

A future authorization decision must state that Evidence Passport architecture design does not create certification guidance, certification readiness, approval status, or certification conclusions.

## No-Runtime Boundary Criteria

A future authorization decision must confirm runtime remains excluded unless a separate future Founder authorization explicitly opens runtime.

## No-Implementation Boundary Criteria

A future authorization decision must confirm implementation remains excluded unless a separate future Founder authorization explicitly opens implementation.

## Decision Record Requirement

Any future authorization decision must be recorded in a dedicated decision record that includes:

- Decision date.
- Decision authority.
- Reviewed governance package.
- Decision outcome.
- Scope.
- Boundaries.
- Blockers or conditions.
- No-automatic-continuation statement.
