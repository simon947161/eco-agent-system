# Task255 Founder And Human Authority Decision Gate

## Purpose

Define Founder and Human Authority requirements for any future Evidence Passport architecture authorization decision.

## Core Statement

Human Authority is a constitutional responsibility layer, not an administrative checkbox.

## Founder Decision Authority

Founder decision authority is required for any future decision about whether Evidence Passport architecture design may be considered.

Founder authority must be explicit, scoped, and separate from Task251-260 closure.

## Human Authority Responsibility

Human Authority is responsible for reviewing Mission alignment, responsibility allocation, high-risk evidence, external implications, partner implications, public communication risks, and misuse risk before any future architecture decision.

## Mission Conflict Trigger

Mission conflict requires Founder review and Human Authority review.

If Mission conflict is unresolved, the future decision must be deferred or rejected.

## High-Risk Evidence Trigger

High-risk evidence requires Human Authority review.

High-risk evidence includes material with potential impact on public claims, community trust, safety, resilience, resource allocation, partner commitments, or prohibited compliance / assurance / certification implications.

## External-Use Trigger

External use requires Founder review.

Task251-260 does not authorize external use.

## Partner-Use Trigger

Partner-facing Evidence Passport language requires review of scope, non-authority, limits, misuse risk, and communication boundaries.

Task251-260 does not authorize partner use.

## Public-Use Trigger

Public use requires Founder review and explicit public communication boundary review.

Task251-260 does not authorize public use.

## Compliance-Related Trigger

Any compliance-related use, wording, request, expectation, or implication requires deferral and separate Founder review.

Task251-260 does not authorize compliance guidance.

## Assurance-Related Trigger

Any assurance-related use, wording, request, expectation, or implication requires deferral and separate Founder review.

Task251-260 does not authorize assurance guidance.

## Certification-Related Trigger

Any certification-related use, wording, request, expectation, or implication requires deferral and separate Founder review.

Task251-260 does not authorize certification guidance.

## Framework-Related Trigger

Any framework-related interpretation, comparison, mapping, requirement satisfaction, or standards-language implication requires deferral and separate Founder review.

Task251-260 does not authorize framework or standards interpretation.

## Deferral Rules

A future decision must be deferred when:

- Founder authorization is incomplete.
- Human Authority review is incomplete.
- Mission conflict is unresolved.
- Risk blockers are unresolved.
- Boundary language is unclear.
- Runtime or implementation implications appear.
- Automatic continuation is requested.

## Rejection Rules

A future decision must be rejected when:

- It creates false authority.
- It creates false certainty.
- It implies compliance, assurance, or certification.
- It requires standards or framework interpretation.
- It creates ESG or carbon conclusions.
- It bypasses Founder or Human Authority.
- It requires runtime or implementation without separate authorization.

## Escalation Rules

Escalate to Founder review when:

- Scope expands.
- Authority changes.
- External use is proposed.
- Partner use is proposed.
- Public use is proposed.
- Architecture design is proposed.
- Any prohibited work appears necessary.

## Decision Accountability

Any future decision must identify who made the decision, what was reviewed, which risks were accepted or deferred, which boundaries remain active, and which next steps are not automatic.
