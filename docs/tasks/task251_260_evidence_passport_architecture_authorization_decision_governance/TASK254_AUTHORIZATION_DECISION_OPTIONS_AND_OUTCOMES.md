# Task254 Authorization Decision Options And Outcomes

## Purpose

Define possible outcomes of an architecture authorization decision.

A decision outcome may recommend a future architecture batch, but must not begin it.

Even an authorization decision does not create implementation authority.

## Authorize Future Architecture Batch

A future decision may permit a separate architecture design batch to be considered only if Founder authorization explicitly says so.

Required condition:

- The decision must specify scope, boundaries, required references, and prohibited work.

Limit:

- It does not create implementation authority.
- It does not create runtime authority.
- It does not automatically start Task261-270.

## Defer Authorization

A future decision may defer authorization when conditions are incomplete or risks remain unresolved.

Deferral may be required when:

- Mission alignment is unclear.
- Human Authority review is incomplete.
- Founder review is incomplete.
- Risk review is incomplete.
- Non-interpretation boundaries are unclear.
- Runtime or implementation implications appear.

## Reject Authorization

A future decision may reject authorization when blockers cannot be resolved within the proposed scope.

Rejection may be required when:

- The request would create false authority.
- The request would imply compliance, assurance, certification, ESG, or carbon conclusions.
- The request would require standards or framework interpretation.
- The request would require runtime or implementation.
- The request would bypass Founder or Human Authority.

## Request Additional Governance

A future decision may request additional governance records before any architecture decision is considered.

Additional governance may address risk, authority, Mission alignment, external communication, partner use, or public use.

## Request Additional Founder Review

A future decision may require more Founder review when scope, authority, boundaries, or risks remain unclear.

## Request Additional Human Authority Review

A future decision may require more Human Authority review when responsibility, Mission conflict, high-risk evidence, external use, public use, or partner use concerns remain unresolved.

## Conditional Preparation Without Architecture

A future decision may allow preparation of a work request package without beginning architecture design.

Allowed preparation may include:

- Scope statement drafting.
- Boundary confirmation drafting.
- Prohibited work list drafting.
- Prior governance reference listing.
- Risk review checklist drafting.

Not allowed:

- Architecture diagrams.
- Data models.
- Schemas.
- APIs.
- Workflows.
- Service designs.
- Runtime plans.
- Implementation plans.

## No Automatic Continuation Rule

No decision outcome may automatically start Task261-270, architecture work, implementation work, runtime work, or operational Evidence Passport work.

## Decision Record Requirements

Each future decision must record:

- Decision option selected.
- Decision authority.
- Date.
- Prior governance reviewed.
- Risks reviewed.
- Boundaries confirmed.
- Conditions or blockers.
- Whether future work is deferred, rejected, conditionally prepared, or separately recommended.
- Explicit no-automatic-continuation statement.

## Status

```text
Decision options: DEFINED
Implementation authority from decision: DENIED
Runtime authority from decision: DENIED
Automatic Task261-270 start: PROHIBITED
```
