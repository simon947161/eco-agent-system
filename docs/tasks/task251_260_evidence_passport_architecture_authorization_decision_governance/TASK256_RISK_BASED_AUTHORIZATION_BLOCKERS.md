# Task256 Risk-Based Authorization Blockers

## Purpose

Define risks that must block, defer, or restrict future architecture authorization.

## False Authority Blocker

Block or defer when Evidence Passport language could be read as official authority, approval, verification, or institutional endorsement.

## False Certainty Blocker

Block or defer when traceability, completeness, or review history could be mistaken for certainty.

## False Compliance Blocker

Block or defer when a request implies compliance guidance, compliance readiness, regulatory interpretation, or compliance conclusions.

## False Assurance Blocker

Block or defer when a request implies assurance guidance, audit readiness, verification outcome, or assurance conclusions.

## False Certification Blocker

Block or defer when a request implies certification guidance, approval, endorsement, certification readiness, or certification conclusions.

## False ESG / Carbon Conclusion Blocker

Block or defer when a request implies ESG performance, carbon performance, carbon accounting conclusions, or environmental performance conclusions.

## Framework Misinterpretation Blocker

Block or defer when a request requires framework interpretation, framework comparison, requirement mapping, or requirement satisfaction claims.

## Standards Interpretation Blocker

Block or defer when a request requires standards interpretation, standards comparison, requirement mapping, or requirement satisfaction claims.

## Automation Misuse Blocker

Block or defer when a request implies automated decisions, automated routing, automated approvals, automated scoring, or agent execution.

## External Misuse Blocker

Block or defer when external audiences could misuse future Evidence Passport language as proof, verification, compliance, assurance, certification, ESG, or carbon authority.

## Partner Misuse Blocker

Block or defer when partner-facing use could exceed scope or create implied authority.

## Public Communication Blocker

Block or defer when public communication could overstate readiness, authority, truth, compliance, assurance, certification, or operational status.

## Human Responsibility Dilution Blocker

Block or defer when a request could transfer responsibility from humans to Evidence Passport labels, metadata, status, or future records.

## Insufficient Mission Alignment Blocker

Block or defer when Mission alignment is unclear, contested, or subordinated to evidence presentation.

## Insufficient Founder Review Blocker

Block or defer when Founder review is missing, unclear, incomplete, or not scoped to the proposed decision.

## Insufficient Human Authority Blocker

Block or defer when Human Authority review is missing, incomplete, or reduced to an administrative approval step.

## Block Decision

Block a future decision when a risk is active and cannot be constrained within the proposed scope.

## Defer Decision

Defer a future decision when additional governance, review, clarification, or evidence is needed before a decision can be responsibly made.

## Escalate Decision

Escalate a future decision when Founder review, Human Authority review, expert review, or legal / regulatory review outside this batch is needed.

## Reject Decision

Reject a future decision when the proposed scope conflicts with Mission, Human Authority, non-interpretation boundaries, no-runtime boundaries, no-implementation boundaries, or prohibited work.
