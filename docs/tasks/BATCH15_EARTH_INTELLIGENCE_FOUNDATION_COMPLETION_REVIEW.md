# Batch15 Earth Intelligence Foundation Completion Review

## Coverage

Task77 through Task78

## Purpose

Batch15 extended the completed Knowledge Foundation into future external
knowledge and Earth intelligence interfaces.

The batch focused on two questions:

```text
How can ClimateOS connect to future knowledge gardens without platform lock-in?
How can ClimateOS interface with Earth intelligence providers without becoming dependent on one provider?
```
