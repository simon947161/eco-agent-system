# Task1270-1289 Controlled Extreme Event And Regional Fitness Evidence Foundation

Status: Completed on isolated branch; Founder review pending

Authorization: Founder statement issued 2026-07-12

## Outcome

This batch establishes a controlled bridge from synthetic assurance foundations
to real regional evidence discovery for Karamay, Xinjiang and NSW South East
and Tablelands. It defines six event families, threshold provenance, sample
support, non-stationarity, OOD review, and a Regional Fitness Evidence Passport.

The source registry records official candidates from the Australian Bureau of
Meteorology, NSW Government AdaptNSW, China Meteorological Administration data
service, and Karamay Municipal Government. Public discovery is not treated as
permission to reuse a product. Product-level licence, cost, variable, coverage,
and checksum review remains mandatory before observational values are ingested.

## Implemented Contracts

- declared two-region registry with subregional limitations;
- six-family extreme-event contract;
- threshold purpose and version governance;
- sample sufficiency, non-stationarity, and OOD gates;
- Regional Fitness Evidence Passport foundation;
- controlled official-source discovery registry;
- tiny synthetic blocker fixtures and automated tests;
- repository-wide Founder resource and cost-control prerequisite.

## Real Evidence Position

Real evidence is authorised and encouraged. This batch does not invent or copy
environmental values merely to claim real-data use. The discovered products
remain metadata-only until their product-level reuse conditions and scientific
fitness are confirmed. Paid access requires a separate Founder cost decision.

## Scientific And Governance Limits

Operational warning thresholds are not model admission thresholds. The two
regions cannot share thresholds without explicit review. No universal minimum
event count is invented. No model is integrated or run, and no model score,
rank, scientific admission, public-safety, compliance, or legal conclusion is
created.
