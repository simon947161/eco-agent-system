# Task616 Sustainable Office And Human Action Interface Concept

## Purpose

Define a conceptual human action interface for sustainable office contexts
without building software, automation, sensors, or operational agents.

## Concept

A Sustainable Office interface would help people connect daily actions,
resource use, observations, and governance review. It should translate
environmental evidence into practical human choices while preserving
uncertainty and avoiding false authority.

## Possible Human Workflows

- record an observation about water, energy, waste, comfort, biodiversity, or
  maintenance;
- attach evidence or uncertainty notes;
- route the note to the relevant domain;
- ask for human review;
- record a proposed action;
- mark whether the action is low-risk, needs review, or requires Founder Gate;
- document what evidence would be needed before any stronger claim.

## Interface Principles

- Show current status, not hidden automation.
- Separate observation from interpretation.
- Separate suggestion from decision.
- Keep domain routing visible.
- Preserve human accountability.
- Avoid scoring, certification, or performance claims.

## Non-Operational Boundary

Task616 does not create:

- office software;
- UI implementation;
- sensors;
- building automation;
- energy management runtime;
- procurement workflow;
- agent execution;
- notification automation;
- scoring;
- compliance or certification output.

## Open Questions

- Which office actions are safe enough for early conceptual examples?
- Which actions require expert review?
- How should community or employee observations be protected from misuse?
- Should this interface belong to ClimateOS, GovernanceOS, or a future
  SustainableOfficeOS domain?
