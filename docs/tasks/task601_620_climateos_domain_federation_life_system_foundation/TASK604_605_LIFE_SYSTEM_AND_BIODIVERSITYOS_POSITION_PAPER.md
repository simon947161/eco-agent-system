# Task604-605 Life System And BiodiversityOS Position Paper

## Purpose

Position the Life System and BiodiversityOS within ClimateOS domain federation.

## Position

The Life System represents living ecological relations that cannot be reduced to
carbon, infrastructure, land parcels, or generic risk objects. BiodiversityOS is
the domain layer that helps ClimateOS reason about habitat, species,
ecological function, restoration context, biodiversity risk, and living-system
evidence.

## Knowledge Points

- Biodiversity evidence may include observation records, habitat descriptions,
  species signals, ecological condition notes, restoration context, field
  review, remote sensing context, and expert interpretation.
- Biodiversity questions often require temporal, spatial, and relational
  context rather than one isolated metric.
- BiodiversityOS should preserve uncertainty and disagreement because living
  systems are dynamic and partly observable.

## Domain Responsibilities

BiodiversityOS should conceptually support:

- habitat and species context;
- ecological function and relationship mapping;
- restoration and disturbance context;
- evidence readiness for living-system claims;
- review questions for human experts and community witnesses;
- cross-domain handoff to WaterOS, LandOS, CarbonOS, ClimateOS core, and
  GovernanceOS.

## Shared Governance Responsibilities

ClimateOS should coordinate:

- evidence traceability;
- review status;
- uncertainty status;
- human deliberation;
- Founder Gate escalation;
- cross-domain conflict documentation.

## Risks

- Carbon reductionism: biodiversity value may be incorrectly reduced to carbon.
- Overclaiming: weak observations may be treated as ecological proof.
- Model substitution: generic models may be treated as site-specific evidence.
- Authority drift: automated outputs may appear to replace expert and community
  review.

## Recommendations

- Treat BiodiversityOS as an autonomous domain with its own evidence needs.
- Require evidence status labels before biodiversity claims are reused.
- Keep current BiodiversityOS work documentation-only until examples and
  review standards are stronger.
- Require human review for biodiversity-sensitive decisions.

## Current Capability

No BiodiversityOS runtime, biodiversity model, sensor integration, scoring
engine, certification process, or EcoChain mechanism is created by Task604-605.
