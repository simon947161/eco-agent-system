# Task246 Human Review Founder Gate Readiness Review

## Purpose

Review whether Human Authority and Founder Gate conditions are ready for any future architecture consideration.

This document is readiness review only and does not authorize architecture.

## Core Statement

Human Authority is a constitutional responsibility layer, not an administrative checkbox.

## Founder Authorization Requirement

Readiness observation:

- Founder authorization is required before any future Evidence Passport architecture batch begins.
- Task241-250 authorization does not authorize Evidence Passport architecture, Runtime, or Implementation.

## Human Authority Requirement

Readiness observation:

- Human Authority is required when evidence context may affect judgment, Mission, public communication, partner expectations, high-risk decisions, or responsibility allocation.

## Mission Conflict Trigger

Readiness observation:

- Mission conflict requires Human Authority review and Founder review.
- Future architecture consideration must defer if Mission conflict is unresolved.

## High-Risk Evidence Trigger

Readiness observation:

- High-risk evidence requires Human Authority review before any future Evidence Passport treatment.
- High-risk evidence includes material that could affect trust, safety, resilience, resource allocation, public claims, partner commitments, or prohibited compliance / assurance / certification implications.

## External-Use Trigger

Readiness observation:

- External use requires Founder review.
- External use remains outside Task241-250.

## Partner-Use Trigger

Readiness observation:

- Partner-facing Evidence Passport language requires review of scope, non-authority, limits, misuse risk, and communication boundaries.
- Partner use remains outside Task241-250.

## Public-Use Trigger

Readiness observation:

- Public use requires Founder review and explicit public communication boundary review.
- Public use remains outside Task241-250.

## Compliance-Related Trigger

Readiness observation:

- Compliance-related use, wording, request, expectation, or implication requires deferral and separate Founder review.
- Task241-250 does not authorize compliance guidance.

## Assurance-Related Trigger

Readiness observation:

- Assurance-related use, wording, request, expectation, or implication requires deferral and separate Founder review.
- Task241-250 does not authorize assurance guidance.

## Certification-Related Trigger

Readiness observation:

- Certification-related use, wording, request, expectation, or implication requires deferral and separate Founder review.
- Task241-250 does not authorize certification guidance.

## Deferral Rules

Readiness observation:

- Deferral rules exist for missing Founder authorization, missing Human Authority review, unresolved Mission conflict, unresolved misuse risk, unclear Knowledge Object dependency, unclear Evidence Readiness dependency, prohibited implications, and any runtime / architecture / implementation / automation / scoring requirement.

## Rejection Rules

Readiness observation:

- Rejection rules exist for false authority, false certainty, automatic verification, compliance authority, assurance authority, certification authority, standards interpretation, ESG or carbon conclusions, replacement of Human Authority, and unauthorized runtime or implementation.

## Escalation Rules

Readiness observation:

- Escalation to Founder review is required when scope moves beyond documentation-only readiness review, when external use is proposed, when authority changes, when architecture is proposed, or when prohibited work appears necessary.

## Readiness Finding

Human Authority and Founder Gate conditions are sufficiently documented for Founder review of a possible future architecture authorization decision.

This finding does not authorize architecture.
