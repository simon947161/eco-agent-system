# Task243 Mission Alignment Readiness Review

## Purpose

Review whether future Evidence Passport architecture would have sufficient Mission alignment constraints.

This document is readiness review only and does not authorize architecture.

## Preserved Mission Statement

Evidence Passport serves Mission.

Mission does not serve Evidence Passport.

## Mission Supremacy Check

Task211-220 and Task231-240 preserve Mission as the higher authority layer.

Readiness observation:

- Evidence Passport remains subordinate to ClimateOS Mission.
- Evidence context is not allowed to become independent authority.
- Mission conflict requires review.

## Evidence Serves Mission Check

The governance record states that evidence may support Mission through context, uncertainty, traceability, and review limitations.

Readiness observation:

- Evidence is framed as support for Mission, not a replacement for Mission.
- Evidence Passport is not allowed to redirect Mission toward presentation, scoring, or external claims.

## Mission Conflict Trigger

Mission conflict triggers are present when future Evidence Passport work could:

- Pull ClimateOS away from Mission.
- Convert evidence context into false authority.
- Prioritize presentation over truthfulness.
- Prioritize completeness over uncertainty.
- Dilute human responsibility.
- Create external pressure to make unauthorized claims.

## Human Review Trigger

Human review is required when evidence context may affect Mission interpretation, authority, public communication, partner expectations, high-risk evidence, or governance boundaries.

## Founder Review Trigger

Founder review is required when future Evidence Passport work may:

- Change Mission interpretation.
- Establish or revise Evidence Passport authority.
- Move from readiness review to architecture.
- Move from architecture to implementation.
- Allow external or partner-facing use.
- Touch compliance, assurance, certification, framework interpretation, ESG conclusions, or carbon conclusions.

## Misalignment Risk

Residual misalignment risk remains if future work:

- Treats readiness as authorization.
- Treats evidence context as authority.
- Treats completeness as certainty.
- Treats future architecture as implementation permission.
- Allows external communication before boundaries are reviewed.

## Readiness Finding

Mission alignment constraints are sufficiently documented for Founder review of a possible future architecture authorization decision.

This finding does not authorize architecture.

## Unresolved Issues

Future authorization must still define:

- The exact scope of any architecture decision batch.
- Whether runtime remains excluded.
- Whether implementation remains excluded.
- How Mission conflict is checked during that future batch.
