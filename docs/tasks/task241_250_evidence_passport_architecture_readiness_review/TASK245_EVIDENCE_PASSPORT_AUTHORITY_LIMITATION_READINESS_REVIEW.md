# Task245 Evidence Passport Authority Limitation Readiness Review

## Purpose

Review whether Evidence Passport authority limitations are clear enough before future architecture consideration.

This document is readiness review only and does not authorize architecture.

## Preferred Framing

Evidence Passport may preserve context only after future authorization.

Evidence Passport must not create authority by itself.

## No Compliance Authority

Readiness observation:

- Evidence Passport is explicitly denied compliance authority.
- It must not create compliance status, compliance readiness, compliance conclusions, regulatory conclusions, or compliance guidance.

## No Assurance Authority

Readiness observation:

- Evidence Passport is explicitly denied assurance authority.
- It must not create assurance status, assurance readiness, assurance conclusions, audit conclusions, or assurance guidance.

## No Certification Authority

Readiness observation:

- Evidence Passport is explicitly denied certification authority.
- It must not create certification status, certification readiness, certification conclusions, approval claims, or certification guidance.

## No Regulatory Authority

Readiness observation:

- Evidence Passport is explicitly denied regulatory authority.
- Regulatory interpretation remains outside Task241-250 and requires separate authorized human and legal review if ever needed.

## No Scoring Authority

Readiness observation:

- Evidence Passport is explicitly denied scoring authority.
- Numeric, categorical, weighted, ordinal, or implied scoring remains prohibited.

## No Standards Interpretation Authority

Readiness observation:

- Evidence Passport is denied authority to interpret standards.
- Any standards interpretation requires separate Founder authorization and appropriate expert review outside this batch.

## No Framework Interpretation Authority

Readiness observation:

- Evidence Passport is denied authority to interpret frameworks.
- Framework mapping, comparison, and requirement satisfaction remain prohibited.

## No Replacement Of Human Authority

Readiness observation:

- Human Authority remains above any future Evidence Passport governance marker.
- Evidence Passport status must not replace human judgment, responsibility, or escalation.

## No Replacement Of Founder Gate

Readiness observation:

- Founder Gate remains required for scope expansion, architecture consideration, implementation consideration, runtime consideration, external use, public use, and authority changes.

## Authority Limitation Readiness Finding

Authority limitations are sufficiently documented for Founder review of a possible future architecture authorization decision.

This finding does not authorize architecture.

```text
Authority limitation readiness: REVIEWED
Compliance authority: DENIED
Assurance authority: DENIED
Certification authority: DENIED
Regulatory authority: DENIED
Scoring authority: DENIED
Standards / framework interpretation authority: DENIED
Human Authority replacement: DENIED
Founder Gate replacement: DENIED
Evidence Passport architecture: NOT AUTHORIZED
```
