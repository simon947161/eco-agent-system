# Task101+ Recommendations Parking List

## Purpose

This document parks ideas, requirements, and recommendations that emerge during Foundation development but are outside the scope of Task100 or require further research before Foundation inclusion.

## Parking List Principles

1. **Ideas park here, not in Foundation roadmap**: Task100 remains Foundation Graduation
2. **Task100 objectives remain unchanged**: Foundation scope is fixed
3. **Task101+ reviewed after graduation**: Post-graduation planning
4. **Parking does not imply acceptance**: Ideas require evaluation before inclusion
5. **Each item requires clear categorization**: Organized parking

## Parking List Categories

### Category 1: Runtime Enhancements

Ideas for future runtime capability that extend beyond Task100.

### Category 2: Domain Expansions

Ideas for new domain runtimes beyond CarbonOS, WaterOS, EnergyOS.

### Category 3: Integration Improvements

Ideas for better Foundation integration.

### Category 4: Governance Refinements

Ideas for governance process improvements.

### Category 5: Tooling Requirements

Ideas for development or documentation tools.

### Category 6: Research Items

Topics requiring research before Foundation inclusion.

### Category 7: Implementation Specifications

Ideas for specific implementation approaches.

## Parking List Governance

### Addition Process

1. Item identified during Foundation development
2. Item categorized (Runtime, Domain, Integration, Governance, Tooling, Research, Implementation)
3. Item documented with description, priority, and rationale
4. Item added to appropriate category
5. Item reviewed during Task101+ planning

### Review Process

1. Task101+ planning review periodic
2. Items evaluated for Foundation inclusion
3. Items rejected, deferred, or accepted
4. Accepted items added to Foundation roadmap
5. Rejected items remain in parking list

### Boundaries

**Inside Parking List**:
- Ideas outside Task100 scope
- Items requiring further research
- Domain-specific items
- Tooling requirements
- Implementation specifications

**Outside Parking List**:
- Task100 scope modifications
- Foundation restructuring
- Milestone changes
- Architectural redesign
