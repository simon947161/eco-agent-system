# Task266 Risk Statement And Misuse Disclosure Requirements

## Purpose

Define required risk and misuse disclosures for any future Evidence Passport Architecture Work Request.

## Core Statement

A future request that does not disclose misuse risks must be deferred or rejected.

## False Authority Risk

A future request must disclose risk that Evidence Passport language could be read as official authority, approval, verification, or endorsement.

## False Certainty Risk

A future request must disclose risk that completeness, traceability, or review history could be mistaken for certainty.

## False Compliance Risk

A future request must disclose risk that Evidence Passport language could imply compliance guidance, compliance readiness, regulatory interpretation, or compliance conclusions.

## False Assurance Risk

A future request must disclose risk that Evidence Passport language could imply assurance guidance, audit readiness, verification outcomes, or assurance conclusions.

## False Certification Risk

A future request must disclose risk that Evidence Passport language could imply certification guidance, approval, endorsement, certification readiness, or certification conclusions.

## False ESG / Carbon Conclusion Risk

A future request must disclose risk that Evidence Passport language could imply ESG performance, carbon performance, carbon accounting conclusions, or environmental performance conclusions.

## Framework Misinterpretation Risk

A future request must disclose risk that framework content could be interpreted, compared, mapped, or treated as satisfied.

## Standards Interpretation Risk

A future request must disclose risk that standards content could be interpreted, compared, mapped, or treated as satisfied.

## Automation Misuse Risk

A future request must disclose risk that request language could be used to justify automated decisions, automated routing, automated approvals, automated scoring, or agent execution.

## External Misuse Risk

A future request must disclose risk that external readers could misuse Evidence Passport language as proof, verification, compliance, assurance, certification, ESG, or carbon authority.

## Partner Misuse Risk

A future request must disclose risk that partner-facing use could exceed authorized scope.

## Public Communication Risk

A future request must disclose risk that public communication could overstate readiness, authority, truth, compliance, assurance, certification, or operational status.

## Human Responsibility Dilution Risk

A future request must disclose risk that responsibility could appear to shift from humans to Evidence Passport labels, metadata, status, or future records.

## Runtime Creep Risk

A future request must disclose risk that architecture language could drift toward runtime.

## Architecture Creep Risk

A future request must disclose risk that request-format governance could drift into architecture design.

## Implementation Creep Risk

A future request must disclose risk that architecture or request language could drift into implementation planning.

## Mitigation Statement Requirement

A future request must include mitigation statements for disclosed risks.

## Escalation Statement Requirement

A future request must state when Founder review, Human Authority review, expert review, or legal / regulatory review outside the batch is required.

## Deferral Statement Requirement

A future request must state when unresolved risk requires deferral.

## Rejection Statement Requirement

A future request must state when unresolved risk requires rejection.

## Status

```text
Risk disclosure requirements: DEFINED
Misuse disclosure requirements: DEFINED
Request without risk disclosure: DEFER OR REJECT
Architecture design: NOT AUTHORIZED
Runtime / implementation: NOT AUTHORIZED
```
