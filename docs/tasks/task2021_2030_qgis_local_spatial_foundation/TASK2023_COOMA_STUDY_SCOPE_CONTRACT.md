## Separation rules

Administrative boundaries, catchment boundaries, waterways, station influence,
model cells and infrastructure service areas are separate spatial objects. They
must never be silently substituted for one another.

The five project bookmarks contain coarse WGS 84 extents solely to help the
Founder navigate an empty project. They are not admitted data, surveyed
coordinates, precise place-name locations or analysis boundaries. Formal
extents require bounded retrieval from an approved source and human review.
