## Evidence Discipline Requirements

All Task111-120 files must maintain evidence discipline by separating:

| Term | Definition | Requirement |
|------|------------|-------------|
| **Raw data** | Unprocessed source records | Must not be presented as evidence without processing |
| **Observation** | Selected human-readable statement from raw data | Must be clearly distinguished from inference |
| **Inference** | Reasoned interpretation of observations | Must be labelled as provisional; not a claim or conclusion |
| **Evidence** | Observation set sufficient for a specific claim | Must be sufficient in quality, provenance, and relevance |
| **Claim** | Reviewable assertion | Must be clearly separated from observations and inferences |
| **Recommendation** | Suggested next step, review action, or decision option | Must not be presented as an authorization or approval |
