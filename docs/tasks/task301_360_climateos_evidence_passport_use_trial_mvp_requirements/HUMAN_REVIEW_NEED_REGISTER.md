# Human Review Need Register

Human review needs are required controls. They do not authorize conclusions.

| Human review ID | Review need | Trigger | Linked records | Required action | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HR-001 | Source identity and retrieval review | Candidate official source path not directly verified. | S001, S002, S004-S006, EC-001, EC-002, EC-004 | Confirm source identity, date, URL, and publication context before future use. | Open for future review |
| HR-002 | Language and translation review | Chinese source or paraphrase may need bilingual review. | S003, CC-003, KO-001 | Review source language and paraphrase boundaries. | Open for future review |
| HR-003 | Political sensitivity review | Governance / planning / policy material may be externally sensitive. | SIG-004, KO-004 | Confirm no political conclusion is implied. | Open for future review |
| HR-004 | Timeliness review | Source date or update cycle may affect future use. | S004-S006, EC-004 | Check source date, version, and current relevance before future use. | Open for future review |
| HR-005 | Source conflict / scope review | News, tertiary, or academic sources may not support case-specific claims. | S007-S011, EC-005, EC-006 | Confirm scope and prevent overclaim. | Open for future review |
