## Task322 Source Gap Note

Source gaps observed in this candidate-only sprint:

- Missing verified official copies for several candidate planning and official-site source paths.
- Missing current time-series hydrology and water-quality evidence.
- Missing source-version metadata for some official-site citation paths.
- Missing independent verification for candidate wetland, restoration, water quality, and resilience references.
- Missing clear update cycles for several public references.
- Language and translation limits remain.
- Browser access limits affected official Chinese source retrieval.

These gaps are requirements insights only. They do not produce a case conclusion.

## Task323 Conflict / Uncertainty Note

Possible uncertainty types:

- Official planning documents may describe objectives, not observed outcomes.
- News sources may describe events but not long-term resilience.
- Tertiary sources may point to useful citation paths but require direct-source verification.
- Academic or institutional materials may model or analyze selected dimensions without validating governance claims.
- Water, wetland, climate, and urban planning sources may operate at different time scales.

This sprint records uncertainty. It does not resolve uncertainty into conclusions.

## Task324 Timeliness / Outdated Source Note

Future evidence handling must record:

- Publication date.
- Access date.
- Version or update status when available.
- Event date when different from publication date.
- Whether the source is a planning document, event report, current monitoring record, historic background, or tertiary pointer.
- Whether a source is stale for the intended future use.

This is a requirements insight, not a conclusion about the case.

## Task325 Boundary Overreach Prevention Note

Future Evidence Passport governance must prevent:

- Scoring overreach.
- Compliance overreach.
- Certification overreach.
- Assurance overreach.
- ESG conclusion overreach.
- Carbon conclusion overreach.
- Standards interpretation overreach.

## Task327 Human Review Workflow Requirements

Human review requirements:

- Founder reviews gate-sensitive decisions and any future authorization question.
- Human reviewer reviews ambiguous sources, potential overclaims, translation concerns, political sensitivity, and source conflicts.
- Source analyst reviews citation paths, publication dates, source identity, and retrieval status.
- ClimateOS assistant may suggest classifications but cannot approve conclusions.
- External reader may only receive bounded, reviewed, non-operational records if Founder-approved.

Cannot be automated in this sprint:

- Source admission as final evidence.
- Compliance, assurance, certification, ESG, or carbon conclusions.
- Political judgment.
- Standards or framework interpretation.
- Founder Gate decisions.

## Task329 Use Trial Lessons Learned

System requirements lessons:

- Source retrieval status must be visible.
- Candidate status must be distinct from evidence readiness status.
- Claim candidates need separate provenance and overclaim checks.
- Knowledge Object candidates need linked review state.
- Human review and Founder Gate must be first-class governance controls.
- Future MVP planning needs archive-friendly Markdown records before any architecture is considered.
- Public communication and partner-facing use require separate review.
