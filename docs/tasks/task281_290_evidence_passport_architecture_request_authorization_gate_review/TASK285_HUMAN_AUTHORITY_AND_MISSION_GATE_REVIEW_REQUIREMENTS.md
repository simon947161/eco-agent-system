# Task285 Human Authority And Mission Gate Review Requirements

## Purpose

Define how Human Authority and Mission Alignment must be reviewed in any future Architecture Request Authorization Gate.

## Core Statements

Evidence Passport serves Mission.

Mission does not serve Evidence Passport.

Human Authority is a constitutional responsibility layer, not an administrative checkbox.

## Mission Supremacy Check

A future gate review must check that ClimateOS Mission remains superior to Evidence Passport architecture, Evidence Passport workflow, Evidence Passport records, operational convenience, technical momentum, and external pressure.

## Evidence Passport Serves Mission Check

A future gate review must check that any proposed Evidence Passport direction remains subordinate to Mission, Human Authority, Founder Gate, and governance.

## Mission Conflict Trigger

A future gate review must require escalation when proposed future work conflicts with Mission, human responsibility, evidence humility, public safety, or long-term governance integrity.

## Human Authority Responsibility Check

A future gate review must check that Human Authority assigns accountability for judgment, refusal, escalation, boundary preservation, and external-use risk.

## Founder Gate Check

A future gate review must check that the Founder Gate is explicit and that no batch begins without the exact Founder authorization phrase required by that future batch.

## Ethics Conflict Trigger

A future gate review must require escalation when a proposed future direction creates ethical uncertainty, social harm risk, public communication risk, human responsibility dilution, or false authority risk.

## High-Risk Evidence Trigger

A future gate review must require escalation when high-risk evidence could affect public claims, partner claims, funding claims, compliance narratives, assurance narratives, certification narratives, ESG narratives, carbon narratives, or operational decisions.

## External-Use Trigger

A future gate review must require escalation when future Evidence Passport outputs, records, packages, or terminology could be used outside the repository.

## Partner-Use Trigger

A future gate review must require escalation when future Evidence Passport materials could be used by partners, vendors, clients, collaborators, funders, or third parties.

## Public-Use Trigger

A future gate review must require escalation when future Evidence Passport materials could be used in public communication, marketing, public claims, reporting, policy discussion, investment material, or regulatory-facing material.

## Deferral Requirement

A future gate review must defer when Mission alignment or Human Authority requirements are incomplete, ambiguous, or unsupported.

## Rejection Requirement

A future gate review must reject when proposed work would subordinate Mission to Evidence Passport, dilute Human Authority, bypass Founder Gate, create unauthorized architecture design, create implementation, create runtime, or produce prohibited conclusions.

## Escalation Requirement

A future gate review must escalate Mission conflicts, ethics conflicts, high-risk evidence triggers, external-use triggers, partner-use triggers, public-use triggers, and unresolved Human Authority questions.

## Decision Accountability Check

A future gate review must check that decisions remain accountable to named human review and not to checklists, templates, automation, agent execution, or assumed system authority.
