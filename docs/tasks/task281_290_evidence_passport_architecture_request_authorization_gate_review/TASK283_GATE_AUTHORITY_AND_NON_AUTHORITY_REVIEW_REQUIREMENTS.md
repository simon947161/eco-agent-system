# Task283 Gate Authority And Non-Authority Review Requirements

## Purpose

Define how the authority and non-authority of any future Architecture Request Authorization Gate must be reviewed.

## Core Statements

A gate may check conditions only after future authorization.

A gate must not create authority by itself.

## Authority Review Boundary

This document defines how future gate authority and non-authority should be reviewed.

It does not create, open, pass, approve, or authorize any gate.

## What A Future Gate May Check

After future authorization, a gate may check:

- Whether the exact Founder authorization phrase is present.
- Whether repository identity is correct.
- Whether working tree state is known.
- Whether Human Authority requirements are present.
- Whether Mission alignment requirements are present.
- Whether prior governance references are present.
- Whether boundary declarations are explicit.
- Whether risk controls are sufficient.
- Whether deferral rules are triggered.
- Whether rejection rules are triggered.
- Whether escalation rules are triggered.
- Whether closure criteria are complete.

## What A Future Gate May Not Authorize By Itself

A future gate may not authorize by itself:

- Architecture Request creation.
- Architecture Request submission.
- Architecture Request approval.
- Evidence Passport architecture authorization.
- Evidence Passport architecture design.
- Evidence Passport implementation.
- Runtime.
- API.
- Database.
- MCP.
- n8n.
- QCloud.
- Automation.
- Scoring.
- Operational Evidence Passport.
- Compliance guidance.
- Assurance guidance.
- Certification guidance.
- Framework interpretation.
- Standards interpretation.

## No Compliance Authority

A future gate must not claim compliance authority.

It must not determine compliance, compliance readiness, regulatory standing, or compliance conclusions.

## No Assurance Authority

A future gate must not claim assurance authority.

It must not create assurance guidance, audit guidance, assurance readiness, or assurance conclusions.

## No Certification Authority

A future gate must not claim certification authority.

It must not create certification guidance, approval status, certification readiness, or certification conclusions.

## No Regulatory Authority

A future gate must not claim regulatory authority.

It must not interpret law, regulation, standards, or reporting obligations.

## No Scoring Authority

A future gate must not create readiness scores, trust scores, evidence scores, compliance scores, assurance scores, certification scores, ESG scores, or carbon scores.

## No Standards Interpretation Authority

A future gate must not interpret standards, map standards, compare standards, or state authoritative requirements.

## No Framework Interpretation Authority

A future gate must not interpret frameworks, compare frameworks, rank frameworks, or create authoritative framework statements.

## No Replacement Of Founder Authority

A future gate must not replace Founder Authority.

Only an exact Founder authorization phrase may permit the specific future batch named by that phrase.

## No Replacement Of Human Authority

A future gate must not replace Human Authority.

Human Authority remains a constitutional responsibility layer for judgment, accountability, escalation, and refusal.

## No Automatic Architecture Authorization

A complete gate review does not automatically grant architecture authorization.

## No Automatic Architecture Design

A complete gate review does not automatically begin architecture design.

## No Automatic Runtime

A complete gate review does not automatically begin runtime.
