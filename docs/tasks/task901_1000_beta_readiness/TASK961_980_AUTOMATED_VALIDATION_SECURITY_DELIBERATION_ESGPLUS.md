## Deliberation

Deliberation requires a claim, challenge, uncertainty and explicit abstention
from conclusion. Correction preserves history. Escalation keeps evidence
disputed and requires a later human response.

## ESG++ Boundary

Environmental evidence may be translated into governance questions and human
review records. It must not become an automated ESG disclosure, rating,
compliance statement, assurance opinion or certification.
