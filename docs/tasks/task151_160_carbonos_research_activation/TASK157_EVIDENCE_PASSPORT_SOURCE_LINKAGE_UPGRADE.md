# Task157 Evidence Passport Source-Linkage Upgrade

## Purpose

Task157 extends the Evidence Passport concept so future evidence records can link to official sources and reviewed factual claims.

This is a documentation-only concept upgrade. It does not create real evidence records, databases, APIs, runtime workflows, automation, calculators, or compliance outputs.

## Source-Linked Evidence Fields

Future Evidence Passport records may include:

- evidence label
- evidence category
- source citation reference
- official source relationship
- factual claim relationship
- framework relationship
- method relationship
- version status
- access date
- review status
- uncertainty status
- limitation note
- non-authoritative warning

Task157 does not populate these fields with real evidence.

## Citation Status

Allowed citation statuses:

- No Citation
- Citation Needed
- Citation Partial
- Citation Complete
- Citation Review Needed
- Citation Rejected
- Source Superseded

Citation status does not determine truth or sufficiency.

## Claim Relationship

Future evidence may relate to:

- proposed factual claim
- reviewed factual claim
- rejected factual claim
- superseded factual claim
- research question
- source-needed placeholder

No relationship creates compliance, assurance, certification, or disclosure authority.

## Framework Relationship

Framework relationship may record a label or research context only.

It must not imply that the framework has been interpreted, satisfied, or compared unless a later approved source-backed research task supports that statement.

## Method Relationship

Method relationship may record:

- method area label
- method reference status
- formula / factor reference status
- source review status
- uncertainty status
- limitation note

It must not create formulas, factors, calculations, or method approval.

## Review Status

Allowed review statuses:

- Draft
- Source Link Needed
- Citation Review Needed
- Claim Review Needed
- Framework Review Needed
- Method Review Needed
- Founder / GPT Review Needed
- Eligible For Future Architecture Use
- Rejected
- Superseded

## Uncertainty Status

Uncertainty status may include:

- source missing
- source stale risk
- source conflict
- claim unresolved
- framework relationship unclear
- method relationship unclear
- version unclear
- applicability unclear
- expert review needed

## Non-Authoritative Warning

Every future source-linked Evidence Passport concept must carry this warning:

```text
This record is non-authoritative. It is not compliance evidence, assurance evidence, certification evidence, disclosure evidence, or real-world carbon conclusion evidence unless separately approved through source-backed review and governance.
```

## No Real Evidence Records

Task157 creates no real evidence records and does not reference real companies, projects, disclosures, assets, products, or carbon claims.

## Task158 Handoff

Task158 may define rules for future abstract non-operational examples.

Task158 must not create examples with real-world entities, factual standards comparisons, calculators, scoring, or disclosure conclusions.
