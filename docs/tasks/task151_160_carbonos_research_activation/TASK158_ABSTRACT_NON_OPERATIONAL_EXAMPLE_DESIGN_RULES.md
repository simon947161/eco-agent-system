# Task158 Abstract Non-Operational Example Design Rules

## Purpose

Task158 defines rules for future abstract examples that illustrate CarbonOS research and review structures without creating real-world conclusions.

This task does not create examples. It creates design rules only.

## Abstract Example Purpose

Future abstract examples may help illustrate:

- source tracking structure
- citation review structure
- claim review structure
- comparative mapping structure
- Evidence Passport linkage structure
- research-to-architecture handoff structure

They must remain non-operational and non-factual unless separately approved with source-backed review.

## Allowed Fictional Structure

Allowed abstract structures may include:

- fictional framework labels such as `Framework A`
- fictional claim labels such as `Claim Type X`
- placeholder source labels such as `Official Source Placeholder`
- blank fields
- review state examples without facts
- uncertainty examples without real-world reference
- architecture diagrams in text form

## Prohibited Real Entities

Do not use:

- real companies
- real projects
- real products
- real assets
- real disclosures
- real jurisdictions as factual examples
- real emissions claims
- real environmental claims

## Prohibited Real Project Claims

Future examples must not state or imply that a real project:

- reduced emissions
- removed carbon
- offset emissions
- achieved net zero
- improved climate outcomes
- improved ecological outcomes
- reduced risk
- met disclosure requirements

## Prohibited Factual Standards Comparison

Future examples must not compare real standards unless:

- current official sources are directly accessed
- citations are complete
- version and freshness are checked
- contradictions are reviewed
- Founder / GPT review approves the specific use

Without those gates, standards may only appear as research targets or blank labels.

## No Calculators

Future examples must not contain:

- formulas for calculation
- factor values
- emissions math
- reduction math
- offset math
- risk math
- confidence math
- pass / fail logic

## No Scoring

Future examples must not score:

- evidence
- claims
- companies
- projects
- methods
- frameworks
- jurisdictions
- risk
- confidence

## No Disclosure Conclusion

Future examples must not generate:

- disclosure language
- compliance status
- assurance status
- certification status
- investor-facing conclusions
- public reporting conclusions

## Founder Approval Requirement

Before any example is created, a separate Founder instruction must approve:

- example purpose
- abstraction level
- allowed labels
- source rules
- prohibited content
- review gate
- completion criteria

## Task159 Handoff

Task159 may define the gate between source-backed research and architecture documents.

Task159 must preserve that research notes do not automatically become architecture.
