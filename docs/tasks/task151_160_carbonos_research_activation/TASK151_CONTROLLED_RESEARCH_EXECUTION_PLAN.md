# Task151 Controlled Research Execution Plan

## Purpose

Task151 defines how CarbonOS may later execute standards research under strict source, citation, version, and review controls.

This is documentation-only research activation planning. It does not execute factual standards research, interpret standards, create compliance claims, create assurance claims, create certification claims, or produce carbon conclusions.

## Research Activation Purpose

The purpose of research activation is to prepare a controlled path for future source-backed research about standards, frameworks, methods, formula references, boundary concepts, evidence expectations, and review requirements.

Research activation creates rules for later research. It does not create the research findings themselves.

## What Research Execution Means

In a later approved task, research execution may mean:

- identifying current official sources
- recording citation metadata
- recording source versions and access dates
- proposing narrow factual statements
- checking source freshness
- recording uncertainty and limitation notes
- routing proposed findings through review

Research execution must be source-backed before any factual statement is used.

## What Research Execution Does Not Mean

Research execution does not mean:

- authoritative standards interpretation
- compliance determination
- assurance determination
- certification determination
- legal advice
- public disclosure support
- real company or project assessment
- calculator creation
- database creation
- runtime implementation
- automated research or scoring

## Source Eligibility

Eligible future sources must be current, traceable, and reviewable.

Preferred source order:

| Priority | Source Type | Use Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Official source from framework owner, regulator, standard setter, or responsible public authority | Required for factual standard-specific claims. |
| 2 | Official implementation guidance or public consultation material | May support context if current and cited. |
| 3 | Recognized technical, academic, or professional analysis | May support research questions or risk notes, but cannot replace official sources. |
| 4 | Internal notes, memory, model recall, or prior repository artifacts | May only suggest questions. Not eligible as factual authority. |

## Citation Requirements

Every future factual statement must record:

- source title
- source owner / publisher
- publication date or effective date when available
- version date or revision marker when available
- access date
- stable URL or locator when available
- section, paragraph, page, table, or clause locator when available
- reviewer
- review state
- uncertainty note

## No-Uncited-Memory Rule

Uncited memory, model recall, old notes, and informal summaries cannot support factual standards claims.

If a claim cannot be tied to a current official source or reviewed citation trail, it must remain a research question or placeholder.

## Review Stages

Allowed review stages:

- Research Target
- Source Search Needed
- Official Source Located
- Citation Captured
- Version / Freshness Checked
- Draft Factual Claim Proposed
- Contradiction Review Needed
- Founder / GPT Review Needed
- Eligible For Architecture Handoff
- Rejected / Superseded

## Founder Review Checkpoints

Founder review is required before:

- any factual standards claim is included in architecture documentation
- any framework-specific comparison is created
- any illustrative example uses standard-specific content
- any research note is promoted into a Task151+ architecture output
- any future Task161+ research execution path begins

## Prohibited Outputs

Task151 prohibits:

- factual standards findings
- standards explanation
- compliance guidance
- assurance guidance
- certification guidance
- calculators
- data files
- APIs
- MCP implementation
- databases
- scoring
- automation
- QCloud dispatch
- real-world carbon conclusions

## Task152 Handoff

Task152 may define a scaffold for tracking future official sources.

Task152 must not create a database, data file, source dataset, factual standards table, or standard-specific interpretation.
