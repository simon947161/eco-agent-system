## Task362 Architecture Principles

- Mission before Evidence Passport.
- Human Authority above automation.
- Founder Gate above execution.
- Candidate-only evidence discipline.
- Source traceability.
- Reviewability before use.
- Non-scoring by default.
- No compliance, assurance, certification, ESG, carbon, standards, or framework conclusions.
