# Task205 Knowledge Harvest Integration

## Purpose

Describe how Knowledge Harvest concepts may integrate with ClimateOS Phase II planning.

## Core Objects

Knowledge Object:
A reviewed unit of knowledge that may later support architecture, governance, evidence, or learning records.

Signal Object:
A potential input that may indicate new knowledge, risk, contradiction, opportunity, or architecture relevance.

CRP Object:
A Climate Resilience Pattern or related record that may preserve practical, local, scientific, or governance insight.

## Harvest Workflow

A future harvest workflow may include:

- Intake.
- Source capture.
- Context note.
- Relevance tagging.
- Maturity classification.
- Human review.
- Architecture routing.
- Evidence Passport routing when relevant.
- AEP routing when architecture change is implied.

## Knowledge Maturity

Potential maturity states:

- Raw signal.
- Captured note.
- Reviewed knowledge object.
- Architecture candidate.
- Evidence candidate.
- Governance reference.
- Deferred or rejected.

## Knowledge Governance

Knowledge Harvest should preserve:

- Source traceability.
- Review status.
- Uncertainty.
- Human reviewer.
- Founder review triggers.
- Relationship to existing architecture records.

## Relationship To Evidence Passport

Knowledge Harvest may produce candidates that later inform Evidence Passport structures.

It does not create operational Evidence Passports in Task201-210.

## Relationship To Runtime Integrity

Knowledge Harvest may support future Runtime Integrity by recording where knowledge came from, how it was reviewed, and whether it is mature enough to influence architecture.

This is planning only.

## Boundary

This document does not create a knowledge database, harvest automation, Evidence Passport runtime, or operational knowledge system.
