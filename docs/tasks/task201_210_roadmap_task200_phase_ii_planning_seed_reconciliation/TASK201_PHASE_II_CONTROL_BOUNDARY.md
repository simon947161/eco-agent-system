# Task201 Phase II Control Boundary

## Purpose

Define the boundary for ClimateOS Phase II planning under restored Roadmap Task200 and Roadmap Task300 terminology.

## Scope

Task201 establishes:

- Phase II purpose.
- Phase II scope.
- Phase II objectives.
- Planning principles.
- Relationship to completed Task141-200.
- Relationship to Roadmap Task200.
- Relationship to Roadmap Task300.
- Boundary controls for Task202-210.

## Phase II Objectives

Phase II is a strategic planning layer for ClimateOS after completion of the CarbonOS Documentation Foundation.

Its objectives are to:

- Restore Roadmap Task200 as the stable runtime expansion and multi-agent preparation milestone.
- Preserve Roadmap Task300 as the long-term autonomous ClimateOS architecture vision.
- Reconcile recovered Task200 seed themes with current architecture records.
- Extend planning readiness without authorizing implementation.
- Maintain compatibility with Architecture Baseline v1.1 and the Architecture Enhancement Protocol.

## Planning Principles

- Documentation-first.
- Founder-review gated.
- AEP-compatible.
- Evidence-aware but not evidence-operational.
- Runtime-aware but not runtime-implementing.
- Human-review preserving.
- Boundary explicit before every future implementation discussion.

## Relationship To Completed Task141-200

Task141-200 remains completed and frozen as the CarbonOS Documentation Foundation and supporting governance preparation.

Phase II treats that work as readiness evidence and architecture substrate. It does not rewrite completed Task141-200 outputs.

## Relationship To Roadmap Task200

Roadmap Task200 remains the larger strategic milestone for ClimateOS runtime expansion, multi-agent runtime preparation, resilience, survivability, and human/fallback architecture planning.

Task201-210 is an early Phase II planning batch under Roadmap Task200. It is not completion of Roadmap Task200.

## Relationship To Roadmap Task300

Roadmap Task300 remains the long-term autonomous ClimateOS architecture vision.

Task201-210 may create a vision seed for Task300 but may not implement autonomous runtime, autonomous agents, or operational decision systems.

## Boundary

Task201-210 does not implement runtime, agents, APIs, databases, MCP, n8n, automation, scoring, QCloud, standards interpretation, framework comparison, operational Evidence Passport records, compliance guidance, assurance guidance, certification guidance, ESG conclusions, or carbon conclusions.
