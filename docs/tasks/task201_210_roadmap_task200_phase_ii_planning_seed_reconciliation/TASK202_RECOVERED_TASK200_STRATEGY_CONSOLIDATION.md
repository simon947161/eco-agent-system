# Task202 Recovered Task200 Strategy Consolidation

## Purpose

Consolidate recovered Roadmap Task200 strategy themes into a single documentation-only planning record.

## Consolidated Themes

Roadmap Task200 is restored as ClimateOS runtime expansion and multi-agent runtime preparation.

Recovered strategy themes include:

- AI sovereignty.
- Multi-model resilience.
- Offline runtime.
- Human runtime.
- Resource-constrained runtime.
- Survival runtime.
- Civilization seed.
- Fallback architecture.
- Model abstraction.
- Cross-model validation.

## Planning Meaning

These themes describe strategic design pressures for later architecture work.

They do not create implementation requirements, runtime modules, agent systems, APIs, databases, automation, or scoring systems.

## Theme Notes

AI sovereignty:
ClimateOS should preserve future optionality across model providers, local models, and human-governed model selection.

Multi-model resilience:
Future architecture should avoid single-model dependency and should support cross-model review concepts before operational use.

Offline runtime:
Future planning should consider disconnected or degraded environments.

Human runtime:
Human review, judgment, and governance should remain first-class architecture constraints.

Resource-constrained runtime:
Future planning should consider low-compute, low-bandwidth, and field conditions.

Survival runtime:
Future planning should consider continuity under disruption without implying autonomous deployment.

Civilization seed:
ClimateOS may preserve durable knowledge, governance, and recovery patterns for long-horizon resilience.

Fallback architecture:
Future designs should include graceful degradation concepts before any implementation.

Model abstraction:
Future runtime planning should separate reasoning roles from specific AI providers.

Cross-model validation:
Future validation planning should define how model outputs could be reviewed, challenged, and reconciled.

## Boundary

This consolidation is not a runtime design specification and does not authorize implementation.
