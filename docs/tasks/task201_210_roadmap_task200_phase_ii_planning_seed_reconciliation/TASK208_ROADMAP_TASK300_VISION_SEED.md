# Task208 Roadmap Task300 Vision Seed

## Purpose

Expand Roadmap Task300 as the long-term autonomous ClimateOS architecture vision without authorizing implementation.

## Vision Statement

Roadmap Task300 preserves the long-term possibility of an autonomous ClimateOS architecture that can support climate resilience, evidence governance, human review, knowledge continuity, and adaptive learning under strict governance.

## Vision Themes

Roadmap Task300 may include:

- Long-term autonomous ClimateOS architecture.
- Human-governed autonomy.
- Evidence-aware decision support.
- Knowledge continuity.
- Multi-model resilience.
- Offline and degraded-context survivability.
- Community-scale resilience support.
- Architecture-level safeguards.
- Civilizational knowledge preservation.

## Relationship To Roadmap Task200

Roadmap Task200 prepares the runtime expansion, resilience, and multi-agent planning foundation that could make Roadmap Task300 thinkable later.

Roadmap Task300 remains downstream of Task200 and should not be collapsed into near-term implementation.

## Non-Authorization

This vision seed does not authorize:

- Autonomous agents.
- Automated decisions.
- Operational runtime.
- Deployment.
- APIs.
- Databases.
- MCP tools.
- Scoring.
- Compliance, ESG, carbon, assurance, or certification claims.

## Boundary

Roadmap Task300 remains a vision-only milestone in Task201-210.
