# Task102 CarbonOS First Human Use Test

## Purpose

Task102 defines the first human-use test for CarbonOS after Task100 and
Task101 were approved and frozen.

The goal is to test whether a human reviewer can use CarbonOS documents to
structure a carbon or ESG claim review without confusing unsupported statements
with verified environmental conclusions.

## Test Scenario

Reference scenario: a company states that an operational site has reduced its
reported emissions through electricity procurement and efficiency measures.

This scenario is illustrative only. It is not a real compliance review, real
investment review, real assurance review, or public environmental conclusion.

## Required Human Actions

1. Identify each reviewable claim.
2. Separate raw data, observation, inference, evidence, claim, and
   recommendation.
3. Record source provenance and missing evidence.
4. Apply the evidence sufficiency checklist.
5. Identify expert review triggers.
6. Assign a practical pass/fail category.
7. State the action authority boundary.

## Evidence Discipline Test

| Term | Required Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Raw data | Source material before interpretation, such as meter readings, invoices, inventory tables, or source documents. |
| Observation | Human-readable record derived from raw data, such as a reported monthly electricity quantity. |
| Inference | Reasoned interpretation of observations, such as possible emissions reduction from lower consumption. |
| Evidence | Observations with provenance, relevance, and sufficiency for a specific claim. |
| Claim | Reviewable assertion requiring evidence. |
| Recommendation | Suggested next review step or decision option, never an authorization. |

## Expert Review Triggers

Expert review is mandatory if any of the following are present:

- The claim could affect compliance, public disclosure, investment, or
  operational decisions.
- Evidence sources conflict.
- Critical source data is missing.
- Carbon accounting methods, emission factors, offsets, certificates, or
  regulatory interpretations are material to the result.
- The reviewer cannot distinguish observation from inference.

## Action Authority Boundary

A CarbonOS / ClimateOS recommendation is not an action authority. It may
identify a possible next step, review requirement, or decision option, but it
cannot authorize implementation, approval, construction, investment,
compliance declaration, public claim, or operational action without the
required human, expert, or governance approval.

## Expected Output

Task102 should produce a human-readable review record that is at least
readable and may become partially usable if evidence gaps are clearly recorded.

It must not present any pilot output as a validated environmental conclusion.
