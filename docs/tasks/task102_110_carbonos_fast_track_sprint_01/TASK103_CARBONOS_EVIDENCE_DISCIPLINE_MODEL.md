# Task103 CarbonOS Evidence Discipline Model

## Purpose

Task103 adapts the Task101 evidence discipline into a CarbonOS-specific review
model for carbon, ESG, and environmental claims.

This is a documentation model only. It does not create a scoring engine,
compliance engine, verification method, assurance method, or automated
calculation system.

## CarbonOS Evidence Chain

| Layer | CarbonOS Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Raw data | Uninterpreted source records | Meter data, invoices, fuel purchase records, certificate serials, project documents |
| Observation | A selected human-readable statement from raw data | "Electricity consumption for April was recorded as X kWh" |
| Inference | A reasoned interpretation of observations | "The lower recorded electricity use may reduce location-based emissions" |
| Evidence | Observation set sufficient for a specific claim | Meter record plus provenance plus period alignment plus method notes |
| Claim | Reviewable assertion | "Site emissions decreased during the review period" |
| Recommendation | Suggested next review action | "Request expert accounting review before disclosure" |

## Discipline Rules

- Raw data is not automatically evidence.
- An observation is not automatically a claim.
- An inference is not a verified conclusion.
- Evidence is always evidence for a specific claim.
- A claim must remain reviewable, bounded, and traceable.
- A recommendation cannot authorize action.

## Evidence Quality Dimensions

| Dimension | Review Question |
| --- | --- |
| Provenance | Who produced the source, when, and under what controls? |
| Relevance | Does the evidence directly support the claim being reviewed? |
| Completeness | Are required periods, assets, scopes, and source categories covered? |
| Method clarity | Are accounting or review methods stated clearly enough for review? |
| Conflict handling | Are contradictory sources disclosed and escalated? |
| Human readability | Can a non-specialist decision-maker understand the evidence boundary? |

## Unsafe Conflations

The following patterns are failed / unsafe unless remediated:

- Treating a supplier statement as confirmed emissions reduction.
- Treating an offset purchase as automatic climate impact.
- Treating an internal estimate as verified evidence.
- Treating missing activity data as immaterial without justification.
- Treating a recommendation as authority to disclose or act.

## Expert Review Triggers

Expert review is required for high-stakes disclosure, compliance claims,
assurance-like language, contested evidence, missing critical data, method
choice disputes, or claims that may influence financial, operational, public,
or regulatory decisions.
