# Task106 CarbonOS Responsibility Boundary Model

## Purpose

Task106 defines responsibility boundaries for CarbonOS claim review.

The model prevents review support from being mistaken for authority,
certification, assurance, compliance approval, investment advice, or operational
instruction.

## Responsibility Roles

| Role | Responsibility | Not Responsible For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Data provider | Supplies source data and provenance. | Proving claims beyond source scope. |
| Claim owner | States the claim and intended use. | Independent validation unless separately assigned. |
| Human reviewer | Separates evidence, inference, claim, and recommendation. | Final approval or expert certification. |
| Domain expert | Reviews technical, accounting, legal, or regulatory issues. | Governance decision unless formally authorized. |
| Governance authority | Makes approved decisions within its mandate. | Replacing evidence requirements. |
| CarbonOS / ClimateOS document | Structures review and identifies gaps. | Authorizing action, disclosure, compliance, investment, or implementation. |

## Boundary Rules

- A claim owner owns the claim text and intended use.
- A reviewer owns the review record, not the truth of the underlying world.
- An expert owns expert judgment within the assigned scope.
- A governance authority owns decisions only within its formal mandate.
- CarbonOS supports traceable review, not final authority.

## Expert Review Triggers

Expert review is required when:

- carbon accounting methods are material;
- legal, regulatory, market, or assurance interpretation is required;
- evidence conflicts cannot be resolved by source inspection;
- missing critical data could change the review result;
- the output could influence public claims, investment, compliance, or
  operational action.

## Action Authority Boundary

A CarbonOS / ClimateOS recommendation is not an action authority. It may
identify a possible next step, review requirement, or decision option, but it
cannot authorize implementation, approval, construction, investment,
compliance declaration, public claim, or operational action without the
required human, expert, or governance approval.
