## Action Authority Boundary

A CarbonOS / ClimateOS recommendation is not an action authority. It may
identify a possible next step, review requirement, or decision option, but it
cannot authorize implementation, approval, construction, investment,
compliance declaration, public claim, or operational action without the
required human, expert, or governance approval.
