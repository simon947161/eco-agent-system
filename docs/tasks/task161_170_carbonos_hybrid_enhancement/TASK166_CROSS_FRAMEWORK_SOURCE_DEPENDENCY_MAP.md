# Task166 Cross-Framework Source Dependency Map

## Purpose

Task166 records a non-interpretive source dependency map for future cross-framework work.

The map identifies possible dependency categories only. It does not compare standards or state that any framework is equivalent to another.

## Dependency Categories

| Dependency type | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Direct source dependency | Future claim may need exact standard or legal text. |
| Guidance dependency | Future claim may need official implementation or application guidance. |
| Amendment dependency | Future claim may be affected by amendments or active projects. |
| Reporting-period dependency | Future claim may depend on reporting period selection. |
| Alignment dependency | Future mapping may depend on official alignment or interoperability documents. |
| Data-cycle dependency | Future review may depend on annual questionnaire or disclosure cycle updates. |

## Initial Candidate Dependencies

| Source area | Possible dependency category | Runtime lens reason | Required future action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IFRS / ISSB climate disclosure | Amendment dependency; alignment dependency | Page exposes standard history, related projects, and amendments signal. | Verify current standard text and amendment status before intake. |
| GHG Protocol | Guidance dependency; method dependency | CarbonOS future accounting concepts may depend on official GHG Protocol source hierarchy. | Verify current standard, scope guidance, and standards-under-development status. |
| EU ESRS | Legal source dependency; jurisdiction dependency | Legal text and annexes may have consolidated or amended versions. | Verify consolidated EUR-Lex status and applicable annexes. |
| AASB sustainability standards | Reporting-period dependency; portal dependency | AASB routes current/future/by-period standards through a digital portal. | Use period-specific portal lookup before factual use. |
| GRI climate and energy standards | Standards development dependency; alignment dependency | Project and final standards materials must be separated. | Verify final standard documents before intake. |
| CDP question bank | Data-cycle dependency; scoring-method boundary | Questionnaire and scoring materials change by disclosure cycle. | Verify exact cycle and exported materials before use. |
| TNFD recommendations | Guidance and recommendation dependency | Nature-related disclosure materials may affect future CarbonOS / ClimateOS boundary work. | Verify current recommendation package and related guidance hierarchy. |

## Boundary

No cross-framework conclusion is created by this map.
