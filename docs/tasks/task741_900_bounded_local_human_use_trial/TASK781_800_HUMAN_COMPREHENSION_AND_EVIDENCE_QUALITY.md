# Task781-800 Human Comprehension And Evidence Quality

## Comprehension Controls

The workbench keeps the following statements visible before the human forms:

- evidence is synthetic and local;
- reviewer labels are declared labels, not authenticated identity;
- integrity hashes do not prove truth or authority;
- the Alpha cannot issue real-world conclusions.

The creation form requires provenance, uncertainty and Human Review through a
fixed synthetic contract. The interface does not offer a control to disable
Human Review.

## Evidence-Quality Result

The trial can demonstrate that required contract fields and warnings persist.
It cannot demonstrate scientific quality, source authority, operator identity
or decision validity. No score or quality grade is created.

## Remaining Risk

A human may still ignore warnings or misunderstand domain terminology. A
future independently observed trial is required before making broader human
comprehension claims.
