workbench permits synthetic evidence creation only through the shared Evidence
Contract fields and keeps the selected domain visible.

- climate and water;
- water and land;
- land and biodiversity;
- energy and climate.

abstention is rejected by schema validation. Conflicting or incomplete
evidence remains a challenge for Human Review, not an automated resolution.
