# Task624 Carbon Energy Governance Example

## Purpose

Provide a fictional example involving CarbonOS, EnergyOS, and GovernanceOS.

## Fictional Scenario

An office team reports that a building procurement decision may reduce energy
use and asks whether the action can be described as a carbon improvement.

## Observations

- A proposed procurement change exists.
- Energy-use reduction is expected but not measured.
- Carbon impact has not been calculated or verified.

## Assumptions

- The procurement action is not yet implemented.
- No energy baseline is attached.
- No emission factor, accounting method, or assurance review is attached.

## Domain Routing

| Domain | Role |
| --- | --- |
| EnergyOS | Identify the energy-use question and required baseline. |
| CarbonOS | Identify whether carbon accounting evidence is sufficient. |
| GovernanceOS | Prevent unsupported claims and track review status. |
| ClimateOS core | Preserve uncertainty and evidence readiness. |

## Interpretation

The action may be a candidate sustainability action, but it is not yet a
verified carbon improvement. It should be recorded as a proposal requiring
evidence, not as a conclusion.

## Required Evidence Before Stronger Claims

- baseline energy use;
- implementation date;
- measured post-action energy use;
- accounting boundary;
- method reference;
- human review;
- claim wording review.

## Prohibited Claims

This example does not support:

- certified carbon reduction;
- compliance claim;
- assurance claim;
- ESG conclusion;
- automatic scoring;
- public disclosure language.

## Current Capability

No carbon calculator, energy model, procurement system, API, database,
automation, or reporting engine is created.
