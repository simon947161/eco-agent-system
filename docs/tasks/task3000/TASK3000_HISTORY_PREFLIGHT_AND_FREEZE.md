## 3. Initial historical finding — not yet the Task 3000 conclusion

The repository already supports four persistent motifs:

1. **Reality:** environmental work must remain tied to physical conditions and evidence.
2. **Possibility:** the system exists to compare possible futures, not merely output a single forecast.
3. **Human responsibility:** AI, agents and runtimes support judgement; humans retain consequential authority.
4. **Stewardship and feedback:** action is not the end; care, maintenance, evidence, review and learning must continue.

Task 3000's proposed `Stewardship + Capability + Agency` direction is therefore historically plausible.

However, the new `Earth Citizen Capability` framing remains a **Founder hypothesis under review**, not yet a proven historical continuity claim.
