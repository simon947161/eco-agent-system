# Task225 Evidence Admission Gate

## Purpose

Define the future governance gate required before any Knowledge Object can be treated as evidence.

This document does not admit any real evidence.

## Admission Purpose

The admission gate prevents unsupported claims, premature interpretation, false authority, and unsafe operational use.

## Admission Criteria

Future admission should require:

- clear source traceability
- provenance record
- ownership and review status
- maturity level
- uncertainty statement
- interpretation boundary
- Mission alignment check
- risk and misuse check
- human review
- Founder review when required

## Required Authority

Evidence admission requires an authorized review context.

No Knowledge Object may admit itself as evidence.

## Human Review Requirement

Human review is required before evidence readiness may be elevated.

Review must consider meaning, uncertainty, misuse risk, and responsibility.

## Founder Review Trigger

Founder review is required when admission may affect:

- Mission interpretation
- public communication
- external partner use
- standards interpretation
- compliance, assurance, certification, ESG, or carbon implications
- runtime or automation scope
- Roadmap Task200 or Roadmap Task300

## Mission Alignment Check

Admission must support the ClimateOS Mission and must not override human responsibility or governance boundaries.

## Source Traceability Check

Admission requires traceability to source, date, context, and review history.

## Interpretation Boundary Check

Admission must confirm what is and is not being interpreted.

No standards, framework, ESG, carbon, compliance, assurance, or certification interpretation is authorized by this gate model.

## Risk And Misuse Check

Admission review should identify:

- false confidence risk
- public claim risk
- automation misuse risk
- human responsibility dilution
- stale source risk
- context loss risk

## Rejection Criteria

Reject when provenance is missing, authority is unclear, uncertainty is unacceptable, source is stale, interpretation would be unauthorized, or Mission alignment fails.

## Deferral Criteria

Defer when the object may become evidence later but requires more source review, human review, Founder decision, translation review, AEP review, or future runtime authorization.

## Boundary

This gate is future governance only. It does not admit real evidence or operate an Evidence Passport.
