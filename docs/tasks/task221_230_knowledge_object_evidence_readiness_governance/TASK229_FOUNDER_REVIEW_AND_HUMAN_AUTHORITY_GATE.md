# Task229 Founder Review And Human Authority Gate

## Purpose

Define when Founder review and Human Authority review are required for Knowledge Object and Evidence Readiness governance.

Human Authority is not a decorative approval step. It is the constitutional responsibility layer.

## Founder Review Triggers

Founder review is required when a Knowledge Object or evidence-readiness decision may:

- affect Mission interpretation
- change roadmap direction
- modify architecture baseline or AEP path
- imply public communication
- imply partner use
- create implementation pressure
- affect Roadmap Task200
- affect Roadmap Task300

## Human Authority Triggers

Human Authority review is required when:

- uncertainty is high
- human responsibility could be diluted
- ethical harm is possible
- public interpretation may occur
- evidence admission is being considered
- operational handoff is being discussed
- automation or runtime is proposed

## Mission Conflict Triggers

Escalate when an object may conflict with long-term environmental flourishing, ecosystem responsibility, human accountability, or civilizational continuity.

## Ethics Conflict Triggers

Escalate when a Knowledge Object may affect vulnerable communities, public trust, ecological harm, privacy, ownership, accountability, or misuse risk.

## High-Risk Interpretation Triggers

Escalate when a record may be interpreted as:

- standard interpretation
- framework comparison
- compliance guidance
- assurance guidance
- certification guidance
- ESG conclusion
- carbon conclusion
- operational recommendation

## External-Use Triggers

Founder / Human Authority review is required before external use, publication, disclosure, partner routing, public presentation, or third-party reliance.

## Partner-Use Triggers

Partner-use review is required when an external project, institution, company, community, or collaborator may rely on ClimateOS knowledge or evidence-readiness language.

## Public-Communication Triggers

Public communication requires review for wording, boundaries, uncertainty, source authority, and prohibited claim risk.

## Deferral Rules

Defer when authority is unclear, source traceability is weak, uncertainty is high, Mission alignment is unresolved, or future authorization is required.

## Rejection Rules

Reject when source integrity fails, risk is unacceptable, interpretation would be unauthorized, or Mission alignment fails.

## Escalation Rules

Escalation should preserve:

- source context
- decision question
- risk statement
- recommended route
- explicit boundary
- responsible human reviewer

## Boundary

This document defines review gates only. It does not create approval automation, decision systems, operational evidence workflows, or public-claim authority.
