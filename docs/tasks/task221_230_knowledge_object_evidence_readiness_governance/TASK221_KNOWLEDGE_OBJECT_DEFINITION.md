# Task221 Knowledge Object Definition

## Purpose

Define what a Knowledge Object is within ClimateOS.

## Definition

A Knowledge Object is a governed unit of knowledge that has been captured, registered, classified, and reviewed enough to preserve context, provenance, maturity, uncertainty, and routing state.

A Knowledge Object is not automatically evidence, not automatically truth, and not automatically usable for decision-making.

## Scope

A Knowledge Object may define:

- what was captured
- where it came from
- why it may matter
- who or what owns review responsibility
- what maturity level it holds
- what uncertainty remains
- what future review or routing is required

## Non-Scope

A Knowledge Object does not:

- verify a claim
- certify truth
- determine compliance
- provide assurance
- create a carbon conclusion
- create an ESG conclusion
- interpret a standard
- operate an Evidence Passport
- authorize runtime execution

## Allowed Examples

- A reviewed scientific-paper note marked as knowledge, not evidence.
- A Founder insight routed to Mission Governance.
- A CRP pattern captured for future review.
- An official source locator recorded with provenance and no interpretation.
- A conversation-derived architecture idea marked for AEP review.

## Disallowed Examples

- A standards requirement interpreted without official-source review.
- A carbon-performance conclusion.
- A compliance determination.
- A certification implication.
- A scored evidence record.
- A runtime-ready operational claim.

## Relationship To Mission

Knowledge Objects must remain subordinate to the ClimateOS Mission.

Knowledge that conflicts with Mission requires review, deferral, or rejection.

## Relationship To Signal

A Signal may become a Knowledge Object only after capture, registration, classification, and review.

Signals do not become Knowledge Objects automatically.

## Relationship To Knowledge Harvest

Knowledge Harvest may produce candidate Knowledge Objects.

Harvested material requires governance before it is treated as ClimateOS knowledge.

## Relationship To PRI Routing

PRI / Matrix may translate and route a Knowledge Object candidate.

PRI routing does not create evidence authority or implementation authority.

## Boundary

This document defines a governance concept only. It does not create a database, runtime object, API object, schema, or operational record.
