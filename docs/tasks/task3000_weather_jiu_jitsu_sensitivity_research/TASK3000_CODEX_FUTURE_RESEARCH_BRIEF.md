## Purpose

Task3000 concerns a theoretical research question inspired by the Weather Jiu-Jitsu framework and Hurricane Sandy case:

> Can AI weather models and physically constrained sensitivity analysis help identify small, strategically located changes in large-scale atmospheric conditions that produce large counterfactual changes in extreme-weather evolution?

The task is exploratory. It is not a weather-control implementation task.
