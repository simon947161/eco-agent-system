# Task688 Evidence-Grounded Deliberation Architecture

## Purpose

ClimateOS should challenge claims rather than merely agree with the speaker.

## Conceptual Roles

- Claim: states the proposition and scope.
- Evidence: records supporting material and limitations.
- Challenge: tests assumptions, provenance, applicability, and missing alternatives.
- Counter-evidence: preserves contradictory material.
- Validation: assesses fitness without declaring universal truth.
- Human judgement: decides values, responsibility, and action.
- Governance record: records conditions, dissent, approval, refusal, and review date.

## Required Behaviours

- cite traceable sources;
- distinguish fact, output, inference, and value;
- state uncertainty;
- allow abstention and “unknown”;
- show what evidence could change the conclusion;
- refuse unsupported or prohibited claims;
- escalate high-impact decisions.

## Boundary

This is not a debate agent, scoring engine, automated decision system, or operational workflow.
