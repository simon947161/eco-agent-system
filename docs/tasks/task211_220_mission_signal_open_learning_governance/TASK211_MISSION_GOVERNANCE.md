# Task211 Mission Governance

## Purpose

Document the ClimateOS Mission and the governance principles required to preserve it across future Runtime, Agent, Workflow, and Intelligence layers.

## ClimateOS Mission

ClimateOS exists to continuously learn, coordinate, and evolve in service of the long-term flourishing of Earth's environment, ecosystems, civilization, and life.

## Mission Preservation Principles

- Mission is the enduring direction of the system.
- Mission is preserved when technology changes.
- Mission is not rewritten by convenience, automation, model capability, workflow pressure, or implementation momentum.
- Later layers must remain compatible with the Mission layer.
- Mission continuity requires human responsibility and review.

## Mission Governance

Mission governance means every future ClimateOS proposal should be checked against:

- Environmental flourishing.
- Ecosystem health.
- Civilizational continuity.
- Human responsibility.
- Evidence discipline.
- Long-term resilience.
- Architecture compatibility.

## Long-Term Continuity

ClimateOS should preserve continuity across:

- task batches
- architecture baselines
- roadmap milestones
- learning inputs
- evidence records
- future runtime planning
- human governance decisions

Continuity does not mean every idea is accepted. It means every accepted idea remains traceable to the Mission.

## Relationship Between Mission And Future Runtime

Future Runtime may execute bounded workflows only if separately authorized.

Runtime must serve Mission. Runtime must not decide Mission.

## Relationship Between Mission And Human Responsibility

Human responsibility remains above workflow execution.

Human review is required where decisions affect mission direction, public claims, governance authority, ethical responsibility, or implementation scope.

## Boundary

This document does not implement runtime behavior, agent behavior, automation, scoring, or decision systems.
