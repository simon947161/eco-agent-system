# Task213 Signal Ownership And Authority

## Purpose

Document conceptual governance for signal ownership, provenance, authority, maturity, review, and trust.

## Signal Ownership

Signal ownership records who or what is responsible for the signal's origin, custody, and review status.

Potential ownership categories:

- Founder-owned signal.
- Human observer signal.
- Project documentation signal.
- Official source signal.
- Scientific literature signal.
- Community contribution signal.
- AI-generated signal.
- Runtime-relevance signal.

## Signal Provenance

Signal provenance should describe:

- source
- capture date
- capture context
- author or originator where known
- transformation history
- review status
- uncertainty

## Signal Authority

Not all signals have equal authority.

Authority may come from:

- Founder decision authority.
- Official source authority.
- Scientific evidence authority.
- Local observation authority.
- Project governance authority.
- Human review authority.

AI-generated signals are routing candidates unless separately reviewed. They are not authority by default.

## Signal Maturity

Potential maturity states:

- Raw signal.
- Captured signal.
- Reviewed signal.
- Routed signal.
- Knowledge candidate.
- Evidence candidate.
- Governance input.
- Deferred signal.
- Retired signal.

## Signal Review

Signal review should check:

- source clarity
- provenance
- relevance
- uncertainty
- authority class
- freshness
- relationship to Mission
- routing destination

## Signal Trust

Trust is contextual. A signal may be trustworthy for routing but not for evidence, trustworthy for discussion but not for claims, or authoritative for governance but not for scientific facts.

## Boundary

This document does not create ranking algorithms, trust scores, automated review, or acceptance logic.
