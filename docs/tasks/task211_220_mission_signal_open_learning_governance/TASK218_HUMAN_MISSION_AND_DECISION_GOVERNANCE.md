# Task218 Human Mission And Decision Governance

## Purpose

Document the role of the human decision layer in ClimateOS mission governance.

## Human Decision Layer

Human judgement remains essential because ClimateOS concerns environmental responsibility, ecosystem futures, public claims, community impact, and long-term civilizational choices.

## Key Principles

- Human judgement remains essential.
- Human responsibility cannot be automated.
- Human approval cannot be silently bypassed.
- Human ethical responsibility remains above workflow execution.
- Automation should reduce repetitive work, not erase meaningful responsibility.

## Human Responsibility

Human responsibility includes:

- Mission interpretation.
- Ethical judgment.
- Public claim approval.
- Governance boundary decisions.
- Implementation authorization.
- Acceptance of uncertainty.
- Review of harm, bias, and misuse risks.

## Relationship To Automation

ClimateOS may later automate repetitive or low-risk tasks only if separately authorized.

Automation must preserve:

- human visibility
- review triggers
- override paths
- auditability
- accountability
- ability to stop or defer

## Approval Boundaries

Human approval is required before:

- implementation starts
- runtime is created
- agents execute
- external standards are interpreted
- public claims are made
- QCloud resumes
- MCP or n8n becomes operational dependency
- Roadmap Task200 or Roadmap Task300 meaning changes

## Boundary

This document does not implement approval workflows, automation, runtime, agents, or decision systems.
