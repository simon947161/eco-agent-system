# Task214 Signal Lifecycle

## Purpose

Document the conceptual lifecycle for ClimateOS signals.

## Lifecycle

```text
Signal
-> Harvest
-> Review
-> Knowledge
-> Evidence
-> Archive
```

This lifecycle is conceptual. It does not imply automation or runtime execution.

## Signal

A signal is a possible input that may matter to ClimateOS.

Signals may originate from observation, research, official sources, Founder insight, governance records, conversations, CRP harvest, or community contribution.

## Harvest

Harvest captures the signal with enough context to preserve traceability.

Harvest should record:

- origin
- date
- context
- possible relevance
- uncertainty
- first routing suggestion

## Review

Review determines whether the signal is relevant, reliable, mature, stale, duplicated, risky, or out of scope.

Review may lead to knowledge routing, evidence routing, governance routing, deferral, retirement, or reopening.

## Knowledge

A signal may become knowledge after review.

Knowledge status requires context, meaning, review state, and relationship to existing ClimateOS records.

## Evidence

A signal may become evidence only after stricter source, provenance, freshness, review, and claim-boundary checks.

Evidence status is not automatic.

## Archive

Signals should remain traceable after closure, retirement, deferral, or rejection.

Archive preserves institutional memory without implying current authority.

## Freshness, Revision, Retirement, Reopening, And Uncertainty

- Freshness: signals may expire or require periodic review.
- Revision: signals may change when sources, context, or understanding changes.
- Retirement: signals may be closed when outdated, superseded, invalid, or out of scope.
- Reopening: signals may reopen when new evidence or Founder direction appears.
- Uncertainty: signals should carry uncertainty instead of hiding it.

## Boundary

This document does not implement lifecycle tracking, databases, automation, runtime hooks, or workflow engines.
