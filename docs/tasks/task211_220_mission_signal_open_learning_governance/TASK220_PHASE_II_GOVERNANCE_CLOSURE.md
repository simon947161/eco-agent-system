## Mission Governance

The ClimateOS Mission is preserved as the enduring direction of the system:

ClimateOS exists to continuously learn, coordinate, and evolve in service of the long-term flourishing of Earth's environment, ecosystems, civilization, and life.

## Signal Governance

Signals are possible inputs to learning, knowledge, evidence, governance, or routing. They are not automatically true, mature, authoritative, or actionable.

## Open Learning Governance

Open Learning is governed, traceable, uncertain when appropriate, and non-automatic. Learning does not authorize implementation.

## Human Decision Governance

Human judgement, approval, and ethical responsibility remain above workflow execution.
