# Task664 Energy Comfort And Habit Example

## Purpose

Describe a safe Sustainable Office example involving energy, comfort, and human
habit without scoring or automation.

## Scenario

A fictional office team notices that a shared workspace often feels too cold in
the morning and too warm in the afternoon. People respond individually: some
bring heaters, some open windows, and some avoid the room.

## ClimateOS Inquiry

ClimateOS should help the team ask:

- What is actually being observed?
- Is the problem comfort, energy use, scheduling, sunlight, airflow, or
  equipment heat?
- What evidence is missing?
- Which actions are low-risk?
- Which actions require facilities review?

## Possible Low-Risk Actions

- record comfort observations for a limited period;
- compare room use patterns;
- discuss scheduling changes;
- identify whether equipment placement is contributing heat;
- ask facilities for review before technical changes.

## Prohibited Claims

- energy savings achieved;
- comfort optimization completed;
- building performance certified;
- emissions reduction calculated;
- behavior compliance measured.

## Human-Language Rule

The system helps people understand the situation. It does not tell them how to
behave or silently optimize the building.
