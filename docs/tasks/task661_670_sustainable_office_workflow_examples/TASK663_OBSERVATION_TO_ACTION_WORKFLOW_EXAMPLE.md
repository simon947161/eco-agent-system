# Task663 Observation To Action Workflow Example

## Purpose

Show a safe fictional workflow from office observation to reviewed human action.

## Example Workflow

| Step | Human Question | ClimateOS Record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Notice | What did someone observe? | Observation note |
| Context | Where, when, and under what condition? | Spatial and temporal context |
| Interpretation | What might it mean? | Hypothesis candidate |
| Evidence need | What would make this clearer? | Evidence checklist |
| Action idea | What small action could be tried? | Proposed human action |
| Review | Is it low-risk or review-required? | Review state |
| Outcome | What happened after the action? | Learning note |

## Fictional Example

Observation: staff notice that a meeting room feels hot every afternoon.

Possible interpretation: afternoon sun exposure, poor ventilation, equipment
heat, or scheduling patterns may be relevant.

Low-risk action: open blinds differently, adjust room booking, or record
comfort observations for one week.

Review-required action: changing HVAC settings, installing sensors, or making
energy-performance claims.

## Boundary

This workflow does not automate building systems, collect sensor data, issue
instructions, or calculate energy savings.
