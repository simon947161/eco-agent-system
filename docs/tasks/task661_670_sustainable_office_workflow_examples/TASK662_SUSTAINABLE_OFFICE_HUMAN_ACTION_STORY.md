# Task662 Sustainable Office Human Action Story

## Purpose

Explain the Sustainable Office concept as a human story rather than a control
system.

## Story

A person notices something in the workplace: a room feels overheated, lights are
left on, plants near a window are failing, water use seems wasteful, or waste
sorting is confusing.

ClimateOS should not immediately command behavior. It should help the person
turn that noticing into a careful record:

- what was observed;
- where and when it was observed;
- what may explain it;
- what is uncertain;
- which domain may care;
- what small action might be tried;
- who should review it;
- what should be checked later.

The goal is not obedience. The goal is shared learning.

## Human Action Principle

ClimateOS should support people in making better environmental choices, but the
choice remains human. The system may structure attention, preserve evidence,
and remind people what is unknown. It must not become a hidden manager of human
behavior.

## Current Capability

This story is conceptual. It does not create office software, dashboards,
notifications, building controls, or workplace monitoring.
