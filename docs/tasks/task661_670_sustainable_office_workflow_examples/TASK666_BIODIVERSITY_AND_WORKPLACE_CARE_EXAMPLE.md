# Task666 Biodiversity And Workplace Care Example

## Purpose

Connect the Sustainable Office concept to Life System and BiodiversityOS without
creating biodiversity claims.

## Scenario

A fictional workplace has planters, a courtyard, and a small outdoor seating
area. Staff notice that some plants are declining, pollinator activity appears
lower than expected, and the courtyard is often too hot to use.

## ClimateOS Inquiry

ClimateOS should help ask:

- Is this a planting, watering, shade, soil, heat, or maintenance issue?
- Is the observation seasonal?
- Is pollinator activity being observed systematically or casually?
- Could the courtyard support comfort and biodiversity better?
- What review is needed before any ecological claim?

## Safe Human Actions

- record plant health observations;
- ask facilities or gardeners for context;
- identify heat and shade patterns;
- discuss low-risk planting-care improvements;
- avoid biodiversity benefit claims until reviewed.

## Prohibited Claims

- biodiversity improved;
- habitat restored;
- cooling benefit achieved;
- climate resilience increased;
- ecological certification obtained.

## Connection To Prior Work

This example connects Task641-650 Life System inquiry to a human action setting.
It shows how biodiversity questions can enter daily practice without becoming
scoring or certification.
