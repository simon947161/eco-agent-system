# Task667 Human Consent And Non-Control Rules

## Purpose

Prevent Sustainable Office examples from becoming workplace surveillance,
behavioral control, or hidden automation.

## Rule 1: Participation Must Be Understandable

People should know what is being recorded, why it is being recorded, and how it
may be used.

## Rule 2: Observation Is Not Performance Management

Environmental observations should not become employee ranking, discipline, or
behavior scoring.

## Rule 3: Suggestion Is Not Instruction

ClimateOS may help frame an option. It must not silently issue commands or
pretend that a suggestion is mandatory.

## Rule 4: Low-Risk Does Not Mean No Review

Even low-risk actions should be reviewable if they affect comfort, access,
equity, health, safety, or workload.

## Rule 5: No Hidden Automation

No notification, sensor, building-control, procurement, or workflow automation
is created in this batch.

## Rule 6: Human Authority Remains Visible

Every proposed action should show who reviewed it, what remains uncertain, and
whether it is optional, blocked, or escalated.

## Current Capability

These are governance rules only. No consent system, permission system, or
workplace software is implemented.
