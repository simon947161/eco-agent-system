## Core Insight

Task100 marks the point where the system begins to have a credible validation foundation.

Task100–150 may explore a higher operational mechanism:

```text
Observe the world
→ Understand different professions
→ Connect different people
→ Find common interests
→ Turn competition into cooperation
```

This is not a slogan. It should be treated as a possible working mechanism for ClimateOS after Task100.

---

## Why It Matters

Human beings have limited lifetime, attention, and cross-domain capacity.

Global climate, ecological, energy, infrastructure, and governance challenges require very deep cross-domain coordination.

Some rare people can do this kind of work across many boundaries, but it is difficult to sustain across generations.

Agents may be able to help preserve and scale part of this horizontal connection ability.

The goal is not to replace human governance, but to help people form shared evidence, shared judgment, shared responsibility, shared benefit, and shared action.
