# Carbon Accounting Evidence Requirements

## Purpose

Future accounting records must remain traceable to reviewable evidence.
Evidence categories do not establish regulatory or professional sufficiency.

| Category | Purpose | Potential limitations | Review notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Energy Records | Describe electricity, fuel, heat, steam, or other energy activity | Missing periods, inconsistent units, estimates, or unclear boundaries | Confirm source, owner, period, quantity, unit, boundary, quality, and revisions |
| Meter Data | Support measured consumption, generation, imports, or exports | Gaps, estimates, configuration errors, aggregation, or calibration issues | Check meter identity, interval coverage, corrections, timezone, and facility relationship |
| Fuel Records | Support fuel purchase, storage, transfer, or consumption | Stock changes, mixed fuels, conversions, missing density or quality data | Reconcile fuel type, quantity, unit, dates, inventory movement, and use |
| Invoices | Support purchased quantities, suppliers, periods, and commercial records | Billing estimates, adjustments, taxes, mixed products, or period mismatch | Identify executed record, service period, quantities, units, corrections, and linked meters |
| Contracts | Define parties, delivery, products, periods, and attribute rights | Ambiguity, amendments, expired terms, or missing signatures | Check version, parties, effective dates, quantities, boundaries, and relevant terms |
| Certificates | Support electricity attributes and classification records | Ownership, matching, retirement, duplication, or scheme differences | Preserve Task51 evidence and review status; do not independently certify validity |
| Internal Reports | Provide operations, allocations, reconciliations, and management context | Self-reporting, uncontrolled versions, undocumented assumptions | Record owner, method, approval, source inputs, version, and independence |
| External References | Document methods, definitions, factors, or contextual rules | Changing versions, licensing, jurisdiction, or secondary summaries | Record publisher, title, version, date, jurisdiction, access date, and applicability |

## Evidence Record

Each item should include an identifier, category, source, owner, relevant
boundary and period, version, location or reference, status, limitations,
retention needs, and relationship to accounting records.

## Emission Factor Boundary

Task52 includes no factors. Future factor evidence must identify the publisher,
dataset, version, geography, period, unit basis, greenhouse gases, uncertainty,
license, selection rationale, and reviewer. Factors must never be invented or
silently substituted.

## Evidence Gaps

Missing, conflicting, expired, inaccessible, or estimated evidence must be
recorded as a gap and routed to human review.
