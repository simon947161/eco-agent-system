# Carbon Accounting Boundaries

## Purpose

Every accounting record requires a declared boundary. Boundaries may overlap,
but their differences and relationships must remain explicit.

## Organisational Boundary

**Definition:** the entities and operations included under an organisation's
declared control, ownership, or reporting method.

**Purpose:** determines which facilities, projects, products, and activities
belong in an organisational inventory.

**Limitations:** ownership and control structures may change or be interpreted
differently by applicable methods.

**Review notes:** record the method, entities, exclusions, period, owner,
changes, and unresolved interpretations.

## Facility Boundary

**Definition:** the physical and operational limits of a site, plant, building,
or other reporting unit.

**Purpose:** connects meters, fuel, electricity, equipment, and activities to
a reviewable site context.

**Limitations:** shared services, tenants, imports, exports, mobile assets, and
off-site operations may complicate attribution.

**Review notes:** identify locations, meters, operations, shared assets,
exclusions, reporting period, and relationship to the organisation.

## Project Boundary

**Definition:** the activities, assets, locations, baseline, and period assigned
to a defined project.

**Purpose:** separates project information from routine operations or other
initiatives.

**Limitations:** baselines, leakage, indirect effects, changing designs, and
overlap with organisational inventories require method-specific review.

**Review notes:** document project owner, purpose, start and end dates,
included activities, baseline assumptions, interfaces, and exclusions.

## Product Boundary

**Definition:** the lifecycle stages, processes, geography, period, and
functional unit included for a product or product version.

**Purpose:** organises product-level activity and future emission records.

**Limitations:** supplier evidence, allocation, lifecycle stages, product
changes, and incomplete value-chain data may limit comparability.

**Review notes:** record product version, functional unit, lifecycle stages,
allocation method, data sources, exclusions, and uncertainty.

## Scenario Boundary

**Definition:** the system, geography, period, assumptions, and activities
included in a hypothetical or exploratory ClimateOS scenario.

**Purpose:** keeps scenario accounting separate from observed or operational
records.

**Limitations:** scenario results depend on assumptions and are not forecasts,
commitments, or verified inventories.

**Review notes:** label hypothetical inputs, baseline, time horizon,
assumptions, evidence status, uncertainty, and prohibited interpretations.

## Boundary Control

Future records should carry a boundary identifier and version. Changes should
create traceable revisions and trigger review of affected records rather than
silently altering historical inventories.
