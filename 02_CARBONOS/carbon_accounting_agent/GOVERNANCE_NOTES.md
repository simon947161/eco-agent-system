# Carbon Accounting Governance Notes

## Purpose

Governance keeps future accounting records transparent, traceable, reviewable,
and limited to their supported interpretation.

## Transparency

Outputs should state boundary, period, scope, method, source records, green
power status, factor references, assumptions, uncertainty, exclusions,
validation findings, review status, and limitations.

## Auditability

Inputs, transformations, versions, evidence, findings, reviewer notes, and
changes should be reconstructable. Corrections must not erase history.

## Evidence-Based Review

Preparers and reviewers should consider supporting and conflicting evidence.
Unknown values, gaps, estimates, and exceptions must remain visible.

## Human Oversight

The agent supports accounting preparation and review. Final accounting
decisions remain subject to human judgement.

Qualified humans select applicable standards, boundaries, methods, emission
factors, materiality, conclusions, claims, and disclosure actions.

## Documentation Quality

Records should use stable identifiers, defined units, dates, versions,
controlled statuses, plain-language notes, and links to source evidence.
Source facts must be distinguishable from assumptions and derived values.

## Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence Owner | Supplies records and source context |
| Accounting Preparer | Organises records and documents methods and assumptions |
| Reviewer | Checks evidence, boundaries, methods, uncertainty, and limitations |
| Decision Owner | Accepts organisational accounting decisions and claims |
| Repository Maintainer | Controls task scope, versions, documentation, and tests |

## Prohibited Interpretations

The foundation is not a completed inventory, audit, assurance opinion,
regulatory filing, certification, carbon-neutrality claim, legal advice,
financial advice, or automated compliance decision.

## Future Data Controls

Before real data or external systems are used, future tasks must address
privacy, confidentiality, licensing, access, retention, security, source
authority, cost ownership, and incident handling.
