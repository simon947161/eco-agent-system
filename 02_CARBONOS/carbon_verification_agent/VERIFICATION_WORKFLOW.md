# Carbon Verification Workflow

```text
Evidence Collection
  |
  v
Evidence Review
  |
  v
Boundary Review
  |
  v
Traceability Review
  |
  v
Uncertainty Review
  |
  v
Verification Summary
  |
  v
Disclosure Preparation
```

## 1. Evidence Collection

Define the review subject and evidence boundary. Register available, requested,
unavailable, confidential, and superseded evidence.

## 2. Evidence Review

Examine provenance, ownership, version, period, relevance, completeness,
quality, limitations, and conflicts without altering source records.

## 3. Boundary Review

Compare declared organisation, facility, project, product, scenario, and
evidence boundaries across classification, accounting, and budget records.

## 4. Traceability Review

Trace material statements to evidence, source records, method versions,
transformations, assumptions, and human decisions. Record broken links.

## 5. Uncertainty Review

Record estimates, proxies, missing records, conflicting evidence, scenario
assumptions, methodological limitations, and unresolved questions.

## 6. Verification Summary

Prepare findings, observations, exceptions, evidence coverage, uncertainty,
limitations, and review status. This summary is not professional assurance.

## 7. Disclosure Preparation

Provide bounded review information to a future disclosure workflow. Human
owners decide whether and how any information may be used.

## Review Loops

Records may return to source owners for clarification or revision. New
versions require traceable re-review and must not erase prior findings.
