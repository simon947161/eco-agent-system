# Carbon Verification Scope

## In Scope

| Activity | Conceptual coverage |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence Review | Provenance, version, relevance, period, ownership, quality, and limitations |
| Boundary Review | Alignment of organisation, facility, project, product, scenario, and evidence boundaries |
| Traceability Review | Links from statements and records to sources, methods, transformations, and decisions |
| Documentation Review | Completeness, clarity, controlled status, versioning, and review notes |
| Uncertainty Review | Estimates, assumptions, missing data, conflicts, sensitivity, and limitations |
| Verification Preparation | Evidence indexes, findings, exceptions, observations, and human-readable summaries |
| Change Review | Differences between record versions and effects on prior findings |
| Disclosure Preparation | Bounded verification information for later human disclosure review |

## Out of Scope

- regulatory enforcement, approval, filing, or legal determination;
- certification, accreditation, audit opinions, or professional assurance;
- automated approval, compliance, eligibility, or claim decisions;
- carbon calculations, accounting transformations, or budget calculations;
- correction of source records without their owner and review process;
- external APIs, registries, databases, meters, or document systems.

## Interpretation

A completed review record means only that declared review activities were
documented. It does not establish truth, compliance, assurance, or permission
to make a public claim.
