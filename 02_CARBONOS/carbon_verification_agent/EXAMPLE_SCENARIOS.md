# Carbon Verification Example Scenarios

## Strong Evidence Scenario

**Inputs:** controlled meter, energy, accounting, and budget records with
aligned boundaries, periods, versions, provenance, and review status.

**Conceptual outcome:** evidence coverage and traceability are documented as
strong, with remaining limitations and human review clearly stated.

## Mixed Evidence Scenario

**Inputs:** some controlled records plus estimates, internal reports, and
unresolved certificate evidence.

**Conceptual outcome:** findings distinguish supported records from weak or
unresolved evidence. No single favorable status is inferred.

## Boundary Conflict Scenario

**Inputs:** organisational inventory and facility budget using different
boundaries or versions.

**Conceptual outcome:** a boundary conflict finding identifies affected
records, owners, and required clarification.

## Incomplete Documentation Scenario

**Inputs:** accounting values without complete methods, factors, source links,
or reviewer records.

**Conceptual outcome:** incomplete-documentation findings and evidence requests
are prepared. The missing material is not invented.

## High Uncertainty Scenario

**Inputs:** scenario data, estimates, proxy evidence, and uncertain future
plans.

**Conceptual outcome:** uncertainty records and limitations are emphasized.
The summary is not presented as a verified forecast or operational result.

## Limitations

These examples illustrate review structures only. They are not professional
assurance conclusions, certifications, or compliance decisions.
