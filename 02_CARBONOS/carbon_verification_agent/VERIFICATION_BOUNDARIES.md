# Carbon Verification Boundaries

## Organisation Boundary

**Definition:** entities and operations covered by the review.

**Purpose:** determines which organisational records and controls are examined.

**Limitations:** ownership and control methods may change or conflict.

**Review notes:** record method, entities, exclusions, period, versions, and
decision owner.

## Facility Boundary

**Definition:** physical and operational site limits included in review.

**Purpose:** connects evidence, meters, activities, and records to a site.

**Limitations:** shared services, tenants, mobile assets, and off-site activity.

**Review notes:** confirm locations, operations, records, exclusions, and
relationship to the organisation.

## Project Boundary

**Definition:** project activities, assets, baseline, locations, and period.

**Purpose:** separates project evidence from routine operations.

**Limitations:** changing designs, baselines, overlap, and indirect effects.

**Review notes:** document project version, assumptions, interfaces, sources,
and unresolved changes.

## Product Boundary

**Definition:** lifecycle stages, processes, functional unit, and period.

**Purpose:** limits review of product carbon information.

**Limitations:** supplier evidence, allocation, lifecycle gaps, and product
version changes.

**Review notes:** confirm functional unit, lifecycle scope, source versions,
allocation, exclusions, and uncertainty.

## Scenario Boundary

**Definition:** hypothetical system, geography, horizon, and assumptions.

**Purpose:** keeps scenario review separate from observed or operational facts.

**Limitations:** scenarios are not forecasts, commitments, or verified outcomes.

**Review notes:** label assumptions, evidence status, uncertainty, baseline,
version, and prohibited interpretations.

## Evidence Boundary

**Definition:** the sources, dates, versions, records, and access limits
included in a specific review.

**Purpose:** makes clear what evidence was and was not examined.

**Limitations:** inaccessible, confidential, expired, translated, sampled, or
later evidence may affect findings.

**Review notes:** record inclusion criteria, cut-off date, unavailable items,
sampling, retention, confidentiality, and reviewer access.
