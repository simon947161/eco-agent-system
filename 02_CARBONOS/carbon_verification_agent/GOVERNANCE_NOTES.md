# Carbon Verification Governance Notes

## Transparency

Every review should state its subject, boundary, period, criteria, evidence,
reviewer, findings, uncertainty, exclusions, status, and limitations.

## Auditability

Evidence indexes, record versions, review steps, observations, responses,
changes, findings, and decisions should be reconstructable.

## Traceability

Findings and statements should link to identifiable source evidence and
records. Reviewer interpretation must remain distinct from source facts.

## Human Oversight

The agent supports verification preparation and review. Final verification
outcomes remain subject to human judgement.

Qualified humans retain responsibility for professional methods, assurance,
certification, regulatory interpretations, closure, and disclosure use.

## Evidence-Based Review

Supporting and conflicting evidence, unavailable records, limitations, and
uncertainty must be considered. Favorable status must not be inferred from
missing evidence.

## Reviewer Independence

Future workflows should identify reviewer role, competence, conflicts of
interest, scope authority, and whether review is internal or external. Task54
does not determine independence.

## Prohibited Interpretations

The foundation is not an audit, assurance engagement, certification,
regulatory approval, legal opinion, compliance result, or permission to make
an environmental claim.
