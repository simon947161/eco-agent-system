# Carbon Verification Evidence Requirements

| Category | Purpose | Limitations | Review notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Meter Records | Support measured electricity, generation, import, export, or fuel activity | Gaps, estimates, configuration, aggregation, calibration, or timezone issues | Confirm identity, boundary, units, period, corrections, and source authority |
| Energy Records | Describe electricity, fuel, heat, steam, and related activity | Inconsistent units, classifications, estimates, or missing periods | Trace to meters, invoices, contracts, owners, and accounting records |
| Accounting Records | Provide inventories, methods, emission records, exclusions, and uncertainty | Incomplete status, method changes, factor limitations, or version conflicts | Confirm exact inventory, method, period, boundary, source lineage, and status |
| Budget Records | Provide targets, allocations, actual references, assumptions, and variance records | Scenario status, unsupported targets, incomplete actuals, or changing versions | Confirm accounting basis, ownership, period, boundary, evidence, and approval state |
| Contracts | Describe parties, delivery, rights, products, and effective periods | Ambiguity, amendments, missing execution, or legal interpretation limits | Record executed version, parties, dates, relevant clauses, and reviewer limits |
| Certificates | Support electricity-attribute evidence and classification | Ownership, matching, retirement, duplication, scheme, or registry uncertainty | Preserve Task51 status and do not claim authentication or regulatory validity |
| Supporting Documentation | Preserve policies, decisions, reports, studies, plans, and contextual sources | Self-reporting, stale versions, restricted access, translation, or jurisdiction limits | Record issuer, owner, date, version, applicability, confidentiality, and limitations |

## Evidence Record Minimum

Each item should retain an identifier, source, owner, date and applicable
period, version, relationship to the subject, status, limitations, access,
retention, and reviewer notes.

## Missing Evidence

Missing, conflicting, expired, inaccessible, or unverified evidence must remain
explicit. The agent must not invent evidence or silently resolve conflicts.
