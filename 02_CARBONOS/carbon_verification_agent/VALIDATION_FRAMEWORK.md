# Carbon Verification Validation Framework

## Completeness

Check whether the declared review scope, subjects, evidence, periods,
boundaries, methods, findings, uncertainty, and reviewer information exist.

## Consistency

Check identifiers, versions, dates, units, statuses, boundaries, and
terminology across evidence, accounting, budget, and review records.

## Traceability

Confirm that material statements link to identifiable evidence, source
records, methods, transformations, assumptions, and decisions.

## Evidence Coverage

Compare reviewed evidence with the declared evidence boundary, period, subject,
and record population. Document sampling and partial coverage.

## Boundary Alignment

Check organisation, facility, project, product, scenario, and evidence
boundaries for mismatch or unexplained change.

## Human Review

Every verification summary requires an identifiable reviewer. Material
findings, professional interpretations, closure, and disclosure use remain
human decisions.

## Uncertainty Recording

Record estimates, proxies, omissions, conflicts, inaccessible evidence,
scenario assumptions, and method limitations without unsupported precision.

## Status Preservation

Upstream `Unknown`, `Needs Review`, incomplete, scenario, and unreviewed states
must not be silently upgraded by validation checks.

## Validation Boundary

No check constitutes approval, compliance, certification, audit, professional
assurance, or legal determination. Task54 implements no approval logic or
automated compliance logic.
