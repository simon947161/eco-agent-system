# CarbonOS Governance

## Purpose

CarbonOS governance keeps future carbon records understandable, traceable,
reviewable, and appropriately limited.

## Transparency

Every output should state its purpose, reporting boundary, period, method,
units, assumptions, evidence status, uncertainty, and limitations. Labels
must distinguish source data, derived records, reviewer findings, and claims.

## Evidence Traceability

CarbonOS records should reference their source evidence and retain provenance,
ownership, collection period, method version, and transformation history.
Unavailable or superseded evidence should be marked, not silently removed.

## Human Review

Material classifications, accounting outputs, budget interpretations,
verification findings, and disclosures require an identifiable human review
point. The system may organise evidence but does not make autonomous approval,
compliance, certification, or management decisions.

## Uncertainty Awareness

Estimated data, proxy factors, incomplete boundaries, conflicting evidence,
and method limitations should be recorded explicitly. Outputs should not imply
more precision or certainty than the evidence supports.

## Auditability

Records should be versioned and reconstructable. A reviewer should be able to
identify who supplied information, what evidence and methods were used, what
changed, what checks were performed, and which issues remain unresolved.

## Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Data Owner | Supplies records and confirms source context |
| Preparer | Organises records using the declared method and boundary |
| Reviewer | Checks evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, and limitations |
| Decision Owner | Makes any organisational decision outside CarbonOS |
| Repository Maintainer | Protects versioning, documentation, and task boundaries |

One person may hold more than one role in a small demonstration, but the role
being performed should remain explicit.

## Claims and Status

- `Planned` means an agent or capability is documented for future work.
- `Foundation Established` means subsystem definitions exist.
- Neither status means implemented, validated, certified, or operational.
- Green, renewable, carbon-neutral, net-zero, compliant, and verified claims
  require evidence and review rules defined by later approved tasks.

## Professional Judgement

CarbonOS does not replace professional judgement. It does not provide legal,
regulatory, financial, tax, engineering, scientific assurance, accreditation,
or certification services. Qualified reviewers remain responsible for
selecting applicable standards and accepting any real-world use.

## Change Control

Future changes should follow the repository task index, Agent Standard,
lifecycle, checklist, and architecture-decision process. New calculations,
external integrations, automated decisions, or regulatory interpretations
require separately scoped and reviewed tasks.
