# CarbonOS Validation Alignment

## Purpose

This document maps CarbonOS verification concepts to future ClimateOS
validation concepts. It is conceptual only; ValidationOS remains scaffold-only.

```text
CarbonOS Verification
  |
  v
ClimateOS Validation Layer
```

## Evidence Review to Evidence Layer

**Purpose:** align evidence identity, provenance, ownership, versions,
coverage, quality, access, and limitations.

**Inputs:** CarbonOS evidence records, source documents, evidence boundaries,
and review criteria.

**Outputs:** evidence indexes, coverage findings, gaps, conflicts, and status.

**Review considerations:** licensing, confidentiality, source authority,
currency, completeness, sampling, and missing evidence.

## Traceability Review to Validation Layer

**Purpose:** align checks that connect CarbonOS statements and derived records
to source evidence, methods, transformations, and versions.

**Inputs:** classification, accounting, budget, evidence, and traceability
records.

**Outputs:** lineage findings, broken-link records, consistency issues, and
human review requirements.

**Review considerations:** status preservation, version compatibility,
boundary alignment, reproducibility, and limits of deterministic checks.

## Uncertainty Review to Scenario Layer

**Purpose:** align uncertainty, assumptions, estimates, proxies, scenario
conditions, and interpretation limits.

**Inputs:** uncertainty records, scenario boundaries, assumptions, evidence
status, and reviewer observations.

**Outputs:** uncertainty summaries, unresolved assumptions, sensitivity needs,
and non-forecast limitations.

**Review considerations:** observed versus hypothetical data, time horizon,
baseline, model limits, missing evidence, and unsupported precision.

## Governance Review to Governance Layer

**Purpose:** align reviewer roles, human authority, audit trails, decision
boundaries, conflicts, retention, and prohibited interpretations.

**Inputs:** governance rules, reviewer records, findings, decisions, access
controls, and change history.

**Outputs:** governance observations, approval boundaries, unresolved
responsibilities, and audit-ready review records.

**Review considerations:** competence, independence, conflicts of interest,
privacy, security, retention, professional authority, and human sign-off.

## Alignment Rules

- CarbonOS keeps domain-specific record meaning and review context.
- Future ClimateOS layers may provide shared structures, not automatic truth.
- Validation findings do not equal assurance, certification, or compliance.
- `Unknown`, `Needs Review`, incomplete, and scenario statuses remain intact.
- Shared runtime contracts require a separate approved implementation task.
