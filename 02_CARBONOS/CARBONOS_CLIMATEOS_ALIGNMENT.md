# CarbonOS ClimateOS Alignment

## Purpose

This document defines the conceptual relationship between CarbonOS and
ClimateOS. It adds no implementation.

```text
CarbonOS
  |
  v
ClimateOS
```

## Carbon Accounting to EnergyOS

**Purpose:** connect carbon accounting records with future energy-domain
evidence and governance concepts.

**Inputs:** energy records, electricity use, fuel records, classification
results, accounting periods, and evidence.

**Outputs:** bounded accounting records for future EnergyOS coordination.

**Future integration opportunities:** shared energy evidence vocabulary,
cross-system validation, and scenario handoffs.

## Carbon Budget to ClimateOS Scenario Layer

**Purpose:** align carbon targets, allocations, actuals, and assumptions with
future scenario coordination.

**Inputs:** reviewed inventories, targets, allocations, budget periods,
assumptions, and scenario records.

**Outputs:** scenario-labelled budget records and review packs.

**Future integration opportunities:** bounded comparisons, scenario reviews,
and governance-ready planning summaries.

## Carbon Verification to Validation Layer

**Purpose:** align evidence, traceability, uncertainty, and review findings
with future ValidationOS concepts.

**Inputs:** evidence records, accounting records, budget records, reviewer
observations, and validation findings.

**Outputs:** verification preparation records and unresolved-issue summaries.

**Future integration opportunities:** shared validation checks, evidence
coverage reporting, and review status conventions.

## ESG Disclosure to Governance Layer

**Purpose:** align disclosure preparation with human decision ownership,
stakeholder context, and governance boundaries.

**Inputs:** reviewed CarbonOS records, verification summaries, limitations,
stakeholder context, and governance notes.

**Outputs:** disclosure-preparation records for human review.

**Future integration opportunities:** governance workflows, approval boundary
tracking, and stakeholder-facing summaries after separate approval.

## Alignment Limits

ClimateOS is treated as an Earth System Governance Platform concept. Task55
does not implement ClimateOS runtime, orchestration, dashboards, APIs,
recommendations, or decisions.
