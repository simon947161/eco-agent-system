# CarbonOS Scenario Alignment

## Purpose

This document aligns CarbonOS scenario concepts with ScenarioOS, ClimateOS, and
EcoEngine. It is documentation only.

```text
CarbonOS
  |
  v
Scenario Layer
  |
  v
ClimateOS
  |
  v
EcoEngine
```

## CarbonOS to Scenario Layer

**Purpose:** convert reviewed CarbonOS records into structured scenario inputs.

**Inputs:** accounting records, budget records, verification findings,
disclosure context, assumptions, and constraints.

**Outputs:** carbon-oriented scenario records, assumption registers,
comparison notes, and review requirements.

**Review considerations:** preserve source status, boundaries, periods,
uncertainty, and unresolved issues.

## Scenario Layer to ClimateOS

**Purpose:** provide carbon-specific scenario context for future ClimateOS
cross-domain coordination.

**Inputs:** carbon scenario records, assumptions, unknowns, and governance
notes.

**Outputs:** scenario context for climate, energy, water, land, biodiversity,
and governance discussions.

**Review considerations:** carbon scenarios must not claim authority over other
domains or imply cross-domain optimisation.

## ClimateOS to EcoEngine

**Purpose:** align scenario records with future evidence, reasoning,
validation, and governance support.

**Inputs:** scenario records, evidence links, assumptions, uncertainty, review
notes, and governance boundaries.

**Outputs:** future validation packs, evidence checks, uncertainty summaries,
and governance review notes.

**Review considerations:** future runtime support must preserve human
judgement and avoid automated recommendations.

## CarbonOS to EcoEngine

**Purpose:** provide carbon scenario assumptions and comparisons for future
EcoEngine review.

**Inputs:** scenario assumptions, comparison notes, evidence records,
verification findings, and uncertainty records.

**Outputs:** validation-ready scenario records and unresolved-issue summaries.

**Review considerations:** no runtime, LLM, API, optimisation, or forecasting
integration exists in Task56.

## Alignment Limits

The Carbon Scenario Agent supports structured possibilities only. It does not
predict, optimise, recommend, approve, or decide.
