# Carbon Budget Validation Framework

## Purpose

These conceptual checks support preparation and review. They do not approve
targets, allocations, performance claims, or compliance.

## Completeness

Check whether required boundaries, periods, targets, allocations, accounting
inputs, evidence, assumptions, owners, and review states are present.

## Consistency

Check identifiers, units, dates, target versions, inventory versions,
allocation methods, statuses, and terminology across related records.

## Boundary Validation

Confirm that targets, allocations, actual records, and scenarios use compatible
organisational, facility, project, product, or scenario boundaries.

## Evidence Coverage

Compare evidence coverage with budget periods, boundaries, targets,
allocations, and performance records. Partial coverage remains explicit.

## Period and Unit Alignment

Check that target, accounting, and variance records use declared periods and
compatible units. Misalignment requires review.

## Allocation Reconciliation

Future checks may identify missing, overlapping, or excess allocations, but
resolution and approval remain human responsibilities.

## Human Review

Targets, allocations, methodological choices, material variances, scenario
assumptions, and disclosure uses require identifiable human review.

## Uncertainty Recording

Record inventory uncertainty, assumptions, estimates, incomplete evidence,
future-action dependencies, and scenario limitations without implying false
precision.

## Invalid Inputs

Future logic should reject malformed records safely, preserve originals,
explain findings, and avoid silent defaults or coercion.

## Validation Boundary

Completed checks would not constitute professional assurance, target approval,
forecast confidence, regulatory acceptance, or proof that a budget will be met.
