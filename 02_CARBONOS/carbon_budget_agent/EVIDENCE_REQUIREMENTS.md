# Carbon Budget Evidence Requirements

## Purpose

Budget records must be linked to evidence that reviewers can identify and
challenge. Evidence categories do not establish target quality or approval.

| Category | Purpose | Limitations | Review notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Carbon Inventories | Provide the accounting basis for budgets and tracking | Incomplete boundaries, old versions, exclusions, uncertainty, or unreviewed records | Confirm boundary, period, method, version, exclusions, uncertainty, and review status |
| Accounting Records | Support detailed actual-emissions references | Method changes, missing records, inconsistent units, or duplicate entries | Preserve record IDs, source versions, status, lineage, and limitations |
| Energy Records | Explain underlying electricity and fuel activity | Estimates, missing periods, classification issues, or boundary mismatch | Check source, quantity, unit, period, facility, classification, and quality |
| Reduction Plans | Document proposed actions, owners, timing, and assumptions | Plans may change and do not prove delivery or impact | Record approval state, dependencies, evidence basis, risks, and uncertainty |
| Internal Reports | Support targets, allocations, performance explanations, and decisions | Self-reporting, uncontrolled versions, undocumented methods | Record owner, approver, version, source inputs, method, and independence |
| Supporting Documentation | Preserve policies, decisions, contracts, studies, and contextual evidence | Expired, incomplete, conflicting, inaccessible, or jurisdiction-specific records | Record source, issuer, date, version, applicability, limitations, and retention |

## Minimum Evidence Record

Each item should include an identifier, source, owner, relevant boundary and
period, version, status, relationship to the budget, limitations, and review
notes.

## Evidence Gaps

Missing, conflicting, estimated, expired, or inaccessible evidence must remain
visible. The agent must not invent targets, plans, actuals, or explanations.
