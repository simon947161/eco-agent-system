# Carbon Budget Workflow

## Purpose

This narrative workflow defines future information movement. It performs no
calculations, forecasts, allocations, or decisions.

```text
Carbon Accounting Records
  |
  v
Budget Definition
  |
  v
Budget Allocation
  |
  v
Performance Tracking
  |
  v
Variance Review
  |
  v
Verification Preparation
  |
  v
Disclosure Preparation
```

## 1. Carbon Accounting Records

Receive reviewed inventory records with exact boundary, period, method,
version, exclusions, uncertainty, evidence, and review status.

## 2. Budget Definition

Humans define the budget purpose, target, baseline, boundary, period, unit,
assumptions, owner, evidence, review requirements, and status.

## 3. Budget Allocation

Humans document any distribution of a parent target to facilities, projects,
products, or other approved boundaries. Methods and reconciliation remain
explicit.

## 4. Performance Tracking

Associate reviewed accounting records with the relevant budget period and
boundary. Incomplete and scenario records remain clearly labelled.

## 5. Variance Review

Future approved logic may prepare variance records. Reviewers examine target
and actual versions, boundary alignment, evidence, uncertainty, explanations,
and unresolved issues. Task53 performs no comparison calculation.

## 6. Verification Preparation

Prepare an evidence index, record lineage, validation findings, assumptions,
changes, and reviewer notes. Preparation is not independent assurance.

## 7. Disclosure Preparation

Prepare bounded summaries of targets, progress records, evidence,
uncertainty, status, and limitations. Human approval remains outside the agent.

## Review Loops

Any stage may return records when boundaries, periods, units, inventory
versions, evidence, targets, allocations, or assumptions conflict. Revisions
must preserve history.
