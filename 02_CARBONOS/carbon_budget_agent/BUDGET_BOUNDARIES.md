# Carbon Budget Boundaries

## Purpose

Every budget must declare what it covers. Boundaries must preserve their
relationship to the accounting records and evidence on which they rely.

## Organisational Boundary

**Definition:** the approved entities and operations included in an
organisation-wide budget.

**Purpose:** coordinates targets and allocations across the reporting entity.

**Limitations:** ownership, control, acquisitions, disposals, and accounting
methods may change.

**Review notes:** record boundary method, entities, exclusions, inventory
version, period, target owner, and changes.

## Facility Boundary

**Definition:** the physical and operational limits assigned to a facility
budget.

**Purpose:** supports site-level planning and review.

**Limitations:** shared services, tenants, mobile assets, and boundary changes
may affect comparability.

**Review notes:** confirm facilities, meters, activities, allocations,
accounting records, exclusions, and responsible owner.

## Project Boundary

**Definition:** the activities, assets, baseline, locations, and period
included in a project budget.

**Purpose:** separates project planning from routine operations.

**Limitations:** baseline choices, overlap, indirect effects, and design
changes can affect interpretation.

**Review notes:** document project owner, baseline, included activities,
interfaces, assumptions, changes, and accounting sources.

## Product Boundary

**Definition:** the lifecycle stages, functional unit, processes, geography,
and period included in a product budget.

**Purpose:** organises product-level targets and allocations.

**Limitations:** supplier data, allocation, product changes, and incomplete
lifecycle evidence may limit use.

**Review notes:** record product version, functional unit, lifecycle scope,
allocation method, sources, exclusions, and uncertainty.

## Scenario Boundary

**Definition:** the system, geography, time horizon, assumptions, and
hypothetical conditions included in a scenario budget.

**Purpose:** supports transparent exploration of planning choices.

**Limitations:** scenario budgets are not forecasts, commitments, approved
targets, or operational facts.

**Review notes:** label assumptions, baseline, horizon, evidence status,
uncertainty, owner, version, and prohibited interpretations.

## Boundary Control

Each future budget should carry a boundary identifier and version. Boundary
changes require traceable revisions and review of affected targets,
allocations, performance records, and comparisons.
