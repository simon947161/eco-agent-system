# Carbon Budget Governance Notes

## Purpose

Governance keeps future budgets transparent, traceable, reviewable, and
separate from unsupported decisions or claims.

## Transparency

Every budget should state boundary, period, baseline, target, unit, accounting
source, allocation method, assumptions, uncertainty, evidence, owner, status,
review notes, and limitations.

## Traceability

Targets, allocations, actual records, and variance explanations should link to
identified source inventories, decisions, plans, and supporting evidence.

## Human Oversight

Carbon Budget Agent provides planning support only. Final decisions remain
subject to human judgement.

Humans retain responsibility for target selection, allocation, resourcing,
corrective action, approval, claims, and real-world use.

## Evidence-Based Review

Reviewers should consider supporting and conflicting evidence. Missing data,
uncertain plans, incomplete inventories, and scenario assumptions must remain
visible.

## Auditability

Inputs, versions, changes, calculations added by future tasks, findings,
reviewer notes, and decisions should be reconstructable. Revisions must not
erase history.

## Prohibited Interpretations

The foundation is not a forecast, optimisation result, approved target,
regulatory plan, assurance opinion, certification, investment recommendation,
or automated compliance decision.

## Future Data Controls

Real data use requires separately approved privacy, confidentiality, access,
retention, security, licensing, cost, and source-authority controls.
