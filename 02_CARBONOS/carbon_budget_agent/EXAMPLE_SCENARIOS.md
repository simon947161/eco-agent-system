# Carbon Budget Example Scenarios

## Purpose

These examples show conceptual records only. They contain no calculations,
forecasts, optimisation, or approved decisions.

## Facility Carbon Budget

**Inputs:** facility boundary, reviewed inventory, budget period, human-defined
target, evidence, assumptions, owner, and review status.

**Conceptual outcome:** a versioned facility budget record linked to its
accounting basis and limitations.

## Multi-Facility Organisation

**Inputs:** organisational boundary, facility inventories, parent target,
human-defined allocations, periods, and evidence.

**Conceptual outcome:** distinct facility allocations remain linked to the
parent target. Missing or inconsistent facility records are flagged.

## Reduction Target Scenario

**Inputs:** scenario boundary, baseline inventory, proposed target, reduction
plan assumptions, horizon, and evidence status.

**Conceptual outcome:** a scenario-labelled budget for review. It is not a
forecast, commitment, or recommendation.

## Budget Variance Scenario

**Inputs:** target version and reviewed accounting records for the same
boundary and period.

**Conceptual outcome:** a future variance record structure with sources,
uncertainty, explanation, and review status. Task53 calculates no variance.

## Insufficient Evidence Scenario

**Inputs:** incomplete inventory, unclear boundary, unsupported target, or
missing allocation method.

**Conceptual outcome:** `Needs Review` planning status with explicit evidence
gaps and responsible owners. No target or result is invented.

## Limitations

Real budgets require approved methods, accounting records, evidence,
governance, resources, and qualified human decisions.
