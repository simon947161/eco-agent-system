# Carbon Budget Agent Purpose

## Purpose

The Carbon Budget Agent is intended to organise carbon budgets and related
evidence for human planning and review. It connects reviewed historical
accounting records with explicitly governed future targets and scenarios.

## Objectives

- define budget boundaries, periods, targets, allocations, and owners;
- preserve source inventory versions, uncertainty, exclusions, and status;
- distinguish observed records from targets, assumptions, and scenarios;
- organise performance and variance records without forecasting outcomes;
- expose missing evidence, inconsistent boundaries, and unresolved decisions;
- prepare human-readable records for verification and disclosure workflows.

## Expected Users

| User | Expected use |
| --- | --- |
| Facilities | Organise site-level budgets and review records |
| Organisations | Coordinate budgets across approved reporting boundaries |
| Carbon Managers | Prepare targets, allocations, evidence, and variance notes |
| ESG Teams | Review budget information for controlled disclosure preparation |
| Scenario Planning Teams | Compare explicitly labelled planning assumptions |
| Human Reviewers | Check evidence, boundaries, uncertainty, and limitations |

## Use Cases

- prepare a facility carbon budget for a declared period;
- allocate an organisational budget across multiple facilities;
- document a project or product budget under an explicit boundary;
- compare reviewed accounting records with a human-approved target;
- organise a reduction-target scenario without predicting achievement; and
- identify insufficient evidence or unresolved variance explanations.

## Limitations

Task53 implements no calculations, target-setting method, forecast,
optimisation, approval, compliance decision, or external connection.
