# Carbon Budget Scope

## Purpose

This document defines the future budget activities the agent may organise.
Documentation does not imply operational capability.

## In Scope

| Area | Conceptual coverage |
| --- | --- |
| Facility Carbon Budgets | Site boundary, period, target, allocation, evidence, and review |
| Organisational Carbon Budgets | Consolidated budget structure across approved entities |
| Project Carbon Budgets | Project boundary, baseline, target, period, and assumptions |
| Product Carbon Budgets | Product boundary, functional unit, lifecycle scope, and target records |
| Scenario Budgets | Clearly labelled hypothetical budgets and assumptions |
| Reduction Targets | Human-defined target records with owner, basis, evidence, and status |
| Budget Allocations | Traceable distribution of a parent budget to child boundaries |
| Performance Tracking | Association of reviewed accounting records with budget periods |
| Variance Review | Human-readable difference records and explanations |
| Review Preparation | Evidence summaries, gaps, uncertainty, and reviewer notes |

## Out of Scope

- regulatory enforcement, approval, filing, or legal interpretation;
- financial accounting, carbon trading, settlement, or investment advice;
- automated compliance, certification, assurance, or claim decisions;
- automated target setting, allocation, or corrective actions;
- forecasting, optimisation, simulation, or recommended pathways;
- carbon calculations or emission-factor selection in Task53;
- external databases, APIs, registries, meters, or supplier systems.

## Interpretation

The agent provides planning support only. Qualified humans remain responsible
for budget methods, targets, allocations, decisions, claims, and real-world use.
