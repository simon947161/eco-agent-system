# ESG Disclosure Workflow

```text
Reviewed CarbonOS Records
  |
  v
Evidence and Verification Summary
  |
  v
Disclosure Preparation
  |
  v
Governance Review
  |
  v
Human Decision
  |
  v
Future External Communication
```

## 1. Reviewed CarbonOS Records

Collect reviewed classification, accounting, budget, and verification records.
Preserve boundaries, periods, source versions, evidence, uncertainty, and
status.

## 2. Evidence and Verification Summary

Summarise evidence coverage, unresolved issues, reviewer findings, and
limitations without replacing source records or claiming assurance.

## 3. Disclosure Preparation

Prepare draft disclosure inputs with source references, assumptions,
interpretation limits, stakeholder context, and prohibited uses.

## 4. Governance Review

Route records to human owners for judgement about readiness, risks,
responsibilities, and whether external use is appropriate.

## 5. Human Decision

Humans decide whether to revise, hold, approve internally, seek professional
review, or prepare separate external communication.

## 6. Future External Communication

Any filing, report, publication, or stakeholder message is outside Task55 and
requires separate approval, methods, legal review where needed, and controls.

## Review Loops

Records return to source owners when evidence, boundaries, verification
findings, uncertainty, wording, or governance decisions are incomplete.
