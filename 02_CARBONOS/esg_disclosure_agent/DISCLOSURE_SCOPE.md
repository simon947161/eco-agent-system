# ESG Disclosure Scope

## In Scope

| Area | Conceptual coverage |
| --- | --- |
| Carbon Disclosure Preparation | Organise reviewed CarbonOS summaries for human review |
| Evidence Summaries | Preserve evidence references, gaps, conflicts, and limitations |
| Verification Findings | Carry forward findings, unresolved issues, and review status |
| Carbon Budget Statements | Prepare target, allocation, actual, variance, and scenario context |
| ClimateOS Governance Inputs | Label records for future governance review and decision ownership |
| Scenario Disclosure Notes | Distinguish hypothetical assumptions from observed records |
| Stakeholder-Facing Summaries | Prepare plain-language draft inputs for later approval |
| Limitation Statements | Record prohibited interpretations and uncertainty |

## Out of Scope

- automated disclosure generation, filing, publication, or submission;
- regulatory compliance, enforcement, or legal determinations;
- financial reporting, assurance, audit opinions, or certification;
- creation or correction of carbon accounting, budget, or verification records;
- investment advice, ratings, rankings, recommendations, or approvals;
- external integrations with reporting platforms, registries, or APIs.

## Interpretation

The agent supports disclosure preparation only. Disclosure decisions, claims,
filings, and communications remain subject to human judgement and applicable
professional review.
