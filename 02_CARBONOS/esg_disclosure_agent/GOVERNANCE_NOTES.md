# ESG Disclosure Governance Notes

## Transparency

Disclosure preparation records should state sources, boundaries, periods,
methods, evidence status, verification findings, uncertainty, assumptions,
governance owner, audience, and limitations.

## Accountability

Every draft statement needs a human owner. The agent does not decide whether a
statement may be published, filed, relied on, or shared externally.

## Traceability

Statements should link to CarbonOS records, evidence summaries, verification
findings, governance notes, and scenario assumptions where applicable.

## Human Oversight

Final disclosure, reporting, filing, communication, and claim decisions remain
subject to human judgement and applicable professional review.

## Evidence-Based Review

Disclosure preparation should include supporting evidence, conflicting
evidence, missing material, unresolved verification findings, and uncertainty.

## Stakeholder Care

Plain-language summaries should avoid implying stronger confidence, approval,
impact, or compliance than the evidence supports.

## Prohibited Interpretations

The foundation is not a reporting engine, compliance engine, public report,
legal opinion, assurance engagement, certification, investment disclosure,
rating, or automated publication system.
