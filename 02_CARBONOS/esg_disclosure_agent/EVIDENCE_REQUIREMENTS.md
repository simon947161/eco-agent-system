# ESG Disclosure Evidence Requirements

| Category | Purpose | Limitations | Review notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CarbonOS Records | Provide classification, accounting, budget, and verification source material | Incomplete status, scenario labels, unresolved findings, or old versions | Preserve exact record IDs, versions, boundaries, periods, status, and limitations |
| Verification Summaries | Carry evidence findings, exceptions, and reviewer observations | Preparation is not professional assurance or certification | Identify reviewer, scope, evidence boundary, unresolved issues, and review date |
| Governance Records | Document decision owners, review boundaries, and approval status | Internal approval does not equal regulatory acceptance | Record owner, role, decision, date, conditions, and restrictions |
| Supporting Documentation | Provides policies, reports, plans, contracts, and context | May be self-reported, expired, confidential, or jurisdiction-specific | Record source, issuer, version, date, applicability, confidentiality, and limits |
| Scenario Records | Supports hypothetical disclosure context | Not forecasts, commitments, or observed results | Label assumptions, time horizon, baseline, uncertainty, and non-operational status |
| Stakeholder Context | Explains audience, purpose, and communication limits | Audiences may infer claims beyond evidence | Record intended use, prohibited interpretations, language risk, and review needs |

## Minimum Evidence Record

Each item should include an identifier, source, owner, version, period,
boundary, relationship to the statement, status, limitations, and reviewer
notes.

## Evidence Gaps

Unresolved evidence, conflicting findings, missing review, incomplete
governance approval, or uncertain stakeholder interpretation must remain
visible and prevent claims of readiness.
