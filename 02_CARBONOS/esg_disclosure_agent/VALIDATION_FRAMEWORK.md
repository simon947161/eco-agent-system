# ESG Disclosure Validation Framework

## Completeness

Check that draft disclosure records include source records, evidence summary,
verification findings, governance owner, stakeholder context, limitations, and
status.

## Consistency

Check that wording, quantities, boundaries, periods, statuses, versions, and
limitations match source CarbonOS records.

## Traceability

Confirm that each material statement traces to evidence, verification findings,
or explicitly labelled assumptions.

## Evidence Coverage

Compare the proposed disclosure subject with reviewed evidence and unresolved
gaps. Partial coverage must remain visible.

## Governance Readiness

Check whether a human decision owner and review path are identified. The check
does not approve disclosure.

## Human Review

Every external-facing statement requires human review. Professional, legal, or
regulatory review may be required outside the agent.

## Uncertainty Recording

Preserve estimates, scenario assumptions, unresolved verification findings,
method limits, confidence limits, and prohibited interpretations.

## Status Preservation

`Unknown`, `Needs Review`, incomplete, scenario, unreviewed, or unresolved
statuses from upstream agents must not be silently upgraded.

## Validation Boundary

Validation checks do not create a report, filing, compliance result, assurance
opinion, legal determination, or permission to publish.
