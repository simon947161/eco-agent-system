# ESG Disclosure Example Scenarios

## Reviewed Inventory Summary

**Inputs:** reviewed accounting inventory, verification summary, evidence
index, limitations, and governance owner.

**Conceptual outcome:** a draft disclosure-preparation record with traceable
sources and explicit human review status.

## Budget Progress Summary

**Inputs:** budget target, allocation, reviewed actual references, variance
notes, uncertainty, and verification findings.

**Conceptual outcome:** a preparation note distinguishing target, actual,
variance, assumption, and unresolved status. No progress claim is approved.

## Scenario Disclosure Note

**Inputs:** scenario assumptions, horizon, baseline, evidence status, and
uncertainty review.

**Conceptual outcome:** a clearly labelled scenario note that is not a forecast
or commitment.

## Incomplete Verification Scenario

**Inputs:** accounting records with unresolved verification observations.

**Conceptual outcome:** disclosure readiness remains blocked or `Needs Review`
until evidence gaps are resolved by humans.

## Stakeholder Plain-Language Summary

**Inputs:** reviewed source records, stakeholder context, limitations, and
governance notes.

**Conceptual outcome:** a plain-language draft for internal review only, with
prohibited interpretations and no publication authority.

## Limitations

Examples are conceptual. They do not create a report, assurance conclusion,
compliance result, rating, or public statement.
