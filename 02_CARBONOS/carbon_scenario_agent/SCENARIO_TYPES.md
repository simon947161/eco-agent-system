# Carbon Scenario Types

## Baseline Scenario

**Purpose:** describe a current or reference state used for comparison.

**Inputs:** reviewed accounting records, budget records, evidence summaries,
boundaries, periods, and assumptions.

**Outputs:** baseline scenario record, assumptions, evidence status, and review
notes.

**Limitations:** a baseline is not a permanent truth and may change with new
evidence or boundary revisions.

## Alternative Scenario

**Purpose:** explore a plausible changed state without selecting it as a plan.

**Inputs:** baseline record, changed assumptions, constraints, evidence,
uncertainty, and review owner.

**Outputs:** alternative scenario record and comparison notes.

**Limitations:** alternatives are possibilities, not recommendations.

## Target Scenario

**Purpose:** explore a state aligned with a human-defined target or budget.

**Inputs:** carbon budget targets, assumptions, possible actions, timing,
constraints, and evidence.

**Outputs:** target-aligned scenario record and gap notes.

**Limitations:** a target scenario does not prove feasibility or achievement.

## Risk Scenario

**Purpose:** explore carbon-related risks, constraints, or adverse conditions.

**Inputs:** risk assumptions, affected records, uncertainty, evidence gaps,
and review notes.

**Outputs:** risk scenario record and review considerations.

**Limitations:** risk scenarios are not predictions.

## Exploratory Scenario

**Purpose:** examine early-stage ideas where evidence may be incomplete.

**Inputs:** assumptions, unknowns, constraints, preliminary evidence, and
scenario boundary.

**Outputs:** exploratory scenario record and evidence needs.

**Limitations:** exploratory scenarios require clear uncertainty labels and
should not be used as operational plans.
