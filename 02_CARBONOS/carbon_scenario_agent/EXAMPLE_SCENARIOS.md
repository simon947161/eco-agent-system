# Carbon Scenario Example Scenarios

## Facility Solar Adoption Scenario

**Inputs:** facility boundary, current electricity records, possible on-site
solar assumption, constraints, evidence status, and review notes.

**Conceptual outcome:** a structured possibility for review, not a forecast or
recommendation.

## Green Electricity Purchase Scenario

**Inputs:** baseline electricity use, possible purchase pathway, certificate
assumptions, contract constraints, and uncertainty notes.

**Conceptual outcome:** a scenario record that preserves trading and evidence
questions for review.

## Carbon Budget Reduction Scenario

**Inputs:** budget target, accounting baseline, possible reduction assumptions,
period, and unresolved gaps.

**Conceptual outcome:** a target-alignment scenario for discussion, not proof
that the target can be met.

## Net-Zero Transition Scenario

**Inputs:** organisational boundary, long-term horizon, scenario assumptions,
budget context, verification findings, and uncertainty.

**Conceptual outcome:** a pathway-style scenario with explicit limits and no
claim of feasibility or approval.

## High-Uncertainty Scenario

**Inputs:** incomplete evidence, broad assumptions, unknown costs, unclear
technology timing, or unresolved boundaries.

**Conceptual outcome:** scenario status remains `Needs Review`, with evidence
needs and unknowns highlighted.

## Limitations

These scenarios are examples only. They contain no calculations, predictions,
optimisation, or recommendations.
