# Carbon Scenario Validation Framework

## Assumption Transparency

Check that assumptions are explicit, sourced where possible, versioned, owned,
and connected to scenario records.

## Evidence Availability

Check whether evidence exists for source records, assumptions, constraints,
and comparisons. Missing evidence remains visible.

## Boundary Consistency

Check whether accounting, budget, facility, organisation, product, and
scenario boundaries are compatible or clearly distinguished.

## Scenario Traceability

Confirm that scenario outcomes and comparison notes trace back to assumptions,
source records, and review notes.

## Human Review

Every scenario requires human review before use in planning, governance, or
disclosure preparation.

## Uncertainty Recording

Record unknowns, constraints, scenario limits, evidence gaps, and possible
misinterpretations.

## Validation Boundary

Validation checks do not approve scenarios, forecast outcomes, recommend
actions, or establish compliance.
