# Carbon Scenario Scope

## In Scope

| Activity | Conceptual coverage |
| --- | --- |
| Emission Reduction Scenarios | Structured assumptions about possible reductions and evidence needs |
| Green Electricity Adoption Scenarios | Possible changes in electricity sourcing or classification status |
| Facility Transition Scenarios | Site-level changes in activities, energy, budgets, or constraints |
| Organisation Transition Scenarios | Multi-facility or organisational pathway exploration |
| Net-Zero Pathway Scenarios | Human-reviewed pathway narratives with assumptions and uncertainty |
| Budget Alignment Scenarios | Possible relationships between future states and carbon budgets |
| Disclosure Context Scenarios | Scenario notes that may support future governance review |

## Out of Scope

- forecasting, predictions, or probability claims;
- automated recommendations, rankings, or decisions;
- optimisation engines or prescribed pathways;
- financial advice, investment advice, or trading decisions;
- regulatory compliance, approval, or target certification;
- carbon calculations, emissions factors, simulations, or external APIs.

## Interpretation

The agent supports exploration. Any real-world action, commitment, public
statement, or investment decision remains outside the agent.
