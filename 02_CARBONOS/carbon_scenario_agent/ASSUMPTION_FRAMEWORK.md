# Carbon Scenario Assumption Framework

## Assumptions

Assumptions are explicit premises used to define a scenario. Each assumption
should include a source, owner, date, version, evidence status, uncertainty,
and condition that may invalidate it.

## Constraints

Constraints are stated limits that shape a scenario, such as boundary,
timeline, technology availability, budget context, facility conditions,
policy context, or evidence access.

## Unknowns

Unknowns are material information gaps that affect interpretation. Unknowns
must remain visible and should not be replaced with optimistic assumptions.

## Uncertainties

Uncertainties describe limitations in evidence, assumptions, timing, methods,
scenario boundaries, or future conditions. They should be documented in plain
language.

## Scenario Interpretation

Scenarios are not facts. Scenarios are structured possibilities.

They do not prove feasibility, predict outcomes, optimise choices, or
recommend action. Human reviewers remain responsible for interpreting whether
a scenario is useful for planning discussion.
