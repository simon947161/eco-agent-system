# Carbon Scenario Agent Purpose

## Purpose

The Carbon Scenario Agent supports structured exploration of possible future
carbon states. It helps organise assumptions, constraints, uncertainties, and
comparisons for human review.

## Objectives

- define scenario boundaries, assumptions, constraints, and unknowns;
- preserve links to accounting, budget, verification, and disclosure records;
- distinguish baseline, alternative, target, risk, and exploratory scenarios;
- record uncertainty and evidence availability;
- prepare human-readable scenario comparisons; and
- support future ClimateOS and EcoEngine validation processes.

## Expected Users

| User | Expected use |
| --- | --- |
| Carbon Managers | Explore structured carbon planning possibilities |
| ESG Teams | Prepare scenario notes with evidence and uncertainty |
| Facilities | Review site-level transition assumptions |
| Organisations | Compare bounded transition options for human planning |
| Scenario Review Teams | Check assumptions, boundaries, and traceability |
| ClimateOS Processes | Use carbon-oriented scenario inputs when future systems exist |

## Use Cases

- compare a baseline with a green electricity adoption scenario;
- document a facility transition scenario;
- explore a carbon budget reduction scenario;
- prepare a net-zero pathway scenario for review; and
- flag a high-uncertainty scenario that needs more evidence.

## Limitations

Scenarios are not facts. Scenarios are structured possibilities. Task56 adds
no forecast, optimisation, recommendation, calculation, or external access.
