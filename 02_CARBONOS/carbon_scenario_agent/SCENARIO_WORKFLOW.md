# Carbon Scenario Workflow

```text
Accounting Records
  |
  v
Budget Records
  |
  v
Scenario Definition
  |
  v
Scenario Comparison
  |
  v
Validation Review
  |
  v
Governance Review
```

## 1. Accounting Records

Use reviewed accounting records as source context. Preserve exact versions,
boundaries, periods, uncertainty, and review status.

## 2. Budget Records

Use budget targets, allocations, assumptions, and scenario labels where they
exist. Do not treat incomplete budgets as approved targets.

## 3. Scenario Definition

Define scenario type, boundary, horizon, assumptions, constraints, unknowns,
evidence status, owner, and limitations.

## 4. Scenario Comparison

Prepare conceptual comparison notes between scenarios. Task56 performs no
calculation or ranking.

## 5. Validation Review

Review assumption transparency, evidence availability, boundary consistency,
traceability, uncertainty, and unresolved issues.

## 6. Governance Review

Humans review whether a scenario can be used for planning discussion,
disclosure preparation, or future ClimateOS coordination.

## Review Loops

Scenarios return for revision when assumptions, evidence, boundaries,
comparisons, or interpretation limits are unclear.
