# Carbon Scenario Agent Roadmap

## Phase 1: Foundation

**Status:** Completed by Task56.

**Expected outputs:** purpose, scope, scenario types, data model, workflow,
assumption framework, validation framework, governance, interfaces, examples,
roadmap, and CarbonOS scenario alignment.

## Phase 2: Scenario Records

**Status:** Not Started.

**Expected outputs:** controlled scenario schemas, assumption records, status
vocabulary, review notes, and human-readable scenario packs.

## Phase 3: Comparison Structures

**Status:** Not Started.

**Expected outputs:** deterministic comparison templates that do not rank,
recommend, forecast, or optimise.

## Phase 4: ClimateOS Scenario Integration

**Status:** Not Started.

**Expected outputs:** alignment with ScenarioOS, governance notes, and
cross-domain scenario boundaries.

## Phase 5: EcoEngine Validation Integration

**Status:** Not Started.

**Expected outputs:** evidence and validation handoffs for scenario
assumptions, unknowns, and uncertainty.

## Phase 6: Demonstration Packs

**Status:** Not Started.

**Expected outputs:** bounded examples that clearly label assumptions and
limitations.

## Next Step

CarbonOS Phase 2 may begin with controlled schemas or deterministic document
builders for selected foundation agents.
