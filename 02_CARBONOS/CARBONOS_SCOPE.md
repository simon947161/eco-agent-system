# CarbonOS Scope

## Purpose

CarbonOS provides a governed structure for organising carbon and green-power
information. Its outputs are intended to be traceable, reviewable, and
human-readable.

## In Scope

| Area | CarbonOS coverage |
| --- | --- |
| Electricity | Consumption periods, quantities, sources, boundaries, and evidence references |
| Energy Consumption | Energy-use records relevant to carbon accounting, with units and provenance |
| Green Certificates | Certificate identifiers, ownership, validity periods, matching, and review status |
| Green Electricity | Evidence-based classification and reporting records |
| Scope 1 | Direct-emission activity and emission records within a declared boundary |
| Scope 2 | Purchased-energy records, declared methods, factors, and green-power evidence |
| Scope 3 | Indirect value-chain records where boundaries, evidence, and uncertainty are explicit |
| Carbon Budgets | Baselines, periods, scope, targets, usage records, variance, and review notes |
| Product Carbon Information | Product boundaries, lifecycle evidence, versions, and verification references |
| Verification | Evidence checks, exceptions, uncertainty, reviewer notes, and status |
| Disclosure Support | Human-readable summaries that preserve evidence and limitations |

## Operating Boundaries

CarbonOS may organise source records, classifications, assumptions, factors,
calculations defined in future approved tasks, review evidence, and reports.
Every future implementation must declare its reporting boundary, method,
units, evidence requirements, uncertainties, and human review point.

## Out of Scope

- Financial trading, brokerage, settlement, wallets, or market transactions
- Regulatory approvals, permits, official filings, or regulator decisions
- Automated compliance, certification, assurance, or eligibility decisions
- Legal, financial, tax, investment, or professional assurance advice
- Unreviewed claims that electricity, products, facilities, or organisations
  are green, carbon neutral, net zero, compliant, or certified
- External API and data-source integrations unless separately approved
- Scientific calculations or emission-factor selection in this foundation task
- Autonomous recommendations, rankings, approvals, or operational control

## Interpretation

Documentation of an area does not mean CarbonOS can currently calculate,
verify, certify, or operate it. Task50 establishes definitions and governance
boundaries only.
