# CarbonOS Baseline Release

## Baseline Name

CarbonOS v0.5

## Purpose

CarbonOS v0.5 documents the first complete foundation sequence for the
CarbonOS domain system. It is a documentation baseline, not an operational
release.

## Current Maturity

CarbonOS is at `Foundation Established` maturity for its five core conceptual
agents. The foundation includes scope, conceptual data models, workflows,
evidence requirements, validation frameworks, governance notes, interfaces,
example scenarios, roadmaps, and alignment documents.

## Future Roadmap

- CarbonOS Phase 2: controlled schemas and deterministic document builders
- CarbonOS Phase 3: validation and governance integration
- CarbonOS Phase 4: scenario integration and demonstration packs
- CarbonOS Phase 5: reviewed disclosure-preparation workflows
- Future product carbon passport work after separate task approval

## Known Limitations

- No calculations, emission factors, forecasting, optimisation, or simulations
  are implemented.
- No reporting engine, compliance engine, filing system, or publication
  workflow exists.
- No APIs, external databases, registries, meters, dashboards, or LLM runtime
  integrations are connected.
- No professional assurance, certification, regulatory approval, legal
  determination, ranking, recommendation, or automated decision is provided.
- ClimateOS and EcoEngine relationships are conceptual alignment documents
  only.

## Baseline Interpretation

CarbonOS v0.5 means the repository has a reviewable foundation for future
agent development. It does not mean CarbonOS is operational.
