# CarbonOS

## Purpose

CarbonOS is the ClimateOS subsystem for organising traceable carbon and green
power information. Its foundation defines a shared structure for future
accounting, budgeting, verification, and product carbon passport agents.

## Scope

The documented scope includes electricity and energy-consumption records,
green electricity and certificate evidence, Scope 1, Scope 2, and Scope 3
information, carbon budgets, emission records, verification records, and
product carbon information.

Detailed boundaries are defined in [CarbonOS Scope](CARBONOS_SCOPE.md).

## Subsystem Relationship

CarbonOS follows the repository-wide Agent Standard and is intended to reuse
future ClimateOS Core evidence, validation, governance, ontology, and
interface conventions. ScenarioOS may organise bounded scenarios,
ValidationOS may support review workflows, and GovernanceOS may record human
decisions. CarbonOS remains responsible for its domain definitions and
human-readable carbon records.

agents have been implemented.
