# Green Power Classification Evidence Requirements

## Purpose

Classification proposals must be connected to evidence that a human reviewer
can identify, interpret, and challenge. Evidence quality depends on context;
the categories below do not establish legal sufficiency.

## Meter Data

**Purpose:** supports the quantity, timing, location, import, export, or direct
consumption of electricity.

**Potential limitations:** missing intervals, estimated readings, meter
configuration errors, boundary mismatches, aggregation, or unavailable
calibration records.

**Review notes:** confirm meter identity, facility relationship, units,
coverage period, completeness, estimates, corrections, and source system.

## Contracts

**Purpose:** describes delivery arrangements, parties, periods, products,
attribute rights, and responsibilities.

**Potential limitations:** ambiguous terms, amendments, side agreements,
expired periods, missing signatures, or provisions that do not establish
attribute ownership.

**Review notes:** identify the executed version, parties, effective dates,
electricity product, attribute language, quantities, geography, and conflicts.

## Certificates

**Purpose:** supports generation attributes, ownership, transfer, and
retirement or cancellation records.

**Potential limitations:** expired or duplicate records, unmatched periods or
geographies, unclear ownership, partial quantities, scheme differences, or
unverified registry status.

**Review notes:** check unique identifiers, scheme, generation period,
technology, quantity, owner, transfer history, retirement status, matching,
and double-counting risk.

## Transaction Records

**Purpose:** documents purchases, transfers, allocations, retirements, or
other events relevant to electricity attributes.

**Potential limitations:** incomplete counterparties, inconsistent quantities,
missing links to contracts or certificates, reversals, or unclear settlement
status.

**Review notes:** reconcile parties, dates, quantities, units, linked
instruments, transaction type, status, and evidence authority.

## Internal Records

**Purpose:** documents organisational boundaries, policies, allocation
methods, approvals, reconciliations, and operational context.

**Potential limitations:** self-reported content, uncontrolled versions,
undocumented assumptions, inconsistent methods, or insufficient independence.

**Review notes:** record owner, approval, version, methodology, source inputs,
reconciliation, change history, and reviewer independence.

## External References

**Purpose:** provides contextual definitions, scheme rules, published methods,
supplier information, or other references used during review.

**Potential limitations:** changing rules, inaccessible sources, licensing,
jurisdiction differences, secondary summaries, or references outside their
effective period.

**Review notes:** preserve title, publisher, version, publication and access
dates, jurisdiction, applicable period, source location, and interpretation
limits.

## Minimum Evidence Record

Every evidence item should record an identifier, category, title, issuer or
source, owner, relevant period, version, relationship to the consumption
record, review status, limitations, and access or retention notes.

Missing evidence must produce an explicit gap. The future agent must not
invent, infer, or retrieve evidence without an approved capability and clear
provenance.
