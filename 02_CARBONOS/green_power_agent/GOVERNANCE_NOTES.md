# Green Power Classification Governance Notes

## Purpose

Governance protects evidence integrity, human authority, and honest
interpretation of classification outputs.

## Transparency

Every proposed result should state its boundary, period, quantity, unit,
classification pathway, method version, evidence, assumptions, uncertainty,
validation status, reviewer status, and limitations.

## Auditability

Inputs, evidence references, proposed results, validation findings, reviewer
notes, and revisions should be versioned and reconstructable. Corrections
should not erase earlier records.

## Traceability

Material statements should link to identifiable meter, contract, certificate,
transaction, internal, or external evidence. Derived interpretations should
remain distinguishable from source facts.

## Human Oversight

The agent provides classification support only. Final decisions remain
subject to human review.

Human reviewers retain responsibility for applicable methods, standards,
legal interpretations, claim approval, and real-world use.

## Evidence-Based Review

Reviewers should examine both supporting and conflicting evidence. Missing
evidence, uncertainty, and unresolved questions must be recorded rather than
replaced with optimistic assumptions.

## Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence Owner | Supplies records and source context |
| Preparer | Organises inputs and proposes a classification |
| Reviewer | Checks evidence, validation findings, uncertainty, and limits |
| Decision Owner | Accepts or rejects any organisational claim outside the agent |
| Repository Maintainer | Controls versions, task scope, tests, and documentation |

## Prohibited Interpretations

A result must not be presented as:

- regulatory approval or legal advice;
- certificate authentication or professional assurance;
- proof of carbon neutrality, net zero, or zero emissions;
- permission to make a public environmental claim;
- a financial trading, purchasing, or investment recommendation; or
- an autonomous compliance or operational decision.

## Data and Access

Future implementation must address privacy, commercial confidentiality,
retention, access control, licensing, and source-system authority before
handling real organisational records or external systems.
