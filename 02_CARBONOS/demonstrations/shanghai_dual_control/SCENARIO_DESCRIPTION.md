# Scenario Description

## Demonstration Name

Shanghai Carbon Control Demonstration

## Scenario Description

This demonstration describes a fictional manufacturing organisation operating
a single facility in Shanghai. The facility is used to explore how CarbonOS
foundation documents could organise a carbon dual-control style review.

The scenario is conceptual and policy-informed, but it does not interpret
Shanghai rules or determine compliance.

## Organisation

The fictional organisation is `Shanghai Example Manufacturing Group`. It has
one demonstration facility and an internal ESG team.

## Facility

The fictional facility is `Shanghai Example Facility`. It has electricity use,
general energy consumption, operational activity records, and internal carbon
management responsibilities.

## Electricity Use

The facility may use grid electricity, possible green-power purchases, and
supporting certificate or contract records. These records are inputs for
conceptual Green Power Classification review.

## Carbon Management Context

The scenario considers two management themes:

- carbon emission control through accounting and budget review; and
- carbon intensity control through documentation of activity, output context,
  and uncertainty.

No intensity values or emissions are calculated.

## Reporting Context

The fictional ESG team prepares internal disclosure material for governance
review. External reporting, regulatory filing, public claims, and compliance
determinations remain outside this demonstration.
