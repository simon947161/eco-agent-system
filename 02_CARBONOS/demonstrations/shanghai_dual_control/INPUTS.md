# Inputs

## Purpose

This document identifies possible conceptual inputs for the Shanghai Carbon
Control Demonstration. Inputs are illustrative and contain no real company
data.

| Input | Purpose | Source | Limitations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Electricity Records | Describe facility electricity consumption by period and boundary | Fictional meter or invoice records | May be incomplete, estimated, or misaligned with reporting periods |
| Energy Consumption Records | Describe broader energy use, such as fuel or heat | Fictional internal operations records | Units, boundaries, and activity links require review |
| Green Power Records | Support potential physical, trading, allocation, unknown, or needs-review classification | Fictional contracts, certificates, or source descriptions | Does not prove green status or compliance |
| Carbon Accounting Records | Organise activity and future emission-record context | Fictional accounting preparation records | No emissions are calculated in this demonstration |
| Carbon Budgets | Provide target, period, allocation, and variance-review context | Fictional internal planning records | Targets are not approved regulatory or operational decisions |
| Verification Records | Record evidence, traceability, uncertainty, and review findings | Fictional reviewer notes and evidence indexes | Verification preparation is not professional assurance |
| Disclosure Preparation Records | Organise internal ESG summary material | Fictional ESG team draft records | No external disclosure or filing is generated |
| Scenario Assumptions | Support alternative or target scenario exploration | Fictional planning assumptions | Scenarios are structured possibilities, not predictions |

## Input Review Needs

Inputs should preserve source, owner, boundary, period, version, uncertainty,
and review status. Missing evidence should be recorded rather than filled with
invented values.
