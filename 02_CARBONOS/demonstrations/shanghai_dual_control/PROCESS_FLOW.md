# Process Flow

## Conceptual Workflow

```text
Inputs
  |
  v

  |
  v

  |
  v
Budget
  |
  v
Verification
  |
  v
Disclosure
  |
  v
Scenario Review
```

## Inputs

Collect fictional electricity, energy, green-power, accounting, budget,
verification, disclosure, and scenario assumption records.

## Classification

Apply the Green Power Classification foundation conceptually to identify which
electricity records need physical, trading, allocation, unknown, or needs
review labels.

## Accounting

Organise energy and activity records into accounting-preparation records with
boundaries, periods, evidence, and uncertainty.

## Budget

Connect accounting-preparation records with fictional budget context, target
records, allocation notes, and variance-review needs.

## Verification

Review evidence quality, traceability, boundaries, documentation, uncertainty,
and unresolved issues.

## Disclosure

Prepare internal ESG disclosure notes with source status, limitations, and
governance review requirements.

## Scenario Review

Explore structured possibilities, such as green electricity adoption or budget
alignment scenarios, without forecasting or recommendations.

## Framework Use
