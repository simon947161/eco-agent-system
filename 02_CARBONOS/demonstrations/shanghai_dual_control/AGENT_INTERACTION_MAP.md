# Agent Interaction Map

## Conceptual Flow

```text
Green Power Classification
  |
  v
Carbon Accounting
  |
  v
Carbon Budget
  |
  v
Carbon Verification
  |
  v
ESG Disclosure
  |
  v
Carbon Scenario
```

## Green Power Classification

Reviews electricity evidence and proposes physical, trading, allocation,
unknown, or needs-review status. It does not certify green electricity.

## Carbon Accounting

Organises activity, energy, and classification records into future accounting
structures. It performs no calculations in this demonstration.

## Carbon Budget

Organises fictional budget periods, targets, allocations, and variance-review
context. It does not set targets automatically or forecast performance.

## Carbon Verification

Prepares evidence, boundary, traceability, uncertainty, and documentation
review records. It does not provide professional assurance.

## ESG Disclosure

Prepares internal disclosure inputs from reviewed records. It does not create
reports, filings, compliance findings, or public statements.

## Carbon Scenario

Organises structured scenario possibilities based on reviewed records and
assumptions. It does not predict, optimise, rank, or recommend.

## Framework Use

The map supports the CarbonOS agent section of the
