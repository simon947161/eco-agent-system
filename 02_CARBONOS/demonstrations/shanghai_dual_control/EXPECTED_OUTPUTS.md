# Expected Outputs

## Classification Summary

A human-readable summary of potential green-power classification status,
evidence used, evidence gaps, uncertainty, and review needs.

## Accounting Summary

A conceptual accounting-preparation summary that organises fictional energy
and activity records by boundary and period. No emissions are calculated.

## Budget Summary

A conceptual budget-preparation summary describing fictional target,
allocation, period, variance-review, and uncertainty context.

## Verification Summary

A verification-preparation summary listing evidence coverage, traceability,
boundary checks, uncertainty, findings, and unresolved issues.

## Disclosure Summary

An internal disclosure-preparation note carrying source status, verification
findings, governance owner, stakeholder context, and limitations.

## Scenario Summary

A structured scenario note describing assumptions, constraints, unknowns,
comparison boundaries, and governance review needs.

## Framework Use

These expected outputs follow the reusable
[Demonstration Evaluation Framework](../DEMONSTRATION_EVALUATION_FRAMEWORK.md).

## Limits

Expected outputs are document concepts only. They are not generated reports,
calculations, compliance findings, forecasts, recommendations, or filings.
