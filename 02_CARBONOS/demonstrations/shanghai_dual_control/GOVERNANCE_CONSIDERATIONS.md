# Governance Considerations

## Transparency

The demonstration should clearly label fictional data, conceptual inputs,
assumptions, limitations, source status, and unresolved review issues.

## Accountability

Each conceptual output should identify a fictional record owner or review role,
such as facility owner, ESG preparer, carbon manager, or governance reviewer.

## Review Processes

Human review remains required before any classification, accounting, budget,
verification, disclosure, or scenario material could be used outside a
demonstration context.

## Governance Risks

Potential risks include over-reading fictional records, treating scenarios as
forecasts, implying compliance, or presenting disclosure-preparation notes as
public reports.

## Documentation Quality

Documents should preserve boundaries, periods, source references, versions,
assumptions, uncertainty, review notes, and prohibited interpretations.

## Framework Use

These considerations satisfy the governance section of the
[Demonstration Evaluation Framework](../DEMONSTRATION_EVALUATION_FRAMEWORK.md).

## Limits

The demonstration provides no regulatory interpretation, compliance result,
public disclosure, certification, or professional assurance.
