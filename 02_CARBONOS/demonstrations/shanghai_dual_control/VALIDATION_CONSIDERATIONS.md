# Validation Considerations

## Evidence Quality

Review whether fictional electricity, energy, contract, certificate,
accounting, budget, and disclosure records have identifiable sources,
versions, owners, and periods.

## Uncertainty

Record estimates, incomplete records, conflicting sources, unclear
classification status, unsupported assumptions, and scenario limitations.

## Boundary Consistency

Check whether organisation, facility, accounting, budget, disclosure, and
scenario boundaries are compatible or clearly distinguished.

## Traceability

Confirm that every conceptual summary can be traced back to input records,
agent foundation concepts, evidence notes, or assumptions.

## Review Requirements

Human reviewers should check all material classifications, accounting
structures, budget statements, verification findings, disclosure notes, and
scenario assumptions.

## Framework Use

These considerations satisfy the validation section of the
[Demonstration Evaluation Framework](../DEMONSTRATION_EVALUATION_FRAMEWORK.md).

## Limits

Validation in this demonstration does not approve records, determine
compliance, verify policy alignment, or provide assurance.
