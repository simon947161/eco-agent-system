# Shanghai Demonstration Roadmap

## Scenario Expansion

Future work may add additional fictional facility types, energy profiles,
green-power evidence pathways, budget contexts, or scenario assumptions.

## Validation Integration

Future work may add controlled validation checklists, evidence coverage
tables, uncertainty registers, and review status templates.

## ClimateOS Integration

Future work may show how Shanghai-style carbon control scenarios could connect
conceptually with ClimateOS governance, energy, scenario, and validation
layers.

## EcoEngine Integration

Future work may define evidence and validation handoffs for a future EcoEngine
runtime after separate approval. No runtime integration exists now.

## Demonstration Comparison

Task58 and Task59 may reuse the
[Demonstration Evaluation Framework](../DEMONSTRATION_EVALUATION_FRAMEWORK.md)
to compare demonstration structures across different contexts.

## Limits

Roadmap items are future possibilities only. They do not create calculations,
forecasting, compliance decisions, policy interpretations, APIs, or runtime
functionality.
