# CarbonOS Workflow

## Purpose

This document describes the intended human-review workflow for future
CarbonOS records. It does not define an executable workflow.

## Reference Flow

```text
Energy Data
  |
  v
Green Power Classification
  |
  v
Carbon Accounting
  |
  v
Carbon Budget Review
  |
  v
Verification
  |
  v
Disclosure
```

## 1. Energy Data

An organisation or facility assembles electricity and other relevant energy
records for a declared period and boundary. Each record should retain its
source, unit, owner, collection date, and data-quality notes.

Missing, conflicting, or estimated data should remain visible rather than
being silently replaced.

## 2. Green Power Classification

Electricity records are reviewed against the declared classification method
and supporting evidence, such as source information or green certificates.
The output records the matched quantity, basis, period, uncertainty, and
review status.

A classification is not a certificate, regulatory approval, or automatic
claim of environmental performance.

## 3. Carbon Accounting

Reviewed activity records are organised by reporting boundary, scope,
category, method, factor reference, and period. Future approved agents may
prepare emission records, but Task50 defines no calculations.

Accounting outputs must preserve source references and method versions so a
reviewer can reconstruct how a record was prepared.

## 4. Carbon Budget Review

Relevant emission records are compared with a documented carbon budget,
baseline, or target. Review notes should explain the period, scope, variance,
assumptions, uncertainty, and any incomplete evidence.

The review supports human interpretation and does not approve corrective
actions or make operational recommendations.

## 5. Verification

A reviewer checks evidence completeness, boundary consistency, method
references, assumptions, uncertainty, and unresolved exceptions. Findings are
recorded separately from source data.

Verification status must distinguish reviewed, conditionally reviewed,
rejected, and unresolved records when future tasks define the controlled
status vocabulary.

## 6. Disclosure

Human-readable outputs summarise the declared boundary, period, methods,
results, evidence references, review status, limitations, and unresolved
issues. Product carbon passport information may be assembled only when its
product and lifecycle boundaries are explicit.

Disclosure support does not constitute legal filing, certification,
professional assurance, or regulatory acceptance.

## Review Loops

Any stage may return a record to an earlier stage when:

- evidence is missing or expired;
- units or reporting periods conflict;
- a boundary or method changes;
- uncertainty is not adequately described;
- a reviewer identifies an exception; or
- a disclosure claim exceeds the reviewed evidence.

Corrections should create a traceable new version rather than erase review
history.
