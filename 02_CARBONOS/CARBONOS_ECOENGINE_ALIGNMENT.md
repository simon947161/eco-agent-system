# CarbonOS EcoEngine Alignment

## Purpose

This document defines the conceptual relationship between CarbonOS and
EcoEngine. It adds no runtime integration.

```text
CarbonOS
  |
  v
EcoEngine
```

## Evidence Records to Evidence Layer

**Purpose:** align CarbonOS source evidence with future runtime evidence
review concepts.

**Inputs:** evidence IDs, sources, owners, versions, periods, boundaries,
limitations, and access notes.

**Outputs:** evidence indexes, coverage notes, gaps, conflicts, and status.

**Review considerations:** provenance, licensing, confidentiality, source
authority, retention, completeness, and missing records.

## Verification Records to Validation Layer

**Purpose:** align CarbonOS review findings with future validation checks.

**Inputs:** verification records, traceability records, reviewer observations,
uncertainty records, and unresolved issues.

**Outputs:** validation findings, status preservation, and review requirements.

**Review considerations:** deterministic checks cannot replace professional
judgement, assurance, or certification.

## Budget Scenarios to Scenario Layer

**Purpose:** align carbon budget assumptions and scenarios with future
scenario-evaluation concepts.

**Inputs:** scenario boundaries, targets, assumptions, time horizons, evidence
status, and uncertainty notes.

**Outputs:** scenario review packs, comparison limits, and non-forecast labels.

**Review considerations:** observed data and hypothetical assumptions must
remain separate.

## Disclosure Outputs to Governance Layer

**Purpose:** align disclosure preparation records with future governance
support concepts.

**Inputs:** disclosure records, governance owners, stakeholder context,
limitations, verification summaries, and unresolved issues.

**Outputs:** governance review notes and human decision boundaries.

**Review considerations:** no automated publication, compliance decision,
legal determination, or stakeholder claim is created.

## Alignment Limits

EcoEngine is treated as an Evidence and Validation Runtime concept. Task55
does not connect to or implement any runtime, model, API, LLM, workflow engine,
or decision-support system.
