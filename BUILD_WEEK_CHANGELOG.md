## Foundation that existed before Build Week

ClimateOS grew from environmental practice and the Eco Agent System lineage:

`EcoAgent → EcoChain → ClaimOS → Eco Decision DNA → CCZPS-Lite → Evidence
Passport and traceability → scientific/multi-model and Environmental Coupling
architecture → human–AI runtime`

Before the eligibility window, the repository already contained substantial
work on deterministic local-first environmental decision support, evidence and
claim structures, model/resource registries, scientific architecture,
provenance, review, governance, stewardship and long-horizon human–AI
collaboration. That foundation is important, but it is not presented as new
Build Week work.

## Work added during Build Week

After the eligibility boundary, the project was meaningfully extended through
repository-backed work on model-neutral scientific-resource discovery,
multi-model comparison, environmental coupling, evidence admission,
reproducibility, permission and execution boundaries, audit receipts, output
quarantine and human review. Some of this work is executable and tested; some
is intentionally a contract or research architecture. The repository records
the distinction.
