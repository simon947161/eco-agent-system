# Conversation & Policy Radar

Purpose: Build a searchable memory, insight and external-policy signal index across ChatGPT, Codex, DeepSeek, GitHub and ClimateOS work.

## Vision
Humans remember moments, not entire conversations. Radar captures key ideas, reasoning, goals, policy signals and next actions so they can be rediscovered later and connected to real projects.

## Core Objects
- Knowledge Points
- Idea Points
- Wish / Intent Points
- Reasoning Points
- Key Decisions
- Unresolved Questions
- Next Actions
- Keywords
- Related Projects
- External Policy Signals
- Institutional / Regulatory Implications

## Radar Layers

### Conversation Radar
Captures internal project reasoning and CRP harvest points across conversations and execution systems.

### ClimateOS Policy Radar
Tracks external policy and governance signals that may affect ClimateOS, ECO Chain, environmental PPP models, water / land resilience and local-government operations.

Current policy tracks include:
- Land–Water–Drought–Resilience Governance, anchored initially by UNCCD COP17.
- Extreme-Event Environmental Compliance Resilience, beginning with Flood × Backflow × LTW × Sewer × Public Health.

See `CLIMATEOS_POLICY_RADAR.md`.

## Operating Principle
Radar entries are signals, not automatic product requirements.

Use:

Signal -> verify -> understand institutional / operational need -> identify evidence requirement -> test workflow -> only then consider building.

This follows the project principle: **USE BEFORE BUILD**.

## Example Query
'Find the discussion about Radar Layer and Task Schedule from June 2026.'

'What policy signals connect drought finance, national reporting and ClimateOS evidence architecture?'

## Long-term Goal
Create a semantic memory and policy-intelligence system that can retrieve important project conversations and external governance signals by concept rather than exact wording, while preserving the distinction between research, policy observation and authorised product scope.
