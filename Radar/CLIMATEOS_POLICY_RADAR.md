# ClimateOS Policy Radar

Purpose: maintain a strategic policy signal layer for ClimateOS so that emerging international, national, state and local governance changes can be connected to real-world environmental operations, evidence, finance and future product decisions.

## Operating Principle

This radar is not a news archive and not an automatic product backlog.

Use the sequence:

Policy signal -> institutional requirement -> operational implication -> evidence requirement -> financing / reporting consequence -> possible ClimateOS response.

Apply USE BEFORE BUILD: observe and verify the real policy or operational need before creating new software functions.

## Track 01 — Land–Water–Drought–Resilience Governance

### Anchor signal
UNCCD COP17, Ulaanbaatar, 2026.

### Current negotiation themes to monitor
- Drought
- Agriculture
- Land tenure
- Migration
- Finance
- Private-sector engagement
- National reporting
- Synergies among the Rio Conventions
- Future Strategic Framework

### Why this matters to ClimateOS
This track may create a governance bridge linking climate risk, land, water, agriculture, infrastructure, private investment and national reporting.

Potential architecture chain:

Climate / drought signal
-> regional land and water stress
-> infrastructure and community exposure
-> intervention / PPP project
-> monitoring and evidence
-> ECO Chain / Evidence Passport traceability
-> finance, reporting and institutional recognition.

### Strategic hypothesis
The future competitive unit may not be a single environmental engineering or consulting service. It may be the ability to connect climate context, physical interventions, operational evidence, accountability and financing into a continuous governance system.

This is a hypothesis to test, not a claim of policy alignment.

### Watch items
1. Final COP17 decision text on drought.
2. Final wording on private-sector engagement.
3. Finance mechanisms for land restoration, drought resilience and adaptation.
4. National reporting obligations, indicators and data requirements.
5. Links between UNCCD reporting and wider climate / biodiversity reporting systems.
6. Australian implementation pathways and any implications for NSW or local government.

### Opportunity questions
- What must governments and project operators measure?
- What evidence must be retained over time?
- Can intervention outcomes be traced from local action to national / international reporting?
- Can ClimateOS supply decision context without replacing statutory or professional judgment?
- Can ECO Chain provide traceability for PPP and environmental interventions?
- What information is required before finance can recognise a resilience outcome?

## Track 02 — Extreme-Event Environmental Compliance Resilience

### Core question
What happens to environmental compliance systems when normal operating assumptions are exceeded by an extreme event?

### Initial focus
Flood x Backflow Prevention x Liquid Trade Waste x Sewer x Stormwater x Public Health.

### Working concept
Environmental Compliance Resilience under Extreme Conditions.

### Failure-chain hypothesis
Extreme rainfall / flood
-> system boundaries are exceeded
-> sewer surcharge / overflow and inundation
-> LTW equipment, waste or chemical storage exposed
-> contaminants and pathogens mobilised
-> potable-water / backflow protection conditions may change
-> multiple regulatory and operational domains interact
-> cascading environmental and public-health risk.

### Research boundary
Do not assume existing controls become useless during floods. Determine their design limits, failure modes, residual protective value and emergency-management interfaces.

### Key questions
- Which normal compliance assumptions fail first?
- Which assets, premises and discharge types become high priority?
- How do backflow, LTW, sewer overflow, stormwater, waste and public-health responsibilities interface?
- What pre-event inspection or warning actions are already required?
- What evidence should be captured during and after an incident?
- Could ClimateOS convert an approaching hazard into a prioritised operational inspection / action list?

### Long-term ClimateOS bridge
ClimateOS: What extreme condition is approaching?

WorkOS: Which local assets, premises and controls are vulnerable?

Operational team: What should be inspected, isolated, warned, prioritised or restored?

ECO Chain / Evidence layer: What happened, what action was taken, what evidence supports the result?

## Governance

Status: WATCH / RESEARCH, not authorised product scope.

Promotion into product work requires:
1. source-backed policy or operational need;
2. clear user / operator workflow;
3. identified evidence requirement;
4. proof that existing tools do not already solve the problem adequately;
