# RWA Tokenization Bridge Notes

## Purpose

This note records how future RWA alignment may depend on high-quality RDA.

## Principle

ClimateOS distinguishes Generic RWA from Earth-System Related RDA.

RDA quality, validation, traceability, and evidence asset formation should come
before any future RWA alignment discussion.

## Boundary

No tokenization, token model, blockchain implementation, financial product, or
asset valuation model is implemented.
