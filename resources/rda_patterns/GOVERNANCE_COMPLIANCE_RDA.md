# Governance Compliance RDA

## Pattern

Governance and compliance systems generate records about approvals, audits,
consultation, inspections, reviews, policies, and decisions.

## RDA Relevance

This pattern can support Governance Process RDA and Proof of Reality.

## Example Records

- approval record
- audit finding
- compliance review
- inspection record
- public consultation record
- meeting record
- governance decision

## Boundary

ClimateOS does not implement compliance automation in this task.
