# ERP Supply Chain Process RDA

## Pattern

ERP and supply chain systems generate procurement, production, inventory,
delivery, maintenance, and resource consumption records.

## RDA Relevance

This pattern can support Operational Process RDA, Product / Material RDA, and
Carbon and ESG RDA.

## Example Records

- purchase order
- supplier record
- production batch
- inventory movement
- delivery record
- resource consumption

## Boundary

ClimateOS does not integrate ERP systems in this task.
