# Carbon Energy RDA

## Pattern

Carbon and energy systems generate data about generation, demand, emissions,
storage, constraints, resource use, and carbon budgets.

## RDA Relevance

This pattern can support Energy RDA, Carbon and ESG RDA, and future CarbonOS
alignment.

## Example Records

- electricity generation
- demand profile
- storage operation
- grid constraint
- emissions record
- carbon budget input

## Boundary

ClimateOS does not implement carbon or energy calculations in this task.
