# Digital Product Passport RDA

## Pattern

Digital Product Passports describe product identity, origin, composition,
environmental footprint, repair, reuse, and recycling information.

## RDA Relevance

This pattern can support Product / Material RDA and circular economy evidence.

## Example Records

- product identity
- material composition
- origin
- environmental footprint
- repair record
- reuse record
- recycling pathway

## Boundary

ClimateOS does not implement Digital Product Passport functionality in this
task.
