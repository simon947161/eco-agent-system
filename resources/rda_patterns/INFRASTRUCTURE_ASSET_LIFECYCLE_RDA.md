# Infrastructure Asset Lifecycle RDA

## Pattern

Infrastructure systems generate data across planning, design, construction,
inspection, operation, maintenance, failure, renewal, and retirement.

## RDA Relevance

This pattern can support Infrastructure RDA and Operational Process RDA.

## Example Records

- asset identity
- design record
- construction record
- inspection record
- maintenance history
- performance record
- failure risk note

## Boundary

ClimateOS does not implement infrastructure asset management in this task.
