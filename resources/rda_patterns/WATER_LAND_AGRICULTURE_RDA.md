# Water Land Agriculture RDA

## Pattern

Water, land, and agriculture systems generate observations and process records
about rainfall, soil, crops, grazing, irrigation, land-use change, and
ecological recovery.

## RDA Relevance

This pattern can support Natural System RDA, Water RDA, and Land and
Agriculture RDA.

## Example Records

- rainfall record
- soil moisture
- streamflow
- crop condition
- grazing pressure
- erosion risk
- ecological recovery note

## Boundary

ClimateOS does not implement water, land, or agriculture runtime in this task.
