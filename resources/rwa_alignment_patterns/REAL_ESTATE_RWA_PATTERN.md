# Real Estate RWA Pattern

## Pattern

Real estate RWA discussions often depend on identity, ownership, location,
valuation, legal status, condition, and governance records.

## ClimateOS Relevance

ClimateOS may only consider real estate-adjacent alignment where land,
infrastructure, resilience, environmental performance, or governance evidence is
traceable.

## Boundary

ClimateOS does not implement real estate tokenization, valuation, or trading.
