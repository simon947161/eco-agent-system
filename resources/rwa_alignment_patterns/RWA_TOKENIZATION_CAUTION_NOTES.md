# RWA Tokenization Caution Notes

## Purpose

These notes prevent ClimateOS RWA Alignment from being confused with direct
tokenization.

## Cautions

- Readiness is not tokenization.
- Evidence is not automatic ownership.
- Proof records are not financial products.
- EcoChain is not a blockchain implementation in this repository.
- RWA Alignment Candidates are not automatically tradable.
- Financial, legal, compliance, and insurance review remain separate.

## Boundary

No token model, blockchain, smart contract, trading system, or financial
product is implemented.
