# Traditional Forecast Models

## Purpose

Traditional forecast models remain important because they are often simpler,
more transparent, and easier to validate than large foundation models.

## Examples

- persistence baselines
- moving average models
- exponential smoothing
- ARIMA / SARIMA
- regression-based forecasting
- state-space models
- domain-calibrated statistical models

## ClimateOS Relevance

Traditional models may provide:

- baseline comparison
- explainability
- sanity checks
- low-cost validation references
- benchmark controls for foundation models

## Limitations

- may underperform on complex nonlinear systems
- may require stationarity assumptions
- may miss cross-system relationships
- may be inappropriate outside calibrated conditions

## Validation Requirements

Traditional model outputs should still be treated as Forecast Candidates until
validated against observed reality.
