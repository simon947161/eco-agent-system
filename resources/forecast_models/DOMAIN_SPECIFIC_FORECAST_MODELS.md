# Domain-Specific Forecast Models

## Purpose

Domain-specific forecast models are designed for particular fields such as
weather, hydrology, energy, agriculture, infrastructure, ecology, or carbon
planning.

## Examples

- weather forecast systems
- hydrology and streamflow models
- energy demand models
- solar and wind generation models
- vegetation stress models
- fire risk models
- infrastructure maintenance risk models
- carbon budget forecast models

## ClimateOS Relevance

Domain models may provide stronger physical or operational context than general
time-series models.

They may become Forecast Provider Candidates when their inputs, assumptions,
scope, and validation requirements are documented.

## Limitations

- may require specialist inputs
- may have hidden assumptions
- may not transfer between regions
- may need calibration and expert review

## Validation Requirements

Domain-specific forecasts should be reviewed against observed reality, known
scientific constraints, local context, and governance relevance.
