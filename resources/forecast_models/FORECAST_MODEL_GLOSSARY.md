# Forecast Model Glossary

## Forecast Provider

An external model, method, system, or expert process that can generate forecast
outputs.

## Forecast Provider Candidate

A forecast provider that may be considered for future ClimateOS planning
support but is not yet integrated.

## Forecast Candidate

A forecast output that may support planning but has not yet been validated
against observed reality.

## Time-Series Foundation Model

A large model trained for general time-series tasks. Exact behavior depends on
model design and must be verified before use.

## Traditional Forecast Model

A statistical or rule-based forecasting approach such as a baseline, smoothing,
ARIMA, regression, or state-space model.

## Domain-Specific Forecast Model

A model designed for a particular field such as weather, hydrology, energy,
agriculture, infrastructure, ecology, or carbon planning.

## Validation

Comparison of forecast outputs against observed reality, assumptions,
uncertainty, bias, and governance relevance.
