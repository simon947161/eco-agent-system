# ClimateOS Revenue Model

## Purpose

ClimateOS is not being developed as a traditional SaaS platform.

The objective is not to maximise software subscriptions, but to build an open and collaborative ecosystem for environmental governance, climate resilience, sustainable development, and Earth system management.

Revenue is considered a supporting mechanism rather than the primary goal.

---

## Phase 1 (2026–2027)

Open Development Phase

Primary focus:

- Open-source development
- Agent architecture
- Demonstration projects
- Community building
- Validation framework

Potential revenue sources:

- Consulting services
- Training workshops
- Research collaborations
- Pilot projects
- Technical reviews

---

## Phase 2 (2027–2028)

Service Expansion Phase

Potential revenue sources:

- ClimateOS assessments
- Carbon accounting support
- ESG reporting support
- Project scenario analysis
- Organisational training

---

## Phase 3 (2028–2030)

Platform Ecosystem Phase

Potential revenue sources:

- Marketplace service fees
- Verification services
- Data services
- Agent deployment services
- Ecosystem partnerships

---

## Guiding Principle

The long-term goal is to create value first and capture value later.

Trust, transparency, and ecosystem development take priority over short-term revenue.
