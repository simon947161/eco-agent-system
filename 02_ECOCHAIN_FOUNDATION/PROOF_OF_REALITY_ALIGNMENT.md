# Proof of Reality Alignment

## Purpose

EcoChain supports the future Proof of Reality concept by preserving validated
evidence history.

## Alignment

```text
Reality Data Assets
-> Evidence Assets
-> Validation Records
-> EcoChain
-> Proof of Reality
```

## Principle

Proof of Reality depends on traceability, validation, and reconstructable
history. It is not a cryptographic claim in this foundation.

## Boundary

No proof system, cryptography, blockchain, or runtime is implemented.
