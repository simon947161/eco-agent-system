# EcoChain Readiness Glossary

## EcoChain Readiness

The conceptual state in which a validated Evidence Asset is prepared to
participate in EcoChain recording concepts.

## Current Validated State

The best reviewed state available at a given time. It remains open to revision.

## EcoChain Participation

Future participation in EcoChain evidence-record concepts. It is not
implemented by this layer.

## Post-entry Revision

The ability for ClimateOS to update review status after EcoChain participation
if new evidence appears.

## Governance Context

The purpose, relevance, risk, and review framing needed to understand why an
Evidence Asset may be relevant to governance.

## Readiness Status

The current conceptual state of EcoChain readiness.

## Not Ready

A state indicating that required evidence, confidence, governance, or revision
context is incomplete.
