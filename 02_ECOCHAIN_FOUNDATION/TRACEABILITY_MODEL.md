# Traceability Model

## Purpose

Traceability preserves how evidence history can be reconstructed.

## Traceability Records

EcoChain may eventually preserve:

- source records
- contributor records
- observation records
- process records
- validation records
- governance records
- version history
- limitation notes

## Reconstruction Question

Can a future reviewer understand what happened, who contributed, what was
validated, and how a decision context was formed?

## Boundary

No traceability runtime or storage system is implemented.
