# Validation Record Model

## Purpose

Validation records describe what was checked and what confidence was assigned.

## Validation Records

Validation records may include:

- evidence reviewed
- reviewer or review process
- assumptions checked
- uncertainty notes
- confidence level
- findings
- limitations
- status

## Validation Meaning

Validation evaluates evidence. It does not automatically approve, certify, or
authorize action.

## Boundary

No validation engine or automated scoring is implemented.
