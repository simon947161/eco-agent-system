# Multi-Language Alignment

## Purpose

Multi-language alignment helps ClimateOS compare evidence statements across
languages without erasing context.

## Supported Examples

- Chinese
- English
- Arabic
- Spanish
- Indigenous Knowledge
- Future AI Languages

## Alignment Questions

- What reality is being described?
- What evidence claim is being made?
- What context is required?
- What uncertainty remains?
- Who should review the interpretation?

## Boundary

No alignment algorithm is implemented.
