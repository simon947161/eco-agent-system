# Contribution Model

## Purpose

The Contribution Model defines who contributed and what kind of record they
provided.

## Contribution Records

Contribution records may include:

- contributor role
- contribution type
- date and time
- location or system context
- source reference
- review status
- limitations

## Contribution Types

- Natural observation
- Operational process record
- Governance review record
- Scientific review
- Community knowledge
- AI-assisted documentation

## Boundary

No identity system, access control, reward system, or reputation system exists.
