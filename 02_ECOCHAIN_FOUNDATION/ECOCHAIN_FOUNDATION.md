# EcoChain Foundation

## Definition

EcoChain is a Validated Evidence Chain.

It records trusted, traceable evidence history across Natural Reality,
Operational Reality, and Governance Reality.

## What Is Recorded

- Contribution records
- Observation records
- Process records
- Validation records
- Governance records
- Traceability records

## Who Contributes

Farmers, land managers, researchers, scientists, communities, businesses,
governments, AI agents, and future participants may contribute records.

## What Creates Trust

Trust comes from provenance, context, validation, transparency, confidence,
review, and reconstructable history.

## Boundary

No blockchain, token model, smart contracts, runtime, or APIs are implemented.
