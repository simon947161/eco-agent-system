# EcoChain Alignment

## Purpose

This document aligns EcoChain with ClimateOS, EcoEngine, CCZPS, ESG++,
NatureHash, NASA integration, multi-source observation, and participation.

## Alignment Notes

- ClimateOS organizes reality into evidence and governance pathways.
- EcoEngine may provide scientific computation outputs.
- CCZPS provides climate zone reasoning.
- ESG++ translates validated evidence into governance language.
- NatureHash describes mutually supporting observation structures.
- NASA and other providers supply observation resources.
- Participation and cultivation provide contribution and stewardship context.
