# Evidence Asset Alignment

## Purpose

This document aligns Evidence Assets with ClimateOS architecture.

## Alignment

- ClimateOS transforms reality into evidence structures.
- EcoEngine may provide scientific computation context.
- CCZPS provides climate zone context.
- ESG++ translates validated evidence into governance language.
- EcoChain preserves validated evidence history.
- NatureHash supports mutually reinforcing evidence concepts.
- NASA and multi-source observation provide external observation resources.
- Participation and cultivation provide contribution and stewardship context.

## RDA and RWA

ClimateOS distinguishes generic RWA from Earth-system related RDA.

RDA is Reality Data Asset. RWA alignment is a future mapping from trusted
evidence structures toward real-world asset contexts.

## Boundary

No RWA product or financial capability is implemented.
