## Project title

ClimateOS — A Living Human–AI Operating System for Climate Stewardship

## Recommended category

Work and Productivity

Rationale: the current executable slice supports structured environmental

## One-line description

ClimateOS turns meaningful environmental questions into traceable,
human-reviewed research workflows that combine human responsibility with AI
memory, synthesis and persistent system work.

## Project description

Climate stewardship is not a one-time prediction or report. It is a long-term
relationship with a place.

ClimateOS is a growing human–AI operating-system direction for shared climate
stewardship and governance. It brings together human life experience, local
observation, values and action responsibility with AI long-term memory,
scientific synthesis, multi-model reasoning, evidence organisation and system
execution.

ClimateOS existed before Build Week. During the eligible period, we
meaningfully extended that foundation toward a directly experienceable Runtime.
The Task2000–Task2002 sequence moved from a minimum supervised control, through
a Founder review that exposed a meaning gap, to a Runtime that can preserve a
real environmental question and structure the evidence needed to investigate
it responsibly. A separate fictional, local-only rehearsal demonstrates human
approval, deterministic execution, a Run Receipt, a quarantined Evidence
Passport and post-run review without pretending that synthetic values are real
environmental evidence.

Task2003 is an emerging Draft persistent-research interface. Its current public
evidence label is only `HTML_CHECK_PASS`; it is not represented as a completed
scientific cycle or merged capability.

The demonstration is a window into ClimateOS, not the system itself.

## What we built during Build Week

## How we used Codex and GPT-5.6

Simon Shu supplied the environmental purpose, professional and lived context,
the meaningful real-world question, experience review, corrections and final
decisions. Codex with GPT-5.6 helped inspect and connect a large repository,
implement bounded Runtime increments, write and run tests, maintain evidence
and decision records, diagnose experience failures, improve the interface and
prepare the submission evidence.

The key collaboration moment was not automatic code generation. After Simon
used the first Runtime and said that the scalar experience was technically
functional but environmentally unmeaningful, GPT-5.6 and Codex preserved the
control foundation and redirected the next increment around a meaningful
environmental question. Later, an AI recency-bias error in submission planning
was also recorded and corrected through a permanent integrity covenant. This
made human judgement, AI memory and honest correction visible parts of the
system-building process.

## Limitations

ClimateOS is an early-stage prototype. The demo does not provide a current
forecast, emergency warning, statutory determination, professional
certification, engineering recommendation, investment advice or regional
environmental conclusion. Human and professional review remain necessary.
