## Narration bridge draft

The preferred narration is now the shorter, prosody-ready script at
`video_assets/voiceover_maple_ready.txt`. It is written for a warm, patient,
quietly hopeful delivery with natural pauses and emphasis. It also states the
long-term universal direction without claiming that the current prototype is
already a finished consumer product.

The earlier detailed bridge is retained below as source material:

ClimateOS grew from years of environmental practice and earlier work on Eco
Agent, contribution memory, evidence-first claims, Eco Decision DNA,
CCZPS-Lite, Evidence Passports, scientific architecture and governance. That
foundation existed before Build Week.

During Build Week, Codex and GPT-5.6 helped turn this foundation into a more
experienceable human–AI Runtime. The first minimum workflow could structure an
experiment, require human approval and produce traceable receipts, but Simon's
hands-on review showed that a scalar-box demo was not yet meaningful
environmental work.

We kept what was valid and changed the experience. The next Runtime preserves a
real question about climate, fire, drinking water and wastewater, and shows the
evidence needed to investigate it responsibly. It does not manufacture a
regional answer. A separate fictional, local-only rehearsal demonstrates
approval, deterministic execution, a Run Receipt, a quarantined Evidence
Passport and human review.

An emerging Draft interface explores how this could become a persistent
research program. Its current evidence is only that the Founder opened and
checked the HTML; a complete monthly cycle is not claimed.

Simon brought place, purpose, responsibility and the judgement that changed
the product direction. Codex with GPT-5.6 brought repository-scale memory,
implementation, testing, synthesis and traceability. ClimateOS is designed to
let both keep learning together.
