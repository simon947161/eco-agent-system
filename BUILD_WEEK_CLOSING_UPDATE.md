# ClimateOS submitted to OpenAI Build Week

Today I submitted **ClimateOS — Human–AI Climate Stewardship** to OpenAI
Build Week.

ClimateOS did not begin with this event. It grew from years of environmental
practice and earlier work on EcoAgent, EcoChain, evidence-first claims, Eco
Decision DNA, CCZPS-Lite, Evidence Passports, scientific architecture and
governance.

During Build Week, I worked with Codex and GPT-5.6 to make one part of that
longer system directly experienceable. The stable Runtime begins with a
meaningful question connecting climate, bushfire risk, drinking water,
wastewater and climate adaptation. It structures the evidence needed to
investigate the question, while refusing to manufacture a real-place
conclusion when evidence is absent.

A separate fictional rehearsal demonstrates explicit human approval,
deterministic execution, a Run Receipt, an Evidence Passport and post-run human
review. The stable demonstration reported 321 passing tests.

The most important lesson was that technical functionality is not enough.
Human judgement changed the direction of the product when the first workflow
worked mechanically but did not yet feel like meaningful environmental work.
We preserved the valid foundation, recorded the failure and built forward.

ClimateOS begins with environmental stewardship, but its deeper direction is
universal: every person has a relationship with a place. Over time, people
should be able to work with AI systems that remember, understand and help them
care for those places responsibly.
