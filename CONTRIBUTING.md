# Contributing to Eco Agent System

Thank you for your interest in Eco Agent System.

This project welcomes developers, planners, environmental researchers, engineers, students, local government practitioners, land managers, climate specialists, insurers, and community contributors.

The goal is to build practical, transparent, and human-reviewed environmental decision-support tools.

---

## What kinds of contributions are useful?

We welcome contributions in:

### Documentation

- improve explanations
- improve diagrams
- improve onboarding material
- improve examples and tutorials

### Environmental Indicators

- climate indicators
- resilience indicators
- environmental monitoring concepts
- vegetation, water, heat, soil, and maintenance metrics

### Agent Logic

- planning workflows
- ESG interpretation logic
- operations and maintenance workflows
- resilience assessment methods
- human-review mechanisms

### Regional Use Cases

- rural communities
- agricultural systems
- dryland environments
- climate adaptation projects
- community resilience initiatives

### Modelling and Integration

Contributions are welcome that explore interoperability with:

- GIS platforms
- environmental simulation tools
- hydrology and terrain models
- CFD systems such as ANSYS Fluent or equivalent tools
- digital twin environments
- smart city platforms
- open climate and infrastructure datasets

The project encourages practical bridges between traditional simulation environments and agent-based decision-support systems.

---

## Climate Risk, Insurance and Resilience Finance Contributions

This project welcomes contributions related to climate risk reduction, climate insurance, resilience finance, and long-term environmental maintenance.

A key purpose of the Eco Agent System is not only to describe environmental conditions but also to support practical actions that may reduce disaster risk over time.

Relevant contribution areas include:

- climate and weather risk indicators
- microclimate monitoring and intervention logic
- bushfire, flood, drought, heat, and storm risk interpretation
- risk-score reduction models
- links between resilience actions and insurance-relevant evidence
- maintenance records for long-term environmental assets
- data structures that help describe how prevention work reduces future losses
- decision-support logic for local governments, communities, land managers, and insurers

The project does not provide insurance, financial advice, actuarial certification, or investment advice. It explores how environmental data, AI-assisted reasoning, and human-reviewed maintenance records may support better risk understanding and future collaboration with climate-insurance and resilience-finance sectors.

## Contribution Principles

Contributors are encouraged to follow these principles:

- Public-good first
- Human review before automation
- Transparent reasoning
- Respect for local knowledge
- Environmental responsibility before financial interpretation
- Practical usefulness over technical complexity

This project does not promote speculative token activity.

Eco Chain is treated as a contribution-recording and environmental value interpretation concept rather than a financial product.

---

## How to Contribute

1. Fork the repository
2. Create a branch
3. Make your changes
4. Document the purpose of the change
5. Submit a pull request
6. Explain how the change improves environmental understanding, resilience, maintenance, or decision support

Small contributions are welcome.

Documentation improvements, examples, regional case studies, testing, and conceptual reviews are just as valuable as code contributions.

---

## Review Standard

Contributions are more likely to be accepted when they:

- improve environmental understanding
- improve transparency
- support resilience and long-term maintenance
- remain understandable to non-technical users
- avoid exaggerated claims about AI capability
- provide practical value for communities, planners, land managers, researchers, or public institutions
