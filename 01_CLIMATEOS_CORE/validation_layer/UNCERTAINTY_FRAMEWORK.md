# Uncertainty Framework

## Purpose

The Uncertainty Framework defines concepts that should be preserved during
future ClimateOS validation.

## Concepts

### Uncertainty

The degree to which evidence, inputs, assumptions, or conclusions remain
incomplete or unresolved.

### Confidence

The level of trust assigned to evidence after review.

### Error

A known or suspected difference between a value, observation, or method and the
real-world condition it represents.

### Assumption

A stated belief or simplification used when direct evidence is incomplete.

### Boundary Conditions

The conditions under which evidence, methods, or calculations are intended to
apply.

## Boundary

No uncertainty calculation or numeric error model is implemented.
