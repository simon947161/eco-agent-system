# Evidence Quality Framework

## Purpose

The Evidence Quality Framework defines concepts that future ClimateOS systems
can use to evaluate evidence quality.

## Quality Concepts

### Source Diversity

Evidence is stronger when different source types support the same pattern.

### Temporal Consistency

Evidence should make sense across relevant time periods.

### Spatial Consistency

Evidence should make sense across relevant places and climate zones.

### Relationship Consistency

Evidence should preserve whether environmental relationships are consistent or
contradictory.

### Historical Consistency

Evidence should be compared with historical context when available.

### Cross Validation

Evidence should be prepared for review against independent observations,
records, or expert knowledge.

## Boundary

This framework does not calculate evidence quality scores.
