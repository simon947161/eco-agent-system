# Validation Glossary

## Validation

Systematic evaluation of evidence quality, confidence, transparency, and
traceability.

## Confidence

The level of trust assigned to evidence after review.

## Evidence Quality

The strength and review readiness of evidence based on source diversity,
consistency, traceability, and uncertainty.

## Transparency

The ability to understand how evidence or a calculation was produced.

## Traceability

The ability to follow evidence or calculations back to sources, assumptions,
methods, and review notes.

## Reproducibility

The ability to repeat a review or calculation using the same stated method.

## Uncertainty

Known and unknown limits around evidence, inputs, assumptions, or conclusions.

## Assumption

A stated belief or simplification used when direct evidence is incomplete.

## Verified

Checked against a defined source or review process.

## Validated Evidence

Evidence that has passed a defined validation process.
