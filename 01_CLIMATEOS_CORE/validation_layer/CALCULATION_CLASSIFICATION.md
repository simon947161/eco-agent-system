# Calculation Classification

## Purpose

Calculation classification helps ClimateOS describe the nature of future
calculation methods.

## Categories

### Physics-Based

Based on physical laws or physical system behavior.

### Hydrology-Based

Based on water movement, storage, rainfall, runoff, or related water processes.

### Climate-Based

Based on climate conditions, climate zones, or climate system behavior.

### Ecology-Based

Based on ecosystem, vegetation, biodiversity, or restoration concepts.

### Statistical

Based on statistical methods or quantitative summaries.

### Empirical

Based on observed relationships or measured patterns.

### Heuristic

Based on practical rules or structured judgement.

### Experimental

Under development and not yet suitable for operational use.

### Placeholder

Reserved for a future method that has not yet been implemented.

## Boundary

This document does not classify existing runtime calculations.
