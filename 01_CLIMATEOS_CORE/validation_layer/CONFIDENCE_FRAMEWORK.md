# Confidence Framework

## Purpose

The Confidence Framework defines simple confidence concepts for future
ClimateOS validation.

## Confidence Levels

### Unknown

Confidence cannot be assessed from the available evidence.

### Low

Evidence is limited, uncertain, weakly supported, or difficult to trace.

### Medium

Evidence has meaningful support but still has important limitations or
uncertainties.

### High

Evidence has strong support, clear traceability, and limited unresolved
uncertainty.

### Very High

Evidence is strongly supported by multiple sources, consistent relationships,
clear methods, and review-ready documentation.

## Boundary

No scoring algorithm, numeric confidence model, or automated classification is
implemented.
