# Validation Types

## Purpose

Validation types help ClimateOS describe how an evidence structure may be
reviewed.

## Categories

### Scientific Validation

Checks whether evidence aligns with relevant scientific concepts.

### Historical Validation

Compares evidence against historical context where available.

### Cross-Source Validation

Checks whether multiple sources support or challenge the same evidence.

### Climate Zone Validation

Reviews whether the evidence makes sense in the relevant climate zone.

### Community Validation

Includes local observations or community knowledge as review context.

### Expert Validation

Includes specialist review where required.

### Model Validation

Reviews whether a future model or calculation method is suitable and explained.

### Scenario Validation

Reviews whether scenario assumptions and outputs are transparent and credible.

## Boundary

These categories are conceptual. No validation workflow is implemented.
