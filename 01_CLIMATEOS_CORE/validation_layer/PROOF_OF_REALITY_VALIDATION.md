# Proof of Reality Validation

## Purpose

Proof of Reality Validation defines how the Validation Layer supports the
ClimateOS Proof of Reality concept.

## Concept

Proof of Reality depends on evidence that can be traced, reviewed, challenged,
and strengthened over time.

Validation supports Proof of Reality by checking:

- Source traceability
- Relationship consistency
- Evidence quality
- Confidence context
- Uncertainty
- Calculation transparency
- Review status

## Relationship To Evidence

Evidence synthesis creates structured evidence.

Validation evaluates whether that evidence is strong enough to support future
governance, preservation, or value mapping.

## Boundary

This document is conceptual only. No proof system, blockchain implementation,
automated validation, or governance runtime is added.
