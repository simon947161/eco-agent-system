# RWA Alignment to Proof of Reality

## Principle

Proof of Reality determines whether the real-world claim is credible.

RWA Alignment cannot proceed from unverified claims.

## Chain

```text
RDA
-> Reality Claim
-> Evidence Package
-> Cross-Validation
-> Confidence Level
-> Proof Record
-> RWA Alignment Review
```

## Boundary

No proof or RWA review runtime is implemented.
