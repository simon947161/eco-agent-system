# RWA Alignment Glossary

## RWA Alignment

The process of determining whether a proven real-world value object has
sufficient identity, evidence, traceability, validation, governance context, and
risk understanding to support future asset expression, finance, insurance,
compliance, audit, or governance use.

## RWA Alignment Candidate

A structured readiness object. It is not automatically a token, financial
asset, or tradable product.

## Real-World Value Object

An object, process, project, place, system, or outcome that may carry value
because it is connected to real-world reality.

## Value Evidence

Evidence that supports why a real-world object may have value.

## NatureHash

A future possible identity / proof-of-nature research layer. Not implemented.
