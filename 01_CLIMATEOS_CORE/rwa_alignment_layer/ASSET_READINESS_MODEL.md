# Asset Readiness Model

## Readiness Levels

- Observed
- Documented
- Evidence-Supported
- Proof-Reviewed
- Evidence-Asset Supported
- EcoChain-Ready
- RWA-Alignment Candidate
- Finance / Insurance / Compliance Ready
- Not Ready
- Disputed
- Rejected

## Principle

Readiness may change over time as evidence improves, risks are discovered, or
claims are disputed.

## Boundary

No automated readiness scoring is implemented.
