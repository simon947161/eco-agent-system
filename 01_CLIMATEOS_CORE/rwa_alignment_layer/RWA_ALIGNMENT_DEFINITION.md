# RWA Alignment Definition

## Definition

RWA Alignment is the process of determining whether a proven real-world value
object has sufficient identity, evidence, traceability, validation, governance
context, and risk understanding to support future asset expression, finance,
insurance, compliance, audit, or governance use.

## Questions

- What is the real-world object?
- What value does it represent?
- What evidence supports that value?
- What reality has been proven?
- What risks remain?
- What governance context exists?
- What may later be used for finance, insurance, compliance, audit, or asset
  expression?

## Boundary

RWA Alignment is a readiness framework. It is not RWA tokenization.
