# RWA Alignment to RDA

## Principle

RDA is the reality-linked data foundation.

RWA Alignment depends on RDA quality.

Poor RDA creates weak RWA alignment.

## Strong RDA Supports

- traceability
- evidence formation
- lifecycle understanding
- operational verification
- value assessment
- risk assessment
- governance review

## Boundary

No RDA runtime or RWA engine is implemented.
