# RWA Scope Boundary

## Generic RWA

Generic RWA may include:

- art
- collectibles
- luxury goods
- general financial assets
- unrelated intellectual property
- speculative tokenized assets

These are outside the current ClimateOS core scope.

## Earth-System RWA Alignment

ClimateOS RWA Alignment focuses on real-world value objects connected to:

- land
- water
- energy
- carbon
- infrastructure
- ecological condition
- agriculture
- resource flows
- resilience
- community projects
- environmental performance
- governance outcomes
- operational performance

## Boundary

ClimateOS does not create a generic RWA platform.
