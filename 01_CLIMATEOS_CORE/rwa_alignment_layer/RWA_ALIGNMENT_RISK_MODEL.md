# RWA Alignment Risk Model

## Risk Categories

- evidence risk
- legal risk
- ownership risk
- governance risk
- environmental risk
- operational risk
- maintenance risk
- financial risk
- social acceptance risk
- fraud risk
- double-counting risk
- tokenization risk
- model risk
- forecast risk

## Principle

ClimateOS does not eliminate risk.

ClimateOS helps identify, document, classify, and reduce uncertainty through
evidence and validation.

## Boundary

No risk scoring or insurance model is implemented.
