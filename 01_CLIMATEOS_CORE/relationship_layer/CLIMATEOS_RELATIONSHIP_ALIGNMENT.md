# ClimateOS Relationship Alignment

## Purpose

This document aligns the Climate Relationship Layer with related ClimateOS and
Eco system concepts.

## ClimateOS

ClimateOS uses relationships to connect observation, radar, evidence,
validation, and governance.

## EcoEngine

EcoEngine may later support evidence and validation processes. The
Relationship Layer can provide concepts for what should be reviewed.

## EcoChain

EcoChain may later preserve traceable records, provenance, and relationship
history. No chain or ledger is implemented here.

## CCZPS

CCZPS can provide climate-zone planning concepts. The Relationship Layer may
explain how relationships vary by climate zone without implementing CCZPS
runtime.

## ESG++

ESG++ may later use relationship-aware evidence for disclosure, governance,
and accountability. This task does not create ESG scoring or reporting.

## Observation Layer

Observation records what happened. Relationship connects observed signals and
explains possible system connections.

## Relationship Layer

The Relationship Layer organises interactions, forcing systems, flows,
feedbacks, disturbance, recovery, and resilience concepts.

## Limits

Alignment is conceptual. No runtime, API, validation engine, governance system,
or external integration is implemented.
