# Relationship Glossary

## Relationship

A connection between environmental, ecological, human, or governance elements.

## Interaction

The way related elements influence, constrain, or respond to each other.

## Forcing

A driver or pressure that shapes system conditions or relationships.

## Flow

Movement or transfer through a system, such as water, energy, carbon,
information, or ecological signals.

## Feedback

A repeating influence where system response changes the conditions that shaped
that response.

## Disturbance

An event or pressure that disrupts system relationships, flows, feedbacks, or
recovery.

## Recovery

Movement toward function, structure, stability, or adaptation after stress or
disturbance.

## Resilience

The capacity of a system to absorb stress, maintain function, recover, or
transform while preserving core value.

## Adaptation

A change in behaviour, structure, management, or relationship in response to
changed conditions.

## Validation

The process of evaluating whether relationship interpretations are supported
by evidence, uncertainty review, and human judgement.

## Evidence

Reviewed observation or source material with provenance, context, limitations,
and review status.
