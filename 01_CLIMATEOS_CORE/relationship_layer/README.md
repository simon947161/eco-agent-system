# Climate Relationship Layer V1

## Purpose

The Climate Relationship Layer defines how ClimateOS understands
environmental systems through relationships rather than isolated indicators or
isolated datasets.

ClimateOS studies relationships between systems:

- weather and ecology;
- water and vegetation;
- human activity and environmental change;
- disturbance and recovery;
- evidence and validation; and
- governance and system response.

## Strategic Position

```text
Observation Layer
  |
  v
Climate Relationship Layer
  |
  v
Radar Layer
  |
  v
Evidence Layer
  |
  v
Validation Layer
  |
  v
Governance Runtime
```

Observation records what happened. Relationship explains why it happened.
Radar detects what is changing. Evidence records verified observations.
Validation evaluates reality. Governance responds to reality.
