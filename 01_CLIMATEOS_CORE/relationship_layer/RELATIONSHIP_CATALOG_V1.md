# Relationship Catalog V1

## Purpose

This catalog lists initial example relationships for ClimateOS. They are
examples only and contain no calculations.

| Relationship | Purpose | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wind <-> Moisture | Explore how wind and moisture conditions may influence drying, recovery, and stress | Requires local observation and review |
| Moisture <-> Vegetation | Connect water availability with vegetation condition | Varies by site and season |
| Vegetation <-> Temperature | Explore how vegetation cover and temperature context interact | No temperature model is implemented |
| Temperature <-> Fire Risk | Preserve conceptual links between heat, dryness, and fire-related concern | No fire prediction is implemented |
| Rainfall <-> Soil Moisture | Connect rainfall observations with soil moisture context | Requires evidence and timing review |
| Soil Moisture <-> Vegetation | Explore plant response to available water | Depends on species and land use |
| Vegetation <-> Carbon Storage | Preserve conceptual relationship between vegetation and carbon context | No carbon calculation is implemented |
| Human Activity <-> Environmental Change | Connect land use, management, community action, and observed change | Requires governance and evidence review |

## Use

Each relationship should eventually have observations, evidence references,
uncertainty notes, validation status, and governance context.

## Limits

Catalog entries are placeholders for future evidence-backed work. They are not
validated knowledge claims.
