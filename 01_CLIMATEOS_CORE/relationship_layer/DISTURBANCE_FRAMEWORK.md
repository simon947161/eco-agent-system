# Disturbance Framework

## Purpose

Disturbance describes events or pressures that disrupt system relationships,
flows, feedbacks, or recovery.

## Disturbance Examples

| Disturbance | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Bushfire | Explore disruption to vegetation, carbon, water, habitat, community, and recovery relationships |
| Flood | Explore disruption to water flow, soil, infrastructure, ecology, and community relationships |
| Heatwave | Explore stress relationships across temperature, vegetation, energy, health, and governance context |
| Drought | Explore relationships between rainfall, soil moisture, vegetation, water use, and recovery |
| Storm | Explore wind, rainfall, damage, runoff, erosion, and community response relationships |
| External Forcing Events | Capture other pressures that alter system behaviour |

## Interpretation

Disturbance is not only an event. It is also a relationship change that may
persist after the event.

## Limits

This framework does not predict disturbance, calculate risk, or recommend
response actions.
