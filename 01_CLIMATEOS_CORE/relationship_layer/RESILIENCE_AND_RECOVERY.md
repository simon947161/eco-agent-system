# Resilience and Recovery

## Purpose

This document defines system response concepts used by the Climate
Relationship Layer.

## Recovery

Recovery is the process by which a system returns toward function, structure,
or stability after stress or disturbance.

## Adaptation

Adaptation is a change in behaviour, structure, management, or relationship
that helps a system operate under changed conditions.

## Resilience

Resilience is the ability of a system to absorb stress, maintain function,
recover, or transform without losing core identity or value.

## System Response

System response includes visible changes in vegetation, water, temperature,
community behaviour, land management, governance, or evidence needs.

## Review Needs

Recovery, adaptation, and resilience statements require observations,
evidence, uncertainty notes, and validation.

## Limits

This document does not score resilience, calculate recovery, or recommend
adaptation actions.
