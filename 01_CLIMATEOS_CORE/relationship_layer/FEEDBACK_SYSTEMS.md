# Feedback Systems

## Purpose

Feedback systems describe repeating relationships where system response changes
the conditions that shaped that response.

## Positive Feedback

A positive feedback strengthens or amplifies a change.

Example:

```text
Dry conditions
  -> vegetation stress
  -> reduced shade and moisture retention
  -> further drying
```

## Negative Feedback

A negative feedback dampens or moderates a change.

Example:

```text
Rainfall
  -> improved soil moisture
  -> vegetation recovery
  -> increased shade and moisture retention
  -> moderated heat stress
```

## Review Needs

Feedback examples require observations, evidence, uncertainty notes, and
validation before they can be used as knowledge claims.

## Limits

No equations, simulations, causal claims, or forecasts are implemented.
