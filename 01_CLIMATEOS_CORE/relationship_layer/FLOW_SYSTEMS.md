# Flow Systems

## Purpose

Flow systems describe movement or transfer through environmental and social
systems.

## Flow Concepts

| Flow | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Water Flow | Movement of rainfall, runoff, groundwater, rivers, soil moisture, and water stress signals |
| Energy Flow | Movement or transformation of heat, radiation, electricity context, and energy demand signals |
| Carbon Flow | Movement or storage context for carbon in vegetation, soil, energy systems, and human activities |
| Information Flow | Movement of observations, evidence, knowledge, validation findings, and governance notes |
| Ecological Flow | Movement of ecological signals such as species activity, flowering, habitat condition, recovery, and stress |

## Interpretation

Flows help ClimateOS describe how one part of a system connects to another
over time and place.

## Limits

This document does not implement hydrology, energy modelling, carbon
accounting, information systems, ecological models, or simulations.
