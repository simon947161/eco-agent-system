# Validation Pack Glossary

## Validation Pack

A structured future runtime output that records validation state and remains
open to revision.

## Review Pack

A structured output describing review findings for one or more review objects.

## Evidence Pack

A structured output preserving evidence context, provenance, and traceability.

## Recommendation Pack

A structured recommendation candidate output. It is not an automated decision.

## Governance Pack

A structured governance-facing output that preserves evidence, confidence, and
review context.

## Pack Metadata

Information that makes a pack traceable, reviewable, and reusable.

## Pack Version

A recorded state of a pack at a point in its revision history.
