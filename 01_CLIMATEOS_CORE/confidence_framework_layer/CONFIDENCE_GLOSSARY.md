# Confidence Glossary

## Confidence

The current level of support for a claim, evidence package, forecast candidate,
relationship, or judgment after review.

## Uncertainty

Known or suspected limits in evidence, assumptions, methods, sources, timing, or
interpretation.

## Consensus

Agreement among multiple sources or participants. Consensus does not
automatically equal truth.

## Disagreement

Conflict between evidence, sources, reviewers, methods, assumptions, or
interpretations. Disagreement is not failure.

## Escalation

Request for deeper review because confidence, risk, disagreement, impact, or
uncertainty requires more attention.

## Confidence Update

A documented change in confidence state caused by new evidence, review,
confirmation, contradiction, or uncertainty.

## Confidence Decay

The weakening of confidence when evidence becomes stale, assumptions expire,
conditions change, or review history becomes outdated.

## Conflicting Evidence

Evidence that points toward different interpretations or conclusions.

## Independent Confirmation

Support from sources or reviewers that do not depend on the same origin,
assumption chain, or copied conclusion.

## Minority Signal

A signal held by a smaller number of contributors or sources that may still be
important, early, or risk-relevant.

## Whistleblower Signal

A signal from a person or participant raising concern about concealed,
misrepresented, or harmful reality.

## Collective Signal

A pattern formed when multiple observations, participants, agents, or providers
point toward a related concern or interpretation.
