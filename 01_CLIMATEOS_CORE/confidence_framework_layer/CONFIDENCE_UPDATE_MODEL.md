# Confidence Update Model

## Purpose

The Confidence Update Model defines how confidence may conceptually change when
new evidence, review, uncertainty, or contradiction appears.

## Confidence Update

A Confidence Update is a documented change in confidence state.

Possible causes include:

- new evidence arrives
- independent confirmation appears
- evidence is challenged
- assumptions are corrected
- forecast candidates are validated or invalidated
- Earth Intelligence outputs are reviewed
- minority or whistleblower signals are assessed
- new uncertainty is discovered

## Confidence Increase

Confidence may increase when:

- evidence quality improves
- sources are independently confirmed
- assumptions are validated
- uncertainty is reduced
- conflicting evidence is resolved
- review history is strengthened

## Confidence Reduction

Confidence may decrease when:

- evidence quality weakens
- source integrity is questioned
- assumptions fail
- conflict grows
- validation cannot reproduce the conclusion
- relevant evidence is missing
- confidence has decayed over time

## Continuous Accumulation

Confidence should evolve through continuous evidence accumulation and
validation. It should not be treated as a fixed label.

## Boundary

No automatic update mechanism, formula, score, or runtime engine is added.
