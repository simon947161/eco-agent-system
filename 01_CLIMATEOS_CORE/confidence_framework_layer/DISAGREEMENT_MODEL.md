# Disagreement Model

## Purpose

The Disagreement Model defines how ClimateOS may preserve conflicting views,
conflicting evidence, uncertainty, and unresolved interpretation.

## Principle

Disagreement is not failure.

Disagreement may reveal missing evidence, hidden assumptions, local knowledge,
data quality problems, or early signals that have not yet been widely
recognized.

## Disagreement Types

- Evidence disagreement
- Interpretation disagreement
- Source disagreement
- Method disagreement
- Timing disagreement
- Scale disagreement
- Governance relevance disagreement

## Conflicting Evidence

Conflicting Evidence should be preserved rather than suppressed.

Future review should ask:

- What sources disagree?
- What assumptions differ?
- What time period is being compared?
- What location or scale is being used?
- Is one source more directly connected to observed reality?
- Does the conflict require escalation?

## Minority And Whistleblower Signals

A single witness, minority signal, or whistleblower signal does not
automatically equal false.

Such signals should be assessed through evidence, traceability, risk, and
validation requirements.

## Boundary

This model does not resolve disagreements automatically or assign final truth.
