# Escalation Model

## Purpose

The Escalation Model defines when ClimateOS may need deeper review before a
claim, confidence state, or governance interpretation is used.

## Escalation

Escalation means that a claim or evidence package requires additional human,
agent, expert, community, or validation review.

## Escalation Triggers

Escalation may be needed when:

- evidence is high impact but low confidence
- minority signals suggest material risk
- whistleblower signals require protection and review
- forecasts conflict with observations
- Earth Intelligence providers disagree
- governance action could create harm
- confidence is based on narrow or stale sources
- assumptions remain unresolved

## Escalation Outcomes

Escalation may result in:

- request for more evidence
- independent review
- expert review
- community review
- updated assumptions
- confidence increase
- confidence reduction
- unresolved status

## Boundary

This model does not create an escalation workflow engine, alert system, API, or
automated decision rule.
