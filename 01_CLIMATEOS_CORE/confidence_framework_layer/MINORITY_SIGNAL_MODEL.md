# Minority Signal Model

## Purpose

The Minority Signal Model defines how ClimateOS may preserve signals that do
not yet have broad consensus.

## Principle

A minority signal may be wrong, early, incomplete, or important.

ClimateOS should not discard minority signals merely because they are not
popular or institutionally dominant.

## Signal Types

- Local witness signal
- Community observation signal
- Whistleblower signal
- Specialist reviewer signal
- AI agent concern
- Forecast anomaly
- Earth Intelligence provider anomaly
- Field observation that conflicts with official summaries

## Review Considerations

Minority signals should be reviewed for:

- traceability
- evidence quality
- risk relevance
- source context
- possible harm if ignored
- possible harm if accepted too quickly
- need for independent confirmation

## Collective Signals

Collective Signals may emerge when multiple independent minority signals point
toward the same concern.

Such convergence may justify escalation even before consensus exists.

## Boundary

This model does not create whistleblower intake systems, protection systems,
legal advice, automated prioritization, or enforcement mechanisms.
