# Review Workflow Glossary

## Review Workflow

The conceptual flow by which ClimateOS moves from inputs toward integrated
review, recommendation candidates, and governance output candidates.

## Cross Validation

Comparison of claims, evidence, forecasts, and observations against independent
sources or review contexts.

## Forecast Candidate

A forecast output treated as a candidate requiring validation, not as truth.

## Confidence Update

A change in confidence state caused by evidence, uncertainty, validation,
conflict, or revision.

## Conflict Analysis

The review of disagreement among sources, evidence, forecasts, interpretations,
or participant signals.

## Revision

The reopening or updating of a previous review position when new evidence or
better understanding appears.

## Integrated Review

The current synthesis of evidence, confidence, conflict, validation, and
revision status.

## Recommendation Candidate

A review-supported suggestion that remains open to human review and future
revision.

## Governance Output Candidate

A governance-relevant output that preserves evidence, uncertainty, confidence,
and revision status.
