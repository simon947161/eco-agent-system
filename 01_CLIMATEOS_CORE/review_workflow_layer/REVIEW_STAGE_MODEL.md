# Review Stage Model

## Purpose

The Review Stage Model defines the conceptual stages in the ClimateOS review
workflow.

## Stages

### Observation

Records what happened or what was reported.

### Knowledge

Organizes observations, references, context, and reusable understanding.

### Evidence

Turns relevant records into reviewable evidence packages or Evidence Assets.

### Reality Claims

Frames a claim about reality that can be tested, supported, weakened, or
revised.

### Cross Validation

Compares claims and evidence against independent sources and review contexts.

### Forecast Candidates

Treats forecasts as candidates requiring validation, not truth.

### Confidence Update

Updates confidence when evidence, uncertainty, conflict, or validation changes.

### Conflict Analysis

Preserves and studies disagreement rather than hiding it.

### Revision

Reopens or updates prior review positions when better evidence appears.

### Integrated Review

Forms the current best evidence-supported judgment.

### Recommendation

Frames a review-supported recommendation candidate.

### Governance Output

Translates review into governance-relevant language without creating automated
decisions.

## Boundary

Stages are conceptual and do not create executable workflow behavior.
