# Computation Request Types

## Purpose

Computation request types describe possible future categories of scientific
questions that ClimateOS may ask EcoEngine.

## Examples

### Dryland Analysis

Future analysis of dryland condition, stress, or recovery context.

### Water Balance

Future analysis of rainfall, moisture, runoff, storage, or water stress.

### Ecological Balance

Future analysis of vegetation, ecosystem condition, or ecological recovery.

### Boundary Analysis

Future analysis of system limits or operating boundaries.

### Threshold Detection

Future analysis of whether a condition may approach or cross a threshold.

### Pattern Detection

Future analysis of repeated environmental patterns.

### Scenario Evaluation

Future analysis of scenario assumptions, outputs, or risk context.

## Boundary

These examples are conceptual only. No computation requests are implemented.
