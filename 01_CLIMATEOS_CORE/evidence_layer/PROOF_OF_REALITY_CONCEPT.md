# Proof of Reality Concept

## Purpose

Proof of Reality is a ClimateOS concept for verifying reality through
relationship consistency and multi-source environmental evidence.

## Concept

ClimateOS seeks to understand reality by connecting:

- Observations
- Relationships
- Radar signals
- Evidence candidates
- Evidence integrity
- Validation review

## Why It Matters

Environmental claims should be grounded in reality that can be traced, checked,
and challenged.

Proof of Reality gives ClimateOS a future direction for making environmental
truth reviewable without depending on a single data source or unsupported claim.

## Boundary

This is a concept only. No implementation, blockchain system, cryptographic
proof, automated validation, or governance runtime is added.
