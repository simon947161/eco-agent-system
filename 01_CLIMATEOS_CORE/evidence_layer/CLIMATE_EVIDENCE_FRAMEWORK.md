# Climate Evidence Framework

## Purpose

This framework defines the ClimateOS evidence lifecycle. It explains how
observed reality can become an evidence structure without claiming that the
evidence has already been validated.

## Evidence Lifecycle

```text
Observation
↓
Relationship
↓
Relationship Pattern
↓
Evidence Candidate
↓
Evidence
↓
Validated Evidence
```

## Lifecycle Stages

### Observation

An observation records something that happened at a time and location.

### Relationship

A relationship connects observations into a meaningful environmental context.

### Relationship Pattern

A relationship pattern appears when similar relationships repeat or persist.

### Evidence Candidate

An evidence candidate is a possible evidence structure that requires review.

### Evidence

Evidence is a synthesized relationship structure with traceable support.

### Validated Evidence

Validated evidence has passed a defined review process and can support
governance, EcoChain, or future value mapping.

## Boundary

This framework defines concepts only. It does not generate evidence
automatically and does not validate evidence.
