# Ecological Signatures

## Purpose

An Ecological Signature is a recognizable representation of ecosystem state.

## Concept

Ecological signatures describe how living systems appear when multiple
conditions are considered together.

## Example State Representations

- Recovering vegetation after rainfall
- Heat stress in exposed landscapes
- Drying vegetation during low rainfall periods
- Improved ground cover after restoration activity
- Disturbance response after fire, flood, or storm events

## Evidence Role

Ecological signatures may support evidence candidates when they are traceable to
observations and relationship context.

## Boundary

This document defines concepts only. It does not classify ecosystems or score
ecological condition.
