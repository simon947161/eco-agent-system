# Climate Zone Evidence

## Purpose

This document explains the relationship between CCZPS and evidence synthesis.

## Concept

Different climate zones may produce different evidence structures. A pattern
that matters in one zone may have a different meaning in another.

## CCZPS Relationship

CCZPS provides climate zone context for interpreting observations and
relationships.

ClimateOS should avoid treating all environmental evidence as location-neutral.

## Examples

- Mountain systems may show rapid moisture and temperature shifts.
- Dryland systems may show long stress and recovery cycles.
- Agricultural systems may include land management as a strong evidence factor.
- Urban systems may include heat, infrastructure, and human activity context.

## Boundary

This document does not add CCZPS runtime logic or classification capability.
