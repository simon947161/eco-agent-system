# Relationship Signatures

## Purpose

A Relationship Signature is a recurring relationship pattern that may become an
evidence structure.

## Concept

Relationship signatures help ClimateOS recognize when environmental variables
move together in meaningful ways.

## Examples

- Rainfall ↔ Evaporation
- Moisture ↔ Vegetation
- Temperature ↔ Fire Risk
- Soil Moisture ↔ Vegetation Condition
- Human Activity ↔ Environmental Change

## Use

Relationship signatures can help identify evidence candidates, but they are not
evidence by themselves.

## Boundary

No calculations, thresholds, or automated signature detection are implemented.
