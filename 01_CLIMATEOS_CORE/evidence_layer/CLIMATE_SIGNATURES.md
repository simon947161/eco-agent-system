# Climate Signatures

## Purpose

A Climate Signature is a recognizable pattern formed by climate conditions over
time.

## Concept

Climate conditions rarely act alone. Temperature, rainfall, humidity, wind,
radiation, and seasonal timing can form patterns that help explain observed
environmental change.

## Example Patterns

- Hot and dry periods increasing landscape stress
- Rainfall followed by vegetation recovery
- Wind and low humidity increasing drying pressure
- Repeated wet periods changing soil moisture context

## Evidence Role

Climate signatures can support evidence candidates when they align with
observations, relationships, and radar-detected change.

## Boundary

No forecasting, weather modelling, or climate simulation is implemented.
