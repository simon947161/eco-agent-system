# Evidence Glossary

## Evidence

A synthesized relationship structure with traceable support.

## Evidence Candidate

A possible evidence structure that requires review.

## Validated Evidence

Evidence that has passed a defined validation process.

## Relationship Signature

A recurring relationship pattern that may support an evidence structure.

## Ecological Signature

A recognizable representation of ecosystem state.

## Climate Signature

A recognizable pattern formed by climate conditions over time.

## Nature Hash

A concept describing how many mutually supporting environmental observations
can form difficult-to-falsify evidence structures.

## Evidence Integrity

The trustworthiness of an evidence structure based on time, location,
observation diversity, consistency, and review readiness.

## Traceability

The ability to follow evidence back to source observations, assumptions, and
review notes.

## Proof of Reality

The concept of verifying environmental reality through relationship consistency
and multi-source evidence.
