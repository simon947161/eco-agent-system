# Review Revision History

## Purpose

Review Revision History defines how changes to Reality Claims and Proof Records
may be documented.

## Revision History Should Record

- prior claim wording
- new claim wording
- evidence that triggered revision
- confidence change
- conflict change
- review notes
- governance relevance
- unresolved questions
- reviewer context

## Revision Principle

Revision is part of review quality.

A changed claim is not automatically a failed claim. It may reflect improved
understanding.

## Relationship To Review Workflow

The [Review Workflow Layer](../review_workflow_layer/README.md) defines
revision as a normal part of review.

## Boundary

No versioning runtime, immutable ledger, blockchain, or automated revision
system is implemented.
