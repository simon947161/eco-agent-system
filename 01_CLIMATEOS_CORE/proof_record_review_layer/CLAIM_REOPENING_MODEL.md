# Claim Reopening Model

## Purpose

The Claim Reopening Model defines when a prior Reality Claim or Proof Record
may need renewed review.

## Reopening Principle

Future evidence can reopen previous conclusions.

Reopening is a strength of ClimateOS review, not a failure.

## Reopening Triggers

- new Evidence Package appears
- prior evidence is challenged
- source provenance changes
- confidence decays
- Collective Validation raises concern
- minority or whistleblower signal appears
- forecast candidate conflicts with observation
- governance context changes
- EcoChain readiness changes

## Reopening Outcome

Reopening may result in:

- strengthened claim
- weakened claim
- unresolved claim
- split hypotheses
- merged claim
- superseded claim
- updated Proof Record

## Boundary

No automatic reopening engine, alert system, API, or governance action is
implemented.
