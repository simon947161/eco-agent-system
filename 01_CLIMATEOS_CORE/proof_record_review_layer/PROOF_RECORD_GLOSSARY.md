# Proof Record Glossary

## Reality Claim

A statement about natural, operational, governance, or observed reality that can
be reviewed, supported, weakened, reopened, split, merged, or left unresolved.

## Proof Record

A living review record connecting a Reality Claim with evidence, confidence,
validation context, revision history, and current status.

## Claim Status

The current review state of a Reality Claim or Proof Record.

## Strengthen

To gain support through better evidence, improved traceability, or stronger
validation context.

## Weaken

To lose support because of conflicting evidence, weaker provenance, uncertainty,
or failed assumptions.

## Reopen

To return a previously reviewed claim to active review because new evidence or
conflict appears.

## Split

To divide one claim into competing hypotheses when evidence supports multiple
interpretations.

## Merge

To combine claims when new evidence shows that they describe the same reality or
relationship.

## Living Record

A record that remains open to future evidence, revision, and review.
