# Reality Claim Review Model

## Purpose

The Reality Claim Review Model defines how ClimateOS may conceptually review a
claim about reality.

## Reality Claim

A Reality Claim is a statement about natural, operational, governance, or
observed reality that may be supported, weakened, revised, or left unresolved.

## Review Questions

- What is being claimed?
- What evidence supports the claim?
- What evidence weakens the claim?
- What Evidence Packages are related?
- What Proof Records are related?
- What uncertainty remains?
- What confidence state applies?
- Has the claim changed over time?
- Should the claim remain open, split, merge, or reopen?

## Review Status

Review status is current and revisable.

It should not be treated as permanent truth.

## Boundary

No automated claim review, truth engine, scoring system, or API is implemented.
