# Proof Record Review Model

## Purpose

The Proof Record Review Model defines how a Proof Record may be reviewed as a
living record.

## Proof Record

A Proof Record is a structured review record that connects a Reality Claim with
evidence, confidence, validation context, review history, and current status.

## Review Dimensions

- claim clarity
- evidence package support
- source traceability
- confidence state
- conflicting evidence
- Collective Validation signals
- revision history
- governance relevance
- EcoChain readiness

## Living Record Principle

Proof Records are never permanently frozen.

They may be updated when new evidence, conflict, confidence change, or review
context appears.

## Boundary

No proof ledger, blockchain, automated validation system, or immutable runtime
is created.
