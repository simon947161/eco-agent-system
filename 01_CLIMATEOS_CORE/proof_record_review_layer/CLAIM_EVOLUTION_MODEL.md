# Claim Evolution Model

## Purpose

The Claim Evolution Model defines how Reality Claims may change over time.

## Evolution Patterns

Reality Claims may:

- strengthen
- weaken
- remain unresolved
- reopen
- split into competing hypotheses
- merge with new evidence
- be superseded by a better claim

## Competing Hypotheses

When evidence supports different interpretations, a Reality Claim may split
into competing hypotheses rather than forcing premature certainty.

## Claim Merge

Claims may merge when new evidence shows that separate claims describe the same
underlying reality or relationship.

## Boundary

No hypothesis engine, automated reasoning, scoring, or claim graph runtime is
created.
