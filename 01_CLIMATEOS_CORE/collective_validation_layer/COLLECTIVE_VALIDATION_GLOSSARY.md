# Collective Validation Glossary

## Evidence-Weighted Collective Validation

Collective judgment based on evidence quality, validation, uncertainty, and
confidence rather than institutional hierarchy alone.

## Authority-Weighted Decision

A decision primarily shaped by rank, institutional status, or hierarchy.

## Evidence-Weighted Decision

A decision shaped by validated evidence and confidence context.

## Direction Before Authority

The principle that ecological direction should be reviewed before
organizational authority.

## Collective Judgment

Shared confidence context produced through evidence review and validation. It
is not an automated decision.

## Minority Signal

A signal that lacks majority support but may still be important.
