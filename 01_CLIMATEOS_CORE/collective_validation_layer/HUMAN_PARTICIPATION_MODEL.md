# Human Participation Model

## Purpose

The Human Participation Model defines how people may contribute to collective
validation.

## Human Participants

- domain experts
- local community members
- volunteers
- witnesses
- project owners
- governance reviewers
- researchers
- reviewers with lived experience

## Human Contributions

- context
- observation
- review
- interpretation
- disagreement
- caution
- governance judgment

## Boundary

No human credentialing system, voting system, or authority hierarchy is
implemented.
