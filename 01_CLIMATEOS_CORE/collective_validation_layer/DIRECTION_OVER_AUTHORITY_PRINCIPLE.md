# Direction Before Authority Principle

## Principle

ClimateOS should first evaluate whether Reality is moving toward or away from
ecological improvement before considering organizational authority.

## Meaning

Direction Before Authority asks:

```text
Is the system moving toward ecological improvement?
Is the system moving away from ecological improvement?
What evidence supports that direction?
```

Only after direction is understood should institutional authority, hierarchy,
or governance position be considered.

## Relationship To EcoEngine

This principle reflects EcoEngine's long-term philosophy that better outcomes
and ecological improvement matter more than formal status alone.

## Boundary

This is a governance and validation principle, not a runtime rule engine.
