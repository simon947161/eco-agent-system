# Collective Validation Governance

## Purpose

Collective Validation Governance defines principles for evidence-weighted
collective judgment.

## Principles

- evidence quality matters more than hierarchy
- authority should not override validated reality
- minority signals should be preserved
- witnesses should not be dismissed automatically
- AI Agents assist but do not decide
- forecasts remain Forecast Candidates until validated
- Earth Intelligence outputs remain provider outputs until reviewed
- human action remains accountable

## Boundary

No governance runtime, voting system, token system, or automated decision
system is implemented.
