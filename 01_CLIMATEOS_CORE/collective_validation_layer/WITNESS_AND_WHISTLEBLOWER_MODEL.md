# Witness And Whistleblower Model

## Purpose

The Witness and Whistleblower Model protects minority or early reality signals
from being dismissed only because they lack institutional status.

## Witness Signals

Witnesses may contribute:

- direct observation
- local context
- event reports
- anomaly reports
- risk signals

## Whistleblower Signals

Whistleblowers may contribute:

- hidden risk signals
- governance failure signals
- suppressed evidence claims
- contradiction of official narratives

## Principle

A single witness does not automatically equal false.

Signals should be preserved, reviewed, and cross-validated where possible.

## Boundary

No legal process, protection system, identity system, or reporting platform is
implemented.
