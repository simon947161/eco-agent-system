# Participant Model

## Purpose

The Participant Model defines who may conceptually contribute to collective
validation.

## Participant Types

- human experts
- community members
- volunteers
- witnesses
- whistleblowers
- AI Agents
- specialized agents
- Earth Intelligence providers
- forecast providers
- future autonomous systems

## Participation Principle

Participation does not imply equal authority.

Each participant may contribute evidence, observations, reasoning, context, or
review signals.

## Boundary

No identity system, voting system, credentialing system, or authorization
runtime is implemented.
