# Evidence Traceability Model

## Purpose

The Evidence Traceability Model defines how ClimateOS may preserve the path
from evidence back to source, context, and review history.

## Traceability Questions

- Where did the evidence come from?
- Who or what provided it?
- When was it observed or recorded?
- What system, place, or relationship does it describe?
- What transformations occurred?
- What assumptions were added?
- What review has already occurred?
- What evidence conflicts with it?

## Provenance

Provenance records the origin and handling history of evidence.

An Evidence Package should preserve provenance without treating provenance as
automatic proof of truth.

## EcoChain Relationship

EcoChain may later support validated evidence chain concepts. Evidence Package
Review prepares the review language needed before any future chain or proof
system is treated as operational.

## Boundary

No blockchain, ledger, hash engine, chain runtime, or automated traceability
system is implemented.
