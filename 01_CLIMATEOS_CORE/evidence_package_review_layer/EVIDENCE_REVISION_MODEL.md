# Evidence Revision Model

## Purpose

The Evidence Revision Model defines how an Evidence Package may remain open to
future evidence and correction.

## Revision Principle

Evidence Package Review is never permanently closed.

Better evidence may update, weaken, strengthen, split, merge, or reopen an
Evidence Package.

## Revision Triggers

- new observation appears
- provenance is clarified
- source is challenged
- context changes
- new relationship is identified
- conflicting evidence appears
- Reality Claim is reopened
- Proof Record status changes
- confidence changes

## Revision Notes

Revision should preserve:

- what changed
- why it changed
- what evidence triggered the revision
- what uncertainty remains
- whether validation readiness changed

## Boundary

No automated revision engine, versioning runtime, or update mechanism is
implemented.
