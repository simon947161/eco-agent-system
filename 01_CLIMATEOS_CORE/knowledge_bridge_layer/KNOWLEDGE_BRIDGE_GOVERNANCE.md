# Knowledge Bridge Governance

## Purpose

Knowledge Bridge Governance defines review principles for future knowledge
bridge work.

## Principles

- preserve provider independence
- keep Obsidian optional
- keep GitHub compatibility
- preserve local knowledge portability
- avoid hidden synchronization
- avoid treating external notes as validated evidence
- preserve citations and source context
- require human review for conflict resolution

## Governance Boundary

The bridge may support knowledge movement, but it must not create automated
governance decisions.

## Boundary

No access control, compliance engine, policy runtime, or automated governance
enforcement is implemented.
