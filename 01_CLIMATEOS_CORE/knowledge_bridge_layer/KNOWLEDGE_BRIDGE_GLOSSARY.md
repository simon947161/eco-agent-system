# Knowledge Bridge Glossary

## Knowledge Bridge

A conceptual interface between ClimateOS Knowledge Runtime and external
knowledge systems.

## Obsidian Bridge

A future bridge concept for Obsidian-style knowledge gardens. Not implemented
in Task77.

## Provider Independence

The principle that ClimateOS should depend on interfaces, not individual
knowledge platforms.

## Synchronization

The future movement or comparison of knowledge between provider systems and
ClimateOS. Not implemented in Task77.

## Mapping

The conceptual translation of provider-specific structures into ClimateOS
Knowledge Object concepts.

## Conflict

A mismatch between versions, sources, classifications, references, or
interpretations that requires review.
