# Governance Coordination

## Purpose

Governance coordination defines how ClimateOS may help participants align
evidence, responsibilities, feedback, and action.

## Coordination Modes

- Bottom-up participation
- Top-down participation
- Peer-to-peer participation
- Community-driven participation
- AI-assisted participation
- Hybrid participation

## Governance As Coordination

Governance should support:

- Shared context
- Evidence review
- Feedback loops
- Responsibility mapping
- Transparent assumptions
- Collective learning
- Resilience improvement

## Boundary

No governance runtime, authority model, compliance engine, or automated decision
system is implemented.
