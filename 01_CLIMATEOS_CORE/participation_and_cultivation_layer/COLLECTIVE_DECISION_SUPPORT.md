# Collective Decision Support

## Purpose

Collective decision support helps participants understand evidence and
coordinate action without replacing human judgement.

## Support Functions

- Shared evidence summaries
- Assumption visibility
- Option framing
- Risk and uncertainty notes
- Stakeholder context
- Review status tracking
- Action learning records

## Principle

ClimateOS should support decisions. It should not make decisions for
participants.

## Boundary

No recommendation engine, voting system, approval workflow, or automated
decision process is implemented.
