# Participation Levels

## Purpose

Participation levels describe different ways participants may engage with
ClimateOS.

## Levels

### Observer

Provides observations, notes, photos, or local signals.

### Contributor

Adds structured context, records, or repeated observations.

### Reviewer

Helps check evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, or interpretation.

### Coordinator

Helps align people, tasks, evidence, and governance needs.

### Steward

Supports long-term maintenance, restoration, monitoring, or resilience work.

### AI-Assisted Participant

Supports summarization, documentation, organization, or review preparation
under human oversight.

## Boundary

No user role system, permission model, or automated authority is implemented.
