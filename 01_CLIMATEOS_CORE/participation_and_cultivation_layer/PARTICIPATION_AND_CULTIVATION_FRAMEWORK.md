# Participation and Cultivation Framework

## Purpose

This framework defines how ClimateOS may support evidence-based participation
and cultivation across many participant types.

## Core Questions

- Who participates?
- What can they contribute?
- How are contributions observed?
- How are contributions validated?
- How are contributions coordinated?
- How do contributions improve resilience?

## Governance Meaning

Governance means coordination, alignment, feedback, and collective action.

It should support bottom-up, top-down, peer-to-peer, community-driven,
AI-assisted, and hybrid participation models.

## Cultivation Meaning

Cultivation means long-term improvement of environmental and social resilience.

It includes observation, stewardship, learning, restoration, maintenance,
adaptation, and shared environmental memory.

## Boundary

This is an architecture framework only. It does not implement participation
tools, governance automation, or decision-making authority.
