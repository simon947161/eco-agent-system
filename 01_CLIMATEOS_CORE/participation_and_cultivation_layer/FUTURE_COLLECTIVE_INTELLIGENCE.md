# Future Collective Intelligence

## Purpose

Future Collective Intelligence describes how ClimateOS may eventually help
participants learn together from evidence, validation, feedback, and action.

## Concept

Collective intelligence may emerge when many participants contribute different
forms of observation, knowledge, review, and stewardship.

## Future Capabilities

- Shared environmental memory
- Multi-participant evidence review
- AI-assisted documentation
- Local and scientific knowledge alignment
- Resilience learning loops
- Governance feedback records
- Cultivation history

## Principle

Collective intelligence should remain transparent, participatory, and
human-reviewable.

## Boundary

This is a future vision document only. No collective intelligence runtime,
machine learning system, or automated decision system is implemented.
