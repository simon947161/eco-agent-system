# Participant Types

## Purpose

Participant types define who may contribute to ClimateOS participation and
cultivation systems.

## Participant Groups

- Farmers
- Land managers
- Gardeners
- Volunteers
- Community members
- Researchers
- Scientists
- NGOs
- Governments
- Businesses
- AI agents
- Future participants

## Principle

No single participant group owns environmental reality.

ClimateOS should allow different participants to contribute different kinds of
observation, knowledge, review, action, and coordination.

## Boundary

This document does not create accounts, permissions, roles, or access control.
