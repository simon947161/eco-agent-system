# Community Contributions

## Purpose

Community contributions preserve local observation, lived experience, and
place-based knowledge.

## Examples

- Vegetation stress notes
- Flood or water level observations
- Local heat stress reports
- Photos of recovery or decline
- Fire, storm, drought, or pest impact notes
- Restoration and maintenance activity records

## Review Needs

Community contributions should preserve date, location, source context,
uncertainty, privacy considerations, and review status.

## Principle

Community knowledge can strengthen ClimateOS when it is respected, traceable,
and reviewed alongside other evidence sources.

## Boundary

No community portal, upload feature, moderation workflow, or automated
validation is implemented.
