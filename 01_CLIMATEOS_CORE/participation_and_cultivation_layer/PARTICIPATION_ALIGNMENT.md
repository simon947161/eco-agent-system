# Participation Alignment

## Purpose

Participation alignment explains how this layer remains compatible with the
wider ClimateOS architecture.

## ClimateOS

Participation adds human and AI contribution context to the governance runtime
architecture.

## EcoEngine

EcoEngine may provide scientific computation outputs that participants review
and interpret through ClimateOS.

## CCZPS

CCZPS provides climate zone context for participation and cultivation.

## ESG++

ESG++ helps translate validated environmental reality into governance and
reporting language.

## EcoChain

EcoChain may preserve validated evidence and contribution history in future
systems.

records.
