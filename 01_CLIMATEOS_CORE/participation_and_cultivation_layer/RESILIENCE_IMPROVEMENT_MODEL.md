# Resilience Improvement Model

## Purpose

The Resilience Improvement Model explains how contributions may support
long-term environmental and social resilience.

## Conceptual Flow

```text
Observation
-> Contribution
-> Evidence
-> Validation
-> Coordination
-> Action Learning
-> Resilience Improvement
```

## Improvement Areas

- Ecological recovery
- Water resilience
- Heat resilience
- Fire resilience
- Soil health
- Community preparedness
- Maintenance quality
- Governance learning

## Boundary

No resilience scoring, forecasting, optimization, or automated intervention
selection is implemented.
