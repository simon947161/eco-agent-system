# Cultivation Principles

## Purpose

Cultivation principles define how ClimateOS frames long-term resilience
improvement.

## Principles

### Long-Term Stewardship

Cultivation values repeated care, monitoring, and adaptive maintenance.

### Evidence-Based Action

Cultivation should be informed by observations, relationships, evidence, and
validation.

### Local Context

Cultivation should respect climate zone, landscape, community, and cultural
context.

### Feedback And Learning

Cultivation should preserve what was tried, what changed, and what was learned.

### Shared Responsibility

Cultivation can involve farmers, land managers, communities, governments,
businesses, researchers, and AI agents.

## Boundary

No cultivation program, operational workflow, or intervention plan is
implemented.
