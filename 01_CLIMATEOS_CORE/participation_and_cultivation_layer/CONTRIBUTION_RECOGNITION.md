# Contribution Recognition

## Purpose

Contribution recognition defines how ClimateOS may acknowledge participant
contributions without implying ownership, authority, or financial reward.

## Recognition Concepts

- Attribution
- Contribution history
- Review participation
- Stewardship records
- Community knowledge preservation
- Scientific review acknowledgement
- AI-assisted documentation trace

## Governance Considerations

Recognition should respect privacy, consent, cultural knowledge, data ownership,
and local governance context.

## Boundary

No token system, payment system, blockchain record, leaderboard, or reputation
engine is implemented.
