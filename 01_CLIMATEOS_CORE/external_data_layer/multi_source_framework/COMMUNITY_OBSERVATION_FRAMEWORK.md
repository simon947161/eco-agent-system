# Community Observation Framework

## Purpose

Community observation allows local people to contribute observations that may
support ClimateOS evidence pathways.

## Examples

- Vegetation stress notes
- Local flooding observations
- Heat stress reports
- Photos of landscape condition
- Fire, storm, or drought impact notes
- Recovery and restoration observations

## Governance Requirements

Community observations should preserve:

- Date and time
- Location
- Observer context where appropriate
- Photo or note references
- Consent and privacy considerations
- Uncertainty and review notes

## Boundary

No community reporting tool, upload system, moderation workflow, or automated
validation is implemented.
