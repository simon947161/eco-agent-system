# Observation Consistency Model

## Purpose

The Observation Consistency Model defines concepts for comparing observations
across providers.

## Consistency Dimensions

### Spatial Consistency

Do observations refer to compatible locations or spatial scales?

### Temporal Consistency

Do observations refer to compatible dates, times, seasons, or event windows?

### Measurement Consistency

Are units, methods, and variable definitions comparable?

### Relationship Consistency

Do observations support the same environmental relationship?

### Narrative Consistency

Do human notes, photos, or community reports support or challenge measured
signals?

## Boundary

This model is conceptual. It does not calculate consistency scores.
