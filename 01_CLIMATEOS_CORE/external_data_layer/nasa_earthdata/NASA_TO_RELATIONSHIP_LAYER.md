# NASA To Relationship Layer

## Purpose

This document explains how NASA inputs may support ClimateOS relationship
analysis.

## Relationship Examples

- Rainfall <-> Soil Moisture
- Soil Moisture <-> Vegetation
- Vegetation <-> Fire Risk
- Elevation <-> Water Flow
- Biomass <-> Carbon Storage

## Interpretation

NASA resources may provide observation inputs that help ClimateOS understand
relationships between environmental systems.

The Relationship Layer should preserve context rather than treating a dataset
as a standalone conclusion.

## Boundary

No relationship analysis, calculations, or automated interpretation is
implemented.
