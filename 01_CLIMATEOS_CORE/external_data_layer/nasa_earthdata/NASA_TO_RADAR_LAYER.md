# NASA To Radar Layer

## Purpose

This document explains how NASA resources may support ClimateOS radar concepts.

## Example Radar Concepts

- New anomaly detection
- Extreme event monitoring
- Fire risk signals
- Vegetation stress signals
- Hydrological stress signals

## Interpretation

NASA resources may help future ClimateOS systems notice change, disturbance, or
emerging risk signals.

Radar signals should remain evidence candidates until they are reviewed in
context.

## Boundary

No monitoring implementation, alerting, web crawling, forecasting, or automated
radar engine is implemented.
