# Multi Source Observation Alignment

## Purpose

This document explains how NASA observations may be compared with other sources
in future ClimateOS systems.

## Potential Sources

- BOM
- Copernicus
- ECMWF
- Destination Earth
- Local sensors
- Community observations
- Open-Meteo

## Purpose Of Alignment

Multi-source comparison can support future evidence integrity by showing where
sources agree, disagree, or describe different parts of the same environmental
condition.

## Future Review Questions

- Do multiple sources support the same pattern?
- Are differences caused by resolution, timing, method, or uncertainty?
- Which source is most suitable for the intended use case?
- What limitations should be preserved during validation?

## Boundary

No source comparison, external integration, or data validation is implemented.
