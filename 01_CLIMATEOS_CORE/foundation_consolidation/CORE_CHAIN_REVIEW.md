# Core Chain Review

## Purpose

This review explains the main reality-to-value chain:

```text
Reality
-> RDA
-> Proof of Reality
-> Evidence Asset
-> EcoChain
-> RWA Alignment
```

## Reality

Reality includes natural, operational, governance, and planning reality.

Examples include rainfall, vegetation stress, infrastructure condition,
resource flows, community observation, governance records, and planning
questions.

Reality is not automatically trusted. It must be observed, recorded, reviewed,
and validated.

## RDA

Reality Data Assets are traceable records derived from real-world processes.

RDA does not mean a token, financial product, or blockchain object. In
ClimateOS, it means a structured reality-linked record that can later support
evidence, validation, and governance review.

## Proof Of Reality

Proof of Reality is a structured proof mechanism for reality claims.

It asks whether a claim has evidence packages, source diversity, cross-
validation, confidence classification, traceability, and human review.

## Evidence Asset

An Evidence Asset is a validated and contextualized evidence object.

It combines reality, data, evidence, context, validation, history, and
confidence. It is still a documentation foundation, not a live asset system.

## EcoChain

EcoChain is the Validated Evidence Chain.

It preserves traceability, validation history, evidence relationships, and
future Proof of Reality alignment. It is not currently a blockchain
implementation.

## RWA Alignment

RWA Alignment reviews whether proven real-world value is ready to be considered
for future alignment with real-world asset concepts.

It does not create financial products, investment advice, tokenization systems,
or trading systems.
