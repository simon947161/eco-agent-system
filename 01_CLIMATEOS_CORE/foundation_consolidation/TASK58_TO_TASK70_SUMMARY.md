## Summary

Task58 through Task70 created a documentation foundation, not a completed
runtime. The strongest current achievement is architectural traceability:
contributors can now follow a concept from reality observation through
relationships, radar, evidence, validation, proof, RDA, Evidence Asset,
EcoChain, RWA alignment, and participation.
