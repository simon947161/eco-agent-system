# Foundation Glossary

## Reality

Natural, operational, governance, and planning conditions or events that exist
outside the documentation itself.

## Observation

A record of what happened or what was noticed.

## Relationship

A connection between environmental, operational, social, or governance factors.

## Radar

A change detection framework that identifies signals worth attention.

## Evidence

A reviewable structure that connects observations, context, relationships, and
validation requirements.

## Validation

The evaluation of evidence quality, assumptions, uncertainty, confidence,
traceability, and calculation transparency.

## Proof Of Reality

A structured mechanism for evaluating whether reality claims are supported by
evidence packages, source diversity, confidence, and traceability.

## RDA

Reality Data Asset. A traceable record generated from real-world processes.

## Evidence Asset

A contextualized and validated evidence object that combines reality, data,
evidence, validation, history, and confidence.

## EcoChain

Validated Evidence Chain. A traceability concept, not currently a blockchain
implementation.

## RWA Alignment

Review of whether proven real-world value may be ready for future real-world
asset alignment. It is not a financial product or tokenization system.

## Participation

Evidence-based contribution, review, coordination, and collective action by
humans, organizations, communities, researchers, governments, businesses, and
AI agents.

## Cultivation

Long-term improvement of environmental and social resilience.

## Forecast Candidate

A future forecast output treated as an input for validation and planning, not
as truth.

## Knowledge Interface

A future interface for project knowledge, CRP notes, Obsidian, Knowledge
Garden, and related structured records.
