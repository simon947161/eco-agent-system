# Validation IO Glossary

## Purpose

This document provides a glossary of terms for Validation IO Model in ClimateOS Foundation.

The glossary ensures consistent terminology across validation IO documentation and implementation.

## A

**Actionability**: The degree to which validation output can be acted upon by governance or operations.

**Aggregation**: The process of combining multiple inputs into a single output.

**Archival**: The process of storing inputs or outputs after processing.

**Architecture**: The overall structure and organization of ClimateOS Foundation.

**Assembly**: The process of assembling validation packs from outputs.

## B

**Batch**: A collection of inputs or outputs processed together.

**Benchmark**: A standard or reference point for validation comparison.

**Blockchain**: A distributed ledger technology (not implemented in Foundation).

**Boundary**: The scope limit of validation IO model.

**Branch**: A parallel path in input or output flow.

## C

**Classification**: The process of categorizing inputs or outputs by type, confidence, urgency, etc.

**Coherence**: The quality of being logical, consistent, and systematic across the system.

**Complexity**: The degree of intricacy or difficulty in processing inputs or outputs.

**Confidence**: The degree of certainty or reliability in inputs, outputs, or validation results.

**Confirmation**: The acknowledgment of output delivery.

**Context**: The circumstances or setting in which inputs or outputs exist.

**Continuity**: The unbroken sequence of evidence chain or traceability.

**Credential**: Evidence of authority or reliability.

## D

**Data**: Information used in validation processes (not implemented as runtime).

**Delivery**: The process of sending outputs to destinations.

**Dependency**: A relationship where one element relies on another.

**Destination**: The target location or system for output delivery.

**Documentation**: Written description of validation IO model (this is documentation-only foundation).

## E

**Evidence**: Information that supports validation inputs or outputs.

**Evidence Chain**: The linked sequence of evidence supporting inputs or outputs.

**Evidence Weight**: The strength or importance of evidence.

**Evolution**: The process of gradual development or improvement.

**Expansion**: The process of generating multiple outputs from a single input.

## F

**Feedback**: Information about output quality or actionability collected from users or systems.

**Flow**: The movement of inputs or outputs through validation processes.

**Foundation**: The base or groundwork for ClimateOS (this is Foundation documentation).

**Framework**: A basic structure underlying validation IO model.

## G

**Generation**: The process of creating outputs from validation processes.

**Governance**: The processes and systems for making governance decisions.

**Governance Readiness**: The degree to which outputs are ready for governance use.

**Glossary**: A list of terms and definitions (this document).

## H

**Heterogeneous**: Composed of different types or kinds (e.g., heterogeneous batch).

**Homogeneous**: Composed of same type or kind (e.g., homogeneous batch).

**Human-Readable**: Capable of being read and understood by humans.

## I

**Implementation**: The process of building runtime software (not part of Foundation documentation).

**Input**: Information or data entering validation process.

**Input Classification**: Categorization of validation inputs.

**Input Flow**: Movement of inputs through validation process.

**Input Object Model**: Structured representation of validation inputs.

**Integration**: The process of combining or coordinating system components.

**Integrity**: The quality of being whole, complete, and unaltered.

## J

**Judgment**: The process of forming an opinion or evaluation.

**Justification**: The reasoned basis for validation decisions or outputs.

## K

**Knowledge**: Information, understanding, or skills gained through experience or education.

**Knowledge Object**: Structured representation of knowledge in Knowledge Runtime.

**Knowledge Runtime**: The system managing knowledge objects in ClimateOS.

## L

**Layer**: A distinct level or stratum in ClimateOS architecture.

**Lifecycle**: The series of stages in the life of an input or output.

**Lineage**: The line of descent or development from ancestor to descendant (similar to traceability).

## M

**Metadata**: Data that provides information about other data.

**Model**: A representation of a system or concept.

**Multi-Source**: Coming from or involving multiple sources.

## N

**Network**: A complex system of interconnected or related inputs and outputs.

**Node**: A point in a network where lines or pathways intersect.

## O

**Object Model**: A representation of objects and their relationships.

**Observation**: The action or process of observing something.

**Observation Layer**: The layer in ClimateOS that manages observation records.

**Output**: Information or data exiting validation process.

**Output Classification**: Categorization of validation outputs.

**Output Flow**: Movement of outputs through delivery process.

**Output Object Model**: Structured representation of validation outputs.

## P

**Pack**: A collection of validation outputs assembled for delivery.

**Packaging**: The process of preparing outputs for delivery.

**Participation**: The action or fact of taking part in something.

**Pipeline**: A sequence of processing stages for inputs or outputs.

**Priority**: The level of importance or urgency.

**Process**: A series of actions or steps taken to achieve a particular end.

**Propagation**: The spreading or transmission of confidence, uncertainty, or revision.

## Q

**Quality**: The standard of something as measured against other things.

**Queue**: A line or sequence of inputs or outputs waiting for processing.

## R

**Relationship**: The way in which two or more inputs, outputs, or concepts are connected.

**Revision**: The process of revising or modifying inputs or outputs.

**Revision History**: The record of changes made to inputs or outputs.

**Review**: A formal assessment or examination.

**Review Engine**: The system that forms evidence-driven judgment in ClimateOS.

**Revision Ready**: Capable of being revised or updated.

**Routing**: The process of sending inputs to appropriate validation pipelines.

**Rule**: A principle or regulation governing conduct or procedure.

**Runtime**: The period during which a program is running (not implemented in Foundation).

## S

**Scalability**: The capacity to be changed in size or scale (not implemented in Foundation).

**Source**: The origin or starting point of inputs.

**Standard**: A level of quality or achievement.

**Storage**: The process of storing inputs or outputs (not implemented in Foundation).

**Structure**: The arrangement of and relations between the parts of something complex.

**Submission**: The action of presenting inputs for validation.

**System**: A set of connected things or parts forming a complex whole.

**System Map**: A visual or textual representation of system components and relationships.

## T

**Task**: A piece of work to be done (e.g., Task93, Task94).

**Template**: A preset format or structure.

**Traceability**: The ability to trace inputs, outputs, evidence, and decisions.

**Transformation**: The action of changing in form, appearance, or structure.

**Transparency**: The quality of being open, honest, and accountable.

## U

**Uncertainty**: The state of being uncertain or not reliably known.

**Update**: A report of recent developments or changes.

**Upload**: The process of transferring data to a system (not implemented in Foundation).

**Urgency**: The importance requiring swift action.

**Usage**: The action of using something.

## V

**Validation**: The action of checking or proving the validity of something.

**Validation IO Model**: The model defining input and output for validation processes (this Task93).

**Validation Pack**: A structured collection of validation outputs.

**Verification**: The action of establishing the truth or accuracy of something.

**Version**: A particular form or variation of something.

**Version Control**: The management of changes to documents, programs, etc.

## W

**Weight**: The importance or influence of evidence.

**Workflow**: The sequence of industrial, administrative, or other processes.

## X

**XML**: Extensible Markup Language (not used in Foundation documentation).

## Y

**Yield**: The full amount of an agricultural or industrial product (not relevant to validation IO).

## Z

**Zero**: The absence of quantity or magnitude (not relevant to validation IO).

## Status

Documentation foundation only.

No runtime implementation, APIs, automated validation, scoring engine, workflow engine, or automated decisions.
