# Earth Intelligence Governance

## Purpose

Earth Intelligence Governance defines review principles for future provider
outputs.

## Principles

- preserve provider neutrality
- treat provider outputs as inputs, not truth
- validate observations and forecasts where possible
- document uncertainty
- preserve source and citation context
- avoid dependency on one provider
- avoid automated governance decisions
- preserve human review

## Boundary

No governance runtime, compliance engine, approval engine, or automated
decision system is implemented.
