# Earth Intelligence Glossary

## Earth Intelligence

Observation, simulation, forecast, digital twin, and provider knowledge about
Earth systems that may support ClimateOS.

## Provider Neutrality

The principle that ClimateOS remains independent from any individual provider.

## Observation Provider

A provider of observed Earth system data or records.

## Simulation Provider

A provider of scenario or process simulation outputs.

## Forecast Provider

A provider of future-oriented Forecast Candidates.

## Digital Twin Provider

A provider of digital twin context, simulation, or scenario information.

## Forecast Candidate

A forecast output that may support planning but is not truth until validated.

## Governance Runtime

Future ClimateOS runtime context for responding to validated evidence. Not
implemented in Task78.
