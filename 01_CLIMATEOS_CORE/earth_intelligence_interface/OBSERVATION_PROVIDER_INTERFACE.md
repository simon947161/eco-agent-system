# Observation Provider Interface

## Purpose

The Observation Provider Interface defines how future providers may supply
observational Earth information to ClimateOS.

## Conceptual Providers

- NASA EarthData
- Copernicus
- ECMWF observations
- local sensors
- community observation providers
- future Digital Earth platforms

## Possible Observation Types

- satellite observations
- meteorological observations
- hydrological observations
- land and vegetation observations
- community observations
- operational observations

## Boundary

No observation data is downloaded, authenticated, transformed, or ingested.
