# Digital Twin Provider Interface

## Purpose

The Digital Twin Provider Interface defines how future digital twin systems may
support ClimateOS.

## Conceptual Providers

- Destination Earth
- Earth-2
- future national or regional digital twins
- future project-level digital twins
- future infrastructure digital twins

## ClimateOS Role

ClimateOS may use digital twin outputs as scenario, observation, simulation, or
planning inputs after validation.

## Boundary

No digital twin runtime, simulation, API, or integration is implemented.
