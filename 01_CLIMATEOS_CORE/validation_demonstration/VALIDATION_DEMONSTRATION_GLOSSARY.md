# Validation Demonstration Glossary

## Purpose

This document provides a glossary of terms for Validation Demonstrations in ClimateOS Foundation.

## A

**Actionability**: The degree to which validation output supports governance or operational decisions.

**Application**: The act of applying IO models or benchmarks to validation scenarios.

## B

**Benchmark**: A standard or reference point for validation comparison (from Task94).

**Benchmark Application**: The process of applying a benchmark to a validation entity.

## C

**Classification**: The process of categorizing inputs or outputs by type, confidence, urgency, etc. (from Task93).

**Completeness**: The quality of being complete, with all required elements present.

**Confidence**: The degree of certainty or reliability in inputs, outputs, or validation results.

**Cross-Layer**: Involving or spanning multiple ClimateOS Foundation layers.

## D

**Demonstration**: A worked example showing how Foundation components apply to validation scenarios.

**Demonstration Framework**: The evaluation criteria and review process for demonstrations.

## E

**Evidence**: Information that supports validation inputs or outputs.

**Evidence Chain**: The linked sequence of evidence supporting inputs or outputs.

**Evidence Package**: A structured collection of evidence (from Task96).

**Evidence Validation**: The process of validating evidence objects or packages.

## F

**Flow Model**: The model describing how inputs or outputs move through validation processes (from Task93).

## G

**Governance Readiness**: The degree to which validation outputs are ready for governance use.

## I

**IO Model**: Input/Output model defining structure, classification, and flow (from Task93).

**IO Relationship**: The relationship between inputs and outputs in validation.

**Integration**: The process of combining or coordinating system components across layers.

## P

**Process Validation**: The process of validating validation processes themselves.

**Process Flow**: The sequence of stages in a validation process.

**Process Quality**: The quality of a validation process.

**Process Benchmark**: A benchmark for evaluating process quality.

## Q

**Quality Score**: A numerical representation of quality assessment.

**Quality Threshold**: The minimum quality score required for approval.

## R

**Reference Object**: A reusable conceptual object from Task96 used in demonstrations.

**Review Pack**: A pack containing review materials (from Task92).

## S

**System Map**: A visual or textual representation of system components and relationships.

## V

**Validation Demonstration**: A worked example showing practical validation using Foundation components.

**Validation Flow**: The complete sequence from input to output in validation.

**Validation Pack**: A structured collection of validation outputs (from Task92).

**Validation Result**: The output of a validation process.

## Status

Documentation foundation only.

No runtime implementation, APIs, automated validation, scoring engine, workflow engine, or automated decisions.
