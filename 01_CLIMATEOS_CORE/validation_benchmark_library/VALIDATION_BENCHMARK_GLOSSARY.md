# Validation Benchmark Glossary

## Purpose

This document provides a glossary of terms for Validation Benchmark Library in ClimateOS Foundation.

The glossary ensures consistent terminology across validation benchmark documentation and implementation.

## A

**Actionability**: The degree to which validation output can be acted upon by governance or operations.

**Applicability**: The scope or domain where benchmark applies.

**Approval**: The act of approving benchmark for release.

**Assessment**: The evaluation of entity against benchmark.

**Authority**: The body with power to approve benchmarks.

## B

**Baseline**: A standard for comparison.

**Batch**: A collection of entities processed together.

**Benchmark**: A standard or reference point for validation comparison.

**Benchmarking**: The process of comparing against benchmarks.

**Body**: A group of people with governance responsibility.

**Boundary**: The scope limit of benchmark library.

## C

**Comparison**: The act of comparing entity against benchmark.

**Completeness**: The quality of being complete.

**Complexity**: The degree of intricacy or difficulty.

**Criteria**: Standards by which something is judged or decided.

**Critical**: Expressing adverse or disapproving comments or judgments.

## D

**Decision**: A conclusion or resolution reached after consideration.

**Documentation**: Written description of benchmark (this is documentation-only foundation).

**Development**: The process of creating benchmark.

## E

**Evidence**: Information that supports benchmark development or application.

**Evaluation**: The making of a judgment about the amount, number, or value of something.

**Evolution**: The process of gradual development or improvement.

**Example**: A representative form or pattern.

## F

**Fairness**: The quality of treating people equally or in a way that is right or reasonable.

**Feedback**: Information about reactions to a product, a person's performance of a task, etc.

**Foundation**: The base or groundwork for ClimateOS (this is Foundation documentation).

**Framework**: A basic structure underlying benchmark library.

## G

**Governance**: The processes and systems for managing benchmark library.

**Guideline**: A general rule, principle, or piece of advice.

## H

**Homogeneous**: Composed of same type or kind (e.g., homogeneous batch).

**Human-Readable**: Capable of being read and understood by humans.

## I

**Implementation**: The process of building runtime software (not part of Foundation documentation).

**Improvement**: The act or process of making or becoming better.

**Integration**: The process of combining or coordinating system components.

**Integrity**: The quality of being whole, complete, and unaltered.

## J

**Judgment**: The ability to make considered decisions or come to sensible conclusions.

**Justification**: The action of showing something to be right or reasonable.

## K

**Knowledge**: Information, understanding, or skills gained through experience or education.

## L

**Layer**: A distinct level or stratum in ClimateOS architecture.

**Lifecycle**: The series of stages in the life of a benchmark.

**Library**: A collection of benchmarks and related documentation.

**Limitation**: A condition of limited ability or restricted access.

## M

**Maintenance**: The process of keeping benchmark library in good condition.

**Maturity**: The state or quality of being mature.

**Metadata**: Data that provides information about other data.

**Model**: A representation of a system or concept.

**Monitoring**: The act of observing and checking the progress or quality of something.

## N

**Navigation**: The process of planning and directing the route or course.

## O

**Object**: A material thing that can be seen and touched (in this context, benchmark object).

**Observation**: The action or process of observing something.

**Output**: Information or data exiting validation process.

**Oversight**: The action of overseeing something.

## P

**Process**: A series of actions or steps taken to achieve a particular end.

**Proposal**: A plan or suggestion put forward for consideration.

**Quality**: The standard of something as measured against other things.

**Query**: A question, especially one addressed to an official or organization.

## R

**Release**: The action of making benchmark available.

**Repository**: A place where benchmarks are stored.

**Revision**: The process of revising or modifying benchmarks.

**Review**: A formal assessment or examination.

**Revision Ready**: Capable of being revised or updated.

**Rubric**: A set of instructions or rules.

**Runtime**: The period during which a program is running (not implemented in Foundation).

## S

**Standard**: A level of quality or achievement.

**Standardization**: The process of implementing and developing technical standards.

**Storage**: The process of storing benchmarks (not implemented in Foundation).

**Structure**: The arrangement of and relations between the parts of something complex.

**Submission**: The action of presenting proposal for benchmark.

**System**: A set of connected things or parts forming a complex whole.

**System Map**: A visual or textual representation of system components and relationships.

## T

**Task**: A piece of work to be done (e.g., Task93, Task94).

**Template**: A preset format or structure.

**Traceability**: The ability to trace inputs, outputs, evidence, and decisions.

**Transparency**: The quality of being open, honest, and accountable.

## U

**Update**: A report of recent developments or changes.

**Upgrade**: The action of raising something to a higher standard.

**Use**: The action of using something.

**User**: A person who uses benchmark.

## V

**Validation**: The action of checking or proving the validity of something.

**Validity**: The quality of being logically or factually sound.

**Value**: The regard that something is held to deserve.

**Version**: A particular form or variation of something.

**Version Control**: The management of changes to documents, programs, etc.

## W

**Weight**: The importance or influence of criterion.

**Workflow**: The sequence of industrial, administrative, or other processes.

**Workforce**: The people engaged in work.

## X

**XML**: Extensible Markup Language (not used in Foundation documentation).

## Y

**Yield**: The full amount of an agricultural or industrial product (not relevant to validation benchmark).

## Z

**Zero**: The absence of quantity or magnitude (not relevant to validation benchmark).

## Status

Documentation foundation only.

No runtime implementation, APIs, automated validation, scoring engine, workflow engine, or automated decisions.
