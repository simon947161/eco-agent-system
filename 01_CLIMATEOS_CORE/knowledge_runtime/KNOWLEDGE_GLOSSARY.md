# Knowledge Glossary

## Knowledge Object

A structured knowledge unit that can be identified, referenced, versioned,
matured, and connected to ClimateOS layers.

## Knowledge Source

The origin or provider of a Knowledge Object.

## Knowledge Provider

A storage or knowledge system such as GitHub, Obsidian, local Markdown,
enterprise knowledge base, cloud knowledge system, or future knowledge API.

## Knowledge Index

A conceptual organization model for locating Knowledge Objects by topic,
project, layer, task, domain, evidence, governance, or runtime relationship.

## Knowledge Reference

A typed relationship between Knowledge Objects.

## Knowledge Citation

A traceable source reference that supports reproducibility.

## Knowledge Context

The project, location, climate zone, governance scope, assumptions, time, and
related evidence that prevent knowledge from being misused.

## Knowledge Maturity

The review state of a Knowledge Object, from Concept through Deprecated.

## Obsidian

A possible future Knowledge Provider. It is not implemented or automated in
Task73.
