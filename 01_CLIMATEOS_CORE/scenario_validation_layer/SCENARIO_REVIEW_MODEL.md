# Scenario Review Model

## Purpose

The Scenario Review Model defines how the ClimateOS Review Engine may review a
scenario.

## Review Questions

- What real-world condition does the scenario address?
- What Knowledge Objects support it?
- What Evidence Packages support it?
- What Proof Records are linked?
- What Forecast Candidates are used?
- What assumptions are explicit?
- What confidence state applies?
- What conflicts or uncertainties remain?
- Can the scenario be revised?

## Review Principle

ClimateOS reviews scenarios as planning objects, not as predictions that should
be trusted automatically.

## Relationship To Review Engine

The [Review Engine Layer](../review_engine_layer/README.md) forms the current
best evidence-supported review position. Scenario Review contributes to that
position without replacing human governance judgment.

## Boundary

No operational review engine, automated reasoning, scoring, or API is added.
