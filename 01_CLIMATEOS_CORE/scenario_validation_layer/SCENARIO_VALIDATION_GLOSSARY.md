# Scenario Validation Glossary

## Scenario

A planning object describing a possible future condition, pathway, or response.

## Scenario Output

A scenario result, projection, pathway, or planning statement. It is not truth.

## Forecast Candidate

A forecast output that may inform a scenario but must remain reviewable.

## Scenario Assumption

A premise used by a scenario that should be explicit and revisable.

## Scenario Confidence

The current level of support for using a scenario in review or recommendation
candidate contexts.

## Recommendation Candidate

A scenario-informed planning output that may support governance review but is
not an automated decision.

## Scenario Revision

The update or reopening of a scenario when evidence, assumptions, confidence,
or governance context changes.
