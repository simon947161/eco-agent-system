# Scenario Confidence Model

## Purpose

The Scenario Confidence Model defines how confidence may be represented for
scenario outputs.

## Confidence Inputs

Scenario confidence may consider:

- evidence quality
- assumption quality
- forecast candidate status
- proof record status
- independent confirmation
- uncertainty
- conflict
- revision history
- governance risk

## Confidence Principle

Confidence is not truth.

Scenario confidence describes the current level of support for using a scenario
in review, planning, or recommendation candidate contexts.

## Confidence Changes

Confidence may increase, decrease, decay, or remain unresolved as evidence
changes.

## Boundary

No confidence scoring engine, mathematical model, or automated confidence
update is implemented.
