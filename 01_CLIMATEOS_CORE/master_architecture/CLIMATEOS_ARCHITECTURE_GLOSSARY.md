# ClimateOS Architecture Glossary

## Reality Data Asset

Data generated from Natural Reality, Operational Reality, or Governance Reality.

## Evidence Asset

Reality + Data + Evidence + Context + Validation + History + Confidence.

## EcoChain

The future Validated Evidence Chain. It is not defined as a blockchain.

## Proof of Reality

A future trust structure based on traceable, validated evidence history.

## RDA

Reality Data Asset.

## RWA Alignment

Future mapping from trusted Earth-system related RDA and evidence structures
toward real-world asset contexts. It is not a financial product.

## NatureHash

A concept for many mutually supporting observations forming difficult-to-falsify
evidence structures. It is not a cryptographic implementation.
