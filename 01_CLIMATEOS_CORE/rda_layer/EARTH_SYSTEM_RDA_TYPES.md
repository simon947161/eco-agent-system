# Earth-System RDA Types

## Natural System RDA

Examples: rainfall data, soil moisture data, vegetation condition, water level,
fire risk, ecological recovery, biodiversity observation.

## Infrastructure RDA

Examples: road condition, building lifecycle data, water infrastructure data,
energy infrastructure data, maintenance records, asset performance data.

## Energy RDA

Examples: generation data, demand data, storage data, grid constraint data,
renewable output, energy resilience data.

## Water RDA

Examples: streamflow, dam level, irrigation demand, stormwater flow, flood
impact, drought stress.

## Land and Agriculture RDA

Examples: crop condition, soil condition, grazing pressure, biomass
availability, land-use change, erosion risk.

## Carbon and ESG RDA

Examples: emissions data, energy use data, material use data, carbon budget
data, ESG disclosure inputs, sustainability evidence.

## Operational Process RDA

Examples: project schedule, procurement records, construction progress,
production records, maintenance history, operating performance.

## Governance Process RDA

Examples: approvals, audits, compliance reviews, consultation records, policy
implementation, meeting records, inspection records.

## Planning and Scenario RDA

Examples: planning assumptions, scenario inputs, forecast candidates, option
comparison data, design alternatives, risk scenarios, future pathway analysis.

Forecast outputs must not be treated as truth. Forecast outputs should be
treated as planning support data or Forecast Candidates until validated.
