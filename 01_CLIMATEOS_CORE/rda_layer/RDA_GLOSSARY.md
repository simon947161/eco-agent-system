# RDA Glossary

## RDA

Reality Data Asset.

## Reality Data Asset

A structured, traceable, and verifiable data object derived from real-world
natural, operational, planning, or governance processes.

## Earth-System Related RDA

RDA connected to climate, water, land, energy, infrastructure, ecology,
resources, agriculture, community resilience, governance, operations, or
planning.

## Generic RWA

General real-world asset categories outside current ClimateOS core scope.

## Forecast Candidate

A forecast output treated as planning support data until validated.
