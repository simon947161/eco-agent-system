# Forecast To Proof Of Reality

## Purpose

This document explains the relationship between forecast outputs and Proof of
Reality.

## Principle

Forecasts are about possible future reality.

Proof of Reality is about whether a reality claim is credible.

```text
Forecast Candidate != Proof of Reality
```

## Future Validation Path

A forecast may later be compared with observed reality. If later observations
support or contradict the forecast, the forecast may become part of validation
history.

## Boundary

Forecasts cannot prove future reality. They can only become reviewable claims
or evidence components after validation.
