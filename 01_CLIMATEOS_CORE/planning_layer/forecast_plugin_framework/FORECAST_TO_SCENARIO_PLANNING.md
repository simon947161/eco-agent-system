# Forecast To Scenario Planning

## Purpose

Forecasts support planning by helping explore possible futures.

## Planning Questions

Forecasts may help ask:

- What may happen if this trend continues?
- What may happen under this scenario?
- What risks may increase?
- What capacity may be needed?
- What design option may perform better?
- What should be monitored next?

## Planning Uses

- pre-feasibility planning
- scenario comparison
- option testing
- risk review
- resilience planning
- project planning
- infrastructure planning
- resource-flow planning
- energy / water / land planning

## Boundary

Forecasts do not replace human judgment, governance, validation, or evidence
review.
