# Forecast Plugin Glossary

## Forecast Provider

An external model, method, system, expert process, or future engine that can
produce forecast outputs.

## Forecast Provider Candidate

A provider that may be considered for future ClimateOS planning support but is
not integrated into ClimateOS core.

## Forecast Candidate

A forecast output that may support planning but has not yet been validated
against observed reality.

## Planning Question

A structured question about possible future conditions, needs, risks, or
options.

## Scenario Input

A variable, assumption, condition, or record used to frame a future planning
scenario.

## Forecast Validation

Future review of forecast outputs against observed reality, other providers,
error, bias, uncertainty, and governance relevance.

## Forecast Confidence

A review status describing the reliability of a forecast for a stated scope.

## Planning / Scenario RDA Candidate

A structured planning record that may become a Reality Data Asset after
recording, review, and validation requirements are met.
