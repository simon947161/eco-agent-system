# Knowledge Workflow Glossary

## Knowledge Workflow

The conceptual movement of knowledge through ClimateOS layers.

## CRP

Conversation Radar output that captures knowledge points, ideas, decisions,
risks, questions, actions, and related keywords.

## Knowledge Object

A structured knowledge unit that can be identified, referenced, versioned,
matured, and connected to ClimateOS layers.

## Evidence Candidate

Knowledge or observation that may support evidence after review.

## Validation Review

Review of assumptions, confidence, uncertainty, evidence, and context.

## Planning Support

Use of reviewed knowledge to help frame scenarios, options, risks, and
decisions.

## Future Runtime Context

Reviewed knowledge that may later support agents or runtime systems.
