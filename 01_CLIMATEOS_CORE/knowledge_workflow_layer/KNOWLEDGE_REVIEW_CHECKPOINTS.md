# Knowledge Review Checkpoints

## Purpose

Review checkpoints help prevent weak knowledge from being mistaken for
validated evidence or runtime-ready context.

## Checkpoints

- source identified
- context preserved
- assumptions listed
- related ClimateOS layers identified
- related tasks identified
- evidence relationship described
- validation requirement described
- maturity status assigned
- governance relevance noted
- limitations recorded

## Review Questions

- Is this observation, assumption, interpretation, prediction, or recommendation?
- What evidence supports it?
- What remains uncertain?
- What could invalidate it?
- Is it ready for planning or runtime use?

## Boundary

No automated review or scoring is implemented.
