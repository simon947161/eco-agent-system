# Observation To CRP Workflow

## Purpose

This workflow explains how observations and discussions may become structured
Conversation Radar output.

## Flow

```text
Observation
-> Discussion
-> Conversation Radar
-> CRP
```

## CRP Outputs

A CRP may capture:

- knowledge points
- idea points
- decisions
- risks
- open questions
- next actions
- related project keywords

## Review Questions

- What was observed?
- Who or what reported it?
- What is assumed?
- What is uncertain?
- What needs validation?
- Which ClimateOS layer does it affect?

## Boundary

This workflow does not implement conversation capture or automated summarizing.
