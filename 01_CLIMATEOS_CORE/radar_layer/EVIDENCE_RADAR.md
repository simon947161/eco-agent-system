# Evidence Radar

## Purpose

Evidence Radar defines how new evidence candidates may be noticed and routed
for review.

## Evidence Sources

| Source | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Research Findings | Academic, technical, or professional findings that may affect understanding |
| Field Observations | Human observations from sites, projects, or field work |
| Sensor Observations | Instrument or system observations, where available |
| Community Observations | Place-based reports, photos, memories, and local experience |

## Evidence Candidate Flow

```text
New observation or source
  |
  v
Evidence candidate
  |
  v
Review
  |
  v
Evidence
```

## Limits

No automation, search engine, database, crawler, validation engine, or evidence
approval process is implemented.
