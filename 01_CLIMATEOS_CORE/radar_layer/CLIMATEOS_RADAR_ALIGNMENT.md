# ClimateOS Radar Alignment

## Observation Layer

Observation records reality and produces structured observation records.

## Relationship Layer

Relationship explains reality by connecting observations to system context,
forcing, flows, feedbacks, disturbance, recovery, and resilience.

## Radar Layer

Radar identifies what is changing and creates evidence candidates for review.

## Evidence Layer

Evidence records what can be proven after provenance, context, limitations,
and human review are established.

## Validation Layer

Validation evaluates whether evidence and interpretations are true enough for
their intended use.

## Governance Runtime

Governance responds to validated reality through human decisions,
accountability, and policy context.

## Alignment Rule

Radar is a bridge. It should not bypass observation, relationship, evidence,
validation, or governance review.

## Limits

This alignment does not implement radar runtime, validation runtime,
governance runtime, APIs, monitoring, or automated decisions.
