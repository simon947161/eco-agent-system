# Emerging Risk Radar

## Purpose

Emerging Risk Radar defines conceptual categories for risk-related change
signals. It does not forecast risk.

## Risk Concepts

| Risk Concept | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Heatwave | Track heat-related stress signals and review needs |
| Flood | Track water, rainfall, runoff, and inundation-related signals |
| Bushfire | Track dryness, vegetation, wind, heat, and disturbance signals |
| Drought | Track rainfall, soil moisture, vegetation stress, and water availability signals |
| Storm | Track wind, rainfall, damage, erosion, and community observation signals |

## Interpretation

Emerging risk signals should become evidence candidates only after source,
context, uncertainty, and human review are documented.

## Limits

No risk prediction, hazard model, warning system, alert engine, or automated
response is implemented.
