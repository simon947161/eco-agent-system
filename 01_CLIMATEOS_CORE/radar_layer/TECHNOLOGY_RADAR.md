# Technology Radar

## Purpose

Technology Radar defines conceptual tracking of technology change that may
affect future ClimateOS capabilities.

## Technology Examples

| Technology | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| NASA | Earth observation and climate data context |
| Copernicus | European Earth observation and environmental data context |
| Destination Earth | Future digital twin and scenario context |
| Earth-2 | Future high-performance Earth-system simulation context |
| OpenAI | Future language, reasoning, and interface capability context |
| Digital Twin | Representation of physical and ecological systems |
| Remote Sensing | Observation of land, vegetation, water, heat, and disturbance signals |

## Review Needs

Technology signals require source, maturity, licensing, reliability, evidence,
cost, governance, and integration review.

## Limits

No technology monitoring, integration, API, model, LLM workflow, or runtime is
implemented.
