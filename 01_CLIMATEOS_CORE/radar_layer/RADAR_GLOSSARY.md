# Radar Glossary

## Radar

A conceptual change detection framework that identifies signals worth human
attention.

## Signal

An observation, record, relationship, policy, technology, project, or
community note that may indicate change.

## Trigger

A reason a signal may need review, such as a threshold, trend, relationship,
disturbance, or governance condition.

## Trend

A repeated directional pattern across observations or records. A trend is not
a forecast.

## Anomaly

A signal that differs from expected context and requires review before
interpretation.

## Disturbance

An event or pressure that may disrupt system relationships, flows, feedbacks,
or recovery.

## Relationship Shift

A possible change in how system elements interact.

## Emerging Risk

A risk-related signal that may require evidence review and governance
attention. It is not a prediction.

## Evidence Candidate

A signal or record that may become evidence after provenance, context,
uncertainty, and human review.

## Policy Change

A change in policy context that may affect ClimateOS interpretation,
governance, or future work.

## Technology Change

A change in technology context that may affect future observation, evidence,
validation, or governance capabilities.
