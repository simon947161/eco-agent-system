# Climate Radar Framework

## Purpose

The Climate Radar Framework defines how ClimateOS identifies changes worth
attention.

```text
Observation
  |
  v
Relationship
  |
  v
Change Detection
  |
  v
Evidence Candidate
```

## Observation

Observation records reality: what happened, where, when, and under what
conditions.

## Relationship

Relationship explains reality by connecting observations to system context,
forcing, flows, feedbacks, disturbance, and recovery.

## Change Detection

Radar identifies signals that something may be changing. A change may relate
to environment, policy, evidence, technology, projects, governance, or
community context.

## Evidence Candidate

Radar output is not evidence by itself. It is an evidence candidate that may
need review, traceability, validation, and governance context.

## Limits

Radar does not forecast, monitor automatically, crawl the web, issue alerts,
rank priorities, or recommend action.
