# Policy Radar

## Purpose

Policy Radar defines how ClimateOS may conceptually track policy change
signals. It does not implement monitoring or legal interpretation.

## Policy Areas

| Policy Area | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Carbon Policy | Track carbon management and reporting policy context |
| Climate Policy | Track climate adaptation, mitigation, and governance policy context |
| Energy Policy | Track electricity, renewable energy, storage, and grid policy context |
| Water Policy | Track water management, resilience, and quality policy context |
| ESG Policy | Track disclosure, assurance, and governance policy context |

## Review Needs

Policy signals require source, jurisdiction, date, version, applicability,
limitations, and human review.

## Limits

No policy crawler, compliance engine, legal advice, regulatory filing, or
automated interpretation is implemented.
