# Relationship Shift Detection

## Purpose

Relationship shift detection identifies when a relationship may be changing.
It does not prove that the relationship changed.

## Relationship Change

A relationship change is a possible shift in how two or more system elements
interact.

Example:

```text
Rainfall increases
  |
  v
Soil moisture decreases
  |
  v
Potential relationship shift
```

This pattern may indicate timing, runoff, soil condition, vegetation demand,
measurement issues, or missing evidence. It is a review prompt, not a
conclusion.

## Review Needs

Relationship shifts require observation records, baseline context, evidence,
relationship principles, uncertainty notes, and validation.

## Limits

No calculations, thresholds, anomaly detection, causal attribution, or
forecasting are implemented.
