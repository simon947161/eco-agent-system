# Evidence Asset Readiness Model

## Purpose

The Evidence Asset Readiness Model defines how ClimateOS may conceptually
determine whether an Evidence Asset is ready for EcoChain readiness review.

## Readiness Conditions

An Evidence Asset may be considered ready when:

- source evidence is traceable
- Evidence Package Review is complete enough
- Proof Record Review is current
- confidence state is documented
- revision history exists
- unresolved conflicts are visible
- governance context is documented
- RDA or RWA alignment context is clear when relevant
- future revision remains possible

## Not Permanent Certification

Readiness does not mean permanent certification.

Readiness means the object is prepared to participate in the next conceptual
review stage.

## Readiness Outcomes

- ready for EcoChain readiness review
- not ready
- ready with unresolved risks
- needs additional evidence
- needs governance context
- reopened for revision

## Boundary

No readiness score, threshold, automated workflow, or certification mechanism is
defined.
