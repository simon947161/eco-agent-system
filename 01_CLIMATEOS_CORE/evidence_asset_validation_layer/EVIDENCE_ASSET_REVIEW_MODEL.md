# Evidence Asset Review Model

## Purpose

The Evidence Asset Review Model defines how the ClimateOS Review Engine may
review Evidence Assets as evolving review objects.

## Review Questions

- What Evidence Packages support the asset?
- What Proof Records are linked?
- What Reality Claims are involved?
- What RDA sources are relevant?
- What confidence state applies?
- What conflicts remain?
- What revision history exists?
- What governance context is attached?
- Is the asset ready for EcoChain readiness review?

## Review Position

Evidence Asset review produces a current review position, not permanent truth.

## Relationship To ClimateOS Review Engine

The Review Engine integrates Evidence Asset review with confidence, proof
records, collective validation, review workflow, RDA, RWA alignment, and
EcoChain readiness.

## Boundary

No operational review engine, automated reasoning, scoring, or API is
implemented.
