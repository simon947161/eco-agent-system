# Evidence Asset Confidence Model

## Purpose

The Evidence Asset Confidence Model defines how confidence may be represented
for Evidence Assets.

## Confidence Conditions

Evidence Asset confidence should consider:

- evidence quality
- source traceability
- proof record status
- independent confirmation
- unresolved conflict
- uncertainty
- collective validation signals
- revision history
- confidence decay

## Confidence Is Not Truth

Confidence describes current support.

It does not prove absolute truth and does not permanently certify an asset.

## Confidence Changes

Confidence may increase, decrease, decay, or remain unresolved as new evidence
appears.

## Boundary

No confidence scoring engine, mathematical model, automated confidence update,
or certification system is added.
