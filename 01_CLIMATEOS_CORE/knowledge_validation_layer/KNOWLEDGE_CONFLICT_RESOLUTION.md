# Knowledge Conflict Resolution

## Purpose

This document defines how conflicting Knowledge Objects should coexist before
review.

## Conflict Types

- source conflict
- version conflict
- interpretation conflict
- maturity conflict
- provider conflict
- evidence relationship conflict
- forecast or planning assumption conflict

## Principles

- preserve conflicting objects until reviewed
- do not silently overwrite knowledge
- identify affected layers
- identify downstream risks
- require human review for high-impact conflicts
- allow superseded knowledge to remain traceable

## Boundary

No automated conflict detection or resolution is implemented.
