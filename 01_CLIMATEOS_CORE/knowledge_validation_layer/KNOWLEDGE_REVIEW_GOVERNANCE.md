# Knowledge Review Governance

## Purpose

Knowledge Review Governance defines principles for responsible Knowledge Object
review.

## Principles

- preserve source traceability
- keep provider boundaries visible
- distinguish knowledge from evidence
- distinguish forecast candidates from truth
- preserve conflicting knowledge until reviewed
- require human review for high-impact use
- avoid automated validation claims

## Relationship To Task100

Task79 prepares knowledge review concepts that Task100 may later integrate into
Validation Architecture.

## Boundary

No governance runtime, approval system, or automated decision system is
implemented.
