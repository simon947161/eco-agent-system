# Collective Attestation Model

## Purpose

This model describes collective, crowd, community, and multi-agent signals.

## Core Principle

Many people or many agents saying something does not automatically make it
true.

Collective expression is still meaningful and may indicate shared observation,
shared concern, emerging risk, public perception, collective experience,
governance pressure, social signal, or coordinated misinformation risk.

## Distinction

```text
Collective Signal != Validated Reality
```

Collective signal should be treated as a high-attention evidence candidate, not
automatic proof.

## Required Fields

- number of observers
- observer diversity
- geographic spread
- timing pattern
- independence level
- repetition frequency
- agreement strength
- disagreement or dissent
- possible coordination risk
- supporting physical evidence
- supporting process evidence
- supporting governance evidence
- confidence level

## Boundary

No crowd validation, social scoring, or automated consensus engine is
implemented.
