# Reality Types

## Natural Reality

Natural Reality describes environmental conditions and processes.

Examples:

- rainfall
- temperature
- soil moisture
- vegetation
- hydrology
- fire condition
- ecosystem change

## Operational Reality

Operational Reality describes projects, infrastructure, work, production, and
maintenance processes.

Examples:

- project schedule
- construction record
- procurement record
- production record
- maintenance record
- infrastructure operation
- resource consumption

## Governance Reality

Governance Reality describes review, approval, public process, and decision
records.

Examples:

- approval
- audit
- compliance review
- inspection
- policy implementation
- meeting record
- public consultation
- governance decision

## Boundary

These categories are conceptual and may be expanded later.
