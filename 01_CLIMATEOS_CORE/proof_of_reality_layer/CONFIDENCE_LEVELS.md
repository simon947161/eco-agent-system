# Confidence Levels

## Purpose

Confidence levels describe the review status of a Reality Claim or Proof
Record.

## Levels

- Unverified
- Single-Source Claim
- Protected Minority Signal
- Collective Signal
- Partially Supported
- Cross-Checked
- Validated
- High Confidence
- Disputed
- Contradicted
- Rejected

## Principle

Confidence may change over time as new evidence arrives.

## Boundary

No confidence scoring or automatic update mechanism is implemented.
