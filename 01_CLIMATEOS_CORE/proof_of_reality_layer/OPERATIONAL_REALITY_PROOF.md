# Operational Reality Proof

## Purpose

Operational Reality Proof concerns work, projects, maintenance, infrastructure,
production, and resource use.

## Examples

- construction has begun
- procurement occurred
- maintenance failure risk remains unresolved
- production record indicates increased resource consumption
- infrastructure operation changed

## Evidence Sources

- project schedules
- construction records
- operational logs
- maintenance records
- sensor records
- worker testimony

## Boundary

No project management, compliance, or operational control system is implemented.
