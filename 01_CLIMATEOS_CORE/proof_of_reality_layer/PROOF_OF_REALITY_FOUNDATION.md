# Proof of Reality Foundation

## Purpose

Proof of Reality defines how ClimateOS may transform claim candidates into
structured proof records through evidence packaging, cross-validation,
confidence classification, and traceability.

## Core Principle

Reality claims are not accepted or rejected by default.

They are routed into review.

```text
Reality Claim Candidate
-> Evidence Package
-> Cross-Validation
-> Confidence Level
-> Proof Record
```

## Supported Reality Sources

- Natural reality
- Operational reality
- Governance reality
- Human testimony
- Agent signals
- Collective observations

## Boundary

This foundation defines proof logic only. It does not implement validation,
forecasting, blockchain, tokens, or automated decisions.
