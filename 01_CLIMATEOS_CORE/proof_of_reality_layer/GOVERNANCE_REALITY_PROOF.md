# Governance Reality Proof

## Purpose

Governance Reality Proof concerns approvals, audits, public processes,
inspections, policies, and governance decisions.

## Examples

- approval was granted
- audit identified a risk
- inspection occurred
- public consultation was recorded
- governance decision changed implementation

## Evidence Sources

- approval records
- audit reports
- compliance reviews
- inspection records
- meeting notes
- policy implementation records

## Boundary

No regulatory compliance engine or approval system is implemented.
