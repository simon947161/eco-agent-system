# Proof to EcoChain

## Purpose

This document explains how proof records may relate to EcoChain.

EcoChain should not record every raw claim.

EcoChain may record:

- validated proof records
- disputed proof records
- whistleblower protected claim references
- collective signal references
- evidence asset references
- validation history
- confidence updates

## EcoChain Definition

EcoChain remains a Validated Evidence Chain.

## Boundary

No blockchain implementation, token model, smart contract, or EcoChain runtime
is implemented.
