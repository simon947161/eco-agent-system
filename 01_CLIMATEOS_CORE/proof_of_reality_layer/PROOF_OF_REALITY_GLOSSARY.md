# Proof of Reality Glossary

## Reality Claim

A statement about a real-world condition, event, process, change, decision,
risk, or outcome.

## Reality Claim Candidate

An unverified claim that requires evidence support, cross-validation,
confidence classification, and traceability.

## Evidence Package

A structured evidence bundle supporting a Reality Claim.

## Cross-Validation

Checking a claim against multiple independent or semi-independent sources.

## Protected Reality Claim Candidate

A minority, first-observer, whistleblower, or lone-witness signal that should
not be erased because it lacks immediate majority support.

## Collective Signal

A crowd, community, or multi-agent signal that deserves attention but is not
automatic truth.

## Proof Record

A structured record created after a Reality Claim has been reviewed through
evidence packaging and validation.

## Evidence Asset

Reality + Data + Evidence + Context + Validation + History + Confidence.

## EcoChain

Validated Evidence Chain.
