# Whistleblower Signal Model

## Purpose

This model protects important minority or lone-witness signals from being erased
because they lack immediate majority support.

## Signal Types

- Whistleblower Signal
- Lone Witness Signal
- First Observer Signal
- Minority Warning Signal

## Core Principle

A single witness should not be dismissed only because they are alone.

A single witness should also not be treated as automatic truth.

Whistleblower signals should be classified as Protected Reality Claim
Candidates and routed into cross-validation.

## Required Fields

- claimant or observer type
- time
- location
- claim content
- risk level
- urgency level
- available supporting evidence
- possible retaliation or suppression risk
- required validation sources
- confidence level
- unresolved uncertainty

## Examples

- a worker reports unsafe construction practice
- a local resident reports pollution
- a farmer reports unusual water stress
- a scientist reports early ecological anomaly
- a lone explorer reports a remote event
- a future space traveller reports an event where no other human witness exists

## Boundary

No whistleblower platform, legal protection system, or automated escalation is
implemented.
