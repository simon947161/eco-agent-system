# Human Testimony Proof

## Purpose

Human testimony may provide important Reality Claim Candidates.

## Sources

- local observers
- farmers
- workers
- researchers
- planners
- community members
- whistleblowers
- NGOs
- government staff

## Principle

Human testimony is not inherently true and not inherently false.

It should be preserved, protected where necessary, and routed into evidence
packaging and cross-validation.

## Boundary

No testimony intake, identity protection, or legal process is implemented.
