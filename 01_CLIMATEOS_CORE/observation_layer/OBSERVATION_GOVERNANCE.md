# Observation Governance

## Data Ownership

Observation records should identify who created the record, who owns the
source material, and who can approve reuse. Community observations may include
local knowledge that requires care and consent.

## Transparency

Records should preserve date, time, place, observer, source type, uncertainty,
limitations, and review status. Missing information should remain visible.

## Versioning

Observation records should be versioned. Corrections should explain what
changed, when it changed, and why.

## Human Review

Observation records are not automatically evidence. Human review is required
before records are used as evidence candidates, evidence, validation material,
or governance inputs.

## Community Participation

Community observation can capture place-based signals that formal data sources
miss. Participation should respect privacy, attribution, cultural context, and
local interpretation.

## Limits

Governance guidance does not implement a governance runtime, approval system,
legal framework, or automated decision process.
