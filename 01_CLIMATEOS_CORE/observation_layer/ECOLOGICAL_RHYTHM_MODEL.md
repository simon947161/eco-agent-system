# Ecological Rhythm Model

## Purpose

The Ecological Rhythm Model gives observation records a simple daily timing
structure. It helps compare observations made at similar times of day without
performing calculations.

## Morning Recovery

**Time window:** 06:00-08:00

**Purpose:** observe overnight recovery, morning temperature, dew, humidity,
vegetation freshness, animal activity, and early community observations.

## Midday Stress

**Time window:** 13:00-15:00

**Purpose:** observe heat, wind, water stress, shade, vegetation wilting,
surface dryness, and human-perceived stress conditions.

## Evening Release

**Time window:** 20:00-22:00

**Purpose:** observe cooling, wind change, moisture return, smoke or haze,
nightfall transition, and recovery signals.

## Limits

This model provides timing labels only. It does not calculate stress, recovery,
risk, or ecological health.
