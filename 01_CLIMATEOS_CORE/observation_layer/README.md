# ClimateOS Observation Layer V1

## Purpose

The Observation Layer preserves structured environmental observations across
multiple places and long periods. It is the first real-world evidence source
for ClimateOS.

## Strategic Flow

```text
Observation
  |
  v
Evidence
  |
  v
Knowledge
  |
  v
Radar
  |
  v
Validation
  |
  v
Governance
```

Observation records do not become evidence automatically. They become evidence
candidates that require review, context, provenance, and governance.
