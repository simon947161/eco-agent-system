# Review Synthesis Model

## Purpose

The Review Synthesis Model defines how ClimateOS may conceptually combine
multiple inputs into a current evidence-supported judgment.

## Synthesis

Synthesis means comparing inputs across evidence, validation, knowledge,
forecast, relationship analysis, confidence, and conflict.

## Synthesis Considerations

ClimateOS review may consider:

- which evidence is strongest
- which evidence is weakest
- which claims are contradicted
- which assumptions are shared
- which sources are independent
- whether confidence should increase or decrease
- whether minority signals require escalation
- whether previous review positions need revision

## Evidence Consistency

The Review Engine should seek the most evidence-consistent judgment currently
available, not the most popular, convenient, or authoritative judgment.

## Relationship To Collective Validation

Collective Validation contributes participant signals and reviewed judgment.

The Review Engine integrates those signals with other inputs and keeps the
review open to revision.

## Boundary

No synthesis algorithm, automated reasoning, scoring model, or executable
review process is created.
