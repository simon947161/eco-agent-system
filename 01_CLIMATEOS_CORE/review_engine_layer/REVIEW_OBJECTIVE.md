# Review Objective

## Purpose

This document states the objective of the ClimateOS Review Engine.

## Objective Statement

ClimateOS is designed to continuously improve its understanding of
relationships in natural systems through observation, evidence, validation,
scientific knowledge, and collective learning.

Its objective is to produce the most evidence-consistent judgment available at
a given point in time while remaining continuously open to revision whenever new
evidence appears.

## What Review Should Do

- connect observation, knowledge, evidence, validation, and confidence
- preserve uncertainty
- preserve disagreement
- compare current claims with new evidence
- update review positions when evidence improves
- support governance outputs without replacing human action

## What Review Should Not Do

- claim absolute truth
- treat consensus as automatic truth
- treat hierarchy as proof
- replace EcoEngine relationship analysis
- replace human decisions
- implement automated decision-making

## Boundary

This is a design objective, not an implemented runtime capability.
