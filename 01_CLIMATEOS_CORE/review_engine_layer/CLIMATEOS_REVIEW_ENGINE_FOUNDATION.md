# ClimateOS Review Engine Foundation

## Purpose

The ClimateOS Review Engine Foundation defines ClimateOS as the integrated
review architecture for forming the best evidence-supported judgment available
at a given point in time.

This is not a human review system, agent voting system, decision engine, or
automated reasoning implementation.

## Core Principle

ClimateOS itself is the Reviewer.

Everything else is an input.

Inputs may include:

- Observation
- Knowledge
- Evidence
- Reality Claims
- Forecast Candidates
- Earth Intelligence Providers
- Human Experience
- Scientific Knowledge
- Expert Opinion
- Community Observation
- AI Agent Outputs
- Minority Signals
- Whistleblower Signals

None of these inputs is final authority by itself.

## Design Philosophy

ClimateOS is designed to continuously improve its understanding of
relationships in natural systems through observation, evidence, validation,
scientific knowledge, and collective learning.

Its objective is to produce the most evidence-consistent judgment available at
a given point in time while remaining continuously open to revision whenever new
evidence appears.

## Review Principles

- Review is continuous.
- Truth is never assumed.
- Confidence evolves.
- Better evidence updates previous conclusions.
- ClimateOS is revision-oriented, not certainty-oriented.

## Relationship To EcoEngine

EcoEngine analyses relationships.

ClimateOS Review Engine performs integrated system-level review based upon those
relationships and other evidence inputs.

ClimateOS does not replace EcoEngine. EcoEngine does not replace ClimateOS.

## Boundary

This document establishes architecture only. It does not implement runtime
review, automated reasoning, scoring, voting, APIs, or decisions.
