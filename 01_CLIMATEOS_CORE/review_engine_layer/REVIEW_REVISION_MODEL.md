# Review Revision Model

## Purpose

The Review Revision Model defines ClimateOS as revision-oriented rather than
certainty-oriented.

## Revision Principle

Every review position remains open to revision when better evidence appears.

## Revision Triggers

Revision may be needed when:

- new observations appear
- evidence quality changes
- assumptions fail
- forecast candidates are validated or invalidated
- Earth Intelligence providers disagree
- EcoEngine relationship analysis changes
- minority or whistleblower signals are confirmed
- confidence decays
- governance context changes

## Revision Outcomes

Revision may produce:

- strengthened confidence
- weakened confidence
- unresolved status
- updated evidence package
- conflict escalation
- changed recommendation
- updated governance output

## Revision Memory

ClimateOS should preserve previous review positions and explain why they
changed. This supports traceability and learning.

## Boundary

No revision engine, versioning runtime, automated update system, or decision
workflow is implemented.
