# Review Glossary

## Review Engine

The ClimateOS architecture responsible for forming the best
evidence-supported judgment currently available from all relevant inputs.

## Reviewer

In this foundation, ClimateOS itself is the Reviewer. Humans, AI agents,
providers, and other contributors are inputs to review.

## Review Input

Any observation, knowledge object, evidence package, reality claim, forecast
candidate, scientific knowledge, human experience, agent output, minority
signal, or provider output that may inform review.

## Review Position

The current evidence-supported judgment available at a given time.

## Review Synthesis

The conceptual process of comparing inputs, evidence, confidence, uncertainty,
and conflict to form a review position.

## Review Revision

The update of a review position when better evidence, new uncertainty, or
conflicting information appears.

## Evidence-consistent Judgment

A judgment that best fits the available reviewed evidence while preserving
uncertainty and remaining open to revision.

## Revision-oriented

Designed to improve over time rather than declaring permanent certainty.
