# Review Engine Model

## Purpose

The Review Engine Model defines the conceptual role of ClimateOS as an
integrated reviewer.

## Definition

The Review Engine is the ClimateOS architecture that compares inputs, evaluates
evidence consistency, tracks uncertainty, preserves disagreement, and maintains
the current best review position.

## Review Is Not Authority

The Review Engine does not assign permanent authority to:

- institutions
- humans
- AI agents
- forecast providers
- observation providers
- scientific models
- community reports

Each may contribute evidence, interpretation, or signals.

## Review Object

A Review Object may include:

- a Reality Claim
- an Evidence Asset
- a Knowledge Object
- a Forecast Candidate
- a relationship interpretation
- a governance concern
- a confidence update
- a disputed conclusion

## Review Position

A Review Position is the current evidence-supported judgment available after
considering inputs, validation, confidence, conflict, and revision history.

## Boundary

No executable reviewer, decision engine, ranking system, scoring system, or
automated conclusion mechanism is created.
