# Knowledge Provider Glossary

## Knowledge Provider

A source or platform that may supply knowledge into ClimateOS.

## Knowledge Adapter

A future connector concept that maps provider records into ClimateOS Knowledge
Object concepts.

## Knowledge Provider Interface

The conceptual boundary between provider platforms and the ClimateOS Knowledge
Runtime.

## Provider Registry

A conceptual inventory of provider IDs, types, status, versions, scopes,
capabilities, and knowledge categories.

## Manual Import

Human-guided movement of knowledge from a provider into ClimateOS.

## Scheduled Import

Future planned import on a schedule. Not implemented in Task74.

## Runtime Import

Future runtime retrieval or import. Not implemented in Task74.

## Event-Driven Synchronization

Future synchronization triggered by provider events. Not implemented in Task74.

## Obsidian

A future Knowledge Provider concept, not an implemented integration.
