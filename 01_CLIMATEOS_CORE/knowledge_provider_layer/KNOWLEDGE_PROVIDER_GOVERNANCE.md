# Knowledge Provider Governance

## Purpose

Knowledge provider governance defines review principles for provider-supplied
knowledge.

## Governance Principles

- preserve source traceability
- keep provider boundaries clear
- avoid vendor lock-in
- avoid treating imported knowledge as automatically validated
- preserve citation requirements
- preserve version history
- require human review for high-impact knowledge
- distinguish internal, external, research, and runtime knowledge

## Relationship To Validation

Provider knowledge may support validation, but provider knowledge is not
validated evidence by default.

## Boundary

This document does not implement access control, compliance systems, automated
approval, or policy enforcement.
