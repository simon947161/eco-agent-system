# ScenarioOS

## Purpose

Organise baseline, future, policy, and risk scenarios as reviewable inputs
rather than predictions or recommendations.

## Future Scope

Scenario provenance, assumptions, versions, uncertainty, comparison boundaries,
and compatibility with ClimateOS reports.

## Example Future Agents

Baseline Scenario Curator, Future Scenario Builder, Policy Scenario Reviewer,
and Risk Scenario Coordinator.
